# House Moffet

_Barony of Ironmere, County Blackvale_  
**“Stand. Defend. Endure.”**

---

## I. Overview

House Moffet is a minor but highly respected baronial house sworn to the Counts of Blackvale. Though neither wealthy nor politically dominant, they are renowned throughout Gorundia for discipline, reliability, and an unyielding adherence to martial honor.

Where other houses seek glory, House Moffet is given responsibility.

> “If you want a charge, send a knight.  
> If you want a line to hold, send a Moffet.”

---

## II. Origins — The Ironbound

Before Gorundia was a kingdom, the clans that would become it gathered in armed distrust to negotiate, feud, and decide war.

These meetings took place in a neutral ground known as:

**The Circle**

No chief entered armed.

No chief trusted the others to keep that rule.

---

### The Exception

One family alone stood within the Circle bearing arms.

They were known as:

**The Ironbound**

---

### Role of the Ironbound

- Armed observers within the Circle
- Enforcers of oath and peace
- Answerable to no single clan

If any chief broke the sanctity of the Circle:

> The Ironbound struck them down without hesitation.

---

### Legacy

This tradition formed the foundation of House Moffet’s identity:

- Law above personality
- Duty above allegiance
- Oath above comfort

The name _Moffet_ is believed to derive from an old Oeridian root meaning:

> “One who stands between blades.”

---

## III. Lands and Seat

**Seat:** Ironmere Hold  
**Domain:** Flatlands surrounding the Iron Mere (dark inland water)

---

### Terrain

- Open, flat, highly visible ground
- Ideal for:
    - horse breeding
    - rapid troop movement
- Poor for:
    - concealment
    - irregular warfare

---

### Ironmere Hold

- Granite construction
- Minimal ornamentation
- Built for defense, not display

Key features:

- **Tower of Vigil** — overlooking the Mere
- **Chamber of Seven Shields** — ancestral relic hall

---

## IV. Military Doctrine

House Moffet is famous for disciplined infantry, despite maintaining excellent horses.

---

### Core Doctrine

> “We ride to the field. We win it on foot.”

---

### Battlefield Identity

- Mounted mobility, **dismounted combat**
- Shield-wall discipline
- Controlled withdrawals
- Terrain exploitation

---

### Known For

- Holding flanks under extreme pressure
- Maintaining cohesion under retreat
- Executing feigned withdrawals without collapse

---

### Reputation

- Nobles: “Reliable. Rigid.”
- Soldiers: “If they’re still standing, we’re not dead yet.”

---

## V. The Battle of Red Gorge (1190 OR)

House Moffet’s reputation was cemented at [[The Battle of Red Gorge]].

---

### The Maneuver

Under Baron Arlen Moffet, the house executed a **feigned retreat** on the Gorundian right flank, luring Dominion forces into a fatal kill zone.

This maneuver enabled the encirclement that broke the Dominion advance.

---

### [[Halvren Moffet]] — “He Held the Lie”

A cousin of the main line, **Halvren Moffet** commanded the rearguard.

- Held position longer than ordered
- Allowed visible collapse to sell the retreat
- Drew the Dominion fully into pursuit

He and his men were overrun.

None survived.

---

### House Memory

> “Halvren held the lie.”

---

### Heraldic Reward

For their decisive role in the battle, House Moffet was granted the rare right to bear **supporters**.

---

## VI. Heraldry

**Arms:**  
_Sable, a tower Or; on a chief Or._

**Crest:**  
A tower Or rising from a black iron coronet

**Supporters:**  
Two war-hounds sable, collared Or

**Mantling:**  
Sable doubled Or

---

### Meaning

- **Tower:** Defense, endurance, Ironmere Hold
- **Sable:** Iron, austerity
- **Or:** Honor, loyalty

---

### Significance

Supporters are rarely granted to baronial houses.

Their presence marks House Moffet as:

> A house that has decided a battle.

---

## VII. Feudal Position

**Liege:** House Bordella (Blackvale)  
**Overlord (former):** Duke Wilhelm Vogel

---

### Relationship to Blackvale

- Loyal but culturally distinct
- Bordella: wealth, ambition, politics
- Moffet: discipline, restraint, martial duty

---

### Current Political Stance

House Moffet has formally recognized the authority of the Lord Marshal while carefully avoiding binding oaths.

This reflects:

- loyalty to law, not individuals
- a cautious repositioning after Vogel’s fall

---

## VIII. The Vogel Years

Following the collapse of Bertram Moffet’s arranged marriage to House Vogel, House Moffet suffered a severe political blow.

---

### Known Consequences

- Loss of favor at ducal level
- Increased scrutiny
- Forced political concessions

Baron Ewan Moffet maintained the survival of the barony through compromise.

---

### Unspoken Reality

The extent of those compromises remains unclear.

Even within the house, the full truth is not openly discussed.

---

## IX. Internal Culture

House Moffet is guided by four principles:

---

### Honor Unsullied

No deception in oath or combat.

### Family is Fortress

The house protects its own above all else.

### Duty Above Comfort

What must be done is done.

### Strength Quiet, Not Loud

Deeds matter more than words.

## X. The Family

### [[Ewan Moffet|Baron Ewan Moffet]]

Austere, disciplined, and rigidly honorable. A war hero who believes in the code above all things—though recent years have tested that belief.

---

### [[Ayla Moffet|Baroness Ayla Moffet]]

Elegant and calculating. Oversees logistics and diplomacy. The quiet stabilizing force of Ironmere.

---

### [[Quinn Moffet|Lady Quinn Moffet (“Mother Hound”)]]

Eldest daughter. Fierce, blunt, and utterly protective. A battlefield presence in her own right.

---

### [[Elise Moffet|Lady Elise Moffet]]

Composed and perceptive. Manages financial and social ties with precision.

---

### [[Mara Moffet|Maester Mara Moffet]]

House mage of [[Orin Kharne]].

Brilliant, beautiful, and still growing into the reality of her own limits. Accustomed to being the smartest person in the room, but beginning to learn that knowledge alone is not enough.

---

### [[Bertram|Knight-Marshal Bertram Moffet]]

Disowned son, now returned to prominence. His actions triggered the house’s greatest recent crisis—and its most uncertain future.
