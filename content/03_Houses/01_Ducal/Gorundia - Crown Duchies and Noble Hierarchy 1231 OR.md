# Gorundia — Crown, Duchies, and Noble Hierarchy, 1231 OR

> [!important] Canon Status
> This article records the current political hierarchy after the fall of Wilhelm Vogel and the opening of the Steel Crusade.
>
> Earlier descriptions remain useful for geography, family history, and estate character, but any earlier statement naming Wilhelm Vogel, Ponzio Bordella, Lynwenden Cron, Remy DeSalle, or Aldren Veymar as an untroubled current ruler is superseded here.
>
> Where the Crown has not completed judgment or settlement, the title is marked **unsettled** rather than assigned by inference.

## Kingdom at a Glance

**Realm:** Kingdom of Gorundia  
**Reigning House:** House Ryomar  
**King:** [[Rimmand II|King Rimmand II Ryomar]], born Priam Ryomar  
**Royal Heir:** [[Porthos Ryomar|Crown Prince Porthos Ryomar]], acknowledged paladin of Pelor  
**Capital and Crown Demesne:** Royal domain surrounding the capital and principal royal estates  
**Current Political Condition:** The Crown is intact and unusually assertive. Grevandar is under post-Vogel settlement; Blackvale has been refounded under a Crown-appointed countess; several nobles remain under judgment or review.

# The Crown

## House Ryomar

**Sovereign:** [[Rimmand II|King Rimmand II Ryomar]]  
**Birth Name:** Priam Ryomar  
**Status:** Living; reigning  
**Seat:** The Royal Palace and Crown Demesne  
**Heir:** [[Porthos Ryomar|Crown Prince Porthos Ryomar]]  
**Late Queen:** Queen Nelessa Gellbeck Ryomar, mother of Porthos  
**Demesne Estimate:** 18,704 sq. miles; population approximately 448,896

King Rimmand II survived the Vogel conspiracy against his life. The attempted regicide was exposed before completion, and the King subsequently presided over the Steel Purge and declared the Steel Crusade.

Porthos remains the lawful royal heir. His conversion and investiture as a paladin of Pelor are genuine and were witnessed publicly during the Lord’s Muster. This has strengthened his religious and political standing beyond the ordinary authority of a crown prince.

## Principal Royal Officers Relevant to the Hierarchy

- **[[Beringer Amadis]]:** Lord Marshal of Gorundia; acknowledged natural son of Galmyr Vogel and therefore a blood claimant to the broken Vogel line. He has not claimed the Duchy of Grevandar.
- **[[Wonderful]]:** Lord Mayor of Bard’s Gate. Municipal authority is separate from the landed hierarchy recorded below.
- **[[Maester Norwin Stoneyeye]]:** Royal Archivist in Ordinary; not a landed noble, but central to certification of disputed records and blood claims.

**Duchess:** Duchess Maris Allaric  
**Seat:** Araisal Keep

### Barony of Fenwyck

**Baron of Record:** Baron Aldren Veymar  
**Baroness:** Baroness Roselle Veymar  
**Seat:** Fenwyck Tower  
**Issue:** None  
**Estimate:** 4,676 sq. miles; population approximately 101,002  
**Current Status:** **Title under review; barony functionally leaderless**

Baron Aldren Veymar was arrested during the Steel Purge for harboring unlawful alchemists, distributing poison through Gorundian cities, and profiting from addiction. No final sentence, attainder, or lawful successor has yet been entered in the current record.

Until judgment is completed, Fenwyck remains under the superior authority of Count Vorias Serrin and House Allaric, with Crown scrutiny over its accounts, alchemical holdings, and succession.

#### House Veymar Arms

The senior arms associated with the barony and House Veymar are:

> **Argent, in base water barry wavy Azure and Argent, thereover a bridge embattled Sable masoned Argent, in chief a mullet of eight points Gules.**

#### House Fenwick, Cadet of Veymar

House Fenwick bears the Veymar arms differenced by a blue engrailed border:

> **Argent, in base water barry wavy Azure and Argent, thereover a bridge embattled Sable masoned Argent, in chief a mullet of eight points Gules; all within a bordure engrailed Azure.**

**Current Cadet Line:**

- Ser Aldren Fenwick, head of the cadet branch
- Ser Joryn Fenwick, his heir
- Perin Fenwick, second son
- Sir Osric Fenwick, Aldren’s younger brother

The Fenwick line is a plausible collateral successor if Baron Veymar is deposed without attainder. It is not yet the recognized baronial line. If the title is declared forfeit rather than merely vacant, the barony may instead escheat upward to Veymont, Araisal, or the Crown.

**Known Cadet Differencing:**

- Ser Joryn Fenwick: a label of three points Azure
- Perin Fenwick: a crescent Gules beneath the chief star
- Sir Osric Fenwick: a crescent Gules at the bridge, accompanied by a martlet Sable

# Duchy of Grevandar

## Ducal Dignity and Former Vogel Demesne

**Former Duke:** Wilhelm Vogel, deceased  
**Former Seat:** Castle Direhold  
**Current Duke:** None recognized  
**Current Status:** **Ducal title unsettled; former Vogel demesne under Crown seizure, military supervision, and continuing investigation**  
**Former Ducal Demesne Estimate:** 18,704 sq. miles; population approximately 516,230  
**Whole Duchy Estimate:** 56,112 sq. miles; population approximately 1,548,682

Wilhelm Vogel died in trial by combat against Lord Marshal Beringer Amadis on 16 Readying 1231 OR. His body was damaged beyond ordinary resurrection. He left no acknowledged legitimate issue.

The Duchy of Grevandar continues to exist as a territorial and legal unit, but House Vogel no longer constitutes a functioning ducal government. No permanent successor has been recognized. Castle Direhold, the former ducal treasury, military stores, records, and occult sites are subject to Crown inventory and investigation.

The Ashen Wing gathers Vogel loyalists and Raven Queen hardliners around promises that Wilhelm will return. This is a rebellion, cultic expectation, or both. It is not a lawful succession.

## County of Wyrmlea

**Countess:** [[Zara Vallytrex|Countess Zara Vallytrex]]  
**Seat:** Wyrmwatch Tower  
**Heir:** Not established  
**Estimate:** 7,014 sq. miles; population approximately 193,585

Wyrmlea controls rich quarries, deep mines, major roads, and Gorundia’s largest dwarven enclave.

Zara has been purified of the corruption that bound her to the Raven Queen’s designs. She reaffirmed her feudal and sacred oaths before King Rimmand II in the Temple of Heironeous and was publicly cleared by the Crown. Her former service in Wilhelm’s orbit is officially treated as arising from feudal obligation rather than voluntary adherence to his treason.

Her guilt concerning the dead of the arena remains personal and unresolved. It does not presently alter her title.

## County of Blackvale

**Countess:** [[Dana Sylvanranth|Countess Dana Sylvanranth]], formerly Dana Freeblade  
**Seat:** Blackvale Citadel  
**Heir:** None established  
**Estimate:** 7,014 sq. miles; population approximately 193,585  
**Status:** Crown refoundation following the fall of House Bordella

Blackvale is Gorundia’s agricultural heart. The Bordella comital line was removed after the Vogel conspiracy and Steel Purge. Ponzio Bordella was executed; Cornelia and Allec Bordella were stripped of independent political authority and placed under Sir Bertram Moffet’s responsibility, later residing in Zara’s lands.

Dana was appointed Countess of Blackvale by the Crown. Her Dragonguard functions as her personal military force, formally household troops but operationally independent and disciplined under its own internal command.

**Arms of the New County Line:**

> **Sable, a gauntlet Or maintaining a torch Or enflamed Tenné.**

Dana has no settled heir. Blackvale’s succession is therefore a live political concern rather than a decorative note for heralds to argue about over lunch.

### Barony of Thunderhold

**Baroness:** Baroness Nerys Cron  
**Former Baron:** Lynwenden Cron, executed  
**Seat:** Thunderhold Fortress  
**Heir:** Not established  
**Estimate:** 7,014 sq. miles; population approximately 193,585

House Cron’s hidden descent from the old Cranne or Crawne cadet line of House Vogel is known only to a handful of archivists and is not part of the public succession record.

# Titles Currently Unsettled or Under Review

## Duchy of Grevandar

- Wilhelm Vogel is dead without legitimate issue.
- No new duke has been recognized.
- The former Vogel demesne and Direhold are under Crown seizure and investigation.
- Beringer Amadis, the Crown, the hidden Cron/Crawn line, and secret surviving Vogel blood each present different kinds of claim.
- None has been converted into a settled ducal succession.

## Barony of Fenwyck

- Aldren Veymar is under arrest.
- No issue exists from Aldren and Roselle.
- House Fenwick is the nearest identified cadet claimant.
- The result depends upon whether Aldren is restored, deposed, or attainted.

## County of Blackvale

- The title itself is settled under Dana Sylvanranth.
- Her personal succession is not.
- The old Bordella comital claim is broken and is not treated as dormant inheritance.

## Barony of Thunderhold

- Nerys Cron remains baroness.
- No heir has been entered in the current record.
- The secret Cron/Crawn descent may become relevant only if it is proved and politically recognized.

## County of Veymont

- Serrin remains count in the absence of a recorded forfeiture.
- His arrest and censure leave the practical limits of his authority subject to Crown oversight.

# Canon Locks

- King Rimmand II lives and reigns.
- Porthos remains Crown Prince and is genuinely a paladin of Pelor.
- Wilhelm Vogel is dead and cannot be restored through ordinary resurrection.
- House Vogel no longer rules Grevandar as a functioning ducal house.
- The exact permanent disposition of the Grevandar ducal title remains unresolved.
- Dana Sylvanranth is Countess of Blackvale.
- Zara Vallytrex is Countess of Wyrmlea, purified, oath-reaffirmed, and publicly cleared.
- Lynwenden Cron is dead; Nerys Cron remains.
- Ponzio Bordella is dead.
- Jareth DeSalle is Baron of Darkstone.
- Aldren Veymar is under arrest; Fenwyck’s succession is unresolved.
