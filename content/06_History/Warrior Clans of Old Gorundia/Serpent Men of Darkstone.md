House DeSalle began as the Serpent Men of Darkstone, an old hill-clan charged with guarding the iron roads and mine-mouths before Gorundia’s noble hierarchy fully hardened. Their chiefs bore a red shield painted with a golden serpent, symbolizing the hidden iron-veins beneath the red earth and the oath to strike any who broke the peace of the hills.

When the early Gorundian kings brought the hill-clans into the realm, the DeSalle ancestor bent the knee but did not surrender the serpent. Instead, the Crown granted the family a gold border, marking the old clan badge as lawful arms.

So the arms mean:

The serpent still strikes, but within the king’s law.
