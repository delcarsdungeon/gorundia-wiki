# Gorundian Civil War - Duchies Against Grevandar

## Strategic Summary

The civil war is no longer best understood as a single front.

It is the other three duchies of Gorundia deciding how to act against the collapse, corruption, and revolt of the fourth: [[Grevandar]].

[[House Vogel]] has fallen from lawful ducal power into chaos, Raven Queen loyalism, opportunistic rebellion, infernal contamination, and armed holdout politics. Some of Grevandar is still loyal to the Crown. Some is frightened. Some is openly hostile. Some is waiting to see who wins, because apparently cowardice becomes strategy if you own enough land.

The Crown’s problem is not simply defeating Vogel remnants.

The Crown must:

- hold [[Blackvale]]    
- contain [[Direhold]]    
- prevent the [[Iron Covenant]] from breaking [[Ironmere]]    
- contain the infernal lattice in the south    
- keep [[Thunderhold]] stable under new rule    
- keep loyalist supply moving through chaos    
- prevent serf-liberation rhetoric from becoming uncontrolled revolt    
- prevent the Dominion from exploiting exposed borders    
- prevent allied duchies from overextending themselves while helping    

This is a civil war fought through roads, granaries, oaths, plague sites, frightened vassals, and very tired people pretending logistics are not the actual monster.

## Core Frame

### Grevandar

Grevandar is the broken duchy.

It contains:

- fallen Vogel authority    
- Raven Queen loyalist ideology    
- armed holdouts    
- infernal lattice sites    
- serfdom backlash    
- Bordella chaos    
- Cron remnants    
- Iron Covenant activity    
- plague-cult fallout    
- loyalist pockets trying to survive    

### Araisal / House Allaric

Araisal is supporting the Crown mostly through [[Sir Beringer Amadis]] and the Lord Marshal's host.

House Allaric has given what ducal force it can spare to Beringer, but the duchy itself is not an infinite reservoir.

Araisal’s internal limits matter:

- [[Vorias Serrin]] is still trying to get his forces and obligations organized.    
- [[County Blackbriar]] has retained much of its own strength because of Dominion probes.    
- [[Barony Drakemoor]] and [[Barony Ashwyn]] have provided a major portion of Beringer’s host.    
- [[Barony Darkstone]] was not asked, but Beringer is passing through DeSalle territory.    
- [[Barony Fenwyck]] is functionally leaderless and disorganized after Baron Aldren Veymar’s disgrace and removal.    

Araisal’s posture:

> Support Beringer, but do not strip the northeast bare.

### Royonad / House Orenic

Royonad is holding the Dominion border.

House Orenic has also sent assistance to [[Thunderhold]], but that help is under-supplied and creates almost as much logistical strain as relief.

Existing commitments:

- hold the border against Dominion pressure    
- keep Royonad secure    
- maintain Orenic honor by aiding the Crown    
- support Thunderhold with limited detachments    
- keep Baron Vorochev’s prior attachment to Beringer politically visible    

Royonad’s posture:

> Guard the frontier, help where honor demands, and try not to bleed dry while doing both.

### Ravenster

Ravenster has provided as much cavalry as it can to the direct royal force.

Its faster elements are already with or supporting the King’s operation in [[Blackvale]]. Slower Ravenster units are now marching south.

Existing commitments:

- cavalry to the direct Crown force    
- slower infantry and support elements moving south    
- [[Baron Vorthal]] previously joined Beringer’s host    
- Ravenster honor and religious legitimacy strongly tied to the Crown response    

Ravenster’s posture:

> The shield of the Crown has moved. The slower weight is coming behind it.

## Grevandar Internal Breakdown

## Vogel Proper

### Status

Chaos, revolt, and spiritual contamination.

Vogel proper is no longer functioning as a coherent lawful ducal command. Pieces of it have fallen under Raven Queen loyalist ideology, armed holdout rhetoric, or outright revolt.

Major hostile or unstable forces include:

- [[Ashen Wing]]    
- Raven Queen loyalists    
- former Vogel road troops    
- Bordella household blades    
- Cron fugitives    
- DeSalle collaborators    
- Iron Covenant remnants    
- frightened officials trying to survive the paperwork of treason    

### Ashen Wing

The [[Ashen Wing]] are organized Vogel loyalists using mourning symbolism and resurrection language.

Observed marks and behavior:

- damaged Vogel standards    
- black feathers    
- soot-darkened sword hands    
- old badges wrapped in black thread    
- refusal to call themselves rebels    
- doctrine around the Shadowed Seat or Interregnum of Shadow    
- village burnings and terror actions    

They claim the King may wear the crown, but that the crown can be shadowed.

This is dangerous because it lets them oppose royal action while pretending they are preserving lawful Grevandar.

Civilization: always innovating new ways to make treason wordier.

### Dawnbound Serf-Liberation Pressure

Opportunistic Pelorite free-the-serfs factions, commonly being called the [[Dawnbound]], have emerged around the collapse of Vogel authority.

They are not automatically hostile to the Crown.

They are hostile to any restoration of Vogel-style bondage.

[[Lyosha Ohotnik]] has contacted and mollified them so far. That matters. Without Lyosha, the Crown risks turning a moral pressure movement into a rural revolt with hymns.

Current Dawnbound logic:

- the Crown may be legitimate    
- Vogel chains are not    
- wartime collapse has suspended old obligations    
- taxes may continue    
- bondage must not return    

Crown danger:

> If the Crown restores order too quickly and too stupidly, the Dawnbound become rebels by conscience.

### Incabulos Plague Cult

An [[Incabulos]] cult flared up in the east of Vogel territory.

Current assessment:

- may have been destroyed completely by Zara directly    
- Triad forces are containing the plague site    
- rumors of plague movement remain dangerous even if the cult is dead    
- grey-hand and well-spoiler stories are spreading faster than safe roads    

Operational danger:

> Even a contained plague site can move panic across three counties.

## County Blackvale

### Status

Loosely held by the King.

[[Blackvale]] proper, including the castle and central seat, is now held by the King.

The King’s direct force is highly mobile but small compared to the full need of the theater. It includes:

- mostly medium cavalry    
- [[Dana Sylvanranth]]    
- [[Dragonguard]]    
- fast royal detachments    
- scouts and couriers    
- enough royal presence to make neutrality look ridiculous    

Dana Sylvanranth is now the new Countess, which gives the King’s occupation of Blackvale political structure rather than just military control.

This is important.

A castle held by soldiers is an occupation.

A castle held by the King with a new countess is a settlement beginning to grow teeth.

### Strategic Value

Blackvale is the agricultural heart.

It provides:

- grain    
- livestock    
- road access    
- storehouses    
- legitimacy    
- leverage over former Bordella networks    

### Current Problems

Blackvale remains messy.

Problems include:

- Bordella loyalists and hangers-on    
- frightened reeves    
- grain factors hedging loyalties    
- rural unrest    
- displaced families    
- possible Ashen Wing infiltration    
- old Vogel tax machinery    
- supply corridors not yet secured    

### Crown Objective

- hold Blackvale Castle    
- secure grain and roads    
- keep Dana alive and visible    
- prevent scorched-earth actions    
- prevent loyalists from using Blackvale as a supply base    
- feed civilians without starving the campaign    
- make the new countess feel inevitable    

## Barony Thunderhold

### Status

Internal stabilization under Baroness Nerys.

[[Thunderhold]] remains under lawful command but is not comfortable.

Baroness Nerys is dealing with:

- Cron’s old rot    
- fleeing Cron retainers    
- northern road insecurity    
- frightened local officials    
- old passwords and storehouses compromised    
- under-supplied Orenic reinforcements    
- the need to look like ruler, not client    

### Orenic Reinforcements

The Orenic troops are brave, disciplined, and useful.

They are also under-supplied.

Net effect:

- real military help    
- real logistical strain    
- almost a non-gain in operational terms    
- politically useful, practically expensive    

Nerys is recording every ration, horse-feed, forge repair, and replacement tack under allied compact.

Translation:

> Thank you for the help. Sign the receipt before eating my barony.

### Crown Objective

- keep Nerys in command    
- secure northern roads    
- root out Cron fugitives    
- keep Orenic help bounded and temporary    
- prevent Thunderhold from looking occupied    
- prevent northern leakage toward Blackvale or Direhold    

## Barony Ironmere

### Status

Under major threat.

[[Ironmere]] appears to be the focus of an attack by a large [[Iron Covenant]] force.

This is one of the most dangerous active military problems in Grevandar.

Ironmere cannot be treated as a normal baronial battlefield because the Iron Covenant is not just a hostile army. It represents infernal contract warfare, sealed black armor, corrupted survivors, and battlefield behavior that may not follow normal surrender logic.

### Strategic Danger

If Ironmere breaks:

- Moffet lands collapse further    
- eastern border pressure worsens    
- refugees flood Blackvale and loyalist roads    
- Orin’s flank becomes worse    
- Crown main army may be diverted    
- Iron Covenant forces gain a symbolic victory against a battered loyal house    

### Crown Objective

- prevent Ironmere from falling    
- identify Iron Covenant command    
- preserve Moffet legitimacy    
- keep refugee corridors open    
- prevent Iron Covenant breakthrough toward Blackvale or Direhold    

## County Wyrmlea

### Status

Firmly loyal to Gorundia.

[[Countess Zara Vallytrex]] is one hundred percent on the Gorundian loyalist side.

This does not make her simple.

It makes her useful.

Which is worse, because useful Zara is how future problems learn to walk.

Zara is currently cleaning up the south with her two vassals.

Primary focus:

- [[Sunhollow]]    
- [[Blackbarrow Keep]]    
- infernal lattice containment    

## Barony Sunhollow

### Status

Main fighting zone in Wyrmlea.

Most of the active fighting in Zara’s county is concentrated in [[Sunhollow]], especially around [[Blackbarrow Keep]].

Blackbarrow is one of the infernal lattice sites.

This makes the Sunhollow fight a containment war, not just a local baronial action.

### Dame Venom and the Gnomes

[[Dame Venom]] is attempting to get the gnomes to arrive in a timely manner and wrap the operation.

This has been delayed by gnomish machinery, bound air elementals, and whatever category of engineering failure occurs when a tool is discovered inside a sealed housing after repairs are supposedly complete.

Current practical effect:

- another day lost    
- Venom’s patience intact only in the official sense    
- timetable confidence damaged    
- Blackbarrow containment delayed    

### Crown Objective

- finish Blackbarrow    
- prevent infernal spread    
- keep Sunhollow from consuming Zara’s whole county    
- bring gnome apparatus online before everyone involved starts inventing new curse words    

## Barony Southmere

### Status

Loyal but tied down.

[[Southmere]] is trying to send what supplies it can north with mercenary protection to help loyalists and feed innocents.

Its forces are tied up in the Sunhollow/Blackbarrow situation.

Southmere’s value right now is less field troops and more:

- grain brokerage    
- storage houses    
- road knowledge    
- hired protection    
- supply convoys    
- practical logistics    

### Crown Objective

- keep Southmere convoys moving    
- protect supplies northbound    
- avoid stripping Southmere from the Sunhollow effort    
- use mercenary guards without letting them become independent armed toll-takers

Supportive but limited.

Disorganized.

[[Count Vorias Serrin]] is still trying to get his shit together.

He has few county forces readily available.

This is partly because Serrin’s county is scholarly, magical, and politically complicated, and partly because Serrin himself is the kind of powerful wizard who can probably see fifteen futures and still misplace the one useful quartermaster.

## Barony Darkstone / DeSalle

### Status

Not formally asked, but Beringer is passing through.

[[DeSalle]] was not asked to commit forces as a clean ally.

Beringer is passing through DeSalle territory as part of his move toward the Grevandar ducal demesne.

This is awkward, because DeSalle territory is not clean ground. It contains compromised officials, frightened men, old Vogel links, and likely people hoping Beringer keeps moving before asking too many questions.

### Crown Objective

- pass through without getting bogged down    
- force local compliance    
- separate collaborators from cowards

- avoid turning every minor official into a cornered rat

Baroness Roselle Veymar may exist as a household authority, but functionally the barony is disorganized until lawful settlement is imposed.

### Crown Objective

- prevent local collapse

- identify who actually commands armed men    
- avoid letting Fenwyck become a safe hole for fugitives

Expected Dominion pressure:

- probes

## Strategic Thesis

The other three duchies are not invading Grevandar.

They are trying to cauterize it.

Araisal is sending legitimacy and disciplined host elements through Beringer.

Royonad is holding the Dominion border while bleeding limited aid into Thunderhold.

Ravenster has sent cavalry to the King and slower forces south.

Inside Grevandar, loyalists like Zara, Dana, Nerys, and Southmere are trying to hold pieces of the duchy together while Vogel loyalists, infernal forces, cult panic, and rural backlash try to tear it apart.

The Crown’s path to victory is not simply taking Direhold.

The Crown must make Grevandar governable after taking Direhold.

That is the unpleasant bit everyone pretends comes later.

It does not.

It is already here.

## DM Bottom Line

This civil war is now a four-duchy intervention into a collapsing fourth duchy.

The map should feel like:

- Ravenster cavalry and royal authority securing Blackvale.    
- Araisal legitimacy and Beringer’s host cutting through the northeast.    
- Orenic border discipline preventing Dominion opportunism while awkwardly feeding Thunderhold’s problems.    
- Zara burning out the southern infernal infection.    
- Sorek clamping Direhold.    
- Orin dealing with western leakage, Ironmere pressure, and the problem of not looking like a loose hammer.    
- Dana trying to make Blackvale believe it has a future.    
- Nerys proving Thunderhold is ruled, not occupied.    
- Lyosha keeping the Dawnbound from becoming a holy peasant revolt.    

This is not one front.

It is a kingdom discovering how many kinds of collapse fit under the word "civil war."
