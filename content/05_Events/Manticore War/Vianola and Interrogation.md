## Vianola’s interrogation philosophy

She would prefer **pure psychology** because it produces cleaner, more defensible information.

Not because she is soft.

Because forced minds are contaminated evidence.

Her line would be something like:

> “A confession compelled is not a confession. It is a spell report wearing a man’s face.”

That is very her. Cold, correct, and mildly devastating to anyone who thought they had invented “enhanced questioning,” because apparently bastards keep naming things.

## Her actual toolkit

### 1. The ordinary tools she prefers

This is her default kit:

- interview technique
    
- silence
    
- controlled room temperature
    
- water, food, blanket, chair
    
- careful repetition of questions
    
- contradiction mapping
    
- body-language reading
    
- emotional baseline observation
    
- letting the subject correct her “mistakes”
    
- staged pauses so liars fill the silence
    

She is not playing “good cop.” She is playing **“I have four centuries and you have tells.”**

Against traumatized witnesses, she uses calm structure:

> “Begin with the room. Not the man. The room. Where was the door?”

That keeps them anchored in sensory facts instead of dragging them face-first through the worst moment immediately.

Against hostile prisoners, she does the opposite:

> “You have already told me three useful things. I am deciding whether the fourth is worth your dignity.”

Which is nasty. Useful. Probably teachable in an advanced seminar no student survives emotionally intact.

## 2. The humane emergency tools

These are tools she can use without feeling dirty afterward.

**Wand of Bravery**  
Used for frozen victims, panicking witnesses, terrified messengers, or captives who need to speak before they collapse.

**Calm / emotional stabilization item**  
Not domination. More like “lower the screaming inside the skull so language can happen.” Useful after Blackbar.

**Sleep / Peaceful Sleep wand**  
For victims, not prisoners. Ends shock spirals. Lets Lyosha and healers do their work. Also lets Vianola stop asking questions before the person breaks, which is apparently a thing decent people do.

**Sense Emotion / Empathy-style charm**  
This is extremely Vianola. Not “tell me the truth,” but “what emotion is attached to this answer?” Fear, shame, loyalty, grief, panic, rehearsed anger. It helps her decide where to press and where not to.

**Truthsayer-style tool**  
Useful, but she treats it as supporting evidence, not sacred truth. A traumatized person can say something false and believe it. A dominated person can report implanted certainty. A fanatic can tell the truth and still be useless. Minds are awful filing cabinets.

## 3. The official Crown emergency kit

This is the stuff she uses only when lives are at stake.

**Compel Truth**  
Allowed only under writ, battlefield necessity, or immediate threat. She would hate casual use. The question must be narrow.

Bad question:

> “Tell me everything.”

Vianola would look at you like you tracked mud into a library.

Good question:

> “Is there another prisoner alive in this post?”

**Mind-Reading**  
Emergency-only. Surface thoughts. Immediate danger. She uses it to detect ambushes, hidden accomplices, suicide spells, poison triggers, names flashing in panic.

She does not use it to browse someone’s soul like a bored noble flipping through scandal sheets.

**Mind-Search**  
This is sealed-report territory. Hostage location. Planted bomb equivalent. Assassin cell inside the castle. Devil contract clause. That kind of thing.

Using this on a common criminal would be, in her view, moral vandalism.

**Suggestion / Command-type magic**  
She would almost never use this for information. She might use it to stop immediate harm:

> “Sit down.”  
> “Release the knife.”  
> “Do not bite through your tongue.”  
> “Look at me.”

Those are emergency containment uses, not interrogation. She knows the difference. Unlike half the legal system, which probably needs diagrams.

## 4. The forbidden tools she absolutely knows

Because she is old, brilliant, and the preeminent mind-mage. She knows the bad spells. She has probably written the ethics lecture that makes younger mages stop sleeping for a week.

**Memory alteration**  
She knows how. She does not use it for interrogation. Your Vianola file already nails her stance: memory is identity, and altering it is trespass.

**False memory implantation**  
Forbidden except maybe in espionage theory lectures under “how to recognize this crime.”

**Permanent loyalty binding**  
Absolutely forbidden. This is the line between law and slavery with better handwriting.

**Personality rewriting**  
Known, studied, despised.

**Emotional conditioning loops**  
She can identify them, undo them, and probably build them. She would consider using them on prisoners abhorrent.

Her position on the Maurakappi makes this even sharper. She is fascinated by their pheromonal cognitive influence but horrified by the autonomy erosion, and she explicitly distinguishes it from normal enchantment as something more like “architecture.” The Maurakappi material itself backs that up: hosts can suffer fogged independence, pacifying aura effects, compulsion toward the colony, and under Kursk, even use as sensory spy-nodes.

So Vianola’s horror is informed, not theoretical.

## What she carries

Give her a compact, terrifyingly professional kit.

### On her person

**Wand of Bravery**  
For trauma response and emergency witness stabilization.

**Silver-black focus ring**  
Boosts Sense Emotion / Truthsayer-type effects. Not flashy.

**Mind Shield amulet**  
Protects _her_ during interviews and can be lent to a witness. Especially useful if devils, larval influence, domination, or fae glamour are suspected.

**Sleep stylus**  
A small wand-like tool used to put a subject into safe magical rest. She uses this more often than people expect.

**Memory thread spool**  
Not literal memory theft. More like an enchanted cord used during interviews: subject holds one end, Vianola holds the other, and it helps organize recall by sensory sequence. Good prop. Very elf-academic. Mildly creepy, because elves can make yarn intimidating.

**Wax-sealed writ case**  
Contains preauthorized emergency permissions from the Crown/University for specific mind-magic use. If she breaks a seal, someone is getting a report afterward.

### In her travel case

**Circle chalks**  
For privacy, anti-scrying, and containment.

**Truth ledger**  
A notebook enchanted to preserve original wording. Not “detect lie,” just prevents later tampering.

**Counter-enchantment lenses**  
For reading signs of Charm, Suggestion, Panic, Daze, possession, larval influence, and memory tampering.

**Mercy draughts**  
Mundane sedatives. Because sometimes the correct answer is not magic. Revoltingly mature.

## Her escalation ladder

This is the clean table-use version.

1. **Interview only.**
    
2. **Stabilize subject:** food, warmth, healer, Bravery, Calm, Sleep.
    
3. **Read the shape of the mind:** Sense Emotion, Aura, signs of tampering.
    
4. **Verify narrow facts:** Truthsayer / Compel Truth on very specific questions.
    
5. **Emergency surface access:** Mind-Reading if lives are in immediate danger.
    
6. **Deep intrusion:** Mind-Search only by writ, immediate necessity, or royal crisis.
    
7. **Memory alteration:** almost never, and not for interrogation.
    

That ladder is Vianola in one sentence:

> “We begin with dignity. We escalate only when time, death, or treason leaves us no cleaner tool.”

## How she behaves in interrogation scenes

She should be quiet. Almost disappointingly quiet.

No threats.

No theatrical spellcasting.

No “you will tell me everything.”

She asks one question, watches the answer land in the body, then asks the actual question.

Example:

> “Were you afraid of him?”

Prisoner says no.

She does not react.

> “No. You were afraid of disappointing him.”

That is the hit.

Then, maybe, if it is urgent:

> “I can prove that magically. I would rather not. Continue.”

That is much scarier than casting the spell.

## Best ruling for her at Blackbar

For victims: Bravery, Calm, Sleep, emotional reading, memory anchoring.

For captured abusers or guards: psychology first, Truthsayer if needed, Mind-Reading only if there are missing prisoners, traps, runners, hidden chains of command, or an imminent threat.

For leaders or magically compromised agents: full kit available, but witnessed and recorded.

Vianola’s real danger is not that she can break a mind.

It is that she knows exactly how easy it would be, and built her entire professional identity around not doing it unless the alternative is worse. Which, naturally, makes her the one person you actually _can_ trust with the tools. Annoying how that works.
