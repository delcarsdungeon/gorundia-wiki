# Air Attack on the Lord Marshal’s Camp

## Narrative Account

The fullest personal account of one landing—and of the people who fought it—is preserved in [[The Second Draft - Homeward]], as the Ravenfast strike team compared the two battles during their return journey.

Dominion forces launched a sudden airborne assault against the [[Lord Marshal's Host]], dropping four pods of troops directly into the camp.

The attack threatened command, supply lines, wounded soldiers, servants, teamsters, horses, and the tightly packed support elements surrounding the army.

[[Beringer Amadis]] prevented the initial shock from becoming general chaos. Orders continued to move, local defenses formed around each landing, and the camp remained functional while fighting erupted inside it.

## The Pods

### Beringer’s Pod

Beringer personally engaged the troops from one pod while continuing to direct the camp’s response.

His immediate intervention prevented the attackers from establishing themselves near the command elements.

### Erinleaf and Aelion

[[Vianola Erinleaf]] trapped the occupants of another pod beneath a Force Dome and subjected them to fear, confusion, and distorted perception.

The dome contained them, but it could not remain indefinitely.

[[Aelion]] joined Erinleaf before the barrier failed. When the terrified survivors attempted to scatter through the camp, the two prevented them from reaching the wounded, wagon lines, and other vulnerable personnel.

### Cwenheld

[[Cwenheld]], the Venerated Shield of Heironeous, met and destroyed the force from a third pod at the camp shrine.

### The Final Pod

The remaining pod was engaged by [[Lyosha]], [[Orin Kharne]], and Beringer together.

Its troops were defeated before they could establish a defensible position or spread farther through the camp.

## Outcome

All enemy landings were contained.

The attackers inflicted casualties and disruption, but failed to:

- break the command structure;
- seize the camp;
- destroy the supply train;
- scatter the host;
- or kill its senior leaders.

The assault demonstrated that Dominion forces could place trained troops directly inside a defended camp.

It also demonstrated that landing among the Lord Marshal, Baron Kharne, the Venerated Shield, an esteemed Pelorite priest, a master enchantress, and Aelion was an extremely poor long-term plan.

## Consequences

The attack forced the royal command to reconsider:

- protection of camps against vertical assault;
- concentration of senior commanders;
- air patrols and early warning;
- the Dominion’s deployment range;
- and the purpose of the captured [[Iron Nails]].

The battle also provided the Crown with its clearest evidence yet that the Dominion possessed a coordinated airborne military capability.
