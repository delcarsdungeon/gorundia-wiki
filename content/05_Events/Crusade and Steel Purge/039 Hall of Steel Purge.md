# The Purge in the Hall of Steel

## Scene Context

This event occurs immediately after [[The Crusade]] is declared.

* The hall is sealed.
* Paladins of multiple orders line the chamber.
* [[Porthos Ryomar]] stands beside the King, haloed in impossible sunlight.
* The [[Krozen]] stands at the King’s shoulder.
* No one leaves.

---

## The Pivot

The King surveys the room.

> “This crusade does not begin beyond our borders.”

> “It begins here.”

> “You have already seen what silence costs.”

> “You have heard it… from one of your own.”

> “And still—you thought yourselves untouched.”

---

## Execution of Lynwenden Cron

> “Bring forth Baron Lynwenden Cron.”

Paladins—not guards—drag him forward.

> “You were given land, men, and trust.”
> “You returned… silence.”

> “You saw what was done in your name.”
> “And chose not to see it.”

> “You have confessed.”
> “You have repented.”

> “You will answer now.”

The Chaplain steps forward.

> “By oath and witness… the Crown is clean.”

---

## The Breaking of Lynwenden Cron

The Royal Executioner steps forward.

Black. Unmarked. Certain.

A block is placed at the center of the hall.

Not dragged. Placed.

---

That is when it hits him.

---

> “No—”

---

> “No, wait—”

---

> “This isn’t—this isn’t how this is done—”

---

He pulls back.

The paladins do not struggle.

They simply do not yield.

---

> “I confessed—!”

> “I confessed, you can’t—!”

---

> “Not here—!”

---

He looks to the room.

To the nobles.

---

No one moves.

---

> “Please—!”

---

The word lands wrong.

---

They bring him to the block.

He resists—

then falters.

---

> “My lord—”

> “Please—”

---

No one answers.

---

The blade rises.

---

It falls.

---

## The Attempted Departure

A chair shifts.

Movement—silk, escort.

She is leaving.

---

> “Sit.”

---

The room freezes.

---

Paladins step inward.

Closing angles.

---

The King turns.

---

> “You may depart when I dismiss this hall.”

> “Not before.”

---

Cron’s body lies where it fell.

Blood spreads across the stone.

---

> “This is what it means… to forget.”

---

---

## Duchess Kylesstra — The Calculation

Kylesstra does not move.

---

Her eyes shift—

to Allaric.

Then briefly—

to Amadis.

---

That is the tell.

---

She is adjusting.

---

The rules have clarified.

---

The King can be challenged.

And survive it.

---

She lowers her gaze.

Composed. Perfect.

---

But the board is already changing.

---

His jaw tightens.

---

His eyes go to Allaric.

And linger.

---

That look is not outrage.

---

It is assessment.

---

Recognition.

---

He exhales once.

---

Then stills.

---

Something just shifted.

No argument given time to form.

Cold air.

---

No one needs to see what waits outside.

---

> “I will read what you write.”

---

Steel flashes.

---

> “No—!”

---

---

Another drop.

---

## The Room Changes

No one looks at one another now.

---

Because now they know:

---

If you run—

They will hold you down themselves.

## The Named Judgments

The King does not pause.

He begins naming them.

---

### Lady Yselle Tarnen

> “Lady Yselle Tarnen.”

A stir. Recognition.

---

> “You fed the poor.”

A beat.

---

> “With one hand.”

> “And with the other—”

> “You emptied the granaries.”

---

> “You diverted shipments meant for winter.”

> “Then sold them back… when hunger came.”

---

> “You made famine… profitable.”

---

She does not stay silent.

---

> “It is common practice—”

---

Not hysterical.

Not broken.

---

**Offended.**

---

> “Grain spoils.”  
> “Transport fails.”  
> “Loss must be accounted for—”

---

She almost gathers herself—

almost turns it into something reasonable—

---

> “This is how it is done.”

---

The King cuts her off.

Not louder.

Not sharper.

---

> “Yes.”

---

A step forward.

---

> “That is the point.”

---

Paladins move.

---

She is taken.

---

### Lord Horick Valtris

> “Lord Horick Valtris.”

He’s already sweating.

---

> “You were raised from nothing.”

> “Given land. Authority. Trust.”

---

> “Protection fees.”

> “From the families of sworn men.”

---

> “You took coin…”

> “From those sworn to protect others.”

---

He tries to speak.

Too fast.

---

> “Your Majesty, it was—”

---

A gauntleted hand settles on his shoulder.

Not forceful.

Final.

---

---

> “You were trusted.”

---

Paladins move.

---

He does not resist.

But he does not walk cleanly either.

---
### Baron Aldren Veymar of Fenwyck

---

Just enough to show it.

---

---

That ends quickly.

---

### Lady Corisande Bellor

From her.

---

> “No—”

Not argument.

---

---

---

---

She doesn’t speak again.

# At the Threshold

They approach the doors.

Cold air seeps in ahead of them.

Behind them—

the King’s voice continues, unbroken:

“—You knew.”
“—You profited.”
“—You chose not to act.”

Boots on stone.

Steel moving.

The doors are already open.

A man is dragged past them.

Close enough to touch.

Stammering.

Half-words. Half-prayers.

Two paladins hold him—

one in blue steel, one in sun-bleached gold.

He doesn’t fight them.

Not really.

He looks at the party as he passes—

like they might still be part of the same world.

They aren’t.

They take him through the doors.

Rope.

Wood.

The noose is already set.

He doesn’t finish what he’s trying to say.

The drop comes hard.

Clean.

The sound carries.

Behind them—

another name is already being called.

# The King’s Command

Just before they pass fully beyond the threshold—

his voice reaches them.

“Lord Marshal.”

The title lands heavier than the name.

“Prepare your men.”

They step forward.

“Soon…”

“We ride.”

The words follow them out.

“This corruption does not end in this hall.”

And finally—

“Wherever it is found.”
