## Statement Record

*Statement recorded by order of the Crown. Speaker: Hanna (servant). Delivered under no compulsion.*

---

Hanna sits on the edge of the table, legs swinging, both hands around a chipped mug of tea. Her voice is small at first, the kind you have to lean in to hear.

“I—I was at supper, m’lords. With [[Baroness Braga]] and the others. The roast had just come out, and [[Sarrielle Redmarch]] was teasing me about the maps again. Then the windows shook. Like thunder, only… closer. The torches went sideways. And then—screams.

Lady Braga didn’t even think. She shouted for us to move—took one look at the shadows coming down the hall and started giving orders like a general. She pushed me and Sarielle toward the kitchen passage, sent Myra and Fayge the other way. I thought she was right behind us.

But Sarielle stopped. Just—stopped. Said she wouldn’t leave her lady alone. She smiled at me—by the gods, she smiled—and said, ‘Hide when I tell you to.’

She waited until Braga was clear, then shoved me behind a wine crate and ran the other way, clanging her dagger on the wall to make noise. I heard her shouting—drawing them after her. Three of them. The ones in black feathers. Raven knights, I think. They followed her down the hall.”

She stops, sips her tea with shaking hands.

“I wanted to run, but I couldn’t make my legs work. When I finally moved, I—I went to look. Just a peek, I thought. Just to see if she was—

And then she was gone. And [[Blue Sylvanranth]] was there.

Blue… she was standing in the corridor. Blood everywhere—on her, on the walls. She had a sword in one hand and… something metal in the other. I didn’t realize what it was at first. Her father’s arm, I think. Gods forgive me, I nearly screamed.

She looked at me—tilted her head, sort of sideways, like she was listening to something I couldn’t hear. Then she smiled. Not cruel. Sad. Just… tired.

She said, ‘Hide.’ That’s all. Just ‘Hide.’ And she stepped aside so I could pass. I ran. I didn’t look back.”

Hanna wipes her eyes quickly, embarrassed.

“She saved me. I think she did. And Sarielle—”

Her voice breaks; she looks straight at [[Sorek Redmarch]], twisting the mug between her fingers.

“I’m so sorry, sir. She—she died fighting for her liege like one of Heironeous’ own. I swear it. Brave as any knight in that hall.”

She sets the cup down carefully.

Sorek, face wet and trembling, reaches across the table and covers her hand with his. He cannot speak for a long moment—just nods once, firmly.
