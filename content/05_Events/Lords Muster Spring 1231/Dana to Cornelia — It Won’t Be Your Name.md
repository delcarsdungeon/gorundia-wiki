# Dana to Cornelia — “It Won’t Be Your Name”

The statue of [[Theron Sylvanranth]] towers above them.  
Bronze. Stern. Watching.

Cornelia approaches carefully, on Bertram’s arm.

“Countess.”

A small incline of her head.

“Dana… I—congratulations.”

She means it.  
Mostly.

Dana doesn’t return it.  
Doesn’t move.  
Too still.

“Don’t.”

Flat.

Cornelia pauses.

“I wasn’t—”

“I know.”

A beat.

“You were being polite.”

Her hands flex at her sides.

“I would give this title…”

A breath.

“…all of it…”

Her voice almost slips—  
but doesn’t.

“For five more minutes with him.”

She glances up.  
Just once.  
At the statue.

Then back.  
Locked again.

“So don’t congratulate me.”

A step closer.  
Measured.

“Because this isn’t a victory.”

A beat.

“It’s a correction.”

Silence.

Cornelia steadies.

“You think I don’t know that?”

Dana nods once.  
Accepts it.

“No.”

“You just lived through it.”

A breath.

“And I’m what comes after.”

Dana’s gaze sharpens.

“If anyone remembers the name Bordella—”

A beat.

“It will be for what failed.”

She tilts her head slightly.

“And what replaces it.”

Closer now.  
Not aggressive.  
Inevitable.

“I’m not going to kill you.”

A beat.

“That would let you keep it.”

That lands.

“I’m going to take the rest.”

A pause.

“A house. A name. A story.”

“A book written by a Bordella?”

A faint edge.

“Ghostwritten.”

She straightens.  
All Countess now.

“You may go.”

Cornelia lingers.  
Just a second.

Dana doesn’t blink.

Not until she turns away.

Then—  
just for a moment—

Dana looks back up at the statue.

Her hand trembles.

Then stillness.

Countess Blackvale stands beneath her father.
