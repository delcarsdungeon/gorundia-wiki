# **Duchess Irena Orenic → Sorek Redmarch**

---
***To Sir Sorek Redmarch,***

*It has been some time since I have had cause to write to you directly.*
*I would prefer this were a more pleasant occasion.*
 *I find myself in the mildly absurd position of having to inquire, through others, after the whereabouts of a lady of my own blood.*
*This is, I am sure, not how you would have preferred matters to unfold.*
*Lady Calira Meroven has been confined for several days within the palace.*
*During a week in which many things were noticed… this one, regrettably, was not addressed.*
*I am told this occurred during the Lord Marshal’s… energetic efforts.*
*I wonder, Sorek—did no one think to mention it to you?*
*Or were you, like the rest of us, choosing your silences carefully?*
*My husband is, at present, becoming increasingly difficult to dissuade from involving himself directly.*
*I would prefer that he not be required to make a spectacle of it.*
*You have always had a talent for resolving matters… before they become louder than necessary.*
*See that this is one of them.*
*I would rather remember you as a man who acted when it mattered.*
*Not one who learned to wait.*
*—Irena*

---

> **To Sir Sorek Redmarch,**
> 
> It has been some time since I have had cause to write to you directly.
> 
> I would prefer this were a more pleasant occasion.

A pause in the ink—intentional.

---

> I find myself in the mildly absurd position of having to inquire, through others, after the whereabouts of a lady of my own blood.
> 
> This is, I am sure, not how you would have preferred matters to unfold.

That’s the first needle.

---

> Lady Calira Meroven has been confined for several days within the palace.
> 
> During a week in which _many things_ were noticed…
> 
> this one, regrettably, was not addressed.

She’s including herself.

Which makes it worse.

---

> I am told this occurred during the Lord Marshal’s… energetic efforts.

You can _hear_ the ghost of a smile.

---

> I wonder, Sorek—
> 
> did no one think to mention it to you?

That’s the knife.

Not accusation.

**Disappointment.**

---

> Or were you, like the rest of us, choosing your silences carefully?

There it is.

She’s not just poking him.

She’s tying him to the whole damn week.

---

> My husband is, at present, becoming increasingly difficult to dissuade from involving himself directly.

Understatement of the year.

---

> I would prefer that he not be required to make a spectacle of it.

Translation: _fix this before Vladek arrives like a war crime with boots._

---

> You have always had a talent for resolving matters… before they become louder than necessary.

That’s half compliment, half reminder of who he used to be.

---

> See that this is one of them.

No “please.”

---

Then—soft landing, just to make it worse:

---

> I would rather remember you as a man who acted when it mattered.
> 
> Not one who learned to wait.

---

> —Irena

No title.

Just her name.

That’s _intentional_.
