# The Lord Marshal’s Commission  
## Envoys to Teenkdiwar

> “When the Marshal starts handing out titles and sealed orders in the same breath, it means he’s already decided how the next month ends.”
> — Sorek Redmarch

---

## Scene

The Lord Marshal’s office churned like a war engine.

Couriers darted through the halls with sealed orders and fresh intelligence. Officers stood in tight clusters, speaking in low tones or not at all, waiting to be called.

Among them stood an unusual pair:

- **Dame Venymmara Gylenda**, composed and watchful  
- **Meister Loxwin Stoneyeye**, hands folded neatly behind his back, eyes already working the room  

They were not left waiting long.

An aide approached with a crisp bow.  
“My lord will see you now.”

---

## The War Room

Inside, the air was thick with ink and strategy.

Maps sprawled across a heavy oak table, pinned and re-pinned with colored markers. Scrolls lay half-open, quills abandoned mid-thought.

At the center of it all stood **[[Beringer]]**, Lord Marshal.

He did not look up immediately.

At his shoulder stood **Radomir**, silent as stone.  
Nearby, **[[Baron Cadaras Kendal]]** and **[[Baron Terevon]]** spoke in low tones before falling quiet as the envoys entered.

Only then did Beringer lift his gaze.

---

## The Appointment

“Dame Venymmara. Meister Loxwin.”

His voice was steady, already moving.

“The office of the Lord Marshal has need of you both.”

He stepped around the table, closing the distance just enough to make the moment formal.

“Dame Venymmara Gylenda—by my authority, I name you **Marshal’s Envoy Extraordinary**.”

A slight pause.

“Meister Loxwin Stoneyeye—you are hereby appointed **Royal Envoy of Gorundia**.”

He gestured to a sealed case resting on the table.

---

## Diplomatic Charge

“With the elven kingdoms now being brought into the Gorundian fold, we open formal negotiations with the gnomish kingdom of [[Teenkdiwar]].”

“Envoy Loxwin—you will carry our invitation to their ruling council.”

“Gorundia seeks trade, cooperation, and mutual strength. I believe we stand to benefit one another greatly.”

---

## Military Directive

Beringer’s hand shifted to a second document.

“Enclosed is a formal request for aid from their **siege corps**.”

His gaze moved to Venymmara.

“That is where you come in.”

“You will proceed to [[Fort Stormarrow]]. There, you will persuade the gnomish detachment to mobilize their siege engines and march with them to **Barony Sunhollow**.”

He inclined his head slightly toward Terevon.

“[[Baron Terevon]] will begin the siege. His forces, supported by a unit of paladins, will establish the encirclement and hold the field until your arrival.”

Beringer’s tone hardened—just a fraction.

“When you arrive, you will break the enemy outpost.”

“Leave no heathen nor demon standing.”

“The paladins will see the site consecrated once the work is done.”

A beat.

“From there, you will advance to **Direhold** and join my host.”

---

## Operational Support

He turned briefly toward Kendal.

“[[Baron Cadaras Kendal]] is securing our supply lines. He will ensure your forces are provisioned and reinforced as needed.”

“The Master of Signals has already made contact with Fort Stormarrow. They are expecting you.”

A final gesture, precise.

“You will not waste time on the road.”

“The Sage of the Realm, [[Norwin]], and his cohort stand ready. They will see you transported directly.”

---

## Final Order

His voice settled into command—clean and final.

“When you are prepared, present yourselves to him.”

A pause—just long enough to let the weight settle.

“Carry this out swiftly.”

“**Gorundia moves.**”
