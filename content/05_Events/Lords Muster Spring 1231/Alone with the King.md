## Apocrypha

> [!note ]
> Party did NOT engage the king separately.  Left here for completeness.

## What They Feel Immediately

The second they’re alone with him (just them + Krozen), it hits:

* He’s calmer than before
* Not tense
* Not angry

**Certain.**

That’s the scary part.

Before, he *weighed things*.
Now?

He’s already decided.

---

## The Core Shift (How to Play Him)

This is the axis:

> Before: *“I must be right.”*
> Now: *“I am right.”*

Not arrogance.

**Mandate.**

That’s straight out of the Triad mindset—especially  Heironeous’ lane:

* Valor
* Certainty
* Righteous action

He’s not guessing anymore.
He believes he has **confirmation**.

---

## His Tone with the Party

He doesn’t posture with them. That’s key.

These are *his people*. The ones who stood in the fire.

So:

* No speeches
* No court voice
* No performance

Just direct truth.

---

## The King – Private Scene Insert

Use this as your drop-in:

---

He doesn’t sit behind the desk.

That’s new.

---

He stands near the window, hands clasped behind his back—not rigid, not at ease.

Measured.

---

When he turns, there’s recognition.

Not of rank.

Of *shared ground*.

---

> “You feel it.”

Not a question.

---

A beat.

He studies them.

---

> “Good.”

---

He moves—not pacing. Just… placing himself.

---

> “For ages… I have been asked to be patient.”

> “Careful.”

> “Measured.”

---

A faint, humorless breath.

---

> “I was.”

---

He meets their eyes.

---

> “I built the case.”

> “I gave them every chance to stand with me.”

---

A pause.

Not anger.

**Finality.**

---

> “Some did.”

---

You can feel it when he says it:

Orenic.

No hesitation there.

---

Then—

quieter:

---

> “Some… did not.”

---

A flicker there.

Glandyr.

Not betrayal.

But not certainty either.

---

And then—

sharper:

---

> “And some are about to learn what it costs to hesitate.”

---

That lands squarely in Allaric’s direction without naming him.

---

He lets that sit.

Then—

---

> “You may be wondering… if I’ve changed.”

---

A beat.

---

> “I haven’t.”

---

Now the hammer:

---

> “I have stopped waiting.”

---

Silence.

---

> “Heironeous does not whisper to men like me lightly.”

---

Not bragging.

Not preaching.

Just fact, as he sees it.

---

> “Theron is dead.”

---

That one lands different.

Not political.

Personal.

---

> “The rot in the Vogel line is no longer rumor.”

---

He steps closer.

Not looming.

Grounding.

---

> “This was always coming.”

---

And here’s where you give the party their read:

---

> “You can call it cruelty.”

> “You can call it justice.”

---

A beat.

---

> “I call it… late.”

---

## How He Handles Pushback

If they go:

### “What the hell was that?”

He doesn’t get defensive.

> “Restraint.”

> “You saw the cost of it.”

---

### “This is too far.”

He nods once.

> “It will look that way.”

> “Until it isn’t enough.”

---

### “About damn time.”

That gets the smallest hint of a smile.

> “Yes.”

---

## The Duke Breakdown (Subtext He Won’t Dump, But You Can Lean On)

You nailed this, so reinforce it subtly:

* **Orenic** → Anchor. War mindset. “Finally.”
* **Glandyr** → Brother. Complicated. Watching.
* **Allaric** → Calculating. Dangerous if pushed too hard.

He doesn’t rant about them.

He **accounts for them**.

That’s what makes him scary now.

---

## The Krozen Factor

Don’t sleep on this.

Krozen being there reinforces:

* The gods are watching
* The king is *validated*
* This is no longer just politics

It’s divine alignment.

And the party will feel that.

---

## Final Vibe

This is the energy you’re going for:

> Not a tyrant.
> Not a hero.
>
> A man who believes hesitation is now a sin.
