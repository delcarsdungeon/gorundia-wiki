## **Cornelia’s Abdication — The Reckoning of Silence**

She stands straight.

No tremor.

No tears.

Just a woman who has decided she’s done pretending.

---

> “Your Majesty… my lords…”
> 
> “I will not insult this court with excuses.”

---

> “What has come to light is not a single failure.”
> 
> “It is a pattern.”
> 
> “Of knowledge held too closely…”
> 
> “Of warnings not given…”
> 
> “Of dangers seen—and allowed to ripen.”

---

> “The rot runs deeper than any one house.”

Let that settle.

Then—

---

> “And now… we know how deep.”

---

> “We have seen what was hidden in blood.”
> 
> “What was buried beneath titles.”
> 
> “What was permitted to stand… because it was dangerous to pull at.”

---

> “House Vogel did not fall in a day.”

Quiet.

Measured.

---

> “It endured… because it was useful.”

That line? That lands like a hammer.

---

> “Men called it strength.”
> 
> “They called it order.”
> 
> “They called it necessary.”

A slight pause.

---

> “And so they endured it.”

No names.

Doesn’t need them.

Everyone in that room just felt it.

---

> “I was to be bound to it.”

There it is. Personal stake.

---

> “Not as a partner.”
> 
> “Not as an equal.”

A breath.

---

> “But as something to be _spent_.”

Let that sit in the gut.

---

> “I knew enough to be afraid.”

---

> “Afraid to speak too soon.”
> 
> “Afraid to speak without proof.”
> 
> “Afraid of what happens to those who are right… at the wrong time.”

---

> “So I remained silent.”

---

> “That silence is mine.”

Own it.

Clean.

---

Then the pivot—this is where the room tightens:

---

> “But it was not mine alone.”

Don’t look at anyone.

Let _them_ feel it.

---

> “Some here knew pieces.”
> 
> “Some suspected more.”
> 
> “Some chose patience… over truth.”

That hits:

- the King
    
- the Dukes
    
- Amadis
    
- Kylesstra
    

All without naming a single one.

---

> “Perhaps they were right to wait.”

That’s the grace note.

She’s not attacking.

She’s acknowledging.

---

> “But waiting has a cost.”

And _that_ lands.

---

> “I paid part of it.”

---

> “Others paid far more.”

Now the ghosts are in the room.

---

Then—_she cuts Vogel cleanly out of history_:

---

> “Let House Vogel answer for what it was.”

---

> “It needs no defense from me.”

---

> “And no silence to protect it any longer.”

That’s the burial.

---

Now—she turns inward again:

---

> “I was not raised for this.”

---

> “I was a daughter of House Bordella…”
> 
> “Sent to an abbey to learn grace, scripture, and obedience.”

> “Not to navigate a court where truth can get you killed.”

---

> “I believed I could walk between powers…”
> 
> “Keep peace where others would make war…”
> 
> “Hold knowledge until the right moment.”

> “I was wrong.”

---

> “A Countess cannot afford to be wrong in such matters.”

---

Now—**the Bordella reckoning**:

---

> “My brother Ponzio will answer for his part.”

No hesitation.

No softness.

That’s important.

---

> “As he should.”

---

Then—_shift tone slightly_:

---

> “But not all who bear our name are the same.”

There it is.

---

> “My brother Allec—”

First time she names him.

Careful. Measured.

---

> “—was raised in the shadow of men who mistook ambition for strength…”
> 
> “and cruelty for necessity.”

---

> “He has been foolish.”

Small, honest.

---

> “He has been angry.”

True.

---

> “But he has not been _this_.”

Let that distinction breathe.

---

> “Do not damn a man for the sins he has not yet chosen.”

That’s a _killer_ line.

---

> “Give him the chance none of us were wise enough to take.”

---

Now—final pivot to abdication:

---

> “For the good of the realm…”
> 
> “For the dignity of this court…”
> 
> “And so that justice may be seen to stand above blood, title, and favor—”

---

> “I relinquish my title.”
> 
> “I abdicate my station as Countess.”

---

> “Not to escape judgment—”
> 
> “But to ensure it is believed.”

That line is gold for the King.

---

> “I would rather stand here as a woman…”
> 
> “than hide behind what I was born into.”

(glance to Bertram)

---

> “And if I am granted the chance…”
> 
> “I will spend the rest of my life proving I can be better than the house that made me.”

---

> “I place myself in the Crown’s judgment.”

---

> “And I accept whatever comes.”

---
