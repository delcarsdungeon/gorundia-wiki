## Scene — The King’s Command

The chamber is not large.
That’s deliberate.
No court. No banners. No audience.
Just the King.
And **Thalrik Vaultforge**.
The door shuts behind him with a solid, final sound.
The King does not waste time.

> *“The Second Draft.”*

A beat.

> “The hammership.”

His eyes lock on Thalrik.

> “It is to be made ready for immediate departure.”
> 
> No flourish. No explanation.
> Just an order.

> “Full crew readiness. Fuel, provisions, spell-anchors checked and primed.”
> "YOUR crew.  Rykeena, Norwin, his brothers."

Then—

> “You will bring Guaco. His family. Anyone he names."

Now there’s a flicker of something underneath.  Urgency.

> “They go aboard. Now.  They remain on board."
### Thalrik, Being Thalrik

Thalrik doesn’t salute.
Doesn’t bow.
He shifts his weight, scratches his beard, and gives the King that look.
The one that’s gotten him in trouble his entire life.

> “That so.”

A beat.

> “You planning a war… or a miracle?”

Silence.
Then—
The King moves.
Fast.
He steps forward and **grabs Thalrik by the front of his shirt**, hauling him half a step closer.  Not rage.  Not quite.
But close enough that it doesn’t matter.

> “Thalrik Vaultforge—”

Low. Controlled.

> “Are you a loyal servant of Gorundia?”

No room for jokes now.
Thalrik meets his eyes.
And answers straight.

> “Of course I am.”

### The Turn

The King holds him there for just a moment longer.
Then—

> “As am I.”

A breath.  Then the weight drops:

> “But I am also your King.”

A beat.

> “Today—”

Tighter.

> “I demand your obedience.”

And then—
Something shifts.
Not softer.
But… honest.

> “And I ask your trust.”
## The Warning Beneath the Order

The King does not step back immediately.  Thalrik is lowered to his feet.
The King keeps his grip a fraction longer than necessary.
Not anger.
Not intimidation.
Something worse.
Deliberate.

> “Listen to me carefully.”

His voice drops.
Not softer.
More controlled.

> “What comes next—”

A beat.

> “—will not be clean.”

No elaboration.
No comfort.

> “There are names already spoken.”
> “Writ already carried.”
> “And I have… set things in motion.”

A flicker.
Not doubt.
Calculation.

> “Some of it will happen in the open.”
> “Much of it will not.”

> “There are hands moving now—”

A beat.

> “—that do not answer to the court.”

That lands.
Different.

> “They have been given direction.”
> “Not leashes.”

Now Thalrik gets it.
This isn’t soldiers.
This is something else.

> “I trust the man who pointed them.”

A fraction tighter.

> “I do not pretend to control where they strike… once they begin.”

There it is.
That’s the truth he *shouldn’t* be saying.

A breath.
Then—
the pivot:

> “Guaco does not stand in that path.”

Flat.
Certain.

> “He is not to be mistaken for it.”

A beat.

> “You will make sure of that.”

No title.
No ‘please.’

> “Because when it turns—”

A fraction closer.

> “—it will not pause to ask who belongs.”

And now the knife:

> “If he is still in the street when it does—”
> “I will not be able to call it back.”

Silence.

Now he lets go.

> “That is why the ship flies now.”

He releases him.

### Thalrik’s Reset

Thalrik takes a half-step back.
Smooths out his shirt.
Slow.
Deliberate.
You can _see_ him recalibrating.
The swagger comes back—
But it’s different now.
Still Thalrik.
Just… pointed in the right direction.
He exhales through his nose.
A faint grin.

> “Aye.”

A nod.

> “Should’ve led with that, Your Majesty.”

Then, more serious:

> “She’ll fly.”

A beat.

> “And if she don’t—”

That glint in his eye again.

> “I’ll push her myself.”
### The Exit Button

The King doesn’t smile.
But there’s approval there.
Buried.
> “See that you don’t have to.”

Thalrik turns to go—
And the King adds, just before the door:

> “Vaultforge.”
> 
> Thalrik pauses.

Half-turn.

> “We may not get a second chance at this.”
