## The Walk to the Silver Willow

**DM Narration:**

Night in the elvish quarter feels different.  
The noise of the Gorundian capital fades like a tide left behind — laughter, hammering, the rumble of hooves — all distant now. The streets here are smooth and pale under the moonlight, laid in winding patterns that only elves remember the meaning of.

The air smells of cedar, rain, and memory. From open balconies drift threads of quiet song — half prayer, half habit. Lanterns glow softly, each a cage of glass holding fireflies instead of flame. The light doesn’t burn here; it breathes.

It’s beautiful, but there’s dignity in it — the sort that makes every footstep sound too loud, every breath feel noticed. You pass doors carved before your people had surnames, gardens that bloom even in moonlight, and always there are eyes — not hostile, not cruel, just watchful.

When you finally find the Silver Willow, it feels like the heart of that stillness.  
A white-barked tree rises through the courtyard stones, its silver leaves whispering in the warm night air. Beneath it stands the inn — windows curved and glowing amber from within, the air alive with the faint harmony of harp and low elven voices.

As you approach the doors, the doorman steps up — not aside.

His posture is elegant but unyielding. One pale hand rests lightly on the polished wood. His eyes meet yours, calm and assessing.

He inclines his head just slightly.

> “Good evening, traveler. You walk far from your quarter. What calls you to the Willow tonight?”

## The Maitre d’s Gate

> “You must forgive the question, master halfling.  
> It is uncommon for our kind and yours to cross paths at this hour… and the lady you name is… unwell of heart.”

If Guaco names her:

> “Ah. You seek **Vynemmara Gylenda**.”

(The name is spoken like music — not correction, but restoration.)

> “She is a guest of this house. Few call upon her uninvited.  
> Tell me, little one… do you come as messenger, or as friend?”

If pressed:

> “If you are her courier, I can see that a message is placed in her hands. She need not be disturbed.”

If Guaco insists — *friend*:

> “A friend…  
> Forgive me. It is not a title we grant lightly.”

> “Did you come to share in her joy… or her sorrow?”

If answered honestly:

> “Then your path is heavier than I guessed.  
> Come, and tread gently. The lady’s spirit is fragile this night.”

> “When she looks upon you, she will see what you carry.  
> Do not try to hide it.”

## The Silver Willow Interior

**DM Narration:**

The Silver Willow isn’t a place for strangers.

Low laughter, half-song language, jasmine and cedar smoke.  
When you step inside, every head turns — not hostile, just aware.

You feel it like weight: a halfling in a place built for long memory.

The room quiets as you pass.  
Not silence — but attention.

---

## The Table by the Hearth

**DM Narration:**

She sits alone.  
Half a bottle into pale golden wine.

Armor gone. White gown. Hair loose.  
For a moment — grace.

Then she sees you.

---

**Vynemmara (too bright, slightly slurred):**

> “Guaco of Vault Row.  
> The one halfling in this city who doesn’t drink me under the table.”

> “Come then — sit. Tell me you’ve brought better news than my wine.”

(She pours without asking.)

> “You’ve heard, I suppose? [[Beringer]] riding off to true love’s arms.  
> Good for him. Truly.”

(A brittle laugh.)

> “I told myself I’d be happy. For a few hours, I almost managed it.”

(Pause. She studies him.)

The smile fades.

---

**Vynemmara (quiet now):**

> “You’ve brought something heavy, haven’t you?”

> “Don’t make me wait.  
> Whatever it is — say it plain.”

## The Three Names

### Braga

> “Braga?”  
> “No. You’re mistaken. She doesn’t get taken. She—”

(She stops.)

> “I left her.”  
> “She needed me and I… gods…”

> “Tell me she fought them.”

(Silence answers.)

---

### Blue

(She goes still.)

> “No… not her. Not the girl.”

> “She was what was good left in him.”

> “She killed him, didn’t she?”

(The glass slips. Wine spills like blood.)

> “Gods… she called me sister.”

---

### Theron

(Silence. Then:)

> “Theron.”

> “He was the best of us, and the worst of us.  
> I hated him for both.”

> “Did he suffer?”

(Pause.)

> “Then that is mercy.”

> “I should have been there.”

> “I ran when he needed a sword… and I wasn’t there when he needed a friend.”

(She breaks — quietly.)

## The Song

**DM Narration (to [[Guaco]]):**

You don’t understand the words.  
Only the sound.

Not a performance. A wound with rhythm.

Her voice begins low — trembling thread in candlelight.

The elves do not join.  
They *hold* her.

Each bows their head. The room leans inward.

She sings:

- of a man lost to darkness  
- of a girl who broke  
- of a warrior taken while her sister looked away  

You don’t know the language — but you feel it.

A candle is placed beside you.  
The flame flickers once.

No one applauds.

---

**Vynemmara (after):**

> “That’s how we bury our dead when the body’s gone.”

> “You carried truth through the dark, Guaco of Vault Row.”

> “Stay a while. The night will remember you kindly.”

---

## After the Song

**DM Narration:**

No one speaks.

They move slowly, deliberately.

- A cup refilled — unseen hands  
- A cloth placed by her elbow  
- Heads bowed as she passes  

She leaves. Older.

---

**Maitre d’ (quietly):**

> “She will grieve in her own fashion, and heal in none.”

> “You did rightly, halfling. Not kindly — but rightly.”

> “The Willow will remember you.”

---

## Epilogue — The Road Back

**DM Narration:**

The door closes.

The warmth is gone.

The city returns — noise, smoke, life.

It should feel like home.

It doesn’t.

You carry something now.

Not grief.

Not quite.

But the echo of a song that refuses to fade.
