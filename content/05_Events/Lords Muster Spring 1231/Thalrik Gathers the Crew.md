## **Archives — No Time**

The door slams open.

---

Thalrik doesn’t slow.

Doesn’t explain.

---

> “We need to go.”

---

Norwin starts—

> “That’s not—”

---

Thalrik is already at the desk.

Scooping.

---

Ledgers. Notes. Loose pages.

All of it.

---

Stuffed straight into the bag of holding.

No sorting. No care.

---

That’s what lands.

---

Norwin freezes for half a beat—

then snaps into motion, grabbing the last of his work.

---

> “You’re telling me while we move.”

---

> “King knows enough.”

---

They’re already out the door.

---

## 🎬 **Temple of Heironeous — Courtyard**

Full.

---

Paladins.

Dozens.

---

Rucksacks stacked.

Travel gear laid out.

Dust still on them.

---

Not ceremony.

---

**Deployment.**

---

No laughter.

No warmth.

---

Just quiet, controlled readiness.

---

They don’t even look at the PCs.

---

They don’t need to.

---

## 🎬 **Street — Pressure Rising**

A dozen **Pelorite paladins** pass.

Hard men.

---

They scan faces as they walk.

Cold.

Measuring.

---

One lingers a fraction too long—

then moves on.

---

Norwin mutters under his breath.

---

> “That’s new.”

---

Thalrik doesn’t answer.

---

## 🎬 **Citadel — The Muster Grounds**

They round the corner.

---

Scaffolding.

Canvas-draped.

Carefully hidden.

---

Thalrik stops half a step.

Just enough.

---

Then:

---

> “No.”

---

Not repairs.

---

The frame is wrong.

The height—

---

> “That’s a gallows.”

---

Right beside the entrance.

---

Three **paladins of Stern Alia** stand watch.

Still. Silent.

---

A man drifts too close—

A hand. A word.

He leaves.

---

No argument.

---

One of the paladins looks up.

Locks onto Thalrik.

---

And **stares.**

---

Judgment.

---

Norwin exhales sharply.

---

> “We’re out of time.”

---

They move.

Fast.

---

## 🎬 **Guaco’s — Halfling Hospitality Meets Panic**

They burst in.

---

Warmth hits them first.

Bread. Butter. Something sweet.

---

Halflings moving everywhere.

Plates already in hand.

---

> “You’re just in time—sit, sit—”

> “Eat before you talk—”

> “You look like you’ve seen a ghost—”

---

Food is **pressed into their hands**.

Not offered.

---

Thalrik takes it automatically.

Doesn’t even look.

---

Shoves a bite in as he moves.

---

Norwin grabs something without breaking stride.

Still muttering.

---

> “Audit cycles, gallows, Pelorites in the street—this is not—this is not how this is supposed to—”

---

No one is sitting.

No one is stopping.

---

The halflings keep feeding them anyway.

---

## 🎬 **Back Table — Rykeena & Reigar**

Quieter.

---

Rykeena and **Reigar** sit together.

Simple breakfast.

Laughing.

---

It stops the second they see Thalrik.

---

Because he’s not angry.

---

He’s **focused**.

---

And just a little—

---

**off.**

---

Norwin is already talking.

Too fast.

---

> “We need to move. Now. Not later, not after food—now—”

---

Reigar stands smoothly.

No panic.

---

But his eyes sharpen immediately.

---

> “What happened?”

---

Thalrik answers.

Flat.

---

> “King’s purging.”

A beat.

---

> “We’re not waiting to see how wide.”

---

Rykeena is already on her feet.

---

Reigar looks between them.

Reads it.

All of it.

---

The food.

The rush.

The tone.

---

And nods once.

---

> “Right.”

---

No questions.

---

Behind them—

a halfling presses another piece of bread into Norwin’s hand.

---

He takes it.

Still talking.

---

No one is staying.

---

They move.

Together.

---

Fast.

---

Outside—

the city hasn’t caught up yet.

---

But it will.
