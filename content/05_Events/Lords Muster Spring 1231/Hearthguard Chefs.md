# The Hearthguard Halflings

*“We have seen the pot of squash. We will see it no more.”*

The **Hearthguard Halflings** are an elite culinary strike team dispatched from the Royal Kitchens of Gorundia. They do not cook.

They **correct**.

Where they go, blandness dies screaming.

---

## Core Concept

A semi-formal unit of halfling master cooks operating under royal authority. Equal parts chefs, inspectors, and terrifying little tyrants of flavor.

They carry:
- Absolute authority over kitchens they are assigned to
- The expectation of obedience
- Zero tolerance for mediocrity

---

---

## Authority

Issued under seal of the Royal Kitchens:

- May **override local cooks**
- May **restructure kitchens**
- May **confiscate ingredients**
- May **publicly shame culinary crimes**

They do not leave until:
> “Your larder is free of mediocrity and your plates sing.”

---

## Cultural Role

Halflings in Gorundia already serve as:
- Hearth-keepers  
- Social stabilizers  
- Quiet moral anchors 

The Hearthguard are what happens when that instinct gets weaponized.

---

## Notable Event: The Soggy Siege

During the Siege of Elderford:
- Army morale collapsed due to terrible food  
- Hearthguard arrived disguised as minstrels  
- Fed both sides  

Result:
- Enemy surrendered  
- Possibly due to diplomacy  
- Definitely due to gravy

## Typical Menu (When Forced to Use Bad Ingredients)

They can redeem anything. Even squash.

- Spiced squash bisque  
- Squash dumplings with brown butter sage  
- Squash tart with candied ginger  

They hate it.

They will still make it incredible.

## Tone at the Table

Use them as:
- Comic relief with **real authority**
- Social disruptors in noble settings
- Unexpected “you are being judged” energy

They should feel like:
> Small. Polite. Completely unstoppable.
