# “He’s Got a Little List, and We’re Marching Down It”

_A Steel Crusade camp-song, heard among the king’s soldiers on the road against the Vogel holdouts, Raven Queen remnants, and other assorted bad decisions wearing armor._

_(Sung loudly, badly, and with great affection. The Lord Marshal is assumed not to approve. This has never stopped anyone.)_

---

### Verse I – The Marshal on the March

As some day it may happen that a realm must be set right,  
With the dead still wearing banners and the crows still loving night,  
There rode a certain Marshal with his jaw clenched like a fist,  
And the army all agreed he’d brought a proper little list.

Oh yes, he has a little list —  
A long and bloody little list —  
And if you serve the Raven’s shade,  
You’re probably on the list!

---

### Verse II – The Steel Crusade

Now the bells have rung for marching, and the priests have blessed the steel,  
And the boys who joked at breakfast learned how holy war can feel.  
For the road is full of ashes, and the fields are full of mist,  
And the Marshal rides before us with both eyes upon the list.

Oh yes, he has a little list —  
It points us through the rain!  
From Vogel’s broken carrion-crown  
To every crow-fed stain.

---

### Verse III – The Dead Men Named

DeSalle is in the cold earth, Cron has made his final plea,  
Wilhelm Vogel counts no prisoners, and Ponzio bends no knee.  
There’s no need to whisper softly or pretend the point was missed,  
For some names written yesterday are scratched clean off the list.

Oh yes, he had a little list —  
And some have left the page!  
A tidy thing, a finished line,  
An end to noble rage.

---

### Verse IV – The Exiles

Allec fled the reckoning, Cornelia fled the same,  
Both carried out of judgment with the ashes of their name.  
Now they brood in Zara’s country, where the river-fog lies kissed,  
And thank the gods for mercy keeping blood off that list.

Oh yes, he has a little list —  
But mercy has a pen!  
For exile is a sharper cage  
Than graves for certain men.

---

### Verse V – Zara’s Turning

Now Zara, countess dragon-eyed, once walked a crooked way,  
But now she stands for king and crown, and none may call it play.  
She came to Triad’s daylight from the old and darker twist,  
And even the Marshal paused before revising that list.

Oh yes, he has a little list —  
But some names change their line!  
A soul may turn from shadow’s cup  
And swear by law divine.

---

### Verse VI – The Wife Beside Him

His lady rides beside him, and she knows the winter stone,  
The way he speaks to captains like he’d rather be alone.  
She knows when he has eaten, which he treats as some offense,  
And when he says, “I’m rested,” she hears treason against sense.

Oh yes, he has a little list —  
But hers is shorter still!  
It reads: “Eat food. Sleep once. Stop glaring.”  
And he obeys her will.

---

### Verse VII – The Army’s Complaint

He lists the boots and spearheads, lists the arrows, grain, and tack,  
Lists the men who limped at dawn and those who tried to wander back.  
He lists the carts, the cooks, the mules, the barrels poorly kissed,  
And somehow knows which bastard pissed too near the supply list.

Oh yes, he has a little list —  
The man can smell a lie!  
He knows who stole the salted pork  
And where the dice-cups hide.

---

### Verse VIII – The Raven Queen’s Leftovers

Now the Raven’s little remnants skulk in chapel, wood, and wall,  
With their bone-charms and their grave-talk and their doom for one and all.  
They mutter from the darkness that the living won’t be missed,  
Then hear the king’s drums answering: “Come step onto the list.”

Oh yes, he has a little list —  
For crows who preach decay!  
We’ll teach their queenless carrion-priests  
How soldiers greet the day.

---

### Verse IX – The Marshal’s Cheer

He does not smile at sunrise. He does not laugh at noon.  
He regards a cheerful campfire like a tactical misfortune.  
Yet when the line is breaking and the fog begins to twist,  
He is exactly where we need him, reading from the list.

Oh yes, he has a little list —  
And thank the gods he does!  
For cheer is fine in taverns, lads,  
But steel remembers blood.

---

### Verse X – The Soldiers’ Truth

Let Bannerhall make music out of perfume, wine, and spite,  
Let cowards call it tyranny when someone wins the fight.  
We’ve seen the graves behind us, and the roads that still persist,  
So give us maps, a hard-eyed lord, and one more cursed list.

Oh yes, he has a little list —  
And we are marching down!  
For law rides out in muddy boots  
To drag rot from the crown.

---

### Final Chorus

He’s grim as cold winter, and polite as sharpened wire,  
He thinks a pleasant evening is a properly banked fire.  
But his lady rides beside him, and the king’s men raise a fist,  
For the realm still stands behind him, and he’s still got the list.

Oh yes, he has a little list —  
A grim and blessed little list!  
If you serve dead Vogel’s shadow,  
Then your name is on the list.

Oh yes, he has a little list —  
And we’ll see the damned thing through!  
With steel for every carrion crow,  
And dawn for me and you.
