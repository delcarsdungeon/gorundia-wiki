# Lady of Steel

(Verse 1) They say the Lady’s heart is ice, her eyes are northern flame, She speaks in words of iron weight and never shows her pain. A hundred years of battle scars lie quiet in her stride, And those who’ve fought beside her know she never turns aside. 

(Verse 2) They whisper of her father’s name—the Dragon cold and grim, Who carved the kingdom’s peace in blood, and shaped the child like him. But when his shadow called her close, she chose another way— To walk the world as Freeblade now, her honor not his chain. 

(Chorus) The Lady of Steel, her mercy concealed, Her vows are her armor, her silence her shield. No crown could command her, no prayer could unseal, The sorrow she carries—the Lady of Steel. 

(Verse 3) Her comrades fall like autumn leaves, and still she does not weep, For every name she’s buried lies awake within her sleep. They call her cold; they do not see the burden she must bear, To live so long and still be kind would take a heart too rare. 

(Bridge) She was the Dragon’s chosen heir, the blade he meant to wield, But turned her back on fire and fear, and swore to guard the field. She fights for those who cannot stand, for causes never won, And though her war will never end, she’ll fight till all is done. 

(Final Chorus) The Lady of Steel, her mercy concealed, Her vows are her armor, her silence her shield. When dragons are dust and the banners all reel, Still one will stand watching—the Lady of Steel. 

(Coda / Soft outro) So raise your cups in sorrow’s name, and drink to what’s unseen— The love behind her hardened face, the ghost of what has been. For those who bear the weight alone, no poet’s words can heal— But we remember still… the Lady of Steel.
