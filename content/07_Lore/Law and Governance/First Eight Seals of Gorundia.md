## The First Seal: The Crown Seal

**Held by:** The King  
**Office:** Sovereign Authority  
**Domain:** Law, succession, royal command, noble recognition

This is the big obvious one. The seal of kingship itself.

It validates royal decrees, noble elevations, attainders, legitimations, pardons, war declarations, and “because I am the king and I said so” paperwork. It is not subtle. It is the legal hammer that makes dukes stop pretending they are independent little weather systems.

**Symbol:** Griffon rampant over crown and sword  
**Use:** Royal charters, declarations of treason, grants of title, emergency proclamations

> “By the First Seal, the Crown speaks.”

## The Second Seal: The Hearth Seal

**Held by:** The Lord Chancellor / Keeper of the Rolls  
**Office:** Civil governance  
**Domain:** Records, inheritance, courts, charters, land law

This is the seal of continuity. It makes sure the kingdom doesn’t collapse every time a baron dies, a cousin lies, or three bastards and a forged will wander into court wearing their best boots.

It controls royal archives, recognized bloodlines, confirmed marriages, wardships, land patents, and noble claims.

**Symbol:** Open book beneath a crowned lamp  
**Use:** Legal confirmations, inheritance rulings, recorded noble oaths, chartered towns

> “By the Second Seal, the realm remembers.”

Very useful. Deeply boring. Which means everyone ignores it until they desperately need it, like most civilization.

## The Third Seal: The Anchor Seal

**Held by:** Thalrik Vaultforge  
**Known Style:** **Holder of the Third Anchor Seal**  
**Office:** Admiral / Sky Fleet Authority  
**Domain:** Naval command, skyfleet authority, royal anchorage, fortified ports, military transport

This one we’ve already touched directly. Thalrik holds it.

The Anchor Seal gives authority over royal anchorage by **sea, sky, and spell**. It covers ships, skyships, docks, military cargo, troop movement, port fortresses, and emergency seizure of vessels for Crown use.

**Symbol:** Anchor wrapped in chain beneath a griffon wing  
**Use:** Fleet orders, port seizures, skyship deployment, royal transport warrants

> “By the Third Seal, the Crown may move.”

This is why Thalrik can say “that ship is now doing king business” and some merchant captain has to discover patriotism at swordpoint.

## The Fourth Seal: The Sword Seal

**Held by:** The High Sword of Heironeous / senior military-religious commander  
**Office:** Sacred martial defense  
**Domain:** Paladin hosts, holy musters, lawful war, battlefield sanctification

This seal bridges Crown and Triad. It does not replace the king’s authority, but it empowers lawful holy defense of the kingdom.

It can call paladins, sanctify banners, authorize crusading action against infernal, undead, or oathbreaking enemies, and bind noble houses to a righteous war effort.

**Symbol:** Upright sword through a sunburst shield  
**Use:** Holy muster, formal crusade, consecrated battle standards, infernal war response

> “By the Fourth Seal, the sword is clean.”

This is the one everyone invokes when they want their war to sound noble instead of “we are killing cousins over taxes again.”

## The Fifth Seal: The Lantern Seal

**Held by:** Master of Heralds / Crown’s diplomatic service  
**Office:** Royal heraldry and formal communications  
**Domain:** Heralds, envoys, diplomatic immunity, formal summons, noble notice

This seal governs the **Royal Heralds**: the king’s voice, eyes, and ears across the realm.

It gives authority to issue summons, witness oaths, carry sealed messages, enter noble halls under protection, and formally notify lords of Crown action. Violating a herald under this seal is a very efficient way to become an example in a sermon and then a corpse.

**Symbol:** Silver lantern over crossed scrolls  
**Use:** Summons to Bannerhall or Lord’s Muster, diplomatic messages, royal notices, protected envoys

> “By the Fifth Seal, the Crown is heard.”

This one makes sense with your existing herald setup: light blue cloaks, silver trim, patents, assigned noble signets, all that deliciously fussy medieval bureaucracy.

## The Sixth Seal: The Scale Seal

**Held by:** High Justiciar / Warden of the King’s Law  
**Office:** Royal justice  
**Domain:** Treason trials, royal courts, writs, executions, seizure of criminal assets

This seal is legal violence in a nice coat.

It authorizes royal investigations, extraordinary courts, seizure of property under lawful judgment, arrest of nobles, trial of officers, and execution warrants. This is the seal that wakes up when someone says, “Surely a duke cannot be arrested.”

The kingdom then replies: “That is adorable.”

**Symbol:** Scales hanging from a sword hilt  
**Use:** Royal warrants, treason inquiries, asset seizure, execution orders, judicial commissions

> “By the Sixth Seal, the Crown judges.”

This is where someone like Arweena Kestral would become very interested, professionally and spiritually. Also terrifyingly polite.

## The Seventh Seal: The Granary Seal

**Held by:** Lord Treasurer / Steward of the Realm  
**Office:** Royal finance and supply  
**Domain:** Taxation, treasury, grain, roads, emergency provisioning

This is the unsexy seal that wins wars.

It covers tax authority, war levies, Crown debt, royal stores, grain reserves, emergency requisition, repair of strategic roads, and paying people who would otherwise become bandits with flags.

**Symbol:** Wheat sheaf bound with a silver chain  
**Use:** War taxes, grain release, payment orders, royal debt instruments, emergency requisitions

> “By the Seventh Seal, the realm endures.”

Everybody mocks the money men until the army needs boots, arrows, oats, flour, and someone who can count past twelve without using relatives.

---

## The Eighth Seal: The Gate Seal

**Held by:** Warden of the Marches / Master of Musters / border command, depending on reign  
**Office:** Defensive readiness  
**Domain:** Fortresses, borders, musters, beacons, emergency levies

This seal governs the walls of the kingdom: border forts, watchtowers, beacons, mustering fields, road gates, emergency levies, and defensive mobilization.

It can compel local lords to open stores, send men, repair walls, man passes, and hold gates in the king’s name.

**Symbol:** Closed gate beneath two raised spears  
**Use:** Border alerts, emergency musters, fortress command, beacon chains, martial road closures

> “By the Eighth Seal, the realm stands.”

This is the seal you want active before the devils arrive, not after, but nobility has a long and glorious tradition of noticing doors only once something awful is chewing through them.
