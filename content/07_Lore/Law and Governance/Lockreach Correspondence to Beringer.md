## **Mage Transcription – Lockreach Correspondence**
**Origin:** Lockreach  
**Recipient:** The Lord Marshal, [[Beringer]]  
**Method:** Third Veil Transmission (verified, immediate dispatch)

---

To the Lord Marshal, Sir Beringer Amadis,

Permit me first to offer my most sincere congratulations on the events now concluded in the capital, and on your continued service to the Crown in this decisive hour.

Word has reached Lockreach—swiftly and with clarity—and I would be remiss not to acknowledge both the justice rendered and the strength of hand required to see it done. Such moments define not only a reign, but those entrusted to safeguard it.

In your capacity as Lord Marshal, you have already demonstrated the steadiness and resolve required to preserve order in a time that might otherwise have unraveled into uncertainty. It is my belief—shared quietly, but widely—that such qualities will prove no less essential in the days ahead, as the future of Grevandar settles into its proper course.

Should that future place greater burdens upon your shoulders, know that Lockreach stands ready to support you, as it always has supported the lawful governance of this county—through stability, diligence, and the uninterrupted flow of provision to crown and command alike.

I would also speak, if I may, to the legacy of your blood. Galmyr Vogel was a figure of undeniable strength, whatever history may choose to make of him, and it is no small thing to see that strength tempered now with the discipline and clarity you have shown. Nor can one overlook the influence of House Amadis, whose name has long stood for honor under strain and duty without flourish. That these lines meet in you is… a fortunate convergence for the realm.

And, though it may be impertinent at such a remove, I would add what has been remarked upon even here at Lockreach: that your lady wife is spoken of with uncommon admiration—not only for her beauty, but for her composure and bearing in a court that has tested both in equal measure. Such things are not overlooked in times such as these.

It is a regret to me personally that my duties at Lockreach prevented my attendance in the capital during these events. I would have counted it a privilege to witness firsthand the restoration of proper order.

As to the late Lord Ponzio Bordella, it is perhaps best said that the weight of leadership requires a certain… constancy of character. It is not always enough to inherit expectation; one must also meet it. In this, I believe the realm has been… corrected.

Lockreach remains, as ever, in service to the Crown, to the King, and to those entrusted with the realm’s defense and governance.

May this mark the beginning of a more stable and prosperous chapter for us all.

In loyal service,

**Lord Cassian Virello-Bordella**  
Warden of the Reach, Keeper of the Chain
