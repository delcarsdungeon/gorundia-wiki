## **The Charter of Bard’s Gate**

_As of 1230 O.R., in the 24th year of the Reign of King Rimmand II, Protector of the Realm and Shield of the Peoples_

---

Let it be known, to all who dwell within the bounds of our lands and beyond, that the city of Bard’s Gate, with its walls and towers, its merchants and guilds, its bards and people, shall henceforth stand as a **Free City under the Crown**, enjoying the rights, liberties, and protections here enshrined.

---
## **Article I: Sovereign Status**

Bard’s Gate is declared a Free City within the realm.

It shall govern itself under the supervision of its Lord Mayor, elect its own magistrates and council, and hold authority over the city’s affairs—

**save for those matters reserved to the Crown.**

---

## **Article II: Rights of the People**

The citizens of Bard’s Gate are granted the following rights, in perpetuity:

- The right to trade freely within the city and across the realm without undue taxation or obstruction
- The right to self-defense and the maintenance of a city guard and militia
- The right to establish and operate guilds, academies, and places of worship without interference
- The right to petition the Crown through their elected council

---
## **Article III: Obligations to the Crown**

As a Free City, Bard’s Gate shall owe the Crown:

- Loyalty in word and deed
- Tribute equal to one-tenth of its yearly revenues, rendered in coin or kind
- Soldiers, ships, or provisions in times of war, at the Crown’s request

---
## **Article IV: Historical Rights**

In recognition of Bard’s Gate’s role as a haven of knowledge, art, and trade:

- Archives, bardic colleges, and libraries are declared **neutral ground**, protected even in times of war
- The Bardic Guild is granted the title **Heralds of the Realm**, with safe passage across the kingdom
- The Festival of the Bard’s Covenant shall be observed throughout the realm with royal blessing

---
## **Article V: Protection and Sovereignty**

No lord or knight of the realm may:

- Station forces within the city without the consent of its council
- Interfere in its governance

Any such violation shall be deemed **treason against the Crown**.

---
## **Article VI: The Year and a Day**

Any man or woman who dwells within Bard’s Gate, free and unclaimed, for the span of **one year and one day**, shall be recognized as a **freeman of the city**, and may not be reclaimed as serf or bond-servant by any lord beyond its walls—

**provided they have committed no crime against the Crown or the City during that time.**

---
## **Seal and Witness**

In the Name of Heironeous, Guardian of Justice, and by My Royal Hand, this Charter is Sealed.

**King Rimmand II**  
Defender of the Realm

---
## **Royal Addendum to the Charter of Bard’s Gate**

_Issued on the 20th Day of Readying, in the 25th Year of the Reign of King Rimmand II, Defender of the Realm_

---

Let it be known that the Crown, having weighed the burdens of rule, the unrest within the realm, and the threats both seen and unseen, does hereby clarify and extend certain authorities within the Charter of Bard’s Gate.

These addenda do not revoke the Charter, but **define its limits under strain**, that the realm may endure what softer laws cannot.

---

## **Addendum I: On Sanctuary and Protection**

The protections of Bard’s Gate, including those granted under Article VI (Year and a Day), shall not extend to:

- Those named by royal writ as traitors to the Crown
- Agents acting on behalf of foreign powers in secret or deception
- Practitioners of forbidden arts deemed a threat to the realm

Such persons may be seized within the city by lawful authority of the Crown, notwithstanding prior protections.

---

## **Addendum II: On Royal Entry and Garrison**

In times of declared unrest, insurrection, or threat to the stability of the realm, the Crown reserves the right to:

- Station forces within Bard’s Gate
- Secure gates, walls, and key districts
- Act in preservation of the King’s Peace

Such presence shall be considered **protective, not intrusive**, and shall not be construed as violation of Article V.

---

## **Addendum III: On Arcane and Divine Oversight**

All persons practicing significant arcane or divine arts within the city must:

- Declare their practice to the appropriate civic or royal authority
- Submit to inquiry when called upon by the Crown or the Triad of the Light

Failure to do so may be treated as concealment of dangerous power and subject to investigation, restraint, or sanction.

No lawful practice is forbidden by this clause—  
but none shall be **hidden from the light of judgment**.

---

## **Addendum IV: On Sworn Men and Civic Defense**

The institution of Sworn Men, as recognized within the wards of Bard’s Gate, is hereby affirmed as lawful service to both City and Crown.

Sworn Men may be:

- Called to service by their Reeves
- Organized in defense of their wards
- Bound to uphold the King’s Peace alongside the Watch

In times of crisis, their duty extends beyond ward and street, as necessity demands.

---

## **Addendum V: On Interpretation and Authority**

Where conflict arises between the liberties of the Charter and the safety of the realm:

- The preservation of the realm shall take precedence
- Interpretation shall rest with the Crown, the Lord Mayor, and the united authority of the Triad

No clause of freedom shall be used to shield that which would bring ruin upon the whole.

---

## **Seal and Witness**

By my hand and by the authority granted me under the gods and the Crown:

Let these words stand as shield and warning alike.

**Rimmand II**  
King of Gorundia  
Defender of the Realm
