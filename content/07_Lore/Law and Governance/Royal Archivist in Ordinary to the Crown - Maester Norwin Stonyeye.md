1. THE RIGHTS AND PRIVILEGES OF MAESTER NORWIN STONYEYE, ARCHIVIST IN ORDINARY TO THE CROWN Issued under the seal of the Royal Chancery in the reign of His Majesty Rimmand II. Title and Standing Maester Norwin Stonyeye is hereby recognized as a Royal Archivist in Ordinary, sworn to the service of the Crown and subordinate to the Master Archivist of Gorundia. He is granted the style and authority appropriate to an officer of the Royal Archives, and shall bear the medallion of his office as proof of charge and legitimacy. Access to the Royal Archives The Archivist in Ordinary shall have free entry to the Common Stacks and Lesser Collections of the Royal Archives. He may, upon written approval of the Master Archivist or the Chancellor, be granted access to Restricted Stacks or sealed documents as required for Crown business. Authority to Examine and Verify Records He is empowered to examine, copy, and compare any document held in the royal collections, to certify the accuracy of copies, and to issue sworn statements regarding archival truth. Such statements hold legal weight until formally challenged before the Master Archivist or the Royal Court. Right of Petition and Inquiry For matters pertaining to the record, genealogy, land grants, or historical dispute, the Archivist in Ordinary may request access to noble archives, household ledgers, or private collections. Subjects of the realm must comply or seek relief through the Chancellor. He may not, without higher leave, compel the production of sealed or sacred writings. Right to Requisition Scribes and Lesser Staff He may command the service of junior scribes, runners, and copyists within the palace, and may request escort or guard accompaniment when traveling on archive business, such protection to be granted at the discretion of the Watch or the Master Archivist. Immunity in the Performance of Duty Any obstruction, intimidation, or harm offered to the Archivist in Ordinary while he performs his charge is deemed an affront to the King’s Record and shall be punished accordingly. He is exempt from conscription or levied service save by the direct command of His Majesty. Custody of a Private Working Archive He is permitted to maintain a private study and personal collection of working notes, copies, and minor records. Documents produced or maintained by him are to be treated as provisionally valid until reviewed by the Master Archivist or entered into the Great Index. Standing in Court and Council Within the palace and in matters of administration, the Archivist in Ordinary shall be afforded precedence above common knights and officers of lesser office. He is entitled to attend public readings of writs, view muster rolls, and review unsealed petitions brought before the Crown. Charge and Oath Maester Norwin Stonyeye is charged to preserve the truth of the realm, guard the memory of the Crown, and uphold the integrity of the written word. He shall discharge his duties faithfully, answer to the Master Archivist, and act always in the spirit of lawful record.
