**Letter Patent for Royal Cartographer** Hanna has a formal patent naming her: “Hanna of Westhold, Royal Cartographer Extraordinary and Keeper of the King’s Measures.” Her Letter Patent grants: -Access to the royal archives (shared domain with Norwin) -Authority to requisition escorts on dangerous survey routes -Permission to enter restricted lands for mapping -Custodianship of the Royal Charts and All High Metes, including the hex-boundaries of the noble estates -Legal protection — harming a Royal Cartographer is treason-adjacent, since she safeguards the kingdom’s borders and land validity Her maps determine disputes between barons, counts, and sometimes dukes. She is, quietly, one of the most powerful “noncombatants” in the bureaucracy.
