## **Mage Transcription – Lockreach Correspondence**
**Origin:** Lockreach  
**Recipient:** Her Excellency, Countess Dana Sylvanranth of Blackvale  
**Method:** Third Veil Transmission (verified, unaltered)

---

To Her Excellency, Countess Dana Sylvanranth,  
Rightful Lady of Blackvale, Warden of the Vale, and Daughter of the Late Lord Theron—

May this message find you in strength and in rightful command of the lands now restored to proper stewardship.

I write with utmost haste upon receiving word of the… recent revelations concerning House Bordella. I beg leave to state, with absolute clarity and without reservation:

**I had neither knowledge of, nor involvement in, any treason, conspiracy, or disloyal conduct undertaken by that house or its members.**

My elevation to Lockreach, while bearing the Bordella name, was one of **function and necessity**, not of blood, counsel, or confidence. I was entrusted with the management of river tolls and the regulation of trade—nothing more. I was not privy to their inner dealings, nor consulted in matters beyond the strict operation of the Reach.

Indeed, I have long conducted my duties in accordance with the laws of the Crown and the stability of Blackvale, and would never knowingly act against either.

Should any record, account, or testimony be required, I stand ready to provide full access to all ledgers, tallies, and operational reports under my authority. Lockreach remains orderly, productive, and—most importantly—**loyal to the County and its rightful ruler.**

If it pleases Your Excellency, I would welcome the opportunity to present myself in person, to reaffirm my service and to receive your guidance as to the continued governance of the Reach under your rule.

Until such time, I remain,

Your obedient and faithful servant,  
**Lord Cassian Virello-Bordella**  
Warden of the Reach, Keeper of the Chain  

---

**Addendum (appended shortly after initial transmission):**

For the sake of absolute clarity, I further affirm that **no instructions, written or otherwise, were ever received by me from Lord Ponzio Bordella or his kin that would conflict with the laws of the Crown or the peace of the realm.**

Should any such claims arise, they are made without my knowledge or consent.
