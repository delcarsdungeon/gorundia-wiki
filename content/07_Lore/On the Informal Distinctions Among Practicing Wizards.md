# On the Informal Distinctions Among Practicing Wizards

> [!important]
> The following is not a formal system, recognized body, or enforceable doctrine.
>
> It is, however, how wizards actually evaluate one another.

---
> *“This is not how the world sees us.  This is how we see each other.  If you leave here with one lesson, let it be this: the nobles think in titles.  We think in outcomes.”*  
> — Norwin Stoneyeye

---

## The Problem of Titles

In Gorundia, a wizard is either:

- not worth mentioning  
- or a **Maester**

To a noble, this is sufficient.

To a wizard, it is meaningless.

The title of *Maester* indicates only that a practitioner is:

- competent enough to be trusted in a court  
- politically safe enough not to embarrass a patron  
- useful enough to justify their keep  

It does **not** indicate mastery, precision, stability, or depth.

Those distinctions exist—but only among us.

---

## On Speaking Plainly (And Why We Do Not)

You will note, as you progress, that wizards do not describe one another in direct terms.

We do not say:

- “He is unstable.”
- “She is derivative.”
- “That one should not be allowed near a live casting circle.”

We say:

- *“The equation wanders.”*
- *“Second-hand geometry.”*
- *“Writes past the page.”*

This serves three purposes:

1. **Precision** — the language is more exact than it appears  
2. **Discretion** — reputations are managed, not destroyed  
3. **Opacity** — nobles hear approval or concern without understanding either  

You may insult a colleague.

You do not casually deprive him of employment.

Unless, of course, you intend to replace him.

## The Veils

Among practitioners, progress is most commonly described in terms of **Veils**.

These are not ranks, but shared markers of capability and control.

---

### First Veil — *The Opened Eye*
Perception and initial manipulation of arcane forces.

---

### Second Veil — *The Steady Hand*
Reliable casting. Minimal variance.

---

### Third Veil — *The Sound Mind*
Predictable outcomes. Court-safe.

> This is the threshold at which most are recognized as Maesters.

---

### Fourth Veil — *The Closed Circuit*
Sustained, layered, and stable workings.

> Most competent court wizards plateau here.

---

### Fifth Veil — *The Unbound Equation*
Original work. Adaptation beyond standard forms.

---

### Sixth Veil — *The Quiet Flame*
Power and restraint in equal measure.

---

### Seventh Veil — *The Final Silence*
A matter of debate, exaggeration, or obituary.

---

## Disciplines and Practical Identity

A wizard is not known by title, but by **what he does reliably**.

Common identifications include:

- **Threadbinder** — maintains correspondence across distance  
- **Keeper of the Line** — stable communication networks  
- **Whisperwright** — discreet or encrypted messaging  

- **Circle-Keeper** — warding and defensive work  
- **Sealwright** — containment and binding  

- **Stormcaller** — large-scale destructive casting  
- **Fieldbreaker** — battlefield disruption  

- **Glasswalker** — illusion  
- **Mind-Turner** — enchantment  

- **Bone-Scribe** — theoretical necromancy  
- **Grave Listener** — sanctioned death-work  

In practice, the first group is more valuable than the third.

Do not repeat that in front of soldiers.

## On Lineage

You will be judged not only by your work, but by your origin.

We remember:

- who trained you  
- who trained them  
- what methods you inherit  

This is rarely stated openly, but always understood.

## Common Arcane Judgments

The following phrases are in common use among practitioners.

You are expected to understand them.

---

### Reliable Work

- *Holds the Line Without Witness*  
- *Carries a Closed Circuit*  
- *Works in True Measure*  
- *Unfractured in the Third Turning*  

---

### Promising, Limited, or Incomplete

- *Bright in the First Light*  
- *Quick to Pattern*  
- *Speaks Cleanly*  

---

### Instability and Concern

- *The Equation Wanders*  
- *Carries Heat in the Margins*  
- *Open at the Edges*  
- *Writes Past the Page*  

---

### Quiet Condemnation

- *The Work Does Not Survive Repetition*  
- *Second-Hand Geometry*  
- *Bound to Borrowed Light*  
- *The Line Breaks Under Silence*  

---

### Matters Best Left Unexamined

- *Carries an Unfinished Shape*  
- *The Mirror Does Not Agree*  
- *Keeps a Door Unnamed*  

---

### Dangerous Competence

- *The Circuit Closes on Itself*  
- *Writes in Black Margin*  
- *The Work Continues After the Hand Stops*  
- *Unanswered by the Veils*  

---

## Practical Usage

A full evaluation is rarely spoken plainly.

Instead:

> “Fourth Veil Threadbinder. Holds the Line Without Witness.”

This is sufficient.

Likewise:

> “Third Veil. Quick to pattern. The equation wanders.”

This is also sufficient.

---

## Final Instruction

You will learn, in time, to hear what is meant rather than what is said.

Until then, remember:

A noble hears:
> “Maester.”

A wizard hears:
> everything that matters.
