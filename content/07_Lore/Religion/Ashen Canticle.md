Ashen Canticle "In the heart of the flame where the fire once slept, A mortal stood vigil while the old embers wept. To walk as a daughter of fire or fade, She chose to remain, though the pyres decayed. But flame is a promise; it never forgets, And the Ashen Canticle sings no regrets. Three daughters of cinders, three voices of light, Now rise to rekindle the warmth of the night. With every step, they weave the ember’s refrain, A song of renewal, the cleansing of pain. For what was forsaken shall not be forlorn, In the ashes of what was, a new flame is born."
