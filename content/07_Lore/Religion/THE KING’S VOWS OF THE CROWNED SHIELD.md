THE KING’S VOWS OF THE CROWNED SHIELD

The Vows of the Crowned Shield are spoken before dawn on the day of coronation, in the sanctuary behind the Bannerhall altar, witnessed only by the Chaplain of the Triad. These vows bind the monarch not by law, but by divine right. They are the measure by which Heironeous judges the man who bears the crown.

VOW OF THE THREE LIGHTS
“The Crown shall bend to no single Light. Valor stands not above Mercy, nor Mercy above Law.”
The King must never favor Heironeous over Pelor or Pholtus, nor permit any one temple to influence policy above the others. He must prevent overreach by all three. He rules in balance, or he does not rule at all.

VOW OF THE OPEN HAND
“The blade of justice sees not birth nor blood, only the deed.”
All subjects of Gorundia stand equal before the King’s justice: human, halfling, dwarf, elf, half-orc. If a noble house uses race or bloodline to twist the law, the King must personally correct the injustice. This vow is the source of his reputation as progressive, and the root of old-guard resentment.

VOW OF THE TEMPERED SWORD
“Let courage guide the stroke, never wrath.”
The King may not begin a war for vengeance, pride, or politics. Only the defense of the innocent, the realm, or a righteous cause justifies force. He must offer one chance for parley unless the foe is bound to tyranny or absolute evil. Heironeous demands valor, not cruelty.

VOW OF THE SANCTIFIED WORD
“A broken oath is a broken Crown.”
The King must uphold every treaty, judgment, and sworn word made in the name of the Crown, unless the oath itself is corrupted by evil or coercion. He may deceive an enemy on the field, but never his own people. His honesty is part of the kingdom’s strength.

VOW OF THE SHARED BURDEN
“A king may hold treasure, but the paladin may not hoard it.”
Royal wealth exists for the kingdom, not the man. The King must not enrich himself personally or maintain excess beyond the dignity of his office. Bribes, indulgence, and unjust gifts are forbidden. What he owns as a man must be only what he could carry on campaign. The rest belongs to the realm.

VOW OF THE FIRSTMARCH
“A king leads from the front.”
The King must take the field at least once per campaign season unless incapacitated. The Crowned Shield is no armchair ruler; his presence in battle is a sign of divine legitimacy and national morale. He bears risk equal to his knights.

VOW OF MERCY WITH BLADE IN HAND
“Strike when you must, spare when you can.”
The King must grant mercy to surrendering foes, forbid torture, and ensure lawful trials for all who stand judged. Yet he may kill without hesitation when innocents are endangered. This vow tempers the Sword with compassion, even when it costs him politically.

THE SECRET VOW OF THE LAST LIGHT
“When darkness rises in the Crown, the Crowned Shield shall fall by his own hand before the realm falls by his shadow.”
If corruption, possession, tyranny, or moral ruin ever takes him, the King must step down or die. This vow is known only to the Chaplain and the Crowned Shield himself. It is the final safeguard of divine right.
