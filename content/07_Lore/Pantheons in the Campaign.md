# 1. Oeridian Pantheon

This is the primary human religious tradition in Gorundia, both because of ancestry and because its gods fit a feudal kingdom built around law, knighthood, warfare, trade, and marching somewhere unpleasant under a banner.

## Law, Chivalry, Justice, and Authority

- **Heironeous** — Chivalry, honor, justice, valor, daring, righteous war.
- **Pholtus** — Light, law, resolution, truth, unwavering order.
- **Zilchus** — Power, prestige, influence, wealth, commerce, business.
- **Delleb** — Reason, intellect, study, learning.

## War, Conquest, and Violence

- **Hextor** — War, discord, massacre, conquest, tyranny.
- **Erythnul** — Hate, envy, malice, panic, slaughter.

Heironeous and Hextor therefore cover two very different interpretations of organized violence: one thinks war should serve justice; the other thinks justice is whatever survives the war. Conveniently, humans have never stopped arguing about this.

## Sky, Sea, Stars, and Travel

- **Procan** — Oceans, seas, salt, storms, sea weather.
- **Celestian** — Stars, space, wanderers, distant journeys.
- **Fharlanghn** — Horizons, distance, roads, travel.
- **Velnius** — Sky, weather, rain and storms.

## Seasons, Winds, and Agriculture

The four Oeridian seasonal powers are collectively known as the **Velaeri**:

- **Atroa** — Spring, east wind, renewal.
- **Sotillion** — Summer, south wind, ease, comfort.
- **Wenta** — Autumn, west wind, harvest.
- **Telchur** — Winter, north wind, cold.

## Luck, Skill, Revenge, and Crime

- **Rudd** — Chance, good luck, skill, games.
- **Kurell** — Jealousy, revenge, thievery.

## Sun, Healing, and Strength

- **Pelor** — Sun, strength, light, healing.

**Edition wrinkle:** Pelor was marked **Flan/Common in 1e**, but placed with the **Oeridian gods in 2e From the Ashes**. For Gorundia, the 2e placement works culturally: his faith has been completely absorbed into Oeridian society and the Triad. His Flan origins are something scholars discuss after everyone else has left the room.

## Widely Worshipped Gods Common in Oeridian Lands

These are not strictly Oeridian by origin, but are widespread enough that Gorundians would encounter them without needing a map and an anthropology lecture.

- **Boccob** — Magic and arcane knowledge.
- **Bleredd** — Metal, mines, smiths.
- **Cyndor** — Time, continuity, infinity.
- **Ehlonna** — Forests, flowers, meadows, woodland creatures.
- **Incabulos** — Plague, nightmares, disease, famine.
- **Joramy** — Fire, volcanoes, anger, quarrels.
- **Lirr** — Prose, poetry, art.
- **Myrhiss** — Love and beauty.
- **Olidammara** — Music, revelry, roguery, wine.
- **Ralishaz** — Chance, ill luck, misfortune.
- **Saint Cuthbert** — Wisdom, dedication, zeal, forthrightness.
- **Trithereon** — Individuality, liberty, retribution.
- **Ulaa** — Hills, mountains, gemstones.
- **Zagyg** — Humor, occult studies, eccentricity.
- **Mayaheine** — Protection, justice, valor; added as a demigoddess in 2e.

The Oeridian classification and portfolios are substantially consistent between the 1e boxed set and the 2e summary, apart from the noted cultural reassignment of Pelor.

# 2. Suel Pantheon

The Suel pantheon is older, harsher, more aristocratic and more comfortable with morally questionable specialists. It has gods for scholarship, music and honest labor, but also gods for poison, murder, lies and crawling around in pits. A complete civilization, apparently.

## Time, Magic, Law, and Death

- **Lendor** — Time, patience, continuity, tedium.
- **Wee Jas** — Magic, death, law, funerary rites.
- **Nerull** — Death, darkness, murder, the underworld.

**Edition wrinkle:** Nerull was **Flan/Common in 1e**, but placed in the **Suel pantheon in 2e From the Ashes**. Wee Jas governs lawful death and proper magical or funerary order; Nerull governs the violent and predatory taking of life.

## Strength, Beasts, Nature, and Cultivation

- **Kord** — Athletics, sports, strength, brawling.
- **Llerg** — Beasts, physical strength, savage endurance.
- **Phyton** — Beauty in nature, cultivation, farming.

## Craft, Labor, Stone, and Industry

- **Bralm** — Insects, industriousness, collective labor.
- **Fortubo** — Stone, metals, mountains, craftsmanship.

Fortubo is frequently honored by dwarves as well as humans, despite technically belonging to the Suel tradition. Gods, unlike bureaucrats, sometimes survive crossing a cultural boundary.

## Air, Sea, Travel, and Trade

- **Phaulkon** — Air, winds, clouds.
- **Xerbo** — Seas, water travel, trade, money, business.

## Knowledge, Music, and Daylight

- **Lydia** — Music, knowledge, daylight, learning.

## Luck and Risk

- **Norebo** — Luck, gambling, risk.

## Murder, Deceit, and Malice

- **Beltar** — Malice, pits, deep caves.
- **Pyremius** — Fire, poison, assassination, murder.
- **Syrul** — Deceit, false promises, lies.

## Later 2e Extensions

Later 2e Greyhawk material also restored or expanded several Suel powers:

- **Dalt** — Doors, locks, keys, portals and enclosures.
- **Jascar** — Hills and mountains.
- **Osprem** — Ships, sailors and sea voyages.
- **Vatun** — Winter, cold and the northern Suel barbarians.

# 3. Baklunish Pantheon

The Baklunish pantheon is comparatively small in early Greyhawk material. Its themes lean toward fate, discipline, hidden knowledge, water and personal mastery rather than European-style feudal authority.

## Fate and Destiny

- **Istus** — Fate, destiny, the course of mortal lives.

## Water and Sustenance

- **Geshtai** — Lakes, rivers, wells and streams.

## Shadow, Subtlety, and Inner Discipline

- **Xan Yae** — Twilight, shadows, stealth, mental discipline, mind over matter.
- **Zuoken** — Physical and mental mastery, disciplined perfection.

## Trade and Social Order

- **Mouqol** — Trade, commerce, negotiation and fair exchange.

## Duty and Guardianship

- **Al’Akbar** — Guardianship, faithfulness, dignity and duty; formally established as a Baklunish demigod in late 2e material.

The original 1e core was chiefly Istus, Geshtai, Xan Yae and Zuoken; 2e and later Greyhawk material developed the pantheon further instead of leaving the Baklunish with four gods and a polite note saying “more culture probably exists off-camera.”

# 4. Non-Human Pantheons

This section covers the major **player-facing nonhuman religions** appropriate to Gorundia. Monster Mythology also includes extensive orc, goblin, drow, giant, dragon, undersea and subterranean pantheons, but most Gorundians regard those as hostile cults rather than respectable alternatives for the village chapel. The 2e material greatly expanded the nonhuman gods beyond the shorter 1e lists.

## Elven Pantheon

### Creation, Magic, Art, and War

- **Corellon Larethian** — Elves, magic, art, music, swordsmanship and warfare.

### Moon, Dreams, Death, and Mysticism

- **Sehanine Moonbow** — Moonlight, dreams, mysticism, death and spiritual journeys.

### Sky and Wilderness

- **Aerdrie Faenya** — Air, wind, weather, birds.
- **Solonor Thelandira** — Hunting, archery and wilderness.

### Love, Beauty, Time, and Memory

- **Hanali Celanil** — Love, beauty and romance.
- **Labelas Enoreth** — Time, longevity, history and memory.

### Change, Trickery, and Outsiders

- **Erevan Ilesere** — Mischief, change, rogues and trickery.
- **Fenmarel Mestarine** — Outcasts, isolation and wild elves; a 2e expansion.

**Gorundian practice:** Ehlonna, called **Ehlenestra** by some elves, is often honored alongside these gods even though she is not strictly a member of the Seldarine. In Enstad, Corellon, Sehanine, Labelas and Ehlonna are already established as major devotions.

## Dwarven Pantheon

### Creation and Smithing

- **Moradin** — Creation, smithing, craftsmanship and the dwarven people.

### Hearth, Family, and Safety

- **Berronar Truesilver** — Home, family, marriage, healing and protection.

### War and Honor

- **Clangeddin Silverbeard** — Battle, valor and honorable warfare.

### Mines, Stone, and Secrets

- **Dumathoin** — Mining, buried wealth and secrets beneath the earth.

### Travel and Expatriates

- **Muamman Duathal** — Travel, exploration and dwarves living away from their ancestral halls.

### Wealth and Trade

- **Vergadain** — Wealth, luck, commerce and clever acquisition.

### Important 2e Extensions

- **Dugmaren Brightmantle** — Scholarship, discovery and invention.
- **Abbathor** — Greed and hoarded wealth.

## Gnomish Pantheon

### Protection, Humor, and Leadership

- **Garl Glittergold** — Gnomes, protection, humor, wit and trickery.

### Forests and Travel

- **Baervan Wildwanderer** — Nature, forests and wandering.

### Illusions and Concealment

- **Baravar Cloakshadow** — Illusions, disguise, deception and protection.

### Smithing and Metalwork

- **Flandal Steelskin** — Mining, smithing, fire and metalwork.

### Vigilance and Defense

- **Gaerdal Ironhand** — Protection, vigilance, fortification and combat.

### Earth and Burrows

- **Segojan Earthcaller** — Earth, burrows, underground life and the dead.

### Dangerous or Later Powers

- **Nebelun** — Invention, devices and inspired experimentation.
- **Urdlen** — Greed, bloodlust, destruction and the corruption beneath the earth.

## Halfling Pantheon

### Family, Protection, and Bounty

- **Yondalla** — Protection, family, fertility and bounty.

### Defense and Vigilance

- **Arvoreen** — Defense, vigilance, watchfulness and guardianship.

### Friendship and Hospitality

- **Cyrrollalee** — Friendship, trust, hospitality, honesty and the hearth.

### Adventure and Mischief

- **Brandobaris** — Adventure, stealth, mischief and wandering rogues.

### Agriculture and Nature

- **Sheela Peryroyl** — Agriculture, nature, weather and seasonal growth.

### Death and Burial

- **Urogalan** — Earth, death, burial and protection of the dead; chiefly developed in 2e.

These first five halfling gods are already the established Gorundian hearth pantheon, with temples typically taking the form of kitchens, inns and village chapels rather than grand basilicas built to impress people who were merely hoping for lunch.

# 5. Flan Pantheon

> **Gorundian note:** The **Flan pantheon as a coherent religious tradition is mostly unknown in the Gorundian area**. Scholars, druids, antiquarians and certain old rural cults may recognize the names, but most ordinary Gorundians would not think of them as a unified pantheon.
> 
> **Pelor is the major exception.** His worship has been completely naturalized through the Triad of Law and Light. Most Gorundians know Pelor as the Sun Father, not as an imported or ancestral Flan god.

## Earth, Nature, and the Wild

- **Beory** — The Oerth Mother, earth, nature, rain.
- **Obad-Hai** — Nature, wildlands, freedom, hunting.
- **Berei** — Home, family, agriculture.

## Sun, Healing, and Life

- **Pelor** — Sun, strength, light, healing.

Historically Flan/Common in 1e, but placed among Oeridian worship in 2e. In Gorundia, this distinction has essentially vanished beneath centuries of organized Pelorite worship.

## Peace, Ethics, Mercy, and Community

- **Rao** — Peace, reason, serenity.
- **Allitur** — Ethics, propriety, respect for law and tradition.
- **Zodal** — Mercy, hope and benevolence.

## Death and Darkness

- **Nerull** — Death, darkness, murder and the underworld.

Historically Flan/Common in 1e, reassigned to the Suel column in 2e.

## Oppression and Ancient Evil

- **Iuz** — Oppression, deceit and pain.
- **Vecna** — Secrets, forbidden knowledge and dark magic; a Flan-born lich elevated into divine status during 2e.

The older Flan gods are therefore less absent from Gorundia than **unrecognized**. Pelor is everywhere, but few worshippers know or care that his deepest cultural roots lie beside Beory, Rao and the other gods of the old Flan peoples. The rest survive mainly in druidic customs, old place-names, isolated shrines and the sort of archaeological discovery that invariably begins with someone saying, “It’s probably harmless.”
