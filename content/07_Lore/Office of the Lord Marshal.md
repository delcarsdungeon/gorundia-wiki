# Office of the Lord Marshal

## Proclamation of the Crown

> “Lords and ladies of Gorundia…
>
> There is a man among you who has carried more burden, more danger, and more responsibility than any of you knew.
>
> In the dark months, while traitors whispered and infernal banners stirred, I entrusted the command of the Crown’s secret armies — the March, the Wardens, the hidden companies — to a knight unswayed by ambition or bloodline.
>
> That man is Sir Beringer Amadis.
>
> By my seal, he has served as High Marshal of the Crown these many months…
>
> and today, I make his commission public.”

---

## The Ninth Seal

At this declaration, His Majesty produced the **Ninth Seal**.

A relic not seen in generations.

A symbol not invoked lightly.

---

### Description

A worn disk of aged silver, bearing:

- A **griffon** in vigilance  
- A **sword** unsheathed  
- A **broken crown**  

This sigil is not ceremonial.

It manifests only under conditions of:
- Usurpation  
- Assassination threat  
- Imminent fracture of the realm  

---

### Nature of the Seal

Nine seals exist within the Crown’s war authority:

- Eight are held by titled officers of the realm  
- The Ninth is reserved  

Not for rank.

Not for inheritance.

But for **necessity**.

---

The Ninth Seal names a single individual as:

> The King’s hand in war  
> The Crown’s will, unmediated  

---

## The Hidden Office

The Office of the Lord Marshal—known in older texts as the **High Marshal of the Crown**—is not a standing title.

It is invoked only in times of existential threat.

---

It is, in truth:

> **The King’s Shadow General**

---

### Authority (Previously Unspoken)

Until this day, the powers of the office were neither recorded nor acknowledged publicly.

They are now understood to include:

- Direct command of royal intelligence assets  
- Oversight of the Crown’s hidden companies and irregular forces  
- Quiet authority over the readiness of the [[Bannerhall]] and feudal levies  
- Coordination with court arcanists, including [[Sapphire Flame]] and the late [[Milo]]  
- Authority to mobilize the King’s March without convening the Lord’s Muster  
- The right, under seal, to countermand ducal authority in military matters  

---

### Practical Meaning

In plain terms:

The Lord Marshal may:
- Move armies without announcement  
- Act without debate  
- Wage war without spectacle  

---

## The Appointment of Beringer

By the King’s own word, **[[Beringer]]** has held this office in secret.

For months.

---

During that time:

- The realm faced internal conspiracy  
- Infernal influence spread within noble houses  
- Assassination plots reached the Crown itself  

---

And through it—

The machinery of war did not fail.

---

## The Dwarven Acknowledgment

In the moments following the proclamation, representatives of the dwarven enclaves stepped forward.

Without hesitation.

Without consultation.

---

They declared themselves:

> **Sworn to the Lord Marshal**

---

Not to the court.

Not to the dukes.

---

To him.

---

## Final Consideration

The revelation did not grant Beringer power.

It revealed it.

---

For months, the kingdom’s wars—

- Unseen  
- Unspoken  
- Unclaimed  

---

Were already being fought.

---

And the man fighting them…

Did not even know the full weight of the authority placed in his hands.
