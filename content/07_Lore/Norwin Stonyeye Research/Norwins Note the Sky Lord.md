# Norwin’s Notes — The Sky Lord

---

## Overview

The Dominion’s “flying castle” is not constructed, not replicated, and not reproducible.

It is a pre-cataclysmic **cloud giant citadel**, currently held and operated by:

**Thraximar of the High Aerie**

All evidence indicates:

- He is not controlled  
- He is not reliably bound  
- He is not deployable on command  

> The Dominion maintains a **retainer relationship** through tribute, formal address, and structured request.

---

- patrol routes  
- staging ground

>

- No action required  
- Location unknown  

---

### Tasking Phase

- Request issued  
- Tribute adjusted  
- Acceptance uncertain  

---

### Execution Phase

- Overwhelming strike  
- No occupation  
- Immediate withdrawal
