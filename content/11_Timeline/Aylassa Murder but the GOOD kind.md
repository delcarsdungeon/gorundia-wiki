You’re summoned quietly.

Not by a guard.

Not by a herald.

By Aylassa.

She doesn’t explain. Just:

“You’ll want to see this before the scrapers come.”

She leads you through a service corridor. Down stone steps. One torch. No fanfare.

Then she opens the door.

The room is… tidy.

Not peaceful. Not chaotic.

Just efficient.

Seven bodies.

One slumped over a table.
Two near the window.
One pinned to the wall with a dagger.
One with his throat opened so cleanly it almost looks surgical.

And one face-down in the corner.

There’s very little furniture damage.

Very little screaming happened here.

Aylassa strolls past a corpse and nudges it with her boot.

“They were saying some rather unkind things about Thranduil.”

Beat.

She glances at you sideways.

“Questioned his aim. Called him provincial.”

She kneels beside one body and flips it over.

“I told them I’d pass that along.”

She stands.

“Before I did, though… I thought you might want to check something.”

She gestures vaguely at the dead men.

“See if any of them were anyone important.”

The implication hangs in the air.

If these were noble sons?
Retainers?
Foreign envoys?

You’re looking at a diplomatic incident.

She watches your faces carefully.

Then she crouches near the table and picks something up.

A smooth silver disc.

No king’s face.

One triangular punch mark.

She flips it once in the air and catches it.

“Oh, relax. Not local nobility.”

She produces a small ivory chip from her sleeve — cold iron laced.

It has been scratched through.

She wiggles it between two fingers.

“Freelance. Foreign silver. Tried to buy capital muscle.”

She leans against the table, blood on her sleeve, utterly unbothered.

“Guild won’t like that.”

Beat.

Then she grins.

Actually grins.

“Which makes this my good deed for the week.”

She tosses the silver Mark to Guaco.

“You’re the reeve. Consider this… jurisdiction adjacent.”

Then to Thalrik:

“If you recognize any of the faces, do tell. I’d hate to have accidentally destabilized something important.”

She pauses.

Then laughs softly.

“Kidding. I checked.”

And now they realize:

She already knew.

She just wanted to watch them process it.
