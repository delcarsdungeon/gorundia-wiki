# 25March26 — Session Notes

## Summary

This is the session where the King stops playing defense.

In private, it becomes clear he’s crossed a line internally—he’s no longer weighing options or negotiating outcomes. He’s decided. What the party is seeing now isn’t escalation, it’s execution. Orders are direct, time is compressed, and even trusted allies like [[Thalrik Vaultforge]] are being told—clearly—that what’s coming is going to get ugly, fast, and not entirely controllable.

The city reflects that shift immediately. Paladins are mobilizing, gallows are going up, and the general mood has gone from “tense” to “we are already in it.” The Crown’s approach is brutally efficient: isolate, fragment, and force decisions at the individual level. Through [[Beringer]], the King offers enemies a way out—but it’s not mercy. It’s controlled collapse. Take the exit, or get broken.

At court, the reckoning continues. [[Cornelia Bordella]] owns her failure publicly and abdicates, framing the crisis as something bigger than one house. The King doesn’t soften—he redirects. Responsibility is reassigned, consequences are formalized, and the message is clear: silence is no longer survivable.

And layered over all of it—this isn’t just politics anymore. Between the Krozen, the druidic intervention, and the prophetic warnings, something bigger is watching. The crusade isn’t just a campaign. It’s a pivot point for the entire kingdom—and probably the start of something that doesn’t stop when this war does.
