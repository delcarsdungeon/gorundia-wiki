# **Thalrik’s Axe Coast Primer**

*(As told over a drink he didn’t pay for)*

---

## **“First thing—don’t trust the map.”**

The lines don’t mean anything.

* Ports change hands without notice
* Lords die and keep collecting taxes
* Half the “cities” are just gangs with docks

If someone says *“this territory belongs to—”*
That just means they haven’t been challenged lately.

---

## **“There’s three kinds of people on that coast.”**

1. **Merchants** — liars with paperwork
2. **Pirates** — liars without paperwork
3. **Dwarves** — the only honest bastards, and that’s worse

---

## **Ports (What Thalrik Actually Thinks of Them)**

### **Myldenär**

> “If you want to move something quietly, this is where you do it.”

* Best place to disappear legally
* Worst place to assume anyone’s on your side
* The Lantern Court smiles while counting how much they can sell you for 

**Thalrik history:**

* Has at least one *burned identity* here
* Probably still owes someone money

---

### **Veyra Saltspire**

> “You don’t smuggle through Saltspire. Saltspire smuggles through you.”

* Everything taxed, tracked, skimmed
* The Salt Compact owns the docks, the guards, and probably your cargo before you unload it 

**Thalrik take:**

* Good for moving bulk goods
* Bad for anything sensitive
* Suicide if the paperwork doesn’t line up

---

### **Karth Hollow**

> “If they let you in, you’re safe. If they don’t… you’re already dead.”

* Pirate-controlled
* Reef maze—needs a guide
* Brotherhood keeps order better than most “governments”

**Thalrik history:**

* Has a contact here he *doesn’t name*
* Trusts them more than most nobles

---

### **Draeven’s Reach**

> “Everything stolen ends up here eventually.”

* Salvage, contraband, Dominion leakage
* If you lost it at sea, it’s probably for sale here

**Thalrik note:**

* You go here when you’re desperate
* Or when you want something no one should have

---

### **Harrowdeep**

> “Don’t go below the waterline.”

* Flooded lower city
* People disappear down there

**Thalrik tone shift here:**

* Not joking
* Won’t elaborate

---

### **Lenor’s Watch (Galan Lenora)**

> “…No.”

* Won’t go
* Won’t talk about it
* Won’t joke about it

If pushed:

> “If you see black sails—
> you drop cargo, you cut lines, you run.
>
> Coin’s replaceable. You’re not.”

---

## **The Black Veil (What He Actually Thinks)**

Not pirates.

Not even people.

> “They don’t rob you.
> They *keep* you.”

* Other pirates avoid them
* Even Dominion doesn’t mess with them directly
* If they show up, *everyone* disengages

Thalrik doesn’t have a story about surviving them.

That tells you everything.

---

## **The Dwarves**

> “You don’t negotiate with dwarves. You survive the meeting.”

* Trade is precise, recorded, enforced
* Violence against them = extinction event

**What Thalrik respects:**

* They don’t lie
* They don’t bluff
* They don’t forget

**What he fears:**

* They don’t forgive either

He’s done business with:

* **Khazdrin** (ledger types)
* Possibly **Helgund** (quiet ones—he won’t confirm) 

---

## **Inland Lords (“Hill Bastards”)**

> “They’re pirates who got tired of the sea.”

* Ironvale → toll thieves with banners
* Red Banner → actual army, which is worse
* Thorn Pact → betray you before you finish the deal

Thalrik rule:

> Never move cargo inland unless you *have to*

---

## **The Elven Ruins & Old Stories**

This is where his tone shifts—less sarcasm, more… irritation.

> “Used to be different.”

* Ships moved on schedule
* Ports honored agreements
* Sea lanes were *enforced*

Now?

> “Turns out when the adults leave, the kids burn the house down.”

He doesn’t romanticize it—but he respects it.

---

## **Nytha Aethel**

> “Leave it alone.”

* Not fear, not quite
* More like… **professional caution**

He knows:

* You can’t reach it by ship
* Pirates avoid it
* Something up there still *has teeth*

---

## **Elathas**

> “I’ve heard three different stories. All of them bad.”

* Elven holdout
* Empty ruin
* Something else entirely

**Translation:**
No reliable intel → which means it’s dangerous

---

## **Draconis Island**

This is the one.

If it comes up:

* He goes quiet
* Looks at Zara if she’s there
* Then deflects hard

> “Places like that don’t stay on maps by accident.”

He knows *something*.
Not everything—but enough to know it’s not worth casual conversation.

---

## **His Actual Operating Rules**

This is the gold—this is how he *plays the region*:

* Never run a single route twice
* Always carry two manifests
* Never trust a calm harbor
* If a deal seems fair → you’re the product
* If dwarves are involved → follow the contract exactly
* If Black Veil is rumored nearby → **leave**

---

* He’s lost a crew here before
* He’s made good money here
* He’s betrayed someone who probably isn’t done with him
