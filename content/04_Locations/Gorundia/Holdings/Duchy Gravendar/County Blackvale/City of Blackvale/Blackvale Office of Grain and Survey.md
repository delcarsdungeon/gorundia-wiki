# Office of Grain and Survey (Blackvale)

![[Blackvale Skells Tower.png|400]]

## Overview

The **Office of Grain and Survey** serves as the administrative heart of Blackvale’s agricultural economy.

From this building, the flow of grain—into the city, through its markets, and outward to the wider kingdom—is recorded, assessed, and regulated.

It is not a grand structure.

It is not meant to be.

> It exists to function, not to impress.

Short, broad, and built of heavy stone, the building sits in a deliberately cleared space within the grain district, surrounded by wagons, warehouses, and the constant churn of labor. Lanterns burn late. Doors open early.

The work does not stop.

---

## Reputation

To officials, it is:

> **“The Office of Grain and Survey.”** or more officially: 
> **"The Office of Grain, Survey, and Provincial Accounting for the County of Blackvale"**

To merchants:

> **“The Counting House.”**

To laborers:

> **“The Tower.”**

To those who have had cause to worry about records:

> **“The Ledger.”**

Few speak of it lightly.

Fewer still ignore it.

---

## Function

The Office oversees:

- Grain intake and storage accounting  
- Weights, measures, and tariffs  
- Shipment manifests and routing  
- Survey records for agricultural lands  
- Emergency response coordination (fires, spoilage, sabotage)

It also serves as a central point of communication between:

- the city watch  
- the garrison  
- merchant houses  
- and the castle  

In times of unrest, its role expands.

Quietly.

---

## Structure & Security

The building maintains its own layered staffing:

- External guard post staffed by **Bordella men-at-arms**
- Internal security overseen by sworn professionals of [[House Moffet]]
- Restricted access floors for records and administrative oversight

Visitors are received—but rarely wander.

You are either expected…

> …or you are noticed.

---

## Senior Staff

### [[Arch-Provost Halvaren Skell]]

Head of the Office and chief administrator of Blackvale’s grain and survey operations.

A man of discipline and precision, Skell is known for:

- methodical decision-making  
- tireless work habits  
- an unsettling ability to recall details  

He is respected by professionals.

Feared by those who prefer things… overlooked.

---

### Senior Wizard — **Magister Albert Vera**

A veteran arcane practitioner tasked with maintaining the city’s agricultural wards and survey enchantments.

Known for:

- conservative methodology  
- strict adherence to established practice  
- little patience for improvisation  

If something works, Varn sees no reason to change it.

---

### Senior Wizard — **Mistress Elidra Veznic**

Younger, sharper, and far more adaptive than her counterpart.

Responsible for:

- field adjustments  
- coordination with survey teams  
- irregular problem-solving  

She has a reputation for noticing patterns others miss.

And asking questions some would rather avoid.

---

### Head Clerk — **Master Pellin Harrow**

The man who ensures the entire operation does not collapse under its own weight.

Harrow oversees:

- record integrity  
- staff coordination  
- intake and distribution logs  

If the Office is a machine, he is the one who keeps it running.

Quietly.

Relentlessly.

---

## Supporting Staff

The Office employs a substantial workforce, including:

- **Junior clerks and scribes** — maintaining records and ledgers  
- **Survey aides** — coordinating field reports and land assessments  
- **Arcane assistants** — supporting the work of the senior wizards  
- **Logistics analysts** — tracking shipments, supply fluctuations, and anomalies  
- **Messengers and runners** — ensuring rapid communication across districts  

Most are not remarkable individually.

Together…

> they are extremely effective.

---

## Military Presence

### Sir [[Garran Bronnel]] (House Moffet)

Head of internal physical security.

A sworn man of [[House Moffet]], Bronnel commands a cadre of disciplined soldiers often referred to as **“Bootmen.”**

They are known for:

- professionalism  
- loyalty  
- a refusal to engage in courtly games  

Bronnel himself is:

- not a large man  
- not a loud man  

But widely regarded as someone:

> you do not test twice

---

## Current State

Following recent political upheaval in Blackvale:

- authority from the castle has grown… distant  
- public communication has diminished  
- internal operations have tightened  

Despite this, the Office continues to function:

- shipments move  
- records are kept  
- order is maintained  

Whatever uncertainty exists elsewhere in the city…

> it has not reached the ledgers

---

## Notes

- The Office is open to official business during daylight hours  
- Access beyond public areas requires authorization  
- Records are considered authoritative in disputes involving grain, land, and trade  

Disputing them is possible.

Winning such disputes is… less common.
