# **Lockreach**

_River Toll Fortress of Blackvale_

> “You can pass. We’ll just make sure it costs you.”

---

## **Overview**

Lockreach sits astride the primary grain artery of [[County Blackvale]], where the river narrows and deepens just enough to make evasion impractical and delay inevitable. It is not the largest stronghold in the county, nor the most heavily garrisoned.

It is, however, the one place where **everything must slow down**.

---

## **The Fortress**  
  
Lockreach is not built to stop an army.  
  
It is built to make movement **slow, visible, and accountable**.  
  
---  
  
### **The River Works**  
  
The river is narrowed and shaped through deliberate construction.  
  
Two squat **chain towers** stand on stone spars driven out from each bank, forcing traffic into a controlled central channel. Between them hangs a heavy iron chain, raised or lowered from the **north bank winchhouse**, which holds the only true control mechanism.  
  
- The chain is rarely fully raised  
- Instead, it is kept at a height that forces vessels to slow, align, and submit to inspection  
  
No barge passes at speed. None pass unseen.  
  
---  
  
### **The Channel**  
  
The space between the towers is not open water.  
  
- Stone pilings and submerged work narrow the flow  
- The current is subtly altered upstream  
- Barges must enter in sequence, one at a time  
  
This creates a natural queue during busy seasons, with vessels stacking upriver under the watch of Lockreach.  
  
---  
  
### **The Docks**  
  
Both banks are worked.  
  
**North Bank (Keep Side):**  
- Primary inspection docks  
- Direct access to the counting house  
- Warehousing and secured offloading  
  
**South Bank:**  
- Overflow docks during harvest  
- Holding areas for delayed or disputed cargo  
- Secondary inspections and labor handling  
  
Traffic can be separated, stalled, or redirected without ever fully stopping the river.  
  
---  
  
### **The Keep (North Bank)**  
  
The keep sits back from the river on slightly elevated ground, overlooking the entire system.  
  
It contains:  
- The **Counting House** (true heart of Lockreach)  
- The **chain winch and control works**  
- Ledger vaults and contract chambers  
- Barracks and signal yard  
- The **Maester’s tower**  
  
The towers on the river enforce.  
  
The keep decides.  
  
---  
  
### **The Manor**  
  
Within sight of the keep, set on drier and more pleasant ground, stands a smaller but refined **manor house**.  
  
This is where Lord Cassian is most often found.  
  
- Guests are received here, not in the keep  
- Meals are taken here  
- Private business is conducted here  
  
The keep is for work.  
  
The manor is for control.

---

## **Lord of Lockreach**

### **Lord Cassian Virello-Bordella**

_Warden of the Reach, Keeper of the Chain_

**Status:** Lord of Lockreach (granted)  
**Standing:** Minor noble by title, major factor by influence

---

Cassian Virello-Bordella holds Lockreach not by inheritance, but by **grant and continuance**.

Born to House Virello, a river-trade family of modest standing, Cassian rose not through bloodline but through **demonstrated command of flow—of goods, coin, and information**. In a period of consolidation within Blackvale, House Bordella elevated him, binding his fortunes to theirs and placing him over the river works at Lockreach.

The hyphen in his name marks the nature of that elevation.

It is not lineage.

It is **license**.

---

### **Demeanor**

Cassian governs without spectacle.

He speaks softly, listens closely, and commits nothing to word that cannot be enforced in practice. His authority is expressed through **delay, correction, and inevitability**.

> “We will review the matter.”

In Lockreach, this is understood as a verdict.

---

### **Reputation**

**Among merchants:**  
Reliable, precise, and expensive.

**Among the old nobility:**  
A necessary impropriety.

**Among officials:**  
Efficient. Predictable. Difficult to dislodge.

---

### **Position**

Cassian’s authority rests on three pillars:

- **The Chain** — control of river passage
- **The Ledger** — authority to weigh, assess, and record
- **The Word** — the ability to speak ahead of events and be believed

He holds little land.

He controls movement.

---

### **Standing in the Present**

With the fall of House Bordella’s main line, the foundation of Cassian’s title has shifted.

The name **Virello-Bordella** was granted.

What was granted may be withdrawn.

---
## **Reeve-Major Halric Venn**  
*Master of Accounts, Acting Warden of Lockreach*

Halric Venn is not of noble blood, nor does he pretend otherwise.

He is a man of figures, raised from the counting houses of Blackvale and set over Lockreach not by lineage, but by necessity. Where others see cargo, Venn sees entries. Where others see delay, he sees correction.

In the frequent absence of Lord Cassian, it is Venn who governs the day-to-day operation of Lockreach:

- Dock assignments  
- Inspection orders  
- Ledger approvals  
- The quiet determination of who waits, and who proceeds  

His authority is not ceremonial. It is accepted because it functions.

The garrison defers to him in matters of enforcement. The clerks answer to him without question. Even the Maester, whose art lies beyond tally and ink, works in concert with Venn’s system rather than apart from it.

Venn does not raise his voice. He does not argue.

He states what will occur, and ensures that it does.

Among merchants, he is regarded as inevitable. Among lesser officials, as immovable. Among those who mistake his station for weakness, as a lesson learned only once.

It is sometimes said that Lord Cassian owns Lockreach.

Those who work there understand a quieter truth:

> Cassian holds the reach.  
> Venn keeps it moving.
## **House Mage**

### **Maester Olessian Kade**

_Sworn to Lockreach_

---

Maester Kade is a wizard of the **Third Veil**, recognized among the Maesters of Gorundia as reliable, controlled, and professionally indispensable.

His work is not spectacle.

It is **continuity**.

---

### **Function**

- Carries messages without delay or rider
- Flags irregular movement ahead of arrival
- Tests deception quietly and without accusation
- Maintains coordination between Lockreach, [[Castle Blackvale]], and [[Drovers’ Stand]]

Through him, Lockreach does not merely observe movement.

It anticipates it.

---

### **Disposition**

Reserved, exacting, and sparing in speech.

> “If it required notice, it has already been noticed.”

---

## **Operational Reality**

Lockreach is not a fortress in the traditional sense.

It is a **mechanism**.

Those who pass through without issue often believe themselves fortunate.

They are not.

They have simply not yet reached the place where the account is settled.
