# Thunderhold Storm Abbeys

The fortress-monasteries of [Thunderhold] are small, self-sufficient training redoubts dedicated to [[Heironeous]]. Built across the storm-lashed plains, they serve as crucibles of discipline where knights are hardened under open sky and divine scrutiny.

Thunderhold doctrine does not seek shelter from storms—it endures them.

These abbeys are intentionally small, maintaining strict population limits to preserve hardship, focus, and spiritual clarity.

---

## Church Hierarchy (Thunderhold Context)

- **Hierophant Imperator of Heironeous** — Supreme spiritual authority of the faith
- **Patriarch-General** — Commander of the militant orders and temple-fortresses
- **Abbott-Commanders** — Leaders of individual storm abbeys (always paladins)
- **Knight-Brothers** — Senior paladins and instructors
- **Clerics** — Support, healing, doctrine, and rites

# Major Storm Abbeys of Thunderhold

---

## Abbey of the Riven Sky

**Headcount:** ~55

- 10 Paladins (including leadership)  
- 18 Trainees  
- 17 Lay Brothers  
- 10 Men-at-Arms  

**Overview:**  
The oldest and most exposed abbey, built deliberately in open terrain to invite the full force of Thunderhold’s storms. Its upper tower is blackened from repeated lightning strikes.

**Leadership:**
- **Abbott-Commander Halvric Storm-Vowed** — Stern, uncompromising veteran; believes fear must be burned out, not soothed  
- **Knight-Brother Edran Ville** — Master of endurance drills; known for training in full armor during active storms  

---

## Saint Halvric’s Redoubt

**Headcount:** ~70

- 12 Paladins  
- 20 Trainees  
- 25 Lay Brothers  
- 13 Men-at-Arms  

**Overview:**  
The most practical and well-supplied abbey. Acts as a regional refuge during major storms, with large cisterns and granaries.

**Leadership:**
- **Abbott-Commander Ser Malyc Pike** — Logistics-minded paladin; values survival and readiness over mysticism  
- **Cleric-Marshal Vessa Thorne** — Oversees medical and civilian intake; respected for calm under pressure  

---

## Cloister of the Iron Gale

**Headcount:** ~60

- 11 Paladins  
- 22 Trainees  
- 16 Lay Brothers  
- 11 Men-at-Arms  

**Overview:**  
A severe, martial cloister specializing in fighting under adverse conditions. Silence is observed during storms—thunder is considered sermon enough.

**Leadership:**
- **Abbott-Commander Gorram Stonehand** — Taciturn, iron-willed; trains infantry to operate in mud, rain, and chaos  
- **Knight-Brother Laden Korr** — Drillmaster; feared and respected in equal measure  

---

## Bastion of Saint Veleran Storm-Witness

**Headcount:** ~50

- 9 Paladins  
- 16 Trainees  
- 15 Lay Brothers  
- 10 Men-at-Arms  

**Overview:**  
Built around a sacred site where Saint Veleran is said to have endured a three-day tempest. More mystical than the others, attracting pilgrims and zealots.

**Leadership:**
- **Abbott-Commander Velric Dawnsteel** — Charismatic and intense; encourages visions and personal trials  
- **Cleric Sevrin Hale** — Keeper of relics and recorded storm-omens  

---

## Cultural Notes

- Storms are considered **acts of divine witness**, not misfortune  
- Promotions and vows are often conducted during active storms  
- Each abbey deliberately limits its size to prevent comfort and complacency  
- Knights trained here are known for exceptional discipline under extreme conditions  

> “A clear sky teaches nothing.”
