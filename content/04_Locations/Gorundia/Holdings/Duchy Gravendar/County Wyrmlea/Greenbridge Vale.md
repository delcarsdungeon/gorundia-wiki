# Greenbridge Vale

A modest rural holding within the domain of [[Zara Vallytrex]], granted to [[Bertram Moffet]] at the request of the Lord Marshal ([[Beringer]]). The vale centers on **Ashmere Hall**, a small fortified manor overlooking a narrow timber bridge crossing a slow, reed-choked stream—the feature from which the holding takes its name.

Greenbridge Vale comprises several hamlets, low-yield farmland, and a strategically modest but economically relevant crossing point. Though legally sound, the holding has suffered from neglect, irregular stewardship, and quietly disputed revenues.

The grant serves both as elevation and removal—placing [[Cornelia Bordella]] beyond the immediate reach of capital politics while establishing a new locus of authority under Bertram’s charge.

---

## Ashmere Hall

The seat of the holding: a two-story stone manor with an attached tower and low defensive wall. Functional rather than grand, it serves as administrative center, residence, and symbol of local authority.

---

## Notable Features

- **Greenbridge** — A narrow timber bridge controlling local trade flow; toll rights exist but have been inconsistently enforced  
- **The Mere** — Shallow wetland providing reeds, fish, and drainage headaches  
- **Outlying Hamlets** — Small population under direct obligation to the hall  
- **Light Woodland** — Fuel, hunting, and occasional trouble  

---

## Political Context

- Granted by [[Zara Vallytrex]] at the urging of [[Beringer]]  
- Places [[Bertram Moffet]] in a position of landed responsibility  
- Removes [[Cornelia Bordella]] from the capital without formal censure  
- Establishes a minor but loyal foothold within Grevandar’s internal structure

## GM Notes
> [!danger] Entanglement Vector
> This holding is not a reward—it is a pressure valve.  
> It simultaneously stabilizes Cornelia, tests Bertram, and extends Zara’s influence.

> [!note] Tone
> The vale should feel *fixable but not solved*.  
> Problems are mundane, human, and persistent—not epic, but meaningful.

> [!warning] Immediate Hook
> The bridge toll is being collected—just not by the hall.  
> Someone is skimming authority, and the locals know it.
