### Serpent Gate — 093.041

The Serpent Gate stands at hex **093.041**, controlling the road from Vogel lands into Barony Darkstone. It was built by Baron Remy DeSalle as a ceremonial border gateway for tourists, noble envoys, capital inspectors, and visitors from Grevandar. Its original function was display, not defense.

Constructed of red stone with gilded serpent reliefs, polished archways, decorative balconies, and ceremonial reception chambers, the gate was meant to announce DeSalle grandeur before travelers reached New Serpentis. It is one of Baron Remy’s most expensive vanity works.

The current border crisis has transformed it into an improvised military checkpoint. Sandbags, wagon barricades, timber platforms, canvas screens, and hurried fieldworks now crowd the ornamental structure. The original guardhouse contains more reception space than barracks, and many of the defensive features are decorative or badly placed.

Despite this, the Serpent Gate controls the road. It is useful for stopping wagons, filtering refugees, catching spies, and slowing cavalry, though it would fare poorly against a determined assault or artillery.

Common nicknames include **Remy’s Welcome Mat**, **The Gilded Throat**, and **Snake’s Smile**.
