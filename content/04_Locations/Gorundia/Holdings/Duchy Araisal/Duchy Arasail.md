# Araisal Ducal Demesne — Road & Settlement Draft

## Existing Settlements to Keep

### Valearyn Gate

**Hex:** around 095.055  
**Type:** fortified border town / internal gate-town / customs town  
This becomes the western-facing gate of the ducal demesne: originally a border fort facing the neighboring internal holdings, now grown into a serious river-road town. Since this is an internal duchy border, its walls are old and ceremonial in places, but the customs houses are very real.

It should sprawl **eastward and northeastward**, away from the original fort line. The old keep/gatehouse faces west; the newer market, mills, and river docks grow toward **Caeliryn Ford**.

### Caeliryn Ford

**Hex:** 096.056  
**Type:** ford village / bridge-market / practical crossing  
This is the working crossing. Less fancy than Valearyn Gate. Drovers, carts, remounts, ferrymen, river pilots, muddy boots, cheap ale, and one inn that absolutely has a sign shaped like a trout with bad opinions.

### Thalanor Spire

**Hex:** 095.051  
**Type:** mage tower / observatory / scholar settlement  
Keep this as a major landmark, but I would not make it the whole mage-guild city. It is the old magical spine of the region: ancient tower, observatory, ward-stone, maybe an elven-built thing repurposed by the Allarics.

### Elf Watch

**Hex:** 093.051  
**Type:** border watch / forest-road fort  
Good as-is. It guards the northwest approach, forest road, and old internal boundary. More watchtower than city.

### Lethirae Watch

**Hex:** 094.061  
**Type:** southern watchfort / road guard  
This guards the southern road out of the demesne, especially traffic moving toward Orenic/Vogel-adjacent trouble. Keep it as a practical fort, not a town.

# New Major Placements

## Araisal Court

**Suggested hex:** 098.053 or 098.054  
**Type:** ducal capital / court town  
Place the capital **east of Valearyn Gate, north of the river bend, and west of the heavier eastern woods**. This keeps it central within the four-hex demesne without making it a river-port.

My pick: **098.053**.

Why there:

- Central enough to feel like the seat.
    
- Close to the river, but not dependent on it.
    
- Between the westward border-town and the eastern forest/mage belt.
    
- Easy to road-link to Thalanor Spire, Valearyn Gate, and the Green Veil.
    

Araisal Court should be a refined old capital: keep, court-town, archives, dueling lawns, old temples, elf-blood noble estates, and enough procedure to kill a horse.

## Letharion

**Suggested hex:** 099.051 or 100.052  
**Type:** mage-guild town / university town  
Place this **east/northeast of Araisal Court**, near the approach to the deeper woods and close enough to Thalanor Spire to form an arcane corridor.

My pick: **099.051** if you want it closer to the capital.  
Alternate: **100.052** if you want it more forest-adjacent.

This is the actual living mage town: guild halls, lecture courts, copyists, alchemical gardens, lens-grinders, spell component markets, student boarding houses, and the inevitable tavern called something like **The Miscast Goose**.

## The Green Veil

**Suggested forest region:** 101.048 through 104.056, especially 102.049–104.053  
**Type:** major diaspora wood-elf forest  
Use the visible heavy woods in the northeast/east as the major forest. This should not be one city marker unless you want a hidden symbol.

Player-facing regional name: **The Green Veil**.  
Elven/formal name: **Lethenvael** or **Caer Lethen Wood**.

This is where many diaspora wood elves live under Allaric protection. Not packed urban settlement. Hidden halls, tree-villages, patrol groves, old stones, memory shrines, and half-elf woodland hamlets around the edges.

## Greenbough Hall / Caer Lethen

**Suggested hex:** 103.052 or 103.053  
**Type:** hidden wood-elf enclave  
Put the main wood-elf settlement inside the woods, not on the main road.

My pick: **103.052**.

It should be connected by a **forest track**, not a full road. Outsiders get guided in. If they try to “just follow the path,” the forest politely tells them to get bent.

## Moonshade Crossing

**Suggested hex:** 097.052 or 098.052  
**Type:** scholar-market / book town  
This is the support town between Araisal Court and Letharion/Thalanor. Scribes, bookbinders, ink makers, mapmakers, small academies, tutors, and minor enchantment shops.

My pick: **098.052**.

Good place for the Allaric Archives annex or the “public” part of the archives, while the serious old stuff remains at Araisal Court.

## Ivyford

**Suggested hex:** 097.056 or 098.056  
**Type:** rural market town / manor-town  
This sits southeast/east of Valearyn Gate and serves the farms, orchards, sheep pastures, and mills along the river road.

My pick: **098.056**.

This is a sane, grounded settlement: wool, apples, barley, cheese, river mills. The kind of place where the mayor has one good coat and deeply resents students.

## Starlingmere

**Suggested hex:** 100.055 or 101.055  
**Type:** quiet water-meadow town / retreat settlement  
This can sit along the river after it bends east. A pretty, slightly decadent Allaric retreat town with water gardens, reeds, fishing, dyes, and noble villas.

My pick: **100.055**.

Good place for retired courtiers, elven musicians, and one murder mystery per decade.

# Road Network Draft

## 1. The Old Western Road

**Route:** Stonewatch → Valearyn Gate → Caeliryn Ford → Araisal Court  
This is the main western approach.

On the map:

- Existing road from **Stonewatch** into **Valearyn Gate**
    
- Continue east/southeast to **Caeliryn Ford**
    
- From Caeliryn Ford, run a proper ducal road northeast/east to **Araisal Court** at 098.053/054
    

This road should be paved or at least well-maintained. Old milestones. Allaric crests. Way shrines. Suspiciously polite tollmen.

## 2. The Spire Road

**Route:** Valearyn Gate → Thalanor Spire → Letharion → Araisal Court  
This should be a slightly older, narrower road, maybe the original arcane road.

On the map:

- Existing black road already goes north from Valearyn toward **Thalanor Spire**
    
- Continue from **Thalanor Spire** east/southeast to **Letharion**
    
- Then south/southwest or southeast to **Araisal Court**
    

This creates a triangle:  
**Valearyn Gate / Thalanor Spire / Araisal Court**

That triangle is the old civilized heart of the demesne.

## 3. The River Road

**Route:** Valearyn Gate → Caeliryn Ford → Ivyford → Starlingmere → eastern forest edge  
This road follows the river east.

On the map:

- Run from **Valearyn Gate**
    
- Through **Caeliryn Ford**
    
- Along the southern or northern riverbank to **Ivyford**
    
- Then to **Starlingmere**
    
- Continue as a lighter road toward the forest edge, maybe ending near **Lethenford** or a small ranger station
    

This is the practical economic route: grain, wool, timber, fish, dyes, tolls.

## 4. The Greenway

**Route:** Araisal Court → Letharion → Greenbough Hall / Caer Lethen  
This should not be a normal road all the way.

Use:

- Proper road from **Araisal Court** to **Letharion**
    
- Light road/track from **Letharion** to the forest edge
    
- Hidden forest path into **Greenbough Hall**
    

In Worldographer terms: road to the edge, then trail/track inside woods.

## 5. The Southern Watch Road

**Route:** Valearyn Gate / Caeliryn Ford → Lethirae Watch → Blackstone / southern border  
Existing road already heads south from Caeliryn/Valearyn toward **Lethirae Watch** and **Blackstone**.

Keep it. This is your military/service road to the southern marches.

Add one small named stop between Caeliryn and Lethirae:

### Willowmere Station

**Suggested hex:** 095.059  
**Type:** patrol post / coaching inn / toll station  
Small but useful. Remounts, courier shelter, road wardens.

## 6. The Eastern March Track

**Route:** Starlingmere → Green Veil edge → eastern border / Orenic-facing road  
This is not a major road. It is a guarded woodland-margin road.

Add:

### Hartbough Post

**Suggested hex:** 104.054 or 104.055  
**Type:** forest-edge watchpost  
This watches the eastern woods and border approaches. Mixed human/half-elf/wood-elf wardens.

# My preferred layout in one sentence

Put **Araisal Court at 098.053**, **Letharion at 099.051**, **Moonshade Crossing at 098.052**, **Ivyford at 098.056**, **Starlingmere at 100.055**, **Greenbough Hall at 103.052**, and make the roads form a triangle between **Valearyn Gate, Thalanor Spire, and Araisal Court**, with a river road extending east and a hidden forest track into the Green Veil.

That gives you a demesne that reads cleanly on the map: **gate-town west, spire north, court center, river towns east, elf forest beyond.**
