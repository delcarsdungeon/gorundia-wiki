# The Black Veil

> “They don’t rob you. They keep you.”

---
![[Black Veil Pirate.png|320]]
## ![[Black Veil Crewman.png|320]]

# Overview

The Black Veil is a feared maritime horror operating along the [[Axe Coast]]. Neither a traditional pirate fleet nor a simple cult, the Veil blends **necromancy, piracy, and religious fanaticism** into something far worse than either alone.

They are widely considered:
- Beyond negotiation  
- Beyond reason  
- Beyond the normal rules of the sea  

Even other pirates avoid them.

---

## Nature of the Organization

The Veil is composed of:
- Necromancers  
- Death cultists devoted to [[Nerull]]  
- Reanimated crew and constructs  

Their ships:
- Sail under black, corpse-marked banners  
- Are crewed partially (or entirely) by undead  
- Often appear without warning, especially in storms or low visibility  

They do not ransom.

They harvest.

---

## Base of Operations

### [[Galan Lenora]] (Lenor’s Watch)

Once a major elven port city, Galan Lenora is now:
- A ruin  
- A necromantic harbor  
- The primary anchorage of the Black Veil  

The city’s fall marked a turning point in the region:
> Trade died there. Something else took its place.

---

## Tactics & Behavior

The Black Veil operates differently from all other maritime forces:

- Strikes at night or during storms  
- Leaves no survivors when possible  
- Reuses the dead as crew  
- Targets both merchant and pirate vessels indiscriminately  

When Black Veil sails are sighted:
> All other activity ceases. Ships flee. No one contests them.

---

## Reputation

Among the Axe Coast:

- Merchants fear them  
- Pirates hate them  
- The [[Dominion]] avoids direct engagement  
- Even hardened smugglers will abandon cargo rather than face them  

They are not part of the political ecosystem.

They are a **disruptive force within it**.

---

## Known Leadership

### Captain Drazar Vyneth
- Leader of the Black Veil  
- Powerful necromancer  
- Known for skeletal augmentations and masked visage  

### Other Figures
- Solris Morgayne (former priestess of Nerull)  
- “Grin” Harvick (heavily augmented enforcer)  
- Ylvena Kaelthorn (arcane quartermaster)  
- Rynar “Rotwind” Vael (helmsman tied to supernatural navigation)  

---

## Strategic Impact

The presence of the Black Veil has reshaped the Axe Coast:

- Trade routes avoid entire regions  
- Pirate alliances collapse when they appear  
- Merchant leagues quietly redirect traffic  
- Coastal settlements live in constant fear  

They represent:
> A breakdown of normal maritime order into something predatory and inhuman.

---

## Notes

- Their long-term goal is unclear, but appears tied to:
  - Necromantic power accumulation  
  - Artifacts of death  
  - Expansion of influence through terror  

- Rumors persist of:
  - Storm-summoning relics  
  - Shadow-plane travel  
  - Soul-binding practices at scale  

---

> [!warning] ⚔ GM ONLY
> The Black Veil should be treated as:
> - A **regional apex threat**  
> - A **tone-setter** for the Axe Coast  
> - Not a routine combat encounter  
> Use sparingly.
> When they appear, it should change player behavior immediately.
