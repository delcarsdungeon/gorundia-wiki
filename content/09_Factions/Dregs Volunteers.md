# **Dregs Auxiliary Patrol**

**Type:** Semi-formal volunteer force  
**Location:** The Dregs, Bard’s Gate  
**Affiliation:** Informally under **Wonderful**  
**Common Name(s):** “Wonderful’s Boys,” “The Volunteers,” “The Tusk Line”

---

## **Overview**

The **Dregs Auxiliary Patrol** is a loosely organized force of mostly half-orc volunteers operating in the Dregs under the direction of **Wonderful**.

Formed in the wake of increased unrest and the Watch’s habitual absence, the Auxiliary exists to:

- Keep violence contained    
- Discourage opportunistic crime    
- Maintain a visible presence in areas the Watch avoids    

They are not officially sanctioned, but neither are they actively opposed—yet.

Their existence is tolerated because they are effective, restrained, and—so far—**controlled**.

---

## **Composition**

- **20+ members**, almost entirely half-orc    
- Predominantly former laborers, pit fighters, dock muscle, or failed mercenaries    
- Humans attempting to join have been **firmly discouraged**    

They are:

- Not elite    
- Not disciplined soldiers    
- Not criminals (anymore, mostly)    

They are **capable men given structure**.

---

## **Equipment & Appearance**

- Issued **undyed gambeson** (uniform by default, not by pride)    
- **Heavy truncheons** (standard)    
- Rough belts, work boots (sometimes absent), practical gear    
- Approximately **one in three carries a lantern**    

**Explicit restriction:**

- No blades while on duty    

This is enforced strictly.

---

## **Operational Doctrine**

The Auxiliary functions under a simple, widely understood code:

- Do not start fights    
- End fights quickly    
- Do not escalate to lethal force    
- Do not draw steel    
- Do not embarrass Wonderful    

Violence is permitted.

**Loss of control is not.**

---

## **Authority Structure**

There are no formal ranks.

Instead, the group operates through **natural dominance and recognition**, with a loose internal hierarchy:

### **The Core (Unspoken Leadership Layer)**

A handful of individuals naturally direct movement and action:

- The ones others **look to before acting**    
- The ones who **step in first when things turn**    
- The ones who can **shut a situation down without shouting**    

They are not officers.

They are:

> The men who keep the others from becoming a mob.

This group shifts over time, but currently stabilizes around one figure.

---

## **Morg (Field Anchor)**

Morg is not formally in charge.

Everyone knows he is.

A former mercenary captain in the service of Baron Lynwenden Cron, Morg is:

- Professionally trained    
- Disciplined under pressure    
- Equipped in **knight-grade armor** that stands out sharply in the Dregs    
- Physically imposing without relying on size alone    

He was defeated decisively by Baron Orin Kharne, an event that ended his prior command.

Since then, he has remained in Bard’s Gate—nominally unattached, but consistently present near Wonderful.

### **Role Within the Auxiliary**

- Acts as a **de facto field leader**    
- Stabilizes engagements before they spiral    
- Sets tone through action, not command    
- Enforces restraint without needing to announce it    

The Auxiliary responds to him because:

- He is clearly the most trained    
- He does not posture    
- He follows Wonderful’s rules    

### **Complication**

Morg’s loyalty is **personal**, not structural.

He remains because of Wonderful.

She has not given him formal authority.

This is intentional.

---

## **Relationship with Wonderful**

Wonderful does not command the Auxiliary like a captain.

She **owns the standard they operate under**.

- She provides work    
- She provides structure    
- She enforces consequences    

When discipline fails, she responds **immediately and publicly**.

Her authority is not debated.

It is understood.

---

## **Public Perception**

### **The Dregs**

- Viewed as necessary    
- Increasingly respected    
- Sometimes feared    

> “They work for her.”

That is usually enough.

---

### **The Watch**

- Relieved to have less work    
- Quietly resentful of the implication    
- Watching for overreach    

---

### **Merchants & Locals**

- Appreciate the stability    
- Wary of dependence    
- Concerned about what happens if control slips    

---

### **Nobility**

- Dismissive at first glance    
- Increasingly attentive    
- Concerned about precedent    

---

## **Current Status**

The Dregs Auxiliary Patrol is stable.

That stability depends entirely on:

- Wonderful’s continued control    
- Morg’s informal leadership    
- The Auxiliary remaining within its limits    

If any of these fail, the group will rapidly transition from:

> **Useful force**

to

> **Problem requiring correction**

---

## **Notable Traits**

- Violence is **expected**, but contained    
- Loyalty is **earned daily**, not sworn    
- Identity is tied to **Wonderful, not the city**    
- Structure exists—but only just    

---

## **Whispers & Observations**

- “Or one good leader away from being something worse.”    
- “She’s keeping them pointed outward. For now.”
