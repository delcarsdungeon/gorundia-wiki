# The Zorin Host

## Zorin

![[Halfblood Khan.png|340]]

## Overview

Zorin is an Ataman of the western frontier, known simply by his name among those who matter.

He is loud, approachable, and quick to laugh—equally at home around a campfire or in the saddle. He drinks freely, speaks plainly, and treats strangers as companions not yet proven otherwise.

This has led many to mistake him for a common rider.

It has also led to several regrettable misunderstandings.

Zorin commands the **Zorin Host**, a loose but formidable coalition of frontier riders bound not by law or blood, but by respect.

---

## The Zorin Host

The Zorin Host is not a standing army in the traditional sense. It is a **gathering of riders, warbands, and frontier families** who answer Zorin’s call.

- Estimated strength: **400–700 riders** at immediate muster  
- Extended strength (with sworn bands): **1,200–1,800 fighters**  
- Composition:
  - Light and medium cavalry
  - Veteran raiders
  - Scouts and irregular fighters
  - Camp followers and extended families

They are mobile, loosely organized, and highly effective in broken terrain and frontier warfare.

Outsiders often underestimate them due to their lack of rigid structure.

Outsiders are often wrong.

---

## Structure

The Host operates on a **personal loyalty model**:

- Zorin is Ataman by **consensus and reputation**
- Sub-commanders maintain autonomy
- Loyalty is earned, not enforced
- Disputes are settled directly—and quickly

When Zorin calls, they come.

## Key Figures

![[Zorin Host Officers.png|340]]
### Marek Vostin

Zorin’s second.

- Well-spoken, composed, and observant
- A half-orc who presents himself with deliberate refinement
- Wears traditional frontier finery with care and precision
- Known for his calm demeanor and sharp judgment

Marek watches more than he speaks.

He misses very little.

> “If Zorin laughs, I listen.
>
> If he goes quiet, I prepare.”

---

### Gorun “the Ox”

A massive ogre-blooded half-orc and one of Zorin’s most physically imposing fighters.

- Towering, heavily muscled build
- Known for directness and absolute confidence in his strength
- Rarely speaks unnecessarily
- Commands respect through presence alone

He does not posture.

He simply stands where he wishes to stand.

---

### Borcha Gorunovich



Gorun’s son.

- Even larger than his father
- Broad, powerful, and still growing into his strength
- Keeps to the background, often cloaked or partially obscured
- Notably young-faced despite his size

Those who dismiss him as slow are making a mistake.

He is learning.

---

## Sub-Bands & Sworn Riders

Several independent groups are known to ride under Zorin’s banner when called:

### The Red Sashes

- Fast cavalry skirmishers
- Known for bold raids and aggressive tactics
- Favor bright sashes as identifiers

### The Blackreed Riders

- Riverland scouts and ambushers
- Experts in marsh and forest terrain
- Rarely seen until it is too late

### The Three Brothers Company

- Veteran fighters bound by oath rather than blood
- Known for discipline uncommon among the Host
- Often serve as Zorin’s hard center in battle

### The Widow’s Hand

- Small but feared group of riders
- Reputation for vengeance raids and night strikes
- Little is confirmed; much is rumor

---

## Reputation

Among the frontier:

- Zorin is seen as a stabilizing force
- His Host as a **necessary power**

Among nobles:

- Viewed with suspicion
- Considered unpredictable
- Often underestimated
