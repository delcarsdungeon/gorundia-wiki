# Bargrum Deep-Anvil & the Anvilstorm Brigade

*"Hold. Then Break."*

![[Bargrum.png|320]]
![[Bargrums Anvilstorm Brigade.png|320]]
---
> [!quote] “Some commanders win battles.  This one ends armies.” - Sorek Redmarch
## Overview

Bargrum Deep-Anvil commands the **Anvilstorm Brigade**, a dwarven war-host built not for speed, nor spectacle—but for **controlled destruction**.

This is not an army that adapts mid-battle.

It is an army that **decides the battle before it begins**, then executes it with grinding certainty.

Where others fight battles, Bargrum builds them like siegeworks—layer by layer, pressure by pressure—until collapse is inevitable.

---

## Bargrum Deep-Anvil

### Appearance & Presence

A thick, barrel-chested dwarf with a neck like a pillar and forearms marked by forge burns and fresh scars.

- Beard braided into **three heavy plaits**, bound in rune-etched steel rings (endurance, storm, iron)  
- Helm: Broad greathelm with a narrow vertical slit  
- Armor: Dull gunmetal plate and mail, built to shed sparks and shrapnel  
- Weapon: **Groundbreaker**, a massive double-headed warhammer  
  - Darkheart oak haft  
  - Storm-silver head  

When Bargrum drives Groundbreaker into the earth, the field listens.

---

### Personality

- **Taciturn:** Speaks in short, exact statements  
- **Analytical:** Treats battle as engineering  
- **Loyal:** Keeps a ledger of the fallen, iron-bound and ever-present  
- **Unsparing:** Lives are spent deliberately, never wastefully  

> Under Bargrum, no one dies for glory.  
> They die because the cost was chosen.

---

### Command Philosophy

Bargrum does not chase victory.

He constructs inevitability.

- Terrain is shaped before engagement  
- Movement is controlled, not reacted to  
- Pressure is applied until failure becomes certain  

He does not gamble.

He **calculates**.

---

## Brigade Composition

The Anvilstorm Brigade is deliberately unbalanced—weighted toward control, pressure, and decisive breakpoints.

- **35% Iron Anvil Line** (heavy infantry core)  
- **20% Stormhammer Cohort** (shock troops)  
- **20% Thunderforge Corps** (artillery and sappers combined)  
- **25% Thunderbolt Crossbow Cohort (“Sky Nails”)** (armored ranged pressure)  

---

## Command Hearth

- Bargrum Deep-Anvil  
- 2–3 **Shield-Thanes** (bodyguards and field leaders)  
- **Runesmith-Advisor** (wards, counter-magic, fire coordination)  
- **Standard-Bearer** (morale anchor and signal relay)  

---

## Core Elements

### Iron Anvil Line (35%)

Heavy infantry core.

- Tower shields, hammers, axes, short spears  
- Lockstep advance under fire  
- Built to endure and pin  

> Their task is simple: fix the enemy in place and refuse to yield.

---

### Stormhammer Cohort (20%)

Elite shock troops.

- Two-handed mauls and warhammers  
- Slightly lighter armor (by dwarven standards)  
- Crested or horned helms  

Held in reserve until the decisive moment.

> They are not sent to test the line.  
> They are sent to end it.

---

### Thunderforge Corps (20%)

Dwarves do not distinguish between artillery and sappers.

To them, both are simply **methods of shaping the battlefield**.

#### Capabilities

- Bolt-throwers, light cannon, rune-loaded storm mortars  
- Demolition charges and controlled collapses  
- Tunnel work, breach creation, and terrain denial  
- Trap-laying and choke-point engineering  

#### Role

- Pre-shape the battlefield before engagement  
- Break enemy formations before contact  
- Collapse terrain behind or beneath advancing forces  
- Deny retreat routes and force enemy movement  

> If the ground does not favor them, they change the ground.

---

### Thunderbolt Crossbow Cohort (“Sky Nails”) (25%)

Armored ranged infantry.

- Heavy crossbows (windlass or cranequin draw)  
- Pavise shields  
- Secondary melee weapons  

Unlike typical archers, they are built to **stand, not skirmish**.

#### Role

- Suppress and shape enemy movement  
- Target leaders and key threats  
- Maintain volleys throughout engagement  
- Seal routs and prevent withdrawal  

#### Identity

They are called the **Sky Nails**—because once they start falling, something is getting pinned.

---

## Battlefield Doctrine

### Phase I — Preparation

- Thunderforge Corps surveys and alters terrain  
- Ranges are measured, firing lanes established  
- Traps and collapse points are seeded  
- Sky Nails begin long-range pressure  

The enemy is already being shaped before contact.

---

### Phase II — Fixing the Enemy

- Iron Anvil Line advances in disciplined formation  
- Crossbow volleys intensify to control movement  
- Artillery strikes punish concentration and advance  

The goal is not damage.

The goal is **immobility**.

---

### Phase III — Pressure & Distortion

- Thunderforge Corps disrupts flanks and rear movement  
- Terrain collapses, traps trigger, formations break  
- Sky Nails shift targets to exposed weaknesses  

The enemy begins to lose cohesion.

Bargrum does not rush this.

---

### Phase IV — The Break

- Stormhammer Cohort commits at the chosen fault line  
- Crossbows narrow escape routes  
- Artillery shifts to precision suppression  

The break is not chaotic.

It is **enforced**.

---

### Phase V — Containment

- Enemy retreat is funneled into prepared ground  
- Survivors are allowed to flee—through terrain already shaped against them  
- The Brigade holds position  

They do not pursue recklessly.

The battlefield has already done the killing.

---

## Visual Identity

- **Banner:** Black anvil struck by three lightning bolts on storm-blue  
- **Armor:** Blackened steel with brass trim  
- **Markings:** White or silver lightning strokes  
- **Motto:** *Hold. Then Break.*
