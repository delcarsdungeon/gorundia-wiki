# The Free Order of the Open Chain

**10 Coldeven 1231**

> “Beringer used to wait for destiny to kick him in the teeth. Now he’s apparently learned to kick it sideways into other men and see who stands up. DeSalle stood up. In the mud, naturally. Would’ve been too easy on the boots otherwise.”
> — [[Sorek Redmarch]], later, on hearing of the founding

## Overview

The **Free Order of the Open Chain** was founded by [[Baron Jareth DeSalle]] in the muddy yard of [[Blackbar Post]], immediately after the liberation of its prisoners and the exposure of its abuses.

The order was created to:

- free the wrongfully imprisoned
- expose unlawful confinement
- hunt chainers, private jailers, slavers, and false debt-holders
- bring hidden captives into lawful witness and care
- serve justice without becoming any noble house’s private hand

Its members are commonly called **Chainwardens**.

## Founding Moment

Standing in the rain before the cages and whipping posts of Blackbar, Jareth announced that [[South DeSalle Village]] would be opened as a refuge for rescued victims.

He then endowed an independent knightly order with land, silver, hall-rights, chapel-rights, infirmary space, and the authority to begin work immediately.

He made clear that the order would not be bound to House DeSalle service.

> “This cannot become another lord’s private hand.”

The founding was deliberately public, immediate, and theatrical.

Which is to say: very DeSalle.

## Charter Charge

> To seek the hidden prisoner,  
> to open the unlawful door,  
> to break the false chain,  
> to bring the jailer before judgment,  
> and to strike the chainer wherever crown law, noble silence, or cowardice has failed.

## Motto

**No Chain Without Judgment**

A secondary phrase, strongly associated with the order’s founding, is:

**The Door Opens**

## Independence

Although founded and endowed by [[Baron Jareth DeSalle]], the order is intended to remain independent.

Jareth’s declared position:

- he grants land
- he grants silver
- he grants founding protection
- he does not command them

The order answers to its charter, lawful royal authority, and the witness of the Light.

## Immediate Role in the War

Jareth specifically did not intend the order to hamper the current war effort.

Instead, he expects the Chainwardens to be needed immediately in the campaign against Vogel’s apparatus.

Likely wartime functions include:

- searching captured posts for hidden prisoners
- documenting unlawful confinement
- escorting victims to refuge
- identifying chainers and private enforcers
- securing prisoner rolls and false writs
- supporting priests, healers, and investigators
- riding ahead where captives may be moved or killed before liberation

## Symbol

The likely badge of the order is:

**A silver key passing through an opened golden chain.**

The symbol deliberately avoids using the DeSalle serpent, preserving the order’s independence from its founder’s house.

## Founding Statement

> “My house helped make this place possible.
> Then my house will help make its answer.
>
> The false village becomes a true refuge.
> The chain becomes evidence.
> And the door opens.”

## Notes

The order’s founding marks a major public turn for [[Baron Jareth DeSalle]].

Where his father’s regime used performance to disguise cruelty, Jareth used spectacle to make retreat impossible.

He did not merely promise reform.

He created witnesses.
