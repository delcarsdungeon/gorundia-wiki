# The Rekindled Temple — Purifiers and Brethren

> “Not every flame is ours to command.  
> Some are ours to tend.  
> Some are ours to learn from.  
> Some are simply welcome beside the hearth.”
>
> — [[Sabry Emerald]]

## Overview

The Rekindled Temple is the restored heart of the [[Order of Purifiers and the Flame Temple]], gathered beneath the authority of [[Sabry Emerald]], Ember-Sovereign and chosen interpreter of the [[The Living Flame|Living Flame]].

The Order remains fundamentally **elven**.

Its rites were developed for elven bodies, elven memory, and the inherited Spark carried through certain elven bloodlines. Full-blooded elves form the majority of its initiates, while half-elves may inherit enough of the Spark to follow the Purifier path.

The Temple is not hostile to other peoples. It simply recognizes that its oldest teachings do not function for everyone.

Those who serve beside the Order without possessing the elven Spark are known collectively as the **Brethren of the Flame**.

## Purifiers and Brethren

A Purifier learns to awaken, endure, and govern the ancestral flame within.

A Brother or Sister of the Flame brings another sacred understanding of fire to stand beside it.

The distinction is theological rather than social. Brethren may be trusted companions, teachers, guardians, scholars, or clergy of allied traditions. They may reside within the Temple and advise its leadership, but they are not initiated into the innermost blood rites of the Order.

The word **Brethren** is retained from an older form of the temple liturgy and is used regardless of gender. Within ordinary conversation, members are commonly called Brother or Sister of the Flame.

Brethren do not hold Purifier rank, although an Ember-Sovereign may grant them authority over matters connected to their own expertise.

## The Ranks of the Rekindled Temple

### Ember-Sovereign

The Ember-Sovereign leads the Order, interprets the will of the Living Flame, approves ordination, assigns missions, and bears final responsibility for dangerous rites and contained entities.

### Ordained Purifier

A full member of the Order, trusted to conduct rites, confront corruption, train initiates, and act independently in the Temple’s name.

### Hearthflame

A senior initiate who has demonstrated discipline, judgment, and control. Hearthflames may instruct Cinders, supervise lesser rites, and manage important areas of temple life.

### Ember

A sworn initiate who has passed the first serious trials of control. Embers possess defined duties and are expected to serve the Temple as well as continue their training.

### Cinder

A novice whose Spark has answered but whose discipline, character, and endurance remain under examination.

Not every Cinder becomes a Purifier.

Some withdraw. Some fail. Some discover that their fire was never meant for the Order.

The Temple regards all three outcomes as preferable to ordaining someone who should not carry sacred flame.

# Current Household

## Sabry Emerald

**Rank:** Ember-Sovereign  
**Race:** Elf  
**Role:** Leader of the Rekindled Temple, voice of the Living Flame, keeper of its true fire

Sabry restored the Order after its long decline and now bears the unpleasant privilege of discovering what portions of its traditions were wisdom, what portions were fear, and what portions were merely old elves refusing to admit they were wrong.

She directs the Temple’s rites, receives petitions, oversees dangerous missions, and serves as custodian of the Living Flame when part of its true fire must be carried beyond the Temple.

Sabry received the [[Censer of the Clear Flame]] from [[Celene]] after helping cleanse a fungal corruption aboard a fallen elven skyship. The censer burns without fuel and produces blue-white smoke that clears poison, blight, foul air, and supernatural corruption.

### Sabry’s manner with the household

Sabry rarely gives the same instruction in the same tone twice.

To Vaerisse:

> “Slow down.”

To Kaelin:

> “Stop touching things.”

To Lethira:

> “Breathe.”

To Nyssa:

> “Inside voice.”

To Orren:

> “Thank you.”

To Althaea:

> “Tea?”

To Sylrae:

> “Show me.”

To Faelor:

> “Walk with me.”

To Thalen:

> “Report.”

# Ordained Purifiers

## Faelor Boundflame

**Rank:** Ordained Purifier  
**Race:** High elf  
**Role:** Senior ritualist, deputy to the Ember-Sovereign

Faelor descends from one of the oldest surviving Spark lineages associated with the Temple’s former wardens.

She is calm, deliberate, and almost impossible to hurry. When Sabry is absent, Faelor conducts formal rites and oversees the initiates.

The younger members are mildly terrified of disappointing her. Faelor has never deliberately encouraged this, which somehow makes it worse.

She believes that haste is not a sign of conviction.

> “A flame need not leap to prove it burns.”

## Thalen Emberwatch

**Rank:** Ordained Purifier  
**Race:** High elf  
**Role:** Temple guardian, keeper of bindings and relic vaults

Thalen oversees the Temple’s physical security, dangerous relics, containment chambers, and any fire spirit whose continued existence requires several locks and an unpleasant amount of prayer.

She possesses a particular talent for binding heat within stone, metal, and prepared circles. Her fire is controlled, dense, and difficult to disrupt.

Thalen rarely smiles and speaks in concise reports.

She secretly loves birds.

The initiates know this. None are foolish enough to mention it.

# Hearthflames

## Althaea Hearthsong

**Rank:** Hearthflame  
**Race:** Half-elf  
**Role:** Instructor of Cinders, keeper of the kitchens and refuge hearths

Althaea’s magic manifests through warmth, nourishment, shelter, and protective flame rather than destruction.

She regards cooking as a sacred act and the shared hearth as the first temple ever built.

She instructs the newest initiates, tends the sick, manages sanctuary arrangements, and has quietly become the person most residents seek when they are frightened, ashamed, or hungry.

Usually all three.

> “People speak of sacred fire as though the gods invented it for battle. Most people first meet fire over supper.”

## Sylrae Ashmantle

**Rank:** Hearthflame  
**Race:** Moon elf  
**Role:** Librarian, archivist, ritual assistant

Sylrae maintains the Temple’s surviving books, copied rites, recovered fragments, mission records, and correspondence.

Her gifts concern smoke, heat currents, drafts, and the movement of air through burning spaces. She is particularly useful when the Order must cleanse a structure without killing everyone inside it, a concern older Purifier texts occasionally treat as an optional refinement.

Sylrae possesses a dry sense of humor and is Sabry’s closest administrative assistant.

She knows where every scroll belongs.

She also knows precisely who failed to return it there.

# Embers

## Serelis Dawnember

**Rank:** Ember  
**Race:** High elf  
**Role:** Cantor, invocation leader, ritual singer

Serelis has a beautiful voice and an exacting memory for liturgy.

She leads invocations and can maintain difficult ritual cadences even during battle or magical disruption.

She cannot improvise.

Any unexpected alteration to a ceremony produces the expression of someone watching civilization collapse in real time.

## Orren Firebranch

**Status:** Brother of the Flame  
**Race:** Dwarf  
**Full Name:** Orren Barag-Khuld  
**Role:** Resident smith, forge scholar, keeper of temple metalwork

Orren is not a Purifier and has no elven Spark.

He is a dwarven smith who came to study the Living Flame because its ability to remember, judge, and communicate offended several perfectly reasonable dwarven assumptions about how fire ought to behave.

The elves call him Firebranch. Orren has never confirmed whether he considers this affectionate.

He maintains ritual braziers, repairs relic fittings, studies damaged enchanted objects, and teaches practical heat control.

Orren respects Sabry deeply but refuses to accept mystical metaphor as a substitute for proper measurements.

> “You said the flame was agitated.”
>
> “Yes.”
>
> “How hot was it?”
>
> “That is not what I meant.”
>
> “Then it is not yet a useful report.”

Although traditionally listed among the Embers because of his daily role and authority, Orren remains formally one of the Brethren of the Flame.

## Mireth Suncinder

**Rank:** Ember  
**Race:** Half-elf  
**Role:** Ceremonial aide, correspondent, liaison to noble households

Mireth comes from a human noble family whose elven ancestry had been politely ignored for generations.

Her Spark awakened late and publicly enough to make continued denial inconvenient.

She joined the Order against her family’s wishes.

Mireth is elegant, disciplined, and extremely capable in formal settings. She handles correspondence, visiting dignitaries, and the portions of Temple business that require everyone involved to pretend politics is not happening.

She is terrified of disappointing Sabry.

Sabry knows this and tries not to use it as leverage.

Usually.

# Cinders

## Vaerisse Emberlain

**Rank:** Cinder  
**Race:** High elf  
**Role:** Novice

[[Vaerisse Emberlain|Vaerisse]] is the most visible of the Temple’s new initiates, largely because she is enthusiastic enough to occupy the space normally reserved for three quieter people.

She hero-worships Sabry, possesses excellent instincts, and has very little control over either her speed or her limbs.

Her formal robes are slightly too large. She trips over them regularly.

When embarrassed, Vaerisse gives off visible warmth and occasionally a faint glow.

She is clumsy, not foolish. Beneath the chaos is a genuine talent for sensing corruption and recognizing when a flame has been altered by outside influence.

Vaerisse apologizes too often.

Hestra Keldrun is attempting to cure her of this.

## Elaris Ashbloom

**Rank:** Cinder  
**Race:** Wood elf  
**Role:** Copyist, student of burned memory

Elaris was raised among grove-tenders familiar with careful burns, firebreaks, and the use of flame to preserve rather than destroy living woodland.

She speaks rarely and prefers manuscripts to conversation.

Elaris always smells faintly of cedar smoke.

Her fire manifests primarily through her hands, which become warm rather than visibly aflame.

She may possess a rare talent known informally as **fire-memory**: by touching a burned object, she can sometimes sense who last handled it, what kind of flame damaged it, or the emotion surrounding its destruction.

## Kaelin Brightspark

**Rank:** Cinder  
**Race:** Half-elf  
**Role:** Novice, experimental nuisance

Kaelin is curious, clever, and unable to leave any magical process alone merely because it is stable, sacred, or currently not exploding.

His favorite sentence begins:

> “What happens if—”

Sabry has forbidden him from completing that sentence near the main sanctuary.

Kaelin has singed off portions of his eyebrows often enough that their continued return is regarded as evidence of divine mercy.

His potential is considerable. So is the projected cost of replacement furniture.

## Lethira Coalveil

**Rank:** Cinder  
**Race:** Moon elf  
**Role:** Novice, student of extinguishing and suppression

Lethira possesses one of the strongest Sparks among the current novices.

She is also deeply afraid of losing control.

Her most instinctive gift is not creating fire, but extinguishing it. Flame gutters near her when she is frightened, and with concentration she can draw heat out of burning objects without spreading the damage.

Lethira apologizes constantly and assumes every mistake proves she does not belong.

Sabry’s most frequent instruction to her is simply:

> “Breathe.”

## Nyssa Flintwind

**Rank:** Cinder  
**Race:** Half-elf  
**Role:** Novice, hunter, physical rescue trainee

Nyssa was raised among forest hunters and remains uncomfortable with enclosed temple life.

She approaches training as military discipline and is the strongest of the current Cinders.

Her Spark manifests through endurance rather than projected flame. Nyssa can resist smoke, heat, exhaustion, and pain far beyond ordinary limits.

She is being trained to enter burning or corrupted structures, carry out the injured, and remain standing when other Purifiers must retreat.

Nyssa is not especially loud by the standards of the forest.

The Temple has different standards.

# Brethren of the Flame

## The Purpose of the Brethren

The Brethren of the Flame are recognized allies whose knowledge of sacred, elemental, domestic, or forge-fire strengthens the Temple.

They do not attempt the elven blood rites.

They are not lesser members.

They are members of a different relationship.

The Ember-Sovereign may grant a Brother or Sister of the Flame authority over instruction, defense, scholarship, or ritual cooperation where their expertise exceeds that of the Order.

## Orren Barag-Khuld

Orren serves as the Temple’s resident Brother of the Flame and is described among the Embers because he lives, works, and teaches alongside them.

His presence has forced both dwarves and elves to admit that their peoples may possess useful knowledge unknown to the other.

Neither side has enjoyed this discovery.

## Furnacewife Hestra Keldrun

**Status:** Sister of the Flame  
**Race:** Dwarf  
**Order:** The Furnacewives  
**Role:** Visiting instructor, defensive fire specialist, protector of refuges

Hestra Keldrun belongs to the dwarven Sisterhood of the Furnacewives, an order drawn from dwarven women unable to bear children and sworn instead to protect the lives of dwarven mothers and children.

Furnacewives command the **Maker’s Fire**, raising walls, pillars, barriers, and circles of defensive flame. When not called to battle, they devote themselves to forgecraft and may rank among the greatest smiths of the dwarven peoples.

Hestra came to the Rekindled Temple to study how Purifier flame identifies corruption and preserves healthy life.

Sabry, in turn, studies the Furnacewives’ ability to hold ground, defend sanctuaries, and place sacred fire between danger and those unable to protect themselves.

Hestra is broad, grey-braided, and calm in the manner of someone who has already decided where the wall of fire will go if negotiations fail.

She does not appreciate sympathy regarding her infertility.

She considers her vows a life chosen, not a consolation prize.

Her fire does not dance or pursue.

It plants itself and refuses passage.

### Hestra and Vaerisse

Hestra likes Vaerisse.

She therefore trusts her with nothing involving:

- stairs
- ceremonial poles
- loose rugs
- unattended braziers
- open oil
- other people

Her usual instruction is:

> “Stop apologizing. Put the fire out.”

### Hestra and Sabry

Hestra respects Sabry without revering her.

This makes her unusually valuable.

> **Sabry:** “The Flame asked me to carry it to Harrowmere.”
>
> **Hestra:** “Did it ask you to bring water?”
>
> **Sabry:** “No.”
>
> **Hestra:** “Divinity is no excuse for poor packing.”

# Life Within the Temple

The Rekindled Temple is too small to behave like an ancient institution and too burdened by history to behave like an ordinary household.

It is consequently both.

The household eats together when duties allow. Senior members teach junior ones. Everyone performs ordinary work regardless of rank, although Thalen is forbidden from reorganizing the kitchens and Kaelin is forbidden from improving them.

The Temple’s residents gradually fall into recognizable roles:

- Vaerisse is the heart.
- Kaelin is the problem currently making a noise.
- Lethira is the worrier.
- Nyssa is the shield.
- Elaris is the quiet observer.
- Serelis is the voice.
- Mireth is the composed face.
- Althaea is the hearth.
- Sylrae is the memory.
- Faelor is the steady hand.
- Thalen is the locked door.
- Orren is the hammer.
- Hestra is the wall.
- Sabry is the one expected to make all of this into an Order.

No pressure, naturally.

# Naming Traditions

Many members of the Order bear surnames connected to fire, ash, sunlight, or the hearth.

Some descend from ancient Purifier families.

Others adopt an **ash-name** after their Spark is recognized or after achieving the rank of Ember.

An ash-name may be chosen by the initiate, granted by the Ember-Sovereign, or discovered through a rite conducted before the Living Flame.

Cinders generally retain their birth names until their position within the Order becomes clear.

The adoption of an ash-name is not mandatory, but refusing one is rare.

# Current Concerns

The Rekindled Temple is still rebuilding its traditions, recovering lost records, and determining which old rites remain safe.

Its immediate concerns include:

- training initiates without repeating the failures of the former Order
- recovering lost Purifier texts and relics
- investigating magical corruption that resists ordinary purification
- studying the nature of the [[Harrowmere Caldera]]
- determining what the Living Flame means when it asks to see distant places
- maintaining relations with [[Celene]]
- establishing formal cooperation with dwarven forge traditions
- deciding how public the restored Order should become
- preventing Kaelin from discovering new states of matter
