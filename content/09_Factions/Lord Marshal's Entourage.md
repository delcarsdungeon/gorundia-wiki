# Offices of the Lord Marshal

The **Office of the Lord Marshal of Gorundia** is the Crown’s supreme military authority beneath the king. It exists to coordinate the realm’s armies, enforce wartime order, audit readiness, maintain military roads and garrisons, and ensure that feudal obligations become actual soldiers rather than pretty promises written in dry ink.

In peacetime, the office is lean, watchful, and bureaucratically sharp. In wartime, it becomes the spine of the kingdom’s military response.

Its current holder is [[Beringer]], appointed by royal writ and charged with restoring discipline, readiness, and lawful command across the Crown’s forces.

---

## Senior Offices

### Lord Marshal of Gorundia

**Holder:** [[Beringer]]

The Lord Marshal is the supreme commander of the Crown’s forces under the king. He holds wartime authority over royal hosts, feudal levies, garrisons, musters, roads, and internal military order.

The office is both military and judicial in nature. The Lord Marshal may issue operational commands, inspect readiness, convene military courts, discipline officers, and coordinate the Crown’s response to rebellion, invasion, border crisis, or large-scale disorder.

The Lord Marshal’s authority does not replace the king’s command. It makes that command usable.

---

### Lieutenant Marshal

**Holder:** [[Sorek Redmarch]]

The Lieutenant Marshal acts in the Lord Marshal’s stead when required and oversees the day-to-day execution of policy.

The office is responsible for translating broad command decisions into orders, assignments, inspections, arrests, escorts, relief movements, and practical outcomes. Where the Lord Marshal determines what must be done, the Lieutenant Marshal often makes certain it actually happens.

In practice, the Lieutenant Marshal is the office’s hard hand and steady eye.

---

### Marshal of the Roads

**Holder:** Sir [Kael Tarrow]

The Marshal of the Roads maintains safe passage across the Crown’s military roads, patrol routes, bridges, mustering roads, courier corridors, and strategic garrison routes.

This office coordinates with regional watches, reeves, road wardens, garrisons, and local nobles to keep troops, messengers, prisoners, refugees, and supplies moving. In troubled times, the Marshal of the Roads may authorize armed escorts, road closures, checkpoint authority, and temporary seizure of transport assets for military necessity.

---

### Warden of the Marches

**Holder:** Sir [Calladrel] of Araisal

The Warden of the Marches monitors border threats, incursions, foreign maneuvering, and frontier readiness.

The office gathers reports from border lords, scouts, garrison commanders, military chaplains, and allied frontier houses. It advises the Lord Marshal on the likelihood of raids, organized incursions, Dominion pressure, or movements by hostile non-state forces.

The Warden’s task is to make sure the kingdom does not learn of invasion from burning villages.

---

### Master of Musters

**Holder:** Sir [Denvar Kline]

The Master of Musters tracks levies, sworn men, feudal obligations, household troops, and emergency manpower throughout the realm.

This office maintains muster rolls, verifies whether nobles can produce the forces they claim, audits manpower obligations, and records shortages in arms, mounts, training, or command structure. The Master of Musters is particularly feared by lords who prefer impressive numbers on paper and vague noises when asked to produce them in mud.

---

### High Provost of the Marshal’s Court

**Holder:** Sir [Edmar Rusk]

The High Provost enforces military law within the jurisdiction of the Lord Marshal.

The office tries officers, knights, soldiers, and sworn men for misconduct, oathbreaking, desertion, mutiny, cowardice, unlawful violence, and dereliction of duty. The High Provost may convene courts, gather testimony, order confinement, and recommend punishment according to military law and royal writ.

The High Provost is not popular. This is generally considered proof that the office is functioning.

---

### Keeper of Oaths

**Holder:** Sir [Ireth Solmere]

The Keeper of Oaths maintains records of knightly vows, wartime compacts, sworn obligations, extraordinary bonds, paroles, battlefield promises, and formal military submissions.

This office exists because Gorundia runs on oaths, and oaths become dangerous when no one remembers the exact words. The Keeper’s ledgers may determine whether a knight is bound, released, forsworn, protected, or condemned.

---

### Quartermaster-General

**Holder:** [Marrec Fenwroth]

The Quartermaster-General controls supply chains, provisioning, equipment distribution, transport, remount assignments, camp stores, siege stores, and emergency wartime logistics.

The office is not glamorous. It is, however, the difference between an army and a crowd of hungry men holding sharp objects.

The Quartermaster-General coordinates with local granaries, royal storehouses, armorers, wagon masters, river transport, road stations, and allied houses to ensure that soldiers arrive with food, boots, arrows, tents, spare tack, and enough discipline not to eat the seed grain.

---

### Master of Signals

**Holder:** Magus Arlen Corvis

The Master of Signals oversees messengers, beacon systems, signal towers, dispatch riders, courier routes, magical communication where available, and rapid military reporting networks.

This office coordinates mundane and arcane communications between the Crown, the Lord Marshal, castles, forts, road posts, border towers, field armies, and allied command structures.

The Master of Signals is expected to make certain that warning travels faster than disaster.

---

### Standard-Bearer of the Realm

**Holder:** Sir Joran Feld

The Standard-Bearer of the Realm carries and represents the king’s war banner in the field.

The office is ceremonial, symbolic, and practical. The Standard-Bearer serves as a visible field deputy of royal authority, particularly in moments where the presence of the Crown must be seen by soldiers, nobles, allies, and enemies alike.

To lose the banner is a disgrace. To abandon it is worse.

---

### Chaplain-General of the Host

**Holder:** Sir Reynard Ironmantle

The Chaplain-General coordinates Triad clergy attached to armies and garrisons.

This office ensures that Pelorite, Heironean, and Pholtine clergy serving with the host maintain unified doctrine, battlefield morale, proper rites, medical support, oath oversight, and lawful conduct. The Chaplain-General also advises the Lord Marshal when military action touches sacred law, divine signs, morale collapse, battlefield atrocity, or crisis of conscience.

---

### Master of Engineers

**Holder:** Undesignated

The Master of Engineers supervises fortifications, siegecraft, military roads, bridges, fieldworks, mining, demolition, and major military construction projects.

This office coordinates masons, sappers, carpenters, siege crews, road crews, and contracted specialists. It is especially important in border defense, fortress repair, river crossings, and campaigns requiring rapid field construction.

---

### Captain of the King’s Vanguard

**Holder:** Undesignated

The Captain of the King’s Vanguard commands elite royal strike units assigned to rapid response, decisive engagements, special escorts, and critical battlefield intervention.

This office answers directly to royal command but may operate in coordination with the Lord Marshal during kingdom-wide military operations.

---

### Captain of the Marshal’s Vanguard

**Holder:** Captain Brannoc Dalreth

The Captain of the Marshal’s Vanguard commands elite units assigned directly to the Lord Marshal for rapid response, dangerous arrests, crisis reinforcement, military escort, and decisive field action.

Unlike the King’s Vanguard, this force exists to support the Marshal’s operational authority. It is not ornamental, and its deployment usually means the Lord Marshal expects resistance.

---

### Inspector of Garrisons

**Holder:** Sir Odran Pell

The Inspector of Garrisons conducts surprise reviews of castles, towers, road forts, border posts, military storehouses, and garrison commands.

This office searches for corruption, negligence, false reporting, empty armories, rotten food stores, unpaid soldiers, untrained levies, compromised officers, and fortifications that exist only in ledgers.

A visit from the Inspector of Garrisons is rarely welcome. It is often overdue.

---

### Marshal’s Envoy Extraordinary

**Holder:** [[Dame Venymmara Gylenda]]

The Marshal’s Envoy Extraordinary acts as the diplomatic arm of the Lord Marshal in military negotiations, crisis mediation, prisoner exchanges, emergency parleys, allied coordination, and delicate confrontations where a drawn sword would be premature but a soft hand would be foolish.

The office requires poise, authority, judgment, and the ability to walk into a room full of armed egos without making the situation worse.

---

# Household Assigned by Royal Writ

By royal writ, a small personal staff is assigned to the Lord Marshal. These positions are not command offices of the Marshalate, but trusted household and administrative roles intended to support the Lord Marshal’s daily function, appearance, correspondence, security, and domestic order.

The staff is deliberately lean, professional, and free of courtly theater.

---

## Valet to the Lord Marshal

**Holder:** Alard Fenric  
**Age:** 38  
**Background:** Former Quartermaster-Sergeant of the King’s Vanguard

Alard Fenric is a campaign veteran, logistics specialist, and personal attendant assigned to the Lord Marshal.

He has seen mud, blood, winter marches, ruined boots, and officers too proud to admit fatigue. None of that shows on him. His linen is crisp, his beard trimmed, and his boots polished without becoming decorative. He does not hover. He observes.

Alard manages armor readiness, wardrobe, travel timing, recovery intervals, and the quiet practical needs of a man expected to appear composed while carrying too much command weight.

He is not ambitious. He is institutional.

He may say, softly:

> “You have been in plate too long, my lord.”

This is not advice. It is a professional diagnosis.

---

## Yeoman and Armsman

**Holder:** Garron Pike  
**Age:** 26  
**Background:** Western border archer; son of a miller

Garron Pike is literate, solid, quiet, and instinctively protective.

He handles weapons, basic maintenance, mount rotation, travel security, camp discipline, and the small violent practicalities that follow an important man on the road.

He does not smell like a barracks. He smells of soap and bowstring wax.

Garron watches doorways before conversations. He speaks little, but when he does, it is direct.

---

## Clerk-Scribe

**Holder:** Brother Caldus Merrow  
**Age:** 32  
**Affiliation:** Pholtine laity, temporarily attached from the White Court

Brother Caldus Merrow is a lay adherent of Pholtus specializing in writ review, audit language, records, procedural comparison, and clean documentation.

He is clean-lined, composed, and deeply committed to clarity.

Caldus does not embellish reports. He does not soften language. He does not dramatize irregularity.

If something is wrong, he will simply write:

> “This deviates from precedent.”

He does not accuse.

He records.

This is frequently worse.

## Lady’s Maid to Elizaveta

**Holder:** Vesna Dragomira  
**Age:** 25  
**Background:** Born in Orenic hill country to a minor sworn household

Vesna Dragomira was educated in household management at a convent-school tied to the Storm Lynx. She speaks formal court Gorundian and the clipped Orenic dialect. Her reputation is steady, unsentimental, and deeply loyal.

Vesna is not ornamental.

She carries herself with compact strength: not imposing, but grounded. Her hair is usually braided tight. Her hands are practical rather than delicate.

She understands how Orenic nobility values endurance over display, when silence is protective, and when someone is being tested socially.

Her duties include managing Elizaveta’s wardrobe and presentation, filtering social approaches without offense, organizing and securing private correspondence, and gently coaching Elizaveta through Orenic court expectations when needed.

She does not gossip.

She does not dramatize.

If Elizaveta falters in a tense moment, Vesna may murmur:

> “Stand straight. They watch posture first.”

Her loyalty is to Elizaveta personally, but her instincts are Orenic. She believes in quiet endurance, family cohesion, and not embarrassing the house.

She will absolutely notice weakness in a room long before most courtiers do.

## Notes for Use

The Marshalate should feel professional rather than ornamental. It is a wartime command structure built for a kingdom that has learned, repeatedly and painfully, that unreadiness kills.

The personal household should feel equally disciplined, but smaller and quieter. These are not courtiers orbiting a great lord. They are the people making sure the Lord Marshal sleeps, reads the correct writs, wears the correct armor, arrives on time, does not get stabbed in a hallway, and does not accidentally become a smell.
