# Letter from Ser Jareth DeSalle to Lord Marshal Sir Beringer Amadis

To **Lord Marshal Sir Beringer Amadis**,

Word of my father’s death has reached me on the eastern road. I have taken the time to gather the accounts of several witnesses before writing to you, for grief is no excuse for haste, and a knight’s first duty is to the truth.

It appears that my father answered a challenge and met his opponent in the sight of honorable men. If this account is correct, then the field has rendered its judgment, and I will not dishonor my house by pretending otherwise.

My father was a proud man. Pride often leads a man where wiser counsel would not. I will mourn him as a son must, but I will not claim injustice where there was choice.

I ask only one courtesy: that the circumstances of the duel be recorded plainly and preserved among the muster rolls, so that rumor does not grow where fact should stand. A house already burdened with its share of ill talk has no need of invention.

When I return to Darkstone, I intend to set my barony in good order and see that its knights serve the realm with steadier judgment than has sometimes been the case in recent years. The men of Darkstone are good soldiers. They deserve a better reputation than they presently carry.

If there are matters of the Crown in which my service is required, you may call upon me without hesitation. My oath to Gorundia stands where it always has.

For the present, I will see my father laid to rest.

— **Ser Jareth DeSalle**
Heir of Darkstone
