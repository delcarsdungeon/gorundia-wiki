Letter from Norwin Stoneyeye to Professor Erinleaf 

To: Professor Erinleaf Gorundia’s University of Magery Department of Enhanced Enchantments 

My dear Erinleaf, 

It’s been too long since we sat beneath that dreadful bronze griffin out in the upper quad and solved the world’s problems — or at least scolded them politely from a distance. 

I won’t bury the lead.  I’ve sent letters to three of your brightest soon-to-be graduates — Joric Althain, Tarn Morsen, and Rudvik Stonebrow — with an offer to join me here in Bard’s Gate. I’ve opened a workshop: enchantment-focused, briskly paced, and better funded than you’d expect given the state of my bookkeeping. 

They’re good lads. Sharp, each in his own way. But young mages (especially ambitious ones) sometimes hesitate when faced with a path that doesn’t come with a ceremonial hood and a gold-lettered scroll. If you find yourself in conversation with any of them, a word of encouragement — subtle, of course — could tip the balance. 

Also, I’d like to extend a personal invitation to you. Bard’s Gate is undergoing something of a renaissance — trade, culture, magic, even manners (though that’s slower going). You’d find no shortage of minds to challenge, oddities to classify, or potions to critique. And I’m long overdue for a conversation that doesn’t involve soot, screaming gemstones, or paperwork. 

With warm regards, 
Norwin Stoneyeye 
Bard’s Gate
