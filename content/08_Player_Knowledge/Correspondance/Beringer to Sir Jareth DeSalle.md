# Letter from Lord Marshal Sir Beringer Amadis to Ser Jareth DeSalle

- To Ser Jareth DeSalle, 
  
  You have my condolences in the loss of your father. Take what solace you may in knowing that he met an honorable fate in the arena. I can confirm that the accounts you have gathered are, in all likelihood, accurate. At your request, I will set down my own testimony here, so that your family may have a clear and formal record of the events. 
  
  Before the eyes of Gods and King alike, Baron Remy DeSalle spoke words that gave grave insult to both our sovereign and the judgment of the Triune Gods. In answer to this, His Majesty acted within his rightful authority in compelling a duel of honor, wherein the Baron was struck down by a duly appointed champion of the Lord Marshal’s office. 
  
  My father, Count Amadis, has expressed his confidence that the Barony of Darkstone will remain steadfast under your capable hand. I am certain that Count Serrin will soon make himself known to you, that you may render your baronial oaths. 
  
  Do not be daunted—my uncle is well aware of my father’s high regard for you and looks forward to seeing you take up your new station. 

- The kingdom stands on the cusp of trials yet to come, and it will have need of strong and resolute men. I have no doubt that the sons of Darkstone will answer that call. 
  
  —Lord Marshal Sir Beringer Amadis
