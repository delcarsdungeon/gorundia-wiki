# The Message Chain — “Landed Fine”

## Lord Marshal’s Field Command

The fighting has broken.

Not ended.

Broken.

The Dominion force has yielded the immediate field. Gorundian formations are reforming, wounded are moving toward the rear, and officers are already demanding numbers from men who have not yet finished bleeding.

At the center of the Lord Marshal’s signal station, **Arlen Corvus** works through the traffic with the clipped efficiency of a man who has spent the last hour being required in six places at once.

He touches the sending focus.

> **CORVUS:**  
> “Priority field update. Crown and senior commands.”
>
> “Enemy forces have yielded the immediate ground.”
>
> “The Lord Marshal is consolidating.”
>
> “Baron Kharne has joined the Lord Marshal and taken the field.”
>
> “He landed safely after departing the *Second Draft* in flight under alchemical flight.”
>
> “Magus Mara Moffet was wrapped around him during descent.”
>
> “Both reached the ground without injury.”
>
> “The *Second Draft* remains operational.”

Corvus moves immediately to the next report.

To him, this is simply an accurate description of events.

He has neither the time nor the inclination to consider how any particular sentence might sound to Baron Kharne’s pregnant wife.

---

## Royal Command

**Sapphire Flame** receives the transmission first.

She listens without interruption.

No surprise.

No commentary.

She strips the report down to what the King actually needs.

> **SAPPHIRE FLAME:**  
> “The Lord Marshal holds the field.”
>
> “The enemy has yielded the immediate ground.”
>
> “Baron Kharne has joined him.”
>
> “Kharne and Moffet are unharmed.”
>
> “The *Second Draft* remains operational.”

The King gets his report.

The signal traffic continues outward.

---

## Sorek Redmarch’s Headquarters

The focus stone brightens.

**Maester Calven Rook** immediately straightens.

> **ROOK:**  
> “Lord Marshal’s signal office, sir.”

Sorek looks over.

That is all.

Rook has learned that Sorek Redmarch does not need to raise his voice to make a young mage feel as though he is sitting an examination he has somehow already failed.

Rook listens.

Then repeats.

> **ROOK:**  
> “Enemy yielded the immediate ground.”
>
> “Lord Marshal consolidating.”
>
> “Baron Kharne joined him and has taken the field.”
>
> “He landed safely after departing the *Second Draft* in flight under alchemical flight.”
>
> “Magus Mara Moffet was... wrapped around him during descent.”
>
> “Both reached the ground without injury.”
>
> “The ship remains operational.”

Sorek closes his eyes for half a heartbeat.

> **SOREK:**  
> “Of course he fucking did.”

Rook studies his writing board.

Sorek looks back toward the map.

Orin is alive.

Mara is alive.

Beringer has him.

Good.

Then another thought occurs.

Braga.

She will hear eventually.

Which means she should hear now.

From him.

> **SOREK:**  
> “Send Stormarrow.”

Rook looks up.

> **ROOK:**  
> “Baroness Kharne?”

Sorek gives him a look.

> **SOREK:**  
> “No, the fucking cook.”

Rook lowers his eyes.

> **ROOK:**  
> “Yes, sir.”

He prepares the sending focus.

> **ROOK:**  
> “Exact wording?”

Sorek looks at him again.

They have had this conversation before.

> **SOREK:**  
> “Always.”

Rook nods.

Sorek thinks for perhaps two seconds.

He is trying to be reassuring.

This will prove important.

> **SOREK:**  
> “Baron Kharne landed fine.”
>
> “He’s joined the Lord Marshal.”
>
> “They’ve taken the field.”
>
> “Moffet is with him.”
>
> “Both unharmed.”

Rook waits.

> **ROOK:**  
> “Anything regarding the *Second Draft*, sir?”

Sorek thinks.

Corvus said the ship was operational.

Nothing to report.

> **SOREK:**  
> “No.”
>
> “Send it.”

Rook sends it.

And Sorek returns to the war.

---

## Stormarrow

The signal focus wakes.

**Magus Elowen Caerwyn**, Crown Mage of Signals, is already beside it.

She usually stays within a few strides of Braga.

That is deliberate.

The King did not send Braga merely a communications specialist.

Elowen is also trained close protection, quiet enough that most rooms forget she is guarding anyone until they notice she has positioned herself between Braga and the door.

She places two fingers against the focus.

Listens.

Then turns slightly.

> **ELOWEN:**  
> “Message from Sir Sorek Redmarch, Baroness.”

Braga stops what she is doing.

> **ELOWEN:**  
> “‘Baron Kharne landed fine.’”
>
> “‘He’s joined the Lord Marshal.’”
>
> “‘They’ve taken the field.’”
>
> “‘Moffet is with him.’”
>
> “‘Both unharmed.’”

For a moment, Braga simply absorbs it.

Orin is alive.

Mara is alive.

The battle has gone their way.

Her shoulders loosen.

Then—

Her brow furrows.

> **BRAGA:**  
> “...landed?”

Elowen says nothing.

Braga looks directly at her.

> **BRAGA:**  
> “What does he mean, ‘landed fine’?”

A beat.

Then the obvious conclusion.

> **BRAGA:**  
> “Is the ship damaged?”

Elowen has already noticed the same ambiguity.

Her expression does not change.

> **ELOWEN:**  
> “The message does not say.”

Braga’s eyes narrow.

> **BRAGA:**  
> “Ask.”

Elowen reaches for the focus.

Braga adds:

> **BRAGA:**  
> “Exactly that.”

> **ELOWEN:**  
> “Naturally.”

---

## Back to Sorek

Calven Rook receives the return signal.

He listens.

His expression changes just enough for Sorek to notice.

> **SOREK:**  
> “What?”

Rook clears his throat.

> **ROOK:**  
> “Baroness Kharne asks—”
>
> “‘What do you mean, landed fine?’”
>
> “‘Is the ship damaged?’”

Silence.

Sorek stops moving.

He stares at the map.

Then slowly closes his eyes.

Because now he hears his own message as Braga heard it.

And remembers the part he left out.

> **SOREK:**  
> “...fuck.”

Rook says nothing.

Good instincts.

Sorek rubs one hand across his face.

> **SOREK:**  
> “I told you not to reword things.”

There is a tiny pause.

> **ROOK:**  
> “I didn’t, sir.”

Sorek looks at him.

Rook very carefully meets his eyes.

> **ROOK:**  
> “That was exactly what you said.”

Another silence.

Somehow, this is worse.

Sorek looks back at the map.

> **SOREK:**  
> “Right.”

A breath.

> **SOREK:**  
> “Send clarification.”

Rook readies himself.

> **SOREK:**  
> “The *Second Draft* is undamaged.”
>
> “Orin jumped from it while it was still airborne.”

Rook’s pen stops for just a fraction.

Sorek notices.

> **SOREK:**  
> “Yes.”
>
> “Jumped.”

Rook resumes writing.

> **SOREK:**  
> “He had taken an alchemical flight draught.”
>
> “Mara went with him.”
>
> “He kept hold of her during the descent.”
>
> “Both reached the ground safely.”
>
> “They joined the Lord Marshal.”
>
> “Neither is injured.”

Sorek pauses.

Then:

> **SOREK:**  
> “And the ship is fine.”

Rook finishes.

Looks down at the message.

Then up at Sorek.

Very cautiously:

> **ROOK:**  
> “Sir?”

Sorek already knows.

> **SOREK:**  
> “What?”

> **ROOK:**  
> “Perhaps the ship being fine might have been useful in the first—”

Sorek turns his head.

Rook touches the focus.

> **ROOK:**  
> “Sending now, sir.”

---

## Stormarrow

Elowen receives the clarification.

Braga watches her.

> **ELOWEN:**  
> “Clarification from Sir Sorek.”
>
> “‘The *Second Draft* is undamaged.’”
>
> “‘Orin jumped from it while it was still airborne.’”

Braga goes absolutely still.

Elowen continues.

> **ELOWEN:**  
> “‘He had taken an alchemical flight draught.’”
>
> “‘Mara went with him.’”
>
> “‘He kept hold of her during the descent.’”
>
> “‘Both reached the ground safely.’”
>
> “‘They joined the Lord Marshal.’”
>
> “‘Neither is injured.’”
>
> “‘And the ship is fine.’”

Silence.

Braga stares at Elowen.

> **BRAGA:**  
> “He jumped off the airship.”

> **ELOWEN:**  
> “Yes, Baroness.”

> **BRAGA:**  
> “While it was flying.”

> **ELOWEN:**  
> “Yes.”

Braga closes her eyes.

One slow breath.

> **BRAGA:**  
> “Of course he did.”

Another breath.

Then:

> **BRAGA:**  
> “Mara went with him.”

Not quite a question.

> **ELOWEN:**  
> “Yes.”

Braga processes that separately.

Her husband had decided, in whatever towering half-orc approximation of judgment had existed at that moment, that he needed to get onto the battlefield immediately.

But he had not abandoned his mage aboard the ship.

He made sure she had flight.

He took her with him.

He kept physical hold of her all the way down.

Braga’s jaw tightens.

> **BRAGA:**  
> “Good.”

Elowen waits.

> **BRAGA:**  
> “If the idiot was going, she needed to be with him.”

A beat.

> **BRAGA:**  
> “And if he was taking her, he damn well better keep hold of her.”

> **ELOWEN:**  
> “That appears to be what he did.”

Braga nods once.

Still furious.

But that part, at least, was right.

Then Elowen glances briefly toward the focus.

There is more traffic arriving.

> **ELOWEN:**  
> “I have the original Lord Marshal signal report now, Baroness.”

Braga looks over.

> **BRAGA:**  
> “Anything Sorek missed?”

A fractional pause.

Elowen Caerwyn is a professional.

A Crown-trained signal mage.

A bodyguard.

A woman presumably taught to deliver intelligence without coloring it.

Unfortunately, she is also capable of understanding Common.

> **ELOWEN:**  
> “Magus Corvus reported the same events in greater detail.”

Braga waits.

Elowen continues.

> **ELOWEN:**  
> “He reported that Baron Kharne departed the *Second Draft* under alchemical flight.”
>
> “And that Magus Moffet was—”

A pause.

Very small.

Braga notices it immediately.

> **BRAGA:**  
> “Was what?”

Elowen commits.

> **ELOWEN:**  
> “‘Wrapped around him during descent.’”

Braga’s head turns.

Slowly.

> **BRAGA:**  
> “Wrapped around him.”

> **ELOWEN:**  
> “Magus Corvus’s exact wording.”

Silence.

Braga looks away.

Then back.

> **BRAGA:**  
> “Well, what the fuck was she supposed to do?”

Elowen wisely does not answer.

> **BRAGA:**  
> “He’s seven feet tall.”
>
> “She’s falling out of the bloody sky.”
>
> “Of course she was hanging onto him.”

A beat.

> **BRAGA:**  
> “And he damn well better have been hanging onto her.”

> **ELOWEN:**  
> “Yes, Baroness.”

Braga folds her arms.

Her expression tightens again.

> **BRAGA:**  
> “Still.”

Elowen waits.

> **BRAGA:**  
> “‘Wrapped around him.’”

> **ELOWEN:**  
> “Yes.”

> **BRAGA:**  
> “Corvus couldn’t find another way to put that?”

Elowen considers this with grave professional seriousness.

> **ELOWEN:**  
> “Several come immediately to mind.”

Braga looks at her.

Elowen’s expression is perfectly composed.

> **BRAGA:**  
> “Don’t.”

> **ELOWEN:**  
> “I haven’t.”

Braga stares another moment.

Then a reluctant breath escapes through her nose.

Nearly a laugh.

Nearly.

> **BRAGA:**  
> “Send Redmarch.”

Elowen prepares the focus.

> **BRAGA:**  
> “Tell him I’m glad they’re safe.”

Elowen writes.

> **BRAGA:**  
> “Tell him Orin did right keeping Mara with him.”

Another line.

Then Braga looks directly at the focus as if Sorek himself were standing inside it.

> **BRAGA:**  
> “And tell that old bastard the next time my husband jumps off a flying airship, the first sentence is—”
>
> “‘Orin jumped off the fucking airship.’”

Elowen’s pen continues.

> **BRAGA:**  
> “Then he tells me everybody is fine.”
>
> “Then he tells me the ship is fine.”
>
> “In that order.”

A beat.

> **BRAGA:**  
> “And he can leave out Corvus’s fucking romance novel.”

Elowen finally loses perhaps half a millimeter at one corner of her mouth.

Braga catches it.

> **BRAGA:**  
> “You’re enjoying this.”

> **ELOWEN:**  
> “Very slightly, Baroness.”

Braga points toward the focus.

> **BRAGA:**  
> “Send the damned message.”

---

## Sorek Redmarch, Again

Calven Rook receives the reply.

He listens.

Sorek watches him.

> **SOREK:**  
> “Well?”

Rook begins.

> **ROOK:**  
> “Baroness Kharne says she is glad they are safe.”
>
> “She says Baron Kharne did right keeping Magus Moffet with him.”

Sorek nods.

Expected.

> **ROOK:**  
> “She further says—”
>
> “‘Tell that old bastard the next time my husband jumps off a flying airship, the first sentence is: Orin jumped off the fucking airship.’”

Sorek closes his eyes.

> **ROOK:**  
> “‘Then tell me everybody is fine.’”
>
> “‘Then tell me the ship is fine.’”
>
> “‘In that order.’”

Sorek nods slowly.

> **SOREK:**  
> “Fair.”

Rook hesitates.

Sorek opens one eye.

> **SOREK:**  
> “There’s more.”

> **ROOK:**  
> “Yes, sir.”

> **SOREK:**  
> “Go on.”

Rook takes a breath.

> **ROOK:**  
> “‘And you can leave out Corvus’s fucking romance novel.’”

Sorek frowns.

> **SOREK:**  
> “What?”

Rook visibly regrets being alive.

> **ROOK:**  
> “Apparently Baroness Kharne received Magus Corvus’s original report.”

A pause.

Sorek remembers it.

His expression changes.

> **SOREK:**  
> “The ‘wrapped around him’ bit.”

> **ROOK:**  
> “Yes, sir.”

Sorek looks toward the distant signal station.

Long.

Judging.

> **SOREK:**  
> “Corvus actually sent that to the Crown?”

> **ROOK:**  
> “Apparently to all senior command channels, sir.”

Another long silence.

Then:

> **SOREK:**  
> “Idiot.”

Rook cannot quite stop himself.

> **ROOK:**  
> “Baron Kharne, sir?”

Sorek looks at him.

> **SOREK:**  
> “Corvus.”

A beat.

Sorek turns back to the map.

> **SOREK:**  
> “Kharne’s a completely different kind of idiot.”

And the war continues.
