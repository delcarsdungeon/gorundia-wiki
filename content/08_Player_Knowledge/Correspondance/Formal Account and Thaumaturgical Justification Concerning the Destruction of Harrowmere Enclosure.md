# Formal Account and Thaumaturgical Justification Concerning the Destruction of Harrowmere Enclosure

**To His Majesty [[King Priam Ryomar]], Sovereign of the Realm, and to Acting Archmage [[Sapphireflame]], Keeper of the High Art in Royal Service**

**From [[Norwin Stoneyeye]], Royal Archivist and Sage of the Realm**

**Subject:** Formal Account and Thaumaturgical Justification Concerning the Destruction of [[Harrowmere Enclosure]]

**Submitted with respect and loyalty**

---

Your Majesty, Acting Archmage,

I submit this report in answer to concerns arising from the destruction of Harrowmere Enclosure, formerly a fortress outpost of the realm, later abandoned, corrupted, rebuilt, and employed by hostile powers as a sacrificial resonance chamber tied into a lattice of unholy sites.

I acknowledge at the outset the gravity of the matter. Harrowmere Enclosure stood within the King’s lands. Its destruction, though undertaken in holy cause and under martial-religious authority already present at the site, bears consequences of royal, military, legal, and arcane importance. I therefore offer this account not as excuse, but as complete explanation: first of the authority under which the destruction was undertaken, second of the condition of the site, third of the insufficiency of conventional demolition, fourth of the necessity and suitability of the Volcano working, and fifth of the resulting strategic condition of the land.

The brief finding is as follows:

Harrowmere Enclosure was not merely a corrupted fortress. It had been converted into a sacrificial resonance engine whose above-ground architecture, foundation stones, subterranean channels, swamp-sunk footings, and underlying ley-line contact points functioned together as one profane instrument. To raze the visible walls alone would have destroyed the shell while leaving the buried function substantially intact. The Volcano spell was selected because it accomplished what siege engines could not: simultaneous destruction of the visible structure, sterilization of the substructure, cauterization of contaminated channels, and geomantic redirection of the underlying currents. The result was not merely demolition, but correction.

---

## I. Chain of Authority and Immediate Circumstance

When I arrived at Harrowmere Enclosure, Dame Cwenhild, Venerated Shield of Heironeous, and the paladins under her command were already present. Conventional siege engines had been brought forward, and the holy company was prepared to raise Harrowmere to the ground by force of arms, stone, fire, and labor.

The objective assigned at that time was the complete destruction of Harrowmere Enclosure.

I did not originate the decision that Harrowmere must be destroyed. That judgment had already been rendered by Dame Cwenhild and the paladins acting in response to the site’s sacrificial and infernal corruption. My intervention concerned the method of destruction, not the object of destruction.

Upon examination of the site and its resonance behavior following the cleansing rite, I determined that conventional demolition would have been ineffective and potentially dangerous, creating the illusion of success while preserving the underlying structure. I therefore advised and performed a more complete arcane solution: the casting of Volcano upon the site.

This action accelerated an operation already underway, but more importantly, it transformed the operation from surface demolition into full geomantic severance.

---

## II. Condition of Harrowmere Enclosure Prior to Destruction

Harrowmere Enclosure had ceased to be a fortress in any ordinary military sense.

Its original function as a swamp outpost had been replaced by a sacrificial architecture of resonance, accumulation, and transmission. The enemy had rebuilt the abandoned ruin not for habitation, defense, or garrisoning, but for ritual amplification. Its apparent walls and chambers were only the visible part of a larger pattern.

The principal corruptions identified were these:

First, the central chamber had been altered into a sacrificial resonance chamber. Its dimensions, materials, and internal alignments were no longer consistent with fortification alone. The chamber served as a receiver and amplifier of suffering, bloodshed, fear, oath-breaking, desecration, or other profane emissions.

Second, the foundations had been made participatory. The stones below the chamber were not inert supports. They had been incorporated into the ritual geometry, likely through repeated sacrificial use, etched bindings, burial of conduits, and contamination of the water table beneath the enclosure.

Third, Harrowmere’s swamp environment had been exploited rather than endured. The saturation of the surrounding ground provided a medium for diffusion. The mire, peat, buried roots, stagnant pools, and old watercourses allowed corrupt resonance to spread laterally in ways that dry stone would not. The swamp held memory, fluidity, and concealment.

Fourth, the site had been tied into a broader lattice of unholy places. Harrowmere did not merely generate corruption locally. It received, shaped, stored, and transmitted force through sympathetic contact with other profaned sites. It was one node in a network.

Fifth, the lattice connection was not solely architectural. It was geomantic. The enemy had discovered, widened, or wounded a point of contact with the natural magical currents of the region. This contact did not make the ley lines evil in themselves. Rather, it imposed a corrupt pattern upon their local expression, much as poison may foul a river without making water evil by nature.

This distinction is essential. The danger at Harrowmere was not only that evil rites had been committed there. The danger was that those rites had impressed a repeating pattern into the land’s magical circulation.

---

## III. Why Conventional Demolition Was Insufficient

The siege equipment present at Harrowmere would likely have succeeded in breaking walls, towers, gates, rooflines, and chamber vaults. It would not have guaranteed the destruction of the ritual engine.

Indeed, there was substantial risk that conventional demolition would have made the matter worse.

A sacrificial resonance chamber does not depend upon architectural wholeness in the same way a barracks, bridge, or curtain wall does. If the visible chamber is shattered but its buried foundation geometry remains intact, the site may continue to function in a diminished, distorted, or intermittent fashion. Such damaged nodes are in some respects more dangerous, for their behavior becomes less predictable while remaining occultly active.

The following hazards would have remained after ordinary razing:

The substructure beneath the chamber could have persisted as a buried profane diagram.

The ley-contact points could have remained open.

Blood-soaked, ritually prepared foundation stones could have been scattered into the swamp, spreading contamination.

Broken masonry could have become a field of lesser anchors rather than one central anchor.

The enemy could have returned to reactivate the site by rebuilding over the same foundation.

Residual resonance could have continued to pulse through the lattice, giving the enemy notice that the visible fortress was gone but the deeper instrument survived.

Most seriously, conventional demolition would have announced victory while leaving behind the very thing that made Harrowmere strategically significant.

For a mundane fortress, to topple the walls is to defeat the structure. For a profane resonance engine, to topple the walls may merely remove the roof from the altar.

---

## IV. Nature of the Volcano Working

The Volcano spell was selected not for spectacle, nor for haste alone, but for correspondence between the nature of the corruption and the nature of the remedy.

Harrowmere’s corruption extended above and below. Therefore the remedy also had to act above and below.

The visible fortress required annihilation. The foundations required melting, burial, displacement, or structural erasure. The contaminated channels required cauterization. The local geomantic pattern required redirection. The swamp-sunk ritual memory required thermal, mineral, and elemental disruption. These requirements could not be met by catapult, ram, pick, fire-pot, or sanctified hammer alone.

Volcanic force is not alien to the world. Magma, pressure, venting, ash, basalt, obsidian, and caldera formation are natural geological functions. They are violent, but not profane. They do not destroy ley lines as a demon tears flesh or as necromancy mocks life. Rather, they reshape terrain, alter subterranean pressure, redirect mineral channels, and impose new structure upon old flow.

This is why Volcano was uniquely suited to Harrowmere.

The working did four things simultaneously:

First, it consumed the above-ground fortress in totality. The sacrificial chamber, walls, rebuilt towers, ritual flooring, and prepared masonry were not merely knocked apart. They were swallowed, burned, fractured, and incorporated into the new geological formation.

Second, it destroyed the integrity of the buried ritual architecture. A resonance chamber depends upon relationship: angle to angle, stone to stone, depth to depth, chamber to foundation, foundation to channel. Magmatic upheaval breaks these relationships absolutely. Even where matter remains, pattern does not.

Third, it cauterized the corrupted ley-contact points. The comparison to a wound is apt. The enemy had opened and infected a point in the land’s magical circulation. The volcanic working closed that wound by heat, pressure, and mineral transformation. It did not sever the ley lines from the world. It forced their local expression into a new shape.

Fourth, it redirected the lattice. The enemy’s profane network depended upon Harrowmere as a usable node. After the working, there was no chamber, no stable foundation diagram, no preserved altar-depth, and no reliable resonance geometry. The current that had once been forced through Harrowmere now meets altered stone, changed elevation, heat-transformed strata, and broken sympathy. The lattice must therefore abandon the region, route around it, or suffer instability.

This was the central theory guiding my action, and the results observed support it.

---

## V. On Ley Lines, Destruction, and Redirection

Because the matter concerns the realm’s deeper magical health, I wish to address a possible concern directly.

The Volcano working did not destroy the ley lines beneath Harrowmere.

Ley lines are not ropes to be cut, nor pipes to be smashed. They are patterns of magical tendency, movement, sympathy, pressure, and exchange within the living world. They may be blocked, poisoned, redirected, widened, narrowed, harmonized, or wounded. They are shaped by geography, history, mineral composition, water, blood, sanctity, desecration, settlement, oath, and catastrophe.

The enemy’s design at Harrowmere did not create power from nothing. It parasitized an existing local current, using sacrificial resonance to impose an artificial rhythm upon it. That rhythm then connected Harrowmere to a lattice of other unholy sites.

The proper remedy was therefore not to attack the ley line itself. The proper remedy was to remove the parasitic structure and change the conditions that allowed the imposed rhythm to persist.

Volcanic transformation is especially effective in this respect because it rewrites the local conversation between stone, depth, heat, water, and pressure. The old swamp-fortress pattern was one of stagnant water, buried rot, hidden foundations, enclosed sacrifice, and lateral seepage. The new caldera pattern is one of exposed stone, cooled flow, elevated mass, sealed depth, and redirected drainage.

In geomantic terms, Harrowmere has been changed from a cup of foul water into a cauterized scar of stone.

A scar is not the same as a wound. A wound remains open. A scar records injury but prevents further bleeding.

---

## VI. On the Completeness of the Destruction

The destruction of Harrowmere Enclosure may be considered complete in the following senses:

Architecturally, the fortress no longer exists.

Ritually, the sacrificial chamber no longer exists.

Geomantically, the former resonance geometry no longer exists.

Militarily, the site cannot be reoccupied in its prior form.

Occultly, the lattice connection has been severed from its original arrangement.

Strategically, the enemy has lost not only a fortress but a prepared node whose construction likely required years of hidden work, sacrificial investment, and regional preparation.

It is possible that some fragments of stone, metal, bone, or ritual matter remain beneath the cooled formation. It is not my judgment that such remnants constitute a continuing engine. Matter is not enough. Pattern is required. The pattern has been destroyed.

Should His Majesty or the Acting Archmage require further verification, I recommend the following examinations:

A three-circle thaumic survey around the caldera rim, measuring residual resonance at dawn, noon, dusk, and midnight.

Aerial survey by mage-sight or sanctified observer to confirm absence of repeating lattice geometry.

Divine consultation by Heironean clergy to test whether oath, sacrifice, or infernal residue remains active.

Geomantic sounding by earth-wise casters to examine whether the underlying currents now flow around, beneath, or through the new formation without repeating the Harrowmere signature.

Water sampling from surrounding swamp channels over one lunar cycle to determine whether corruption continues to leach outward.

Ward-stake placement at eight compass points to test for sympathetic pull toward other known unholy sites.

I am confident such examinations will support the conclusion of this report.

---

## VII. On Proportionality and Necessity

I understand the concern that Volcano is an extreme measure.

It is.

It should not be used casually, politically, theatrically, or in answer to an ordinary military problem. No loyal servant of the realm should treat the making of a mountain as a convenience.

In this instance, however, the extremity of the measure matched the extremity of the danger.

Harrowmere was not an occupied royal keep serving the Crown. It was an abandoned fortress illegally rebuilt into an unholy engine. It was not merely held by enemies. It had been repurposed into a weapon aimed through the land itself. The continued existence of its buried structure posed a danger beyond the ordinary calculus of walls and banners.

Had Harrowmere been destroyed by mundane means, the realm might have possessed a ruined fortress and a hidden wound. By volcanic destruction, the realm now possesses no fortress, but also no functioning wound.

That exchange was, in my judgment, necessary.

Furthermore, time was a material factor. A conventional razing would have required extended labor, repeated exposure of the paladins and work crews to corrupted ground, handling of ritually contaminated stone, and prolonged opportunity for enemy observation or interference. The Volcano working collapsed that period of danger into a single decisive act.

Speed was beneficial. Completeness was essential.

---

## VIII. On Dame Cwenhild and the Paladins Present

I further record that Dame Cwenhild, Venerated Shield of Heironeous, and the paladins under her command had already committed to the destruction of Harrowmere Enclosure before my use of Volcano.

Their intention was righteous and militarily sound according to the information then practically available. Siege equipment had been prepared. The site was to be raised to the ground. The holy company understood that Harrowmere could not be permitted to stand.

My contribution was the determination that raising the structure to the ground was not sufficient, because the ground itself had been made part of the structure.

Thus, if fault is to be examined, let it be examined accurately. The paladins ordered and prepared the destruction of a profaned enemy site. I selected the arcane means by which that destruction could be made complete.

I accept responsibility for that arcane judgment.

I also maintain that the judgment was correct.

---

## IX. Strategic Consequence for the Enemy

The enemy’s loss is greater than the loss of Harrowmere’s walls.

They have lost a functioning sacrificial node.

They have lost a prepared resonance chamber.

They have lost a swamp-hidden anchor point.

They have lost local access to a shaped ley contact.

They have lost whatever stored profane investment had accumulated within the chamber and its foundations.

They have lost the ability to quickly reclaim the site by repairing a ruin.

They must now either abandon this region of the lattice, construct a new node elsewhere at great expense and risk, or attempt to route power around a geomantically altered zone that no longer answers to the old pattern.

The new volcanic formation is hostile to their previous design. It is not merely empty. It is contradictory. The old Harrowmere pattern required concealment, saturation, enclosure, and buried resonance. The new caldera presents exposure, elevation, cooled stone, and sealed depth.

This is why I state that the enemy has been forced to abandon the region in practical lattice terms. They may send scouts. They may conduct tests. They may attempt blasphemous repair. But they cannot simply resume use of Harrowmere. Harrowmere, as an occult fact, no longer exists.

---

## X. On Royal Authority and Future Procedure

None of the above is meant to diminish His Majesty’s rightful concern.

A castle within the realm has been destroyed. The Crown has an interest in every fortification, ruin, road, bridge, mine, watchtower, and outpost within its lands, even those abandoned or profaned. The fact that Harrowmere had been corrupted does not erase the Crown’s authority over the land where it stood.

I therefore recommend that future operations against corrupted royal holdings be governed by a threefold procedure where circumstances permit:

First, immediate military or holy authority may contain, cleanse, and prevent enemy use.

Second, royal notice should be sent as soon as the site is identified as formerly belonging to the realm.

Third, if destruction beyond ordinary demolition is contemplated, an appointed arcane officer should submit an emergency finding explaining whether the danger is architectural, spiritual, geomantic, infernal, necromantic, or lattice-bound.

However, I must also state that there will be cases in which delay preserves the enemy’s advantage. Harrowmere was such a case. Once the site’s deeper function was understood, incomplete demolition would have been an error, and delayed action would have risked recontamination, interference, or withdrawal of the enemy’s more subtle traces before they could be burned out.

The law should guide the hand. It should not require the hand to leave poison in the wound.

---

## XI. Final Finding

It is my formal conclusion that the destruction of Harrowmere Enclosure by Volcano was arcane, military, and geomantic necessity.

The act was severe, but proportional.

It was destructive, but corrective.

It removed a fortress, but saved the land beneath it from continued profane use.

It did not destroy the realm’s ley lines, but reshaped and redirected a wounded local expression of them.

It did not merely deny the enemy a holding, but broke a lattice node and forced abandonment of the region as an occult conduit.

It was not undertaken in contempt of royal authority, but in service to the realm’s survival, under circumstances where the site’s visible identity as a castle had become secondary to its hidden function as an unholy engine.

I remain ready to submit to examination by the Crown, the Acting Archmage, or any lawful commission His Majesty appoints. I further stand prepared to assist in verification, mapping, warding, and future restoration of the site.

Submitted with respect and loyalty,

**Norwin Stoneyeye**  
Royal Archivist and Sage of the Realm

# Postscript: Concerning the Present and Future Use of the Harrowmere Caldera

While preparing this report and conferring with Vice Admiral Thalrik, I have also considered the future utility of the new landscape where Harrowmere Enclosure once stood.

The site is no longer a swamp fortress. It is now a dormant caldera surrounded by swamp lands, with a broad rise of newly formed volcanic stone creating a large, low mountain in an otherwise difficult region. Though born of necessity, this alteration offers the Crown a rare strategic opportunity.

The cleared and elevated ground would make an excellent site for airship docking, repair, refit, and resupply.

The advantages are considerable.

First, elevation provides visibility above the surrounding marsh. Watch posts, signal towers, beacon fires, and mage-lanterns placed along the caldera rim would command lines of sight that Harrowmere never possessed as a low swamp fortification.

Second, the surrounding swamp remains a natural deterrent against conventional enemy advance. Infantry, cavalry, siege engines, wagons, and heavy supply trains would all be slowed, channeled, or exposed by the mire. A hostile army would find the site difficult to approach in strength, while airships could reach it directly.

Third, the cooled volcanic stone offers firmer foundations than the former swamp ground. With proper engineering, the caldera rim and slopes could support docking pylons, anchor towers, crane works, warehouses, fuel stores, lift-gas chambers, repair gantries, and hardened magazines.

Fourth, the site’s isolation is a defensive asset. An airship station requires space, guarded approaches, and distance from crowded civilian districts. Harrowmere Caldera offers all three.

Fifth, the new terrain permits separation of functions. The upper rim may serve docking and signal purposes. The inner caldera may hold protected workshops, dry storage, and repair yards. The outer slopes may be terraced for barracks, cisterns, foundries, machine sheds, or artillery platforms. The surrounding swamp approaches may be controlled by causeways, watch-houses, and retractable bridges.

Sixth, the symbolic value should not be ignored. A place once used as a sacrificial engine by the enemy may be reconstituted as a royal airship yard, transforming a wound in the realm into an instrument of defense, mobility, and industry.

I therefore recommend that Harrowmere Caldera be surveyed not merely as a destroyed site, but as a potential military-industrial station under Crown authority. With appropriate warding, drainage, stone-shaping, consecration, and engineering, it may become a secure airship harbor and manufacturing ground: difficult to besiege from below, accessible from above, and useful in projecting royal power across regions where roads are poor, rivers unreliable, or enemy movement must be answered quickly.

Such development would also answer the political concern created by Harrowmere’s destruction. The Crown would not merely have lost an abandoned fortress. It would have gained a defensible elevated airship station in a region where no such station previously existed.

Respectfully appended,

**Norwin Stoneyeye**
