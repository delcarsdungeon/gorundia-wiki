# Plain Notice of the Charter of Bard's Gate

**Posted by order of Wonderful, Lord Mayor of Bard's Gate**

Hear this and understand it.

Bard's Gate is a Free City under the Crown.

That means the city has rights.

It does not mean the city is above the King.

---

## What You Keep

You may trade, work, worship, sing, drink, swear, sleep, argue, and earn your bread under the law.

Guilds may keep their trades.

Temples may keep their altars.

Citizens may petition the city and the Crown.

The Watch and sworn men may keep the streets.

If you live peaceably in Bard's Gate for **one year and one day**, and no lawful writ names you, no outside lord may drag you away like property.

---

## What You Do Not Get

The Charter does not protect traitors.

It does not protect spies.

It does not protect foreign agents.

It does not protect forbidden sorcery.

It does not protect murderers, oath-breakers, or fools hiding behind false names.

If royal writ names you, the city walls will not save you.

---

## On Soldiers in the City

No lord may march men into Bard's Gate just because he feels important.

But if the Crown declares unrest, war, treason, invasion, or danger to the realm, royal soldiers may enter, hold gates, guard walls, and keep the King's Peace.

That is not an invasion.

That is the Crown doing its duty.

Try not to make it necessary.

---

## On Magic and Holy Power

If you wield serious magic, divine power, curses, bindings, summoned things, or other dangerous workings, you will declare it when lawfully required.

If the Crown, the Lord Mayor, or the Triad calls you to answer, you will answer.

Lawful magic is not forbidden.

Hidden danger is.

---

## On Reeves and Sworn Men

Reeves keep order in the wards.

Sworn men serve the ward, the city, and the Crown.

When called lawfully, they may guard streets, carry writs, collect fines, stop riots, fight fires, aid arrests, and defend Bard's Gate.

Refusing lawful call may cost you coin, office, freedom, or worse.

---

## The Short Version

Keep the peace.

Pay what is owed.

Do not hide treason behind city law.

Do not hide sorcery behind city law.

Do not hide foreign coin behind city law.

Do not mistake mercy for blindness.

Bard's Gate protects its people.

It does not shelter those who would burn it.

---

By order of the Lord Mayor,

**Wonderful**  
Lord Mayor of Bard's Gate

Witnessed by the Mayor's Bench, the Reeves of Bard's Gate, and officers of the Crown.
