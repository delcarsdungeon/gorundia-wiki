# Corrected Charter of Bard's Gate

**Posted by order of Wonderful, Lord Mayor of Bard's Gate, under the authority of the Crown and the Charter.**

Let it be known to all citizens, guilds, reeves, sworn men, clergy, merchants, visitors, petitioners, troublemakers, and persons who believe themselves cleverer than the law:

The Charter of Bard's Gate remains in force.

The Royal Addendum issued by King Rimmand II is likewise in force.

For the avoidance of confusion, abuse, selective reading, and other civic diseases, the Charter is here presented in corrected and consolidated form, with the Crown's clarifications set into their proper articles.

This posting does not create new law.

It states the law as it now stands.

---

## Article I: Sovereign Status

Bard's Gate is declared a Free City within the realm of Gorundia.

It shall govern itself under the supervision of its Lord Mayor, who shall hold office by appointment of the Crown and by oath to the Charter.

The city shall maintain its own laws, magistrates, council, courts, wards, guild customs, and civic officers, save for those matters reserved to the Crown.

The liberties of Bard's Gate are liberties under the Crown, not liberties against the Crown.

---

## Article II: Rights of the People

The citizens of Bard's Gate are granted the following rights, in perpetuity:

- The right to trade freely within the city and across the realm without undue taxation or obstruction.
- The right to self-defense and the maintenance of a city guard and militia.
- The right to establish and operate guilds, academies, and places of worship without unlawful interference.
- The right to petition the Crown through the city's council.
- The right to judgment under city law, except where royal writ, treason, foreign agency, forbidden arts, or threat to the realm removes the matter to higher authority.

No lawful trade, worship, craft, or learning is forbidden by this Charter.

No liberty granted by this Charter shall be used as cover for treason, conspiracy, forbidden sorcery, foreign intrigue, or violence against the peace of the realm.

---

## Article III: Obligations to the Crown

As a Free City, Bard's Gate shall owe the Crown loyalty and tribute, to be rendered in coin or kind, equal to a tenth part of its yearly revenues.

The city shall also provide soldiers, provisions, transport, intelligence, healing, or other lawful aid in times of war, unrest, insurrection, invasion, plague, or threat to the stability of the realm, at the Crown's request.

No guild, ward, college, temple, or private house may obstruct lawful service owed to the Crown under this article.

---

## Article IV: Historical Rights

In recognition of Bard's Gate's role in the history of the realm as a haven for knowledge, art, and trade, the following privileges are enshrined:

- The city shall maintain its archives, bardic colleges, and libraries as neutral ground, protected even in times of war.
- The Bardic Guild shall hold the honorary title of Heralds of the Realm and shall be granted safe passage across all lands of the kingdom.
- The festival of the Bard's Covenant, marking the city's founding, shall be observed throughout the realm with the Crown's blessing.

These protections shall not shelter records, relics, songs, spells, ledgers, correspondence, or persons proven to serve treason, foreign powers, forbidden arts, or designs against the realm.

---

## Article V: Protection and Sovereignty

No lord or knight of the realm may encroach upon the sovereignty of Bard's Gate, nor station forces within its walls without lawful consent.

For ordinary purposes, such consent rests with the Lord Mayor and council.

In times of declared unrest, insurrection, invasion, threat to the stability of the realm, or danger to the King's Peace, the Crown reserves the right to:

- Station forces within Bard's Gate
- Secure gates, walls, towers, bridges, docks, and key districts
- Assume command of defensive works
- Act in preservation of the King's Peace

Such presence shall be considered protective, not intrusive, and shall not be construed as violation of this Charter.

Any lord, knight, captain, guild, ward, or private company acting without lawful authority under this article shall be deemed in violation of the Charter and answerable to the Crown.

---

## Article VI: Year and a Day

Any free person dwelling peaceably within Bard's Gate for one year and one day, owing no proven debt of bondage, lawful sentence, or active writ of seizure, shall be recognized as a free citizen of the city.

No outside lord, master, creditor, or claimant may seize such a person within the walls without lawful judgment before the city's court or writ of the Crown.

The protections of Year and a Day shall not extend to:

- Those named by royal writ as traitors to the Crown
- Agents acting on behalf of foreign powers in secret or deception
- Practitioners of forbidden arts deemed a threat to the realm
- Persons fleeing lawful sentence for murder, treason, heresy, sorcery, or like grave crimes
- Persons acting under false name, false oath, or concealed allegiance for hostile purpose

Such persons may be seized within the city by lawful authority of the Crown, notwithstanding prior protections.

---

## Article VII: Arcane and Divine Oversight

All persons practicing significant arcane or divine arts within Bard's Gate must:

- Declare their practice to the appropriate civic or royal authority
- Submit to inquiry when called upon by the Crown, the Lord Mayor, or the Triad of the Light
- Refrain from concealment of powers, pacts, bindings, summoned servants, curses, forbidden relics, or dangerous workings

Failure to do so may be treated as concealment of dangerous power and subject to investigation, restraint, sanction, or removal from the city.

No lawful practice is forbidden by this article.

None shall be hidden from the light of judgment.

---

## Article VIII: Sworn Men and Civic Defense

The institution of Sworn Men, as recognized within the wards of Bard's Gate, is hereby affirmed as lawful service to both City and Crown.

Sworn Men may be:

- Called to service by their reeves
- Organized in defense of their wards
- Bound to uphold the King's Peace alongside the Watch
- Used to carry writs, collect lawful fines, guard streets, aid arrests, respond to fire, suppress riot, and answer levy in time of crisis

In times of crisis, their duty extends beyond ward and street, as necessity demands.

A sworn man who refuses lawful call may be fined, stripped of office, imprisoned, declared oath-broken, or brought before the Mayor's Bench.

---

## Article IX: Reeves and Ward Authority

The reeves of Bard's Gate shall keep the King's Peace within their wards.

They may command sworn men, direct ward watches, maintain rolls, enforce lesser judgments, collect lawful dues, and report disorder to the Lord Mayor.

No reeve may claim authority independent of the Charter, the Lord Mayor, or the Crown.

No reeve may use ward authority to shelter treason, foreign intrigue, forbidden arts, unlawful guild violence, or private armies.

In time of unrest, a reeve's duty is first to the peace of the city and the safety of the realm.

---

## Article X: Interpretation and Authority

Where conflict arises between the liberties of the Charter and the safety of the realm:

- The preservation of the realm shall take precedence.
- Interpretation shall rest with the Crown, the Lord Mayor, and the united authority of the Triad.
- No clause of freedom shall be used to shield that which would bring ruin upon the whole.

Disputes of ordinary civic law remain with the city's courts, reeves, magistrates, and Mayor's Bench.

Disputes touching treason, divine threat, forbidden arts, foreign agency, or the safety of the realm may be removed to royal or sacred authority.

---

## Seal and Posting

This corrected Charter is posted for public knowledge by order of Wonderful, Lord Mayor of Bard's Gate.

Let no citizen claim ignorance where the words have been nailed to gate, hall, market, and court.

Let no guild plead confusion.

Let no noble pretend surprise.

Let no traitor mistake mercy for blindness.

By order of the Lord Mayor, under the Crown and Charter,

**Wonderful**  
Lord Mayor of Bard's Gate

Witnessed by the Mayor's Bench, the Reeves of Bard's Gate, and the officers of the Crown.
