Writ of Extraordinary Inventory and Provisional Seizure

Issued Under the Authority of the Lord Marshal of Gorundia

By the Hand and Seal of
Sir Beringer Amadis,
Lord Marshal of the Realm,
Warden of the King’s Peace,
Bearer of the Marshal’s Chain,

Whereas

The Crown has vested in the Office of the Lord Marshal broad authority to secure, inspect, and preserve arms, relics, and properties reasonably believed to bear upon matters of treason, murder, unlawful warfare, or threats to the peace of the realm;

The death of Theron Sylvanranth constitutes a matter of exceptional gravity, involving military command, noble lineage, and the disposition of personal arms of historic and strategic significance;

It has been credibly attested that certain properties, chattels, and weapons presently held within the domicile and estates of Baron Cron may include items formerly borne by the said Theron Sylvanranth, without lawful transfer, writ, or surrender to the Crown;

Therefore Be It Ordered
I. Authority of Entry

That officers acting in the name of the Lord Marshal shall enter, inspect, and inventory the said properties without need of invitation, consent, or presence of Baron Cron or his retainers.

Resistance to this writ shall constitute Obstruction of the King’s Peace, to be answered immediately.

II. Scope of Inventory

The inspection shall include, but not be limited to:

All weapons, armor, banners, heirlooms, trophies, or curios of martial origin;

Any blade, scabbard, pommel, or fitting reasonably matching descriptions of Theron Sylvanranth’s personal arms;

Any records, ledgers, correspondence, or trophies pertaining to the acquisition thereof.

III. Custodianship

All inventoried items shall be cataloged, valued, and—where deemed prudent—placed under provisional custody of the Office of the Lord Marshal for safekeeping.

Custody shall not constitute forfeiture (yet), but preservation pending review.

IV. Appointed Officers

The following are hereby named as lawful agents of this action:

Norwin Stoney Eye, Royal Archivist, charged with catalog, valuation, and record;

Guaco, Reeve in good standing, charged with witness, tally, and civic oversight.

Their word and mark shall stand as the Crown’s own for the duration of this inspection.

V. Costs and Compensation

All costs incurred in the execution of this writ—including labor, valuation, storage, transport, and clerical time—shall be assessed against the estate of Baron Cron.

An itemized bill shall be rendered upon completion.

Failure to remit payment shall be treated as debt to the Crown, enforceable by levy, lien, or further seizure.

Final Clause

This writ shall remain in force until satisfied or revoked by the Lord Marshal himself, the King’s Bench, or writ of His Majesty.

Given this day, under seal and chain,
Sir Beringer Amadis
Lord Marshal of Gorundia
