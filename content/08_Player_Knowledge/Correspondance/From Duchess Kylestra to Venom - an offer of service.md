**[SCENE: The Griffon’s Roost – the fire’s low, the hour late, and the conversation quietly tense. A knight approaches Sir Beringer.]**

**Sir [[Thelan Garet]]:** 

>“Sir Beringer, good eve. Thelan Garet, knight in service to House Ravenster. May I join you a moment?”

*(He waits for the nod, then eases into the seat with the polite discomfort of a man who knows this was_never* just a delivery job.)*

> “I was sent with a letter—now read and respectfully declined—by Dame Venom. It was written by none other than **Kylestra Ravenster** herself. The seal was hers, and it was Venom’s right to break it first. I don’t mind your seeing it. You’re a knight of honor—and, well, I suspect it concerns you more than it lets on.”

 _(He produces the folded letter, carefully resealed with a ribbon and laid flat like a peace offering.)_

> “Truth be told, I was honored to meet her. More than I expected to be. She’s... well, she’s not like the rest of us. Over a hundred years of doing good without banners or song? No gilded titles, no campaign for fame. Just—doing it. Because it needs to be done. That sort of knight, the kind our oaths _wish_ they made? That’s legend, Sir Beringer.

> And yet... she turned it down. Quickly. Cleanly.

> And listen, I’m not trying to stir pots. I just deliver letters and follow orders. This? This is above my pay grade.”

_(He raises his palms with a wry shrug and glances toward the hearth.)_

> “But between you and me? I think House Ravenster misread her. She’s not after land. Not after power. And _definitely_ not interested in running away.”

_(A pause.)_

> “Still… I was glad to meet her. Even if she made me feel like I’ve spent my knighthood polishing the wrong kind of steel.”

### The Letter – elegantly penned, formal but personal. Sealed with the crest of House Ravenster.

> [!note] 
> 
> _To Dame Venom, of the Black Hall,_
> You have walked the width of the kingdom, and in your shadow, others have found safety, clarity, even hope. It is time your service be _formally recognized_, and your legacy _institutionalized_.
> 
> I offer you command of the **Eastern Wealdmarch**, along with the founding charter and authority to establish your own **Knightly Order**—one that may outlast us all. Its laws, its banner, its code—_yours to write_. The Duchy would provide land, stipend, oaths of fealty, and full autonomy beneath the Borderwatch’s aegis.
> 
> In a generation, such a post could become its own chapterhouse. In two, a tradition. We both know the kind of strength that can come from structure—for those who never had one.
> 
> The offer is genuine. The need is real.
> 
> If accepted, the Ravenster Council will dispatch your initial cohort within the fortnight.
> 
> —In duty and in honor, 
> **Kylestra Ravenster** 
> _Ducal Marshal of the Borderwatch_

---

###  **Venom’s Reply – direct, gracious, unbending.**

> [!note] To the Lady Kylestra,
> 
> The offer is as noble as its source. And the honor you extend would tempt any knight with sense enough to dream of legacy.
> 
> But I am not finished here.
> 
> What I serve is not yet complete—and I am not yet finished serving it.
> 
> With full respect and gratitude, —
> 
> Venom, of the Black Hall
