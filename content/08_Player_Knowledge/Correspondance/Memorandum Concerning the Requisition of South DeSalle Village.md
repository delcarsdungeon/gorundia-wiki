# Memorandum Concerning the Requisition of South DeSalle Village

_To the Lord Marshal, Sir Beringer Amadis, Keeper of the Ninth Seal_

My lord,

Upon learning that your host had passed through South DeSalle Village and been received there under my house’s name, I rode to the settlement with armed men and placed it under guard.

I regret that this was not done before your arrival.

The village is not, in any honest sense, a village. It is a staged civic display maintained for presentation, hospitality, and the appearance of prosperity. I knew the place as a child and found it charming then. Later, with better judgment, I found it false. Children I knew there would vanish between visits for small failures of conduct or presentation, and when I asked questions, I was given answers meant to end inquiry rather than satisfy it.

I now have authority to ask better questions.

Mayor Bellweather has been removed from effective command. The Unity Tavern has been sealed. The civic books, tavern records, household rosters, costume stores, food accounts, visitor lists, and village assignment records are under guard. No resident, assigned laborer, handler, servant, or official is to be moved from the settlement without written order.

I propose that South DeSalle no longer bear my house’s name in formal military use.

I propose the site be redesignated:

**Crownward Station**

Its false construction makes it useful. The cottages are oversized. The tavern is large. The lanes are clean. The approaches are controlled. The stores are sufficient for more people than honestly live there. It is close enough to support movement toward Blackbar and the Gravendar frontier, yet removed enough from the road to serve as an ordered rear station.

I therefore request your authority to convert the site into a Crownward staging area in support of the advance.

Initial uses may include:

- supply collection and distribution;    
- temporary billets for detached troops, clerks, messengers, and wounded;    
- wagon repair and remount organization;    
- controlled storage of seized goods and records;    
- witness registration from the surrounding feeder villages;    
- guarded holding for officials or persons under inquiry;    
- and, if necessary, a temporary military camp or prisoner station.    

I do not yet know the full measure of what South DeSalle concealed or whom it served. That is precisely why it must be held, not merely passed through.

Should further inquiry at Blackbar reveal injured persons, unlawful labor, coerced service, or displaced dependents requiring protection, I submit that Crownward Station may also be adapted for relief and rehabilitation. If that proves necessary, I would ask that such persons be kept apart from prisoners and accused officials wherever possible. A useful site should not become another place where the harmed are made to live beside those who harmed them.

For now, my purpose is plain.

South DeSalle was built to stage obedience.

Crownward Station will serve the Crown.

By my hand,

**Baron DeSalle**
