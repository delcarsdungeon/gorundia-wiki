# FIELD RETURN — STRENGTH, LOSSES, AND PRESENT CONDITION

**To:** Lord Marshal Beringer Amadis  
**From:** Dame Venom  
**Subject:** Strength and casualty return following conclusion of siege operations in Sunhollow  
**Classification:** Field Command  
**Status:** Preliminary pending final prisoner and hospital rolls

Lord Marshal,

The following is the strength and casualty return for the forces under my immediate command during the Sunhollow siege.

The forces of **Baron Valdrik Terevon** are not included herein. The Baron retained command of the investment upon his own lands. My command operated in concert with his force, principally as mobile reserve, engineering support, reconnaissance, and ultimately the assault element employed at the breach.

The distinction is worth preserving. Baron Terevon conducted his siege competently and does not require me to steal credit from him after the fact.

# XI. COMMAND ASSESSMENT

The gnomish detachment justified its attachment.

The Crabs are useful field weapons and sufficiently reliable for continued service.

The heavy crossbow detachment performed particularly well against protected and heavily armored defenders.

The Badger accomplished the task for which it was brought.

Its defects remain numerous.

Its maintenance burden remains excessive.

Its mobility remains poor.

Its crew requirements remain absurd.

None of those observations alter the result.

A defended position that would otherwise have required continued investment or a costly surface assault was penetrated at a point of our choosing.

I therefore recommend the Badger program continue.

I further recommend that any future machine be accompanied by:

1. a permanent trained gnomish operating and repair establishment;
    
2. experienced conventional sappers;
    
3. infantry specifically trained to exploit the breach immediately after penetration;
    
4. an officer with sufficient authority to prevent enthusiastic engineers from conducting experiments during an assault.
    

I recommend **Master Sapper Dorrik Slatemantle** for continued Crown employment should he consent to it.

I recommend favorable notice for **Major Brasswhistle**, **Senior Mechanist Tarrow-Gauge**, and **Signalist Nix**.

I have no recommendation regarding **Colonel Aelric Vanner**.

The Army has apparently failed to get rid of him for decades.

I see no reason to begin trying now.
