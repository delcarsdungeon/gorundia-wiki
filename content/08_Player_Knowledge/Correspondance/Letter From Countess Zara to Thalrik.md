# Zara’s Note to [[Thalrik Vaultforge]]

*Recovered from the private quarters of [[Zara Vallytrex]] aboard her vessel.*

---

> [!note]
> Thalrik,
> 
> I find I prefer writing when I am… *obliged* to be careful.
> 
> It encourages clarity.
> 
> You amused me, at first.
> 
> That is not an insult. Very few people manage even that. You were loud where others whispered, bold where others calculated, and just irreverent enough to make serious men uncomfortable.
> 
> I assumed you were like the rest.
> 
> I was wrong.
> 
> I began to watch more closely.
> 
> You take.
> 
> Openly. Efficiently. Without apology.
> 
> And then—almost immediately—you give.
> 
> Not as performance. Not as investment. Not as penance.
> 
> As instinct.
> 
> That is… rare.
> 
> Most men who gather wealth do so to become larger in their own reflection. You seem intent on making others harder to break.
> 
> A peculiar vice.
> 
> You keep strange company for a man of your reputation.
> 
> A priestess who burns herself away to warm others.  
> A gutter-child you sharpened into something dangerous—and, more importantly, loyal.  
> Halfbloods you treat as people rather than problems, despite every reason your blood and upbringing might suggest otherwise.
> 
> You do not surround yourself with mirrors.
> 
> You surround yourself with… weight.
> 
> That tells me more about you than any ledger ever could.
> 
> You are not a rogue, Thalrik.
> 
> You are what happens when a crusader refuses to kneel long enough to be named one.
> 
> The law would call you inconvenient.
> 
> Those of us who are accustomed to moving pieces rather than being moved… tend to find you **useful**.
> 
> ---
> 
> I, on the other hand, keep less admirable company.
> 
> That is not entirely by preference.
> 
> There are… expectations. Agreements. Structures of loyalty that bind more tightly than they appear when spoken aloud.
> 
> You may judge me for that.
> 
> You would not be wrong to do so.
> 
> Wealth and power do not change a person. They remove the need to pretend otherwise.
> 
> Make of that what you will.
> 
> ---
> 
> You would do well to ensure your ship is prepared for immediate departure.
> 
> Fully provisioned. Quietly.
> 
> There are currents moving that will not announce themselves until they break against something solid. When they do, they will do so quickly.
> 
> If you are in a position to take flight, you may find yourself uniquely suited to intervene… where others cannot reach in time.
> 
> Call it instinct.
> 
> Or experience.
> 
> ---
> 
> I will not insult you by pretending coincidence governs the present moment.
> 
> Nor will I pretend I am free to act as directly as I might prefer.
> 
> But I will say this:
> 
> I have seen men in your position before.
> 
> They usually die.
> 
> You… might not.
> 
> ---
> 
> **Postscript**
> 
> I have made arrangements.
> 
> Information has a way of continuing its journey, even when the hand that started it is required to remain still.
> 
> Should matters become… less stable… you may hear from individuals not burdened by the same expectations I currently labor under.
> 
> They will be known to you by their actions.
> 
> Not their introductions.
> 
> For what it is worth—
> 
> I find myself hoping you succeed.
> 
> Do try not to disappoint me.
> 
> —Z

The handwriting in the postscript seems strained, not rushed, but as if there was a strong PHYSICAL EFFORT to get it on paper.
