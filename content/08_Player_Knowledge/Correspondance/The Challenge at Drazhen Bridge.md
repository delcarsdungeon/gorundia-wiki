# Field Report — The Challenge at Drazhen Bridge

**Date:** 20th of Coldeven, 1231 OR  
**Classification:** Field Report—Karsk Approaches

**To:** His Grace Vladek Orenic, Duke of Royonad  
**Copy under ducal seal:** Sir Beringer Amadis, Lord Marshal of Gorundia  
**From:** Sir Yaros Veln, Captain of the Lower Karsk Gate

**Subject:** Challenge Made Under the Shield Mother

FIELD REPORT—KARSK APPROACHES  
THIRD DAY OF THE DOMINION INCURSION

To His Grace Vladek Orenic, Duke of Royonad  
Copy under ducal seal to Sir Beringer Amadis, Lord Marshal of Gorundia

From Colonel Yaros Veln, commanding the Lower Karsk defenses

Subject: CHALLENGE MADE UNDER THE SHIELD MOTHER

Your Grace,

At second bell yesterday, a Dominion herald approached the Drazhen Bridge beneath a gray-and-white banner bearing the face and shield of Stern Alia.

The banner was not false.

Mother Halena examined the herald, his seals, and the accompanying challenge. She confirms that the rites were properly observed and that the Dominion priest who witnessed them possessed the Lady’s favor.

The enemy proposed the Old Mother’s Measure.

If their champion prevailed, we would abandon the bridge redoubt by sunset. We would retain our arms and remove our wounded and householders without interference. The enemy would not cross the bridge until first light.

If our champion prevailed, the Dominion would withdraw its engines beyond the sixth marker and neither bombard nor advance for one full day.

The village behind the bridge had not yet been emptied.

I accepted.

The Dominion champion named himself Shield-Captain Arkady Surn, sworn paladin of Stern Alia and keeper of a Shieldhouse in the Khorovišk Valley. He wore Dominion armor but carried no sign of Hextor. His shield bore the Mother’s face.

Dame Danica Veles answered for Royonad.

Before the duel, both champions approached the same field shrine.

Surn prayed first.

He asked the Mother to preserve the law behind him, strengthen his arm, and make him worthy of those who slept beneath his protection.

Dame Danica gave the same prayer.

The words differed in three places.

The meaning did not.

Surn then asked her:

“Whom do you hold?”

She answered:

“All behind me.”

She asked him the same.

His answer was:

“All behind me.”

They saluted.

Then they attempted to beat one another to death.

Surn fought with a mace and tall shield. He was disciplined, powerful, and entirely without flourish. Dame Danica yielded ground through the first six exchanges and allowed him to believe her left leg was failing.

On the seventh, she caught his mace against her shield rim, turned beneath his arm, and broke his knee.

Surn continued from the ground.

She struck the mace from his hand and placed her sword beneath his jaw.

He yielded.

Dame Danica asked whether his oath would hold.

Surn replied:

“My defeat does not release my word.”

She removed her sword and allowed his people to carry him away.

The Dominion officers did not contest the judgment. Their Hextorian commissar was visibly displeased but made no interference. Surn’s priestess ordered the sixth marker reset under witness, and the enemy engines withdrew before noon.

They kept the Measure exactly.

We used the day to empty Drazhen village, remove its grain, strengthen the bridge works, and bring two additional companies into the lower defenses.

At first light this morning, Shield-Captain Surn was carried to the Dominion line on a litter so that he could personally witness the expiration of the truce.

He raised his shield.

Their artillery commenced immediately.

I advise against mistaking any part of this event for friendship.

The Dominion paladin fought to take our road. Had he won, he would have taken it. Dame Danica spared him because judgment had been reached, not because his cause was just.

Nevertheless, the old rites remain recognized.

The Dominion’s Alian clergy possess true power.

Their Hextorian officers do not command them as completely as we believed.

And when they swear before the Mother, they can be made to keep their word.

This may matter before the war is done.

Karsk Pass holds.

Signed beneath field seal,

SIR YAROS VELN 
Captain of the Lower Karsk Gate
Army of Royonad
