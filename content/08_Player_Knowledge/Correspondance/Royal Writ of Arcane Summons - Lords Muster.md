Royal Writ of Arcane Summons

By the Grace of the Crown and under the Seal of His Majesty,
Rimmand II, King of Gorundia, Defender of the Realm

Let it be known to all who bear talent in the Arcane Arts:

In light of present tensions and in preservation of the King’s Peace, the Crown hereby calls upon all mages of recognized training, proven aptitude, or demonstrated arcane capacity within the realm to attend the Lord’s Muster in service to the defense of the Crown and the assembled peers.

This summons extends to:

Court wizards and sworn battlemages

Licensed guild arcanists

Hedge practitioners of notable reputation

Scholars of recognized magical discipline

Archivists entrusted with arcane custody

And any individual known to possess talent sufficient to shape, dispel, or detect magical influence

Attendance is not optional.

Each summoned mage shall present themselves at the appointed hour to the Marshal of the Muster or his designated adjutant, there to be assigned a defensive post within the hall and its immediate environs.

Their duties shall include, but are not limited to:

The detection of hostile enchantment

The reinforcement of wards and countermeasures

The dispelling of illicit workings

The identification of arcane interference

The preservation of evidence in the event of magical breach

Failure to answer this writ shall be recorded as neglect of duty to the Crown and may be interpreted as willful obstruction of the King’s Peace.

Let no man or woman of talent claim ignorance.

Let no mage presume themselves above the call.

By writ and seal this day, under the authority of the Crown.

Rimmand II
King of Gorundia

Countersigned:
The Lord Marshal of the Realm
