# Letter from Lady Milena Amadis to Dame Rykeena

To Dame Rykeena,

I hope this letter finds you in good health and favorable circumstance.

Though we have not yet had the pleasure of formal introduction, I have heard your skill spoken of with considerable respect. In particular, I am told that your unarmed prowess is of a rare and practical sort: not merely graceful, but tested, disciplined, and effective. Such qualities are too seldom found together, despite what many instructors are pleased to claim when there is wine nearby.

I write, then, to ask whether, should your travels bring you into Amadis territory, you might permit me the honor of a training session at your first opportunity. Naturally, I would arrange suitable compensation for your time and expertise, in whatever form would be most convenient and agreeable.

I confess my own chances for proper practice have become disappointingly limited. The soldiers and trainers available to me are courteous, diligent, and unfailingly respectful, which is to say they are nearly useless for the purpose. The men are, almost without exception, gallant and chivalrous in their refusal to risk harm to a lady of rank. The few women willing to train with me have been no less kind, though they are plainly restrained, and seem unwilling to strike with full conviction.

The equipment provided has been similarly unsatisfying, being light, over-padded, and, I increasingly suspect, intentionally fragile.

In Orenic we say: _“Bez ognya stal’ ne znaet sebya.”_ Roughly translated: “Those that are not tested become weak.” It is not a gentle saying, but I have found few true things are improved by making them gentle.

I would therefore be most grateful for the opportunity to learn from someone whose reputation suggests both competence and candor. I do not ask to be indulged. I ask to be instructed, corrected, and, where necessary, thrown properly.

Should such an occasion present itself, I would consider it a genuine privilege.

With sincere respect,

Lady Milena Amadis  
of House Amadis
