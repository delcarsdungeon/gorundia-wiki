## Halfblood Rumors & Stereotypes About Other Peoples

### Humans

**Common halfblood take:** humans are obsessed with bloodlines because their own blood expires in five minutes.

They see humans as flexible, ambitious, emotional, and wildly hypocritical. A human noble will lecture a half-orc about “proper breeding” while marrying his cousin for a vineyard and calling it civilization. Humans are also seen as the race most likely to change their mind if money, sex, fear, or religion enters the room.

**Street saying:**  
“Humans don’t hate you forever. Just until someone richer tells them not to.”

**Misunderstanding:** halfbloods often assume humans are _all_ socially calculating, when plenty of peasants are just trying to eat, avoid taxes, and not get conscripted by some baron with cheekbones.

### High Elves / Court Elves

**Common halfblood take:** elves are beautiful, ancient, and completely allergic to admitting they fucked up.

Halfbloods view high elves as polite jailers: soft voices, perfect hair, absolute control issues. They don’t yell “you don’t belong here”; they arrange a seating chart that says it for them. The Araisal/Allaric connection complicates this, because Allaric lands are more pro-halfblood, but that just makes the old elven style feel even more passive-aggressive: “We support your rights. Please stand three ceremonial paces away from the antique grief.”

**Street saying:**  
“An elf can insult your grandmother, your boots, and your entire species without raising his voice or technically using an adjective.”

**Misunderstanding:** halfbloods often assume elven patience is cowardice or condescension. Sometimes it is. Sometimes it is a six-century-old trauma response wearing silk.

---

### Wood Elves / Grugach

**Common halfblood take:** wild elves don’t pretend. They may kill you, but at least they skip the wine pairing.

Halfbloods often respect grugach more than high elves because the rules are clearer: strength, territory, debt, vengeance, survival. Grugach are seen as dangerous but honest in the way wolves are honest. No one expects a wolf to say “with all due respect” before biting your calf.

**Street saying:**  
“Court elf smiles before the knife. Forest elf just throws the knife. I can work with that.”

**Misunderstanding:** halfbloods may assume grugach are simple or feral. Bad call. That is how you end up becoming an instructional corpse.

---

### Dwarves

**Common halfblood take:** dwarves respect craft, contracts, and grudges — in that order, unless the grudge is especially vintage.

Halfbloods see dwarves as less interested in blood purity than in usefulness. A dwarf might hate your tusks, your manners, and your smell, but if you can hold a shield line or cut stone square, you’ve got a chance. The darker stereotype is that dwarves can be brutally clannish and will treat halfbloods as labor, muscle, or “surface nonsense” until proven otherwise. Callia’s humiliation by Galibano-aligned dwarves fits this nicely: halfbloods know dwarven respect is not automatic, and dwarf-run patronage can become ugly fast.

**Street saying:**  
“A dwarf won’t ask who your father was until after he checks your work.”

**Misunderstanding:** halfbloods sometimes think dwarves are fair because they are blunt. Blunt prejudice still hits like a hammer. It just has better joinery.

---

### Halflings

**Common halfblood take:** halflings are either the kindest people alive or the reason your purse is gone. Sometimes both.

Among halfbloods, halflings have a better reputation than most. They are more likely to judge behavior over ancestry, and their “companions sit with me” attitude wrecks rigid court hierarchy in a way halfbloods tend to appreciate. Neltara’s household treating half-orcs as companions and equals is exactly the sort of thing that would make Gorundian halfbloods go, “Wait, that’s allowed?”

**Street saying:**  
“Halflings don’t care what you are. They care whether you’re rude at dinner.”

**Misunderstanding:** halfbloods may underestimate halflings as harmless or soft. This is adorable. Like assuming the kitchen knife is decorative.

---

### Gnomes

**Common halfblood take:** gnomes are tiny wizards who ask too many questions and write down the answers before you finish lying.

Halfbloods tend to view gnomes as safer than humans but more exhausting than dwarves. Gnomes are curious enough to treat a half-orc wizard as fascinating instead of impossible, which is a nice change — right up until they start asking whether tusk shape correlates with spell stability.

**Street saying:**  
“A gnome will call you a miracle, then ask for a cheek swab.”

**Misunderstanding:** halfbloods may mistake gnomish curiosity for acceptance. Sometimes it is. Sometimes it is just taxonomy with shoes.

---

### Full-Orcs / Orc-Blooded Tribes

**Common halfblood take:** full-orcs are either the missing half of the song or the reason everyone else spits when they see your face.

This one is thorny. Half-orcs often inherit the fear created by full-orc warbands, Dominion shock troops, and old border stories. Some halfbloods romanticize orc strength and directness. Others hate full-orcs for giving humans an excuse to treat every tusked child like a future raider.

**Street saying:**  
“Human sees my tusks and remembers a war I wasn’t born for.”

**Misunderstanding:** halfbloods may assume full-orcs will accept them automatically. Nope. Full-orcs can be just as blood-pure and tribal as anyone else. Congratulations, prejudice has franchise locations.

---

### Drow

**Common halfblood take:** nobody knows what the hell is true, but elves are terrified, so something is going on.

For most Gorundian halfbloods, drow are fireside horror, myth, or “elf business.” The Furyondy material makes clear that even Gorundian humans mostly know drow as bogeymen, while elves react with ancestral panic. Halfbloods are more likely to ask the practical question: “Is he trying to kill us, pay us, or marry into halflings?” Which is honestly healthier than 90% of court reactions.

**Street saying:**  
“If the elves are scared and the halflings are feeding him, I’m waiting before I draw steel.”

**Misunderstanding:** halfbloods may overcorrect and assume “hated outsider” equals “natural ally.” That is how you get recruited into something with robes, spiders, and a regrettable retirement plan.

---

### Clergy / Holy Orders

Not a race, but halfbloods would absolutely treat them like one socially.

**Common halfblood take:** Pelorites may help you, Heironeans may judge you fairly, Pholtans will measure your soul with a ruler and still mark the margin wrong.

The Triad is central to Gorundian life, and in crisis the churches can unite as the Inquisition of the Light. Halfbloods know temples can be refuge, court, hospital, recruitment office, or gallows-adjacent paperwork engine depending on which door they walked through.

**Street saying:**  
“Pelor asks if you’re hurt. Heironeous asks if you stood. Pholtus asks why your name is spelled differently on three forms.”

**Misunderstanding:** halfbloods often lump all clergy together as judgmental. That is unfair to Pelorites. Fair to some Pholtans. Heironeans are complicated because “honorable” and “comfortable with violence” are roommates in that house.

## The Halfblood Meta-Stereotype

The ugly truth: halfbloods often assume every “pure” people has a script for them.

Humans want them useful.  
Elves want them explained.  
Dwarves want them proven.  
Halflings want them fed.  
Gnomes want them footnoted.  
Orcs want them claimed or rejected.  
Churches want them redeemed, tested, or documented.

That makes halfblood humor sharp as hell.

### A few halfblood tavern lines

> “Humans call it mixed blood. Elves call it a tragedy. Dwarves call it a problem with documentation. Halflings call it ‘another plate, dear.’”

> “Never trust a man who says he doesn’t see tusks. Means he’s about to say something stupid about your soul.”

> “Court elves don’t hate halfbloods. They just wish we’d stand somewhere more symbolically convenient.”

> “A dwarf once told me I was ‘well-made, considering.’ I took it as a compliment. Then I took his beer.”

> “The Dominion says we’re unstable. Rich, coming from an empire held together with chains, sermons, and forged ledgers.”
