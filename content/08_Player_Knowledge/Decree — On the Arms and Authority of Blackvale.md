# Decree — On the Arms and Authority of Blackvale

By order of the Crown, and in recognition of lawful succession,

I, Dana Sylvanranth, Countess of Blackvale, do hereby declare:

That the former insignia, livery, and heraldic devices of the Bordella line are retired from all official use, effective immediately.

No banner bearing those arms shall be raised in any hall, garrison, or office under my authority.  
No seal bearing that mark shall be affixed to any decree, contract, or writ henceforth recognized as binding within this county.

All authority now derives from the office of the Countess of Blackvale, and not from any prior holder of that title.

Records shall be preserved as required by law, but no distinction, honor, or precedence shall be granted to the Bordella name beyond what is necessary for accuracy of account.

Where merit existed, it shall be attributed accordingly.  
Where failure occurred, it shall be recorded plainly.

A new standard is established.

Let it be understood:

The past is not erased.  
It is corrected.

Given under my hand and seal,

**Dana Sylvanranth**  
Countess of Blackvale
