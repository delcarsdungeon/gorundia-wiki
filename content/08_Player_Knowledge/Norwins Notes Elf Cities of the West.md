# **Norwin — Elven Cities (Gorundian Side)**

_(Delivered like a man correcting a bad lecture)_

---

## **Myldenär**

> “Myldenär is a surviving interface point. It remembers what it was built for—even if no one else does.”

**History:**  
An **elven-aligned trade and arbitration hub**, where human commerce was permitted—but quietly controlled.

**Now:**  
A **stable imitation of elven order**. The forms remain—contracts, guild hierarchy, polite law—but enforcement is social, not absolute.

> “It works because everyone benefits from pretending the system still exists.”

---

## **Elathas**

> “Elathas is where the system _should_ still be anchored.”

**History:**  
A **primary elven coastal command center**—naval, arcane, and administrative. Likely the keystone for the entire regional network.

**Now:**  
Unknown—and that is the problem.

- No confirmed trade
- No confirmed ruin    
- No consistent reports    

> “If it had fallen, we would know. If it were open, we would know. So—it is neither.”

---

## **Nytha Aethel**

> “Nytha Aethel is not gone. It has simply decided not to participate.”

**History:**  
A **high-order elven site**, likely arcane or administrative, tied to—but not dependent on—the coastal network.

**Now:**  
Still **intact and active** by all indicators.

- Avoided by sailors and scouts    
- Not reachable by conventional approach    
- No successful intrusion accounts    

> “That is not a ruin. That is a boundary.”

---

# **Norwin’s Summary (Clean and Brutal)**

> “On this side of the coast, there were three functional elven assets:  
> a trade interface (Myldenär),  
> a command center (Elathas),  
> and a high-order enclave (Nytha Aethel).
> 
> One adapted.  
> One vanished.  
> One withdrew.
> 
> That is the entire story—everything else is fallout.”
