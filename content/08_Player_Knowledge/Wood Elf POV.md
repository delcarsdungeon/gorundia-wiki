## Wood Elves Looking at the Other Races

## Wood Elf Baseline

Wood elves consider themselves the proper balance of nature, refinement, freedom, law, and civilization.

They are not feral.  
They are not ivory-tower dreamers.  
They are not “high elves with leaves.”

They believe a people should know how to build a hall without insulting the forest, sing an old song without becoming trapped inside it, and hunt without forgetting gratitude.

Their private opinion of most other peoples is:

> “You are not wrong to live differently. You are wrong to live carelessly.”

They are gracious, observant, and usually kind — unless someone despoils nature. Then the politeness shuts off like somebody yanked the tavern lantern.

---

### Humans

**Wood elf private take:**  
Humans are bright, brave, short-lived creatures who build too quickly and apologize too late.

Wood elves admire human courage and energy, especially among farmers, hunters, healers, soldiers, and craftsfolk. They are far less fond of human kingdoms, because human kingdoms have a terrible habit of looking at a living valley and seeing “available land.”

**Wood elf polite version:**  
“Humans have a rare gift for beginning.”

**What they mean:**  
“They should try finishing without ruining the hillside.”

**Common wood elf saying:**  
“A human sees an empty meadow. The meadow disagrees.”

---

### Nobles

Wood elves do not see nobles as a race, but they absolutely judge them as a dangerous invasive species.

**Wood elf private take:**  
Nobles are humans who put banners on their appetites.

A good noble can be a steward, protector, and oath-keeper. A bad noble thinks a title means the land has stopped having opinions.

**Wood elf polite version:**  
“His house carries many obligations.”

**What they mean:**  
“Let us see whether he understands even one of them.”

**Common wood elf saying:**  
“A crown is only a wreath until it remembers the roots.”

---

### High Elves

**Wood elf private take:**  
High elves are beloved elder cousins who climbed into towers, closed the windows, and began mistaking altitude for perspective.

Wood elves admire high elven learning, beauty, memory, and magic. They also resent high elven evasiveness, over-refinement, and their habit of turning guilt into poetry.

The high elves preserved the stars.  
The wood elves preserved the soil.  
The wood elves have opinions about which one feeds children.

**Wood elf polite version:**  
“The high courts preserve many beautiful things.”

**What they mean:**  
“Including their excuses.”

**Common wood elf saying:**  
“A tower sees far, but it does not feel the roots burning.”

---

### Grugach / Wild Elves

**Wood elf private take:**  
The grugach are free spirits, fierce cousins, and living proof that grief can grow claws.

Wood elves respect grugach courage, survival, honesty, and closeness to the wild. They also think the grugach could use a little culture. Not high-court nonsense. Just the basics: dinners without knife disputes, songs not entirely about blood, and maybe a bath that is not technically an ambush.

To wood elves, grugach are not savages. They are rednecks with sacred trauma and excellent archery.

**Wood elf polite version:**  
“The forest-born keep faith in ways that do not always fit a hall.”

**What they mean:**  
“They are impossible, but they are ours.”

**Common wood elf saying:**  
“A wild branch still belongs to the tree.”

---

### Dwarves

**Wood elf private take:**  
Dwarves are honorable, stubborn, useful, and dangerously confident around mountains.

Wood elves respect dwarven craft, loyalty, and memory. Dwarves understand place, oath, and patient labor. That counts for a lot.

The problem is that dwarves hear stone beautifully and are half-deaf to leaves.

**Wood elf polite version:**  
“The stone-folk remember deeply.”

**What they mean:**  
“They remember downward. We remember outward.”

**Common wood elf saying:**  
“Trust a dwarf with a bridge. Watch him near a spring.”

---

### Halflings

**Wood elf private take:**  
Halflings are proof that civilization does not have to be stupid.

Wood elves like halflings.

Halflings understand gardens, paths, hedgerows, kitchens, guest-right, seasonal work, small shrines, and not making every disagreement into a monument. They build low, waste little, notice social rot early, and rarely look at a living hill and think, “Needs a fortress.”

This makes them one of the few peoples wood elves can relax around.

A little.

**Wood elf polite version:**  
“The hin remember the wisdom of small places.”

**What they mean:**  
“Finally, someone with sense.”

**Common wood elf saying:**  
“A halfling garden is a treaty with the earth.”

---

### Gnomes

**Wood elf private take:**  
Gnomes are clever, useful, delightful, exhausting, and should not be allowed to improve sacred objects without supervision.

Wood elves appreciate gnomish craft, wit, and curiosity. They also know curiosity can turn into meddling so quickly it ought to have a warning bell attached.

**Wood elf polite version:**  
“The gnomish mind follows many bright paths.”

**What they mean:**  
“And half of them lead into someone else’s pantry, shrine, or root-cellar.”

**Common wood elf saying:**  
“If a gnome asks whether a thing is sacred, he has already touched it.”

---

### Halfbloods

**Wood elf private take:**  
Halfbloods are not mistakes. They are crossings.

Wood elves are usually more comfortable with mixed heritage than high elves, because forests are full of borders that are not walls: dusk, marsh-edge, riverbank, meadow, rootline, seedfall.

That said, wood elves can still be patronizing. They may treat halfbloods as symbols of balance, renewal, or healing instead of people who are tired of being interpreted.

**Wood elf polite version:**  
“They carry more than one song.”

**What they mean:**  
“We should listen before deciding which song is louder.”

**Common wood elf saying:**  
“A graft may bear strange fruit, but the tree knows whether it lives.”

---

### Orcs

**Wood elf private take:**  
Orcs are appetite without covenant.

This is one of the wood elves’ harsher prejudices.

Wood elves can respect an orc who understands territory, restraint, oath, and rightful taking. But their stereotype of orcs is ugly: raiders, burners, despoilers, people who enter a place without asking what already lives there.

Wood elves do not hate violence. They hate violence that denies relationship.

**Wood elf polite version:**  
“The orcish peoples have not always listened before entering.”

**What they mean:**  
“They burn first and name the ashes later.”

**Common wood elf saying:**  
“An axe may be useful. It is not therefore wise.”

---

### Drow

**Wood elf private take:**  
Drow are a deep wound with a voice.

Wood elves react to drow with dread, but less courtly panic than high elves. To them, the horror of drow is not only that they are dark elves. It is that they represent a severed covenant: cleverness without humility, survival without sunlight, beauty without mercy.

**Wood elf polite version:**  
“Some roots grow where no dawn reaches.”

**What they mean:**  
“Do not invite that shadow into the grove without watchers.”

**Common wood elf saying:**  
“The deepest root may still drink poison.”

---

### Clergy / Holy Orders

**Wood elf private take:**  
Human priests are useful, sincere, and far too fond of turning divine moments into institutions.

Wood elves respect Pelorites when they heal, Heironeans when they defend, and even Pholtans when truth genuinely matters. But they distrust hard religious machinery. A shrine is one thing. A bureaucracy with holy warrants is another beast entirely.

**Wood elf polite version:**  
“The young gods burn brightly in human hands.”

**What they mean:**  
“Please stop building offices around miracles.”

**Common wood elf saying:**  
“When men hear a god, they look for parchment.”

---

## Wood Elves About Despoilers

Wood elf courtesy ends with despoilers.

A despoiler is not merely someone who cuts trees, hunts, farms, mines, or builds. Wood elves are not children. Civilization requires use.

A despoiler is someone who takes without covenant.

Despoilers include those who:

- clear-cut sacred groves
- poison streams
- hunt breeding herds for sport
- mine beneath old-root sites without rite or warning
- burn forest to flush enemies
- bind spirits into wood, stone, or flesh
- break seasonal taboos
- kill dryads, treants, unicorns, or guardian beasts
- treat land as empty because no crown claims it

**Wood elf warning:**  
“You have taken what was not silent. You mistook patience for permission. Leave now, and the forest may remember you as fools. Stay, and it will remember you as meat.”

## Wood Elf Translation Table

**“This place is old.”**  
There are rules here you do not know.

**“Walk softly.”**  
Stop acting like the world is furniture.

**“The trees have already noticed you.”**  
So have the archers.

**“You are welcome beneath these boughs.”**  
For now.

**“You may speak freely.”**  
We will judge you by what you choose to reveal.

**“That is a high-elven answer.”**  
Beautiful, useless, and probably evasive.

**“That is a grugach answer.”**  
Direct, violent, and missing two social steps.

**“You have a human urgency.”**  
You are about to make a short-lived decision with long-lived consequences.

**“The hin understand.”**  
The halflings are being sensible. Try that.

**“No.”**  
No.

When a wood elf finally uses the word directly, things have gone badly.

## Sample Wood Elf Lines About Everyone

> “A road is not a wound if it remembers the hill.”

> “The high elves kept the stars. We kept the soil. Guess which one feeds children?”

> “The grugach are not wrong. They are merely exhausting about being right.”

> “A halfling plants beans as though making peace. This is why I trust them.”

> “Humans build quickly because they fear death. They rarely ask what the land fears.”

> “Dwarves hear stone beautifully. They are sadly half-deaf to leaves.”

> “Gnomes are delightful, provided all sacred relics are stored above their climbing height.”

> “A halfblood is not a broken branch. It is a graft. Whether it thrives depends on the gardener.”

> “Do not call a forest empty. Empty things do not watch you back.”

> “Civilization is not the enemy. A city may be honorable. But if its drains poison the river, its songs are lies.”

## The Core Wood Elf Horror

Wood elves are quietly horrified because most other peoples cannot tell the difference between use and violation.

Humans rush.  
High elves detach.  
Grugach reject refinement.  
Dwarves dig without listening.  
Gnomes meddle.  
Orcs take.  
Drow sever.  
Clergy institutionalize.  
Halflings, blessedly, usually bring lunch and ask where the path goes.

The wood elf worldview is basically:

> “Civilization is not the enemy. Forgetting that the world is alive is the enemy.”
