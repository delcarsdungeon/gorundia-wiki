## **I. Summary Judgment**

Incanbulos is not merely a god of disease.

He is the **embodiment of inevitable decay**—of flesh, faith, and order.

Where other hostile powers seek dominion or destruction, Incanbulos represents a quieter truth:

> **That all things rot—and that rot may be guided.**

His cults do not conquer.  
They do not proclaim.

They **persist**.

---

## **II. Nature of the Threat**

Cults of Incanbulos operate with consistency across regions:

### **1. Infiltration Over Declaration**

- No permanent temples of note
- Presence embedded within:
    - hospice houses
    - plague wards
    - itinerant healer circles

### **2. Patience as Doctrine**

- Cells remain dormant for extended periods
- Activation coincides with:
    - famine
    - war
    - population displacement

### **3. Weaponized Suffering**

- Disease is directed, not incidental
- Spread is:
    - encouraged
    - prolonged
    - selectively intensified

---

## **III. Structure of Cult Cells**

### **Rotfather / Rotmother**

- Cell authority
- Often elderly, visibly afflicted
- Operates under guise of caretaker or undertaker

### **Bearers**

- Deliberately infected vectors
- Display unnatural calm and pain tolerance

### **Witnesses**

- Record-keepers and coordinators
- Maintain continuity between outbreaks

### **The Unwitting**

- Civilians unknowingly utilized
- Preferred over overt recruitment to avoid detection

---

## **IV. Methods of Operation**

### **A. Plague Seeding**

- Contamination of:
    - wells
    - grain stores
    - refugee concentrations

### **B. Medical Subversion**

- Cultists posing as healers provide:
    - incomplete treatment
    - incorrect remedies
    - delayed recovery protocols

**Intent:** prolong suffering, increase spread

---

### **C. Ritual Amplification**

- Subtle rites performed during outbreaks:
    - often mistaken for funerary custom
- Effects include:
    - increased virulence
    - delayed mortality
    - sustained transmission

---

### **D. Psychological Erosion**

- Fatalistic doctrine spread among afflicted:
    - “All must rot”
    - “The body is already lost”

Result:

- breakdown of morale
- distrust of legitimate healers
- resistance to quarantine

---

## **V. Indicators of Cult Activity**

- Unnatural clustering of disease
- Healers discouraging isolation or cleansing rites
- Repeated contamination of supply sources
- Survivors displaying abnormal emotional detachment
- Symbolic traces:
    - blackened circles
    - cracked wheels
    - carrion or insect motifs

---

## **VI. Theological Assessment**

The Triad recognizes:

- Disease as part of natural order
- Suffering as trial, not purpose
- Healing as sacred duty

Incanbulos perverts all three:

- Suffering becomes the goal
- Healing becomes defiance
- Death becomes a **process to be extended**

This places his cults in direct opposition to:

- [[Pelor]] — life, healing, mercy
- [[Heironeous]] — protection and righteous defense
- [[Pholtus]] — truth, order, and structure

---

## **VII. Recommended Response**

### **1. Immediate Quarantine**

- Enforced without exception
- Civil resistance expected

### **2. Triad Deployment**

- Pelorite healers
- Pholtan adjudicators
- Heironean enforcement units

### **3. Purification Measures**

- Burning of contaminated goods
- Blessing of water sources
- Sanctification of burial sites

---

### **4. Interrogation Protocol**

- Cultists rarely resist
- Common behaviors:
    - partial confession
    - fatalistic rhetoric
    - attempts to demoralize

**Directive:**  
Do not engage philosophically.

---

### **5. Final Sanction**

> **Where containment fails, purge the site.**

Fire is preferred.

## **IX. Closing Assessment**

Cults of Incanbulos do not fear death.

They welcome it.

Their defeat is measured not in bodies, but in **containment**.

> _“Where rot is denied, life endures. Where it is permitted, all follows.”_
