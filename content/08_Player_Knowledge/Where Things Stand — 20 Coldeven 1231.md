# Where Things Stand

The Steel Crusade broke the central Vogel conspiracy, but it did not end the war.

Wilhelm Vogel is dead. His surviving allies, military remnants, and compromised institutions continue to resist across Gorundia. Some fight for the old cause. Others fight because surrender would expose what they did beneath it.

Beyond the civil war, the struggle in the [[Forest Kingdom]] continues. Kursk is under increasing pressure, but the corruption it created no longer depends entirely upon Kursk to survive.

---

## The Lord Marshal’s Host

[[Beringer Amadis]], Lord Marshal of Gorundia, commands the principal royal host in the east.

Baron [[Orin Kharne]] began the western cleanup through [[Gravendar]], helping set operations against the remaining resistance in motion. He has since rejoined the [[Lord Marshal's Host]].

The host now serves as the principal concentration of royal military strength against the remaining organized Vogel factions.

---

## Ravenfast

[[Ravenfast Hold]] has fallen.

A royal strike force destroyed the Ashen Wing contingent holding the fortress. These were the same knights who had previously driven Orin from the position.

At least one surviving Ashen Wing knight was taken prisoner.

The fall of Ravenfast removed a Vogel strongpoint, avenged the earlier defeat, and demonstrated that the remnants of the old order can no longer assume their fortifications will protect them.
## Aftermath

The returning strike force reconstructed much of the battle aboard the [[Second Draft]]. Their conversation, including the origins of the name “Brute Squad,” appears in [[The Second Draft - Homeward]].

## The Remaining War

### Direhold

[[Direhold]] remains under siege.

While it stands, organized resistance can still claim that the civil war continues. Its capture remains both a military objective and a political necessity.

### Blackvale

The war in [[Blackvale]] continues.

The region remains one of the major active fronts, consuming royal troops, command attention, and resources badly needed elsewhere.

### The War Above

Enemy forces have demonstrated the ability to deploy troops from the air.

The captured devices known as [[Iron Nails]] interfere with ships and other flying craft. Their existence has made control of the air—and protection of commanders, supply lines, and camps—a continuing concern.

A crashed enemy spy wagon has also been recovered. Some of its crew survived.

## Thalrik and the Crown

[[Thalrik Vaultforge]] has been brought fully into the present strategic situation.

He and his loyal right hand, [[Jorven Hale]], have been engaged in an exhausting private conference with the King, the Admiral, and military advisors.  Reviewing what they now know about the Dominion's air capabilities.

The discussions have taken place away from the principal action. Whatever conclusions they reach have not yet been presented to the wider command.

Thalrik now understands the military and political pressures facing the Crown; he has a decision to make on how much to bring the king in on the council/air-field 'situation', or whether he and his fellow miscreants are going to get creative.

How he explained not being on the SHIP and whether the King now knows he is enjoying himself at his new casino remains to be defined.

## The Moffet Front

[[Glandyr Ryomar|Duke Ravenster]] has entered Moffet territory and is coordinating with [[Quinn Moffet]], who is functioning as the principal Moffet authority in the field.

[[Mara Moffet]] has repeatedly attempted to contact [[Ewan Moffet]] without success.

Distance, wards, movement, underground shelter, magical countermeasures, or interference could explain the silence.

At present, none of those explanations has been confirmed.

The location and condition of Elder Moffet remain unknown.

## Blackberry

The surviving members of the [[Underworld Crew]] have reached the [[Blackberry Public Knowledge|Town of Blackberry]].

They found a town caught between the corruption of its former government and the severe but effective authority that replaced it.

### The Mayor

Blackberry’s former mayor, [[Cedric Bale]], used local government as a private machine of patronage and extraction.

His administration controlled:

- permits;
- taxes;
- contracts;
- Watch appointments;
- boundary disputes;
- public works;
- licenses and market access.

When his position collapsed, Bale fled with much of the treasury, valuable financial instruments, and probably the records most capable of proving what his administration had done.

Members of his family and administration fled with him.

### The Protector

Authority passed to [[Anselm Varr]], former Gravender Ducal War Captain of the Levy.

Varr declared an **Emergency Protectorate**.

Under his rule:

- roads and streets became safer;
- neglected residents received greater protection;
- trade and administration became more reliable;
- surveillance increased;
- travel became controlled;
- emergency authority became normal;
- residents were required to swear loyalty oaths.

Blackberry is therefore not a simple occupied town waiting to be liberated.

Some residents seem to genuinely prefer Varr’s harsh order to Bale’s corruption.

### The Break-In

The Underworld Crew entered Blackberry covertly.

They infiltrated Varr’s residence, captured the Protector inside his own house, tied him up, and began questioning him.

Varr was understandably displeased by the intrusion and restraint.

He appeared considerably more offended, however, when treated as a possible Dominion collaborator.

Whether that reaction came from genuine innocence, professional pride, personal hatred of the Dominion, or outrage at the accusation remains unclear.

### The Orchard

The crew has been told that a possible [[Dominion]] agent may be working at an orchard in Blackberry.

They have not yet uncovered the full situation.

At present they have:

- a captured Protector;
- an offended denial;
- a lead pointing toward an orchard;
- and a town whose apparent order may conceal problems that have not yet been identified.

They are still deciding how to proceed.

## The Forest Kingdom

Deep within the [[Forest Kingdom]], [[Jinwing]], [[Sabry]], [[Taleon Thunderhoof]], and [[Rhovanion]] are moving toward the restoration of the [[Great Treant]].

They are already seeing evidence that [[Kursk]] is being forced onto the defensive.

Elven patrols range farther than before. Kursk’s secure territory is contracting. Its forces increasingly defend positions they once used as bases for expansion.

That does not mean the forest is healing on its own.

The [[Ashroot]] and [[Greylife]] corruption have developed lives and hungers independent of the people who first cultivated them. Even if Kursk were destroyed tomorrow, the damage it unleashed would remain.

### Elunaeth

[[Elunaeth]] used an exchange between her aspects to carry Rhovanion across a great distance.

The act left the manifested aspect traveling with the group dangerously weakened. She became tangible, vulnerable, and separated from the greater existence that normally sustained her.

The group initially considered taking her to the sacred pools.

They did not.

Instead, Elunaeth’s manifested aspect rejoined her primary body elsewhere.

At that moment, her other remaining aspects collapsed like puppets whose strings had been cut.

One of those bodies remains with the [[Lord Marshal's Host]].

Another was taken by Orin’s attached Grugach warriors. They carried it back toward the Great Forest with profound reverence and respect.

The condition of Elunaeth’s primary body—and what the reunion ultimately restored or changed—has not yet been confirmed.

Her presumed recovery remains important to what comes next.

### The Great Purification

The [[Grand Druid]] and the Master Druids are preparing a great purifying ritual for the Forest Kingdom.

The ritual is intended to strike at corruption that ordinary patrols, fire, steel, and local cleansing can no longer contain.

Its commencement depends upon Elunaeth’s presumed recovery.

Until her condition is confirmed—or the druids decide they must proceed without her—the ritual remains prepared but incomplete.

Meanwhile, Jinwing, Sabry, Taleon Thunderhoof, and Rhovanion continue toward the Great Treant.

If the Treant can be restored, it may become more than a weapon against Kursk.

It may become one of the living anchors around which the Forest Kingdom can begin to heal.
