# Jinwing — Character Foundation

---

## Basic Information

Born: unknown  
Age: ~672  
Height: 5’10”  
Weight: 120 lbs  
Parents: unknown  
Siblings: unknown  

---

## Appearance

Jinwing differs from most wood elves in subtle but noticeable ways.

- Light green hair with streaks of honey brown  
- Peaches-and-cream complexion  
- Cognac brown eyes  
- Willowy, graceful build  

She moves lightly and deliberately, with controlled gestures. Her posture is upright and composed, though she often places herself at the edge of groups rather than their center.

She is most at ease in nature.

---

## Personality

- Quiet  
- Thoughtful  
- Observant  
- Hopeful  
- Determined  
- Somewhat detached  
- Carries a quiet sadness  

She listens more than she speaks, and often processes before acting.

---

## Core Truths (What Jinwing Knows)

---

### 1) The Deep Sleep

The Deep Sleep is a **ritual state**, not a place.

Jinwing underwent this ritual in:

> **The Rootbound Hollow**

A rare and sacred site used for preservation rites.

Purpose of the ritual:

> To preserve a druid aligned to the old balance, untouched by the fracture that followed.

She was not chosen for power or rank.

She was chosen because she would **hold steady**.

---

### 2) Why She Was Not Corrupted

Jinwing is not immune to corruption.

She was protected because:

- she was never exposed during its spread  
- the ritual isolated and anchored her  
- the Hollow preserved her in alignment with the old balance  

> She is not incorruptible. She is untested.

---

### 3) Who Chose Her

Jinwing was chosen by:

> [[Khelarion Duskseer]]

Others were aware of the decision, but it was his to make.

Elunaeth Starbough influenced **timing**, not selection.

---

### 4) The Amber Druid

The Amber Druid did not create the Deep Sleep.

He performed:

> an emergency, less stable version of the same underlying ritual

Same principle.

Worse conditions.

---

### 5) What She Was Before

Before the Deep Sleep, Jinwing was:

- a trained druid, not a master  
- primarily aligned with the Order of the Tree  
- exposed to other Orders, but not fully formed within them  

She understood:

- balance between nature and settlement  
- cycles of life and season  
- stewardship of the land  

She did not:

- lead at scale  
- make large strategic decisions  
- operate in political or multi-cultural conflict  

---

### 6) What She Has Now

Since waking, Jinwing has seen:

- Grugach survival and adaptation  
- Human strength and persistence  
- Elven stagnation and fragmentation  
- Blight within the land  

This gives her something rare:

> She understands both the world as it was… and as it is.

---

### 7) What She Is Not

Jinwing is not:

- a High Druid  
- a replacement for Duskseer  
- a default leader of others  

She does not carry authority unless she chooses to act.

---

### 8) What She Is

Jinwing is:

- a druid formed by the old balance  
- living in a changed world  
- trying to act without losing what she believes is right  

She is still defining herself.

## History (Condensed)

Jinwing was found as a toddler by Duskseer in Druid Valley, with no memory of her origins.

He searched for her family for decades and found nothing.

He raised her as his daughter.

She trained under all four Orders over centuries, intentionally without revealing her connection to Duskseer.

She returned home between periods of training, integrating what she learned.

She encountered Elunaeth Starbough during her time studying the Moons and Stars, receiving guidance tied to a future event.

She completed her training across all Orders, forming strong relationships with her teachers.

At some point following her training, Duskseer chose her for the Deep Sleep ritual.

She was placed within the Rootbound Hollow and remained there for centuries.
