# Sabry Emerald

> [!quote] [[Jinwing]]
> “The first time I met Sabry, she feared her own fire. Now the Living Flame trusts her to carry its own.”

## Overview

**Full Name:** Sabry Emerald  
**Titles:** Ember-Sovereign • Keeper of Bound Ash • Voice Before the Living Flame  
**Age:** 111  
**Race:** Wood Elf  
**Affiliation:** [[The Rekindled Temple — Purifiers and Brethren|The Rekindled Temple]]  
**Residence:** The Rekindled Temple, [[Great Forest]]

**Sabry Emerald** is the Ember-Sovereign of the Rekindled Temple, leader of the restored Order of Purifiers, and chosen interpreter of the [[Living Flame]].

Once a frightened Spark-born child imprisoned within the Temple of Eternal Flame, she has become one of Gorundia’s foremost authorities on sacred fire, elemental purification, and the destruction of magical corruption.

Although Sabry is renowned as one of the greatest pyromancers of her age, those who know her best rarely speak first of her power.

They speak of her restraint.

## Appearance

Sabry is young by the standards of her people, possessing the timeless elegance common to elves without the effortless confidence often associated with elven nobility.

Her most striking features are her long emerald-green hair and brilliant, gem-like green eyes. Their calm intensity can be unsettling to those expecting theatrical displays from a master of fire.

She favors flowing robes of crimson, green, and gold, embroidered with flames, leaves, and the rising sun. During formal rites she may carry the [[Censer of the Clear Flame]], vessels of sacred ash, or specially prepared reliquaries containing a portion of the Living Flame.

When Sabry invokes the oldest rites of the Temple, white-gold fire threaded with faint green lightning gathers around her without consuming her clothing or surroundings.

It is said that the Living Flame recognizes its steward.

> [!quote] [[Thalrik Vaultforge]]
> “She can discuss sacred fire, dinner, and the end of the world in exactly the same calm voice. It’s unsettling.”

## Personality and Demeanor

Sabry is quiet, thoughtful, and remarkably observant.

Decades spent in isolation left her with the habit of studying people before speaking. She often learns more by watching than others do by interrogation, and she rarely interrupts merely to prove that she has understood.

Her humor is dry, understated, and most often revealed around companions she trusts.

Sabry enjoys books, forgotten ruins, campfires, ancient lore, and the simple satisfaction of preparing a good meal over an open flame. She dislikes cruelty, arrogance, needless destruction, and anyone who confuses possessing power with deserving to use it.

Although she commands the restored Purifiers, Sabry remains deeply uncomfortable with being treated as an object of worship.

Respect, she accepts.

Reverence, she redirects toward the Flame.

## House Emerald and the Spark

Sabry was born among the wood elves of **House Emerald**, an old Purifier bloodline whose members were traditionally called upon when rot, undeath, plague, or magical corruption had spread beyond the power of gentler remedies.

The family’s oldest traditions speak of **the Spark**, a child born only once in five centuries with an extraordinary affinity for elemental fire.

Such children were not meant to become weapons. They were taught that fire is neither enemy nor servant, but a force requiring discipline, patience, and harmony with the living world.

Sabry never received that education.

When she was approximately fifty years old, a radical splinter of the corrupted Purifiers abducted her. They believed the Spark-born should be separated from ordinary restraint and transformed into a conduit for an ancient power beyond the mortal world.

She was taken to the Temple of Eternal Flame.

She remained there for more than half a century.

## Captivity and Escape

Sabry spent decades confined within magical cages of fire, her world reduced to stone walls, sacred flame, fragments of forbidden lore, and the occasional voices of her captors.

Rather than breaking her spirit, the isolation sharpened it.

Denied teachers, she became self-taught.

Denied freedom, she mastered patience.

With little beyond fire and earth upon which to practice, she developed extraordinary command of both elements through relentless experimentation and careful observation.

When Sabry finally escaped, several of her captors attempted to stop her and died in the effort.

Others simply watched her leave.

> [!quote] [[Beringer Amadis|Sir Beringer Amadis]]
> “Many people learn how to wield fire. Sabry learned when not to.”

## The Long Road

Freedom proved more difficult than escape.

Having spent most of her formative life in isolation, Sabry possessed little understanding of ordinary conversation, friendship, humor, or the thousand small customs by which people reassure one another that they are not enemies.

She learned through imitation.

Through observation.

Through the patience of companions willing to accept a quiet, awkward elf who sometimes approached ordinary life with childlike curiosity.

Sabry still unconsciously mirrors the gestures and expressions of people she trusts, an old habit formed while teaching herself how to exist among others.

One of her earliest journeys ended in tragedy when an accidental loss of control devastated much of the village of **Oxlight**.

The disaster became the defining lesson of her life.

From that day forward, Sabry treated fire not as power to be unleashed, but as responsibility to be carried.

## Adventuring Career

Travelling beside companions who would become some of Gorundia’s most celebrated and troublesome heroes, Sabry transformed from a frightened survivor into one of the realm’s most respected spellcasters.

Her recorded accomplishments include:

- Defeating the Avatar of Urdlen.
- Winning the Mage Championship of [[Bard's Gate]].
- Being recognized as a Maester of Pyromancy.
- Helping defend [[Fort Stormarrow]] during its siege.
- Preserving and restoring the Great Library of the Temple.
- Learning the sacred Phoenix Transformation.
- Cleansing a fungal corruption aboard a crashed Celene skyship alongside [[Thalrik Vaultforge]], [[Norwin Stoneyeye]], [[Rhovanion]], and [[Jinwing]].
- Receiving the [[Censer of the Clear Flame]] as a sacred gift from Celene.
- Carrying a portion of the Living Flame into the [[Harrowmere Caldera]] so that it could witness the scarred land through mortal eyes.

## The Rekindled Temple

Sabry did not abandon the Temple that had stolen her childhood.

She reclaimed it.

Following the defeat of the efreeti and their servants who had corrupted the old Order, Sabry remained within its halls to restore what had been damaged, hidden, or deliberately forgotten.

The Great Library was preserved.

Ancient rites were recovered.

The surviving Daughters of Flame were freed from their former masters.

The Living Flame accepted Sabry as its steward and interpreter.

Under her guidance, the Temple has become a sanctuary, reliquary, library, and place of disciplined instruction. Its halls are once again warm with inhabited life rather than merely containing an imprisoned power.

Within the Temple, Sabry is remembered more simply than her titles suggest.

She is the one who came home.

## The Living Flame

The ancient presence called **Nárë Aerlindor** by the elves and **Kha’zir al-Keth** in older traditions is not merely a magical fire.

It watches.

It judges.

It remembers.

The Flame does not treat Sabry as its owner or priestess. She is its chosen steward, interpreter, and mortal witness.

> [!quote] The Living Flame
> “She came to Me in chains. She returned by choice. That is why she bears My fire.”

When the Flame asked Sabry to carry a portion of its true fire into the [[Harrowmere Caldera]], its instructions were characteristically brief:

> “Stand where the stone cooled.”
>
> “Have eyes far from the Temple.”

The Flame wished to perceive the caldera directly through the fire Sabry carried. It sought to understand what Harrowmere had become: whether it had truly been cleansed, whether something remained bound beneath the cooled stone, and whether the scarred land was dead, corrupted, sacred, or awakening.

Sabry did not carry a message for the Flame.

She carried the Flame itself.

## The Restored Purifiers

Sabry has not created a new purpose for the Order of Purifiers.

She has restored its oldest one.

Before its corruption, the Order worked beside druids and wardens to burn out blighted groves, plague-bearing creatures, undead infestations, cursed ground, and magical rot that could not safely be healed.

Their calling was never destruction for its own sake.

It was the preservation of life through the careful removal of what threatened to consume it.

Under Sabry, every student is taught that discipline precedes power and that fire must never be allowed to choose its own purpose.

The Purifiers advance through four recognized ranks:

- **Cinder**
- **Ember**
- **Hearthflame**
- **Ashkeeper**

The Order remains fundamentally elven. Those who serve the Temple but cannot undertake the Purifier path are welcomed among the **Brethren of the Flame**, honored allies whose crafts, scholarship, faith, and courage sustain the Rekindled Temple.

Among Sabry’s youngest students is [[Vaerisse Emberlain]], whose enthusiasm, determination, and occasional collisions with Temple furniture continually remind Sabry of the mentor she never had.

> [!quote] [[Vaerisse Emberlain]]
> “When the Ember-Sovereign says ‘Again,’ that’s usually praise.”

## Reputation

Throughout Gorundia, Sabry’s name has become associated with impossible fire and even more improbable restraint.

Scholars seek permission to study within the Temple’s restored library.

Druids increasingly recognize the Purifiers as allies against corruption rather than enemies of the wild.

The elves of Celene honor Sabry for cleansing the corrupted skyship and presented her with the Censer of the Clear Flame, whose blue-white smoke clears poison, blight, foul air, and supernatural corruption.

Among common folk, stories of Sabry vary wildly. Some call her a fire-saint. Others insist she can command volcanoes, converse with candles, or burn a lie out of a man without leaving a mark.

Sabry discourages all three claims.

She is more successful with some than others.

## Trivia

- Sabry genuinely enjoys the sight, smell, sound, and warmth of ordinary fire.
- She possesses extensive knowledge of forgotten magical lore and ancient elemental traditions.
- She knows the location of at least one Ravager lair within the Great Forest.
- She is an excellent cook and insists she could happily roast and eat an entire “bull-et.”
- She unconsciously mirrors the mannerisms of trusted companions.
- She is mildly claustrophobic despite leading the Temple in which she was imprisoned.
- She often spends evenings cataloging books in the Great Library because she finds it relaxing.
- She prefers solving problems through understanding before resorting to force.
- Being calm does not mean Sabry is uncertain. Usually it means she has already decided.

> [!quote] [[Thranduil]]
> “People think Sabry’s greatest gift is fire. They’re wrong. It’s that she never lets the fire decide who she is.”
