# Jinwing Timeline

> “She remembers the world before the wound.  
> She does not remember the wound being made.”

## Core Timeline Lock

**Current Year:** 1231 OR  
**Current Age:** approximately 672  
**Approximate Birth Year:** 559 OR  
**Deep Sleep Duration:** approximately 330 years  
**Deep Sleep Site:** [[Rootbound Hollow]]  
**Chosen By:** [[Khelarion Duskseer]]  
**Timing Influenced By:** [[Elunaeth Starbough]]  
**Status:** Trained druid of the old balance, not a High Druid

Jinwing is not a finished authority figure, hidden High Druid, or replacement for Duskseer. She is a preserved druid of the old balance, awakened into a world that broke while she slept.

---

## 559 OR — Approximate Birth

Jinwing is born somewhere unknown among the old wood-elven or grugach-adjacent bloodlines.

Her birth family remains unknown to her.

### Hidden Lineage

Jinwing’s birth father was also the maternal grandfather of [[Thranduil Stormarrow]].

This means:

- Jinwing and Thranduil’s mother are half-sisters.
- Jinwing is Thranduil’s maternal half-aunt.
- Jinwing does not know this.
- Thranduil knows because [[Serelith Bludwulf]] told him.
- Jinwing’s player knows out of character.

This is not public knowledge.

---

## c. 562 OR — Found by Duskseer

As a toddler, Jinwing is found in Druid Valley by [[Khelarion Duskseer]].

She is too young to explain where she came from or name her family clearly. Duskseer believes she answers to “Jin,” and begins calling her **Jin Broken Wing**, because she reminds him of a bird that wants to go home but cannot fly there.

He assumes returning her to her family will be simple.

It is not.

---

## 562–577 OR — The Search for Her Family

Duskseer spends roughly fifteen years searching for Jinwing’s family.

He asks around, seeks visions, speaks with trees, visits camps and settlements, and searches through every means available to him.

He finds nothing.

No family.  
No missing-child report.  
No clear trail.  
No answer.

This failure is real, not negligence.

---

## c. 577 OR — Jinwing Chooses Duskseer

Duskseer asks Jinwing whether he should continue searching or whether she wants to become his daughter forever.

Jinwing still has vague impressions of warmth and kindness from her birth family, but her clearest memories are now of Duskseer: forests, stars, songs, stories, safety, laughter, and care.

She chooses him.

From then on, she calls him **Father**.

This does not erase her birth family.

It gives her a true chosen one.

---

## 577–779 OR — Druid Valley Formation

Duskseer raises Jinwing in Druid Valley for roughly two centuries.

She grows from a lost child into a gifted young druid. Within the valley, she becomes beloved. Outside it, almost no one knows Duskseer has adopted a daughter.

During this period she learns:

- nature-speech
- woodland survival
- old druidic ethics
- seasonal rhythms
- stewardship of land
- Duskseer’s Moon-and-Stars-influenced teachings
- the idea that nature and settlement can exist in balance

She is gifted, but still sheltered.

Duskseer eventually worries that she has been given too narrow a view of the world.

---

## c. 779 OR — Sent Out for Formal Training

At roughly age 220, Duskseer sends Jinwing out to study under the druidic Orders.

He instructs her not to reveal her connection to him. He wants her trained as herself, not as Duskseer’s daughter, pet project, or living political question.

He teaches her the phrases that will open the way to the Orders.

She leaves Druid Valley to become broader than the valley that saved her.

---

## 779–899 OR — Formal Order Training

Jinwing studies across the four druidic Orders for roughly 120 years total.

This replaces the older draft’s longer study span, which breaks the age math so badly it should be taken behind the barn and apologized to.

### Order of the Moon and Stars

**Approximate Duration:** 25 years  
**Teacher:** [[Fenlithan Duskwillow]]  
**Entry Phrase:** “The stars showed me the way here, please teach me.”

This Order feels familiar because Duskseer himself came from this tradition.

Jinwing studies:

- celestial rhythms
- omens
- foresight
- time as pattern
- patience before revelation

During this period she encounters [[Elunaeth Starbough]], whose guidance is tied to a future event Jinwing does not fully understand.

Elunaeth influences timing.

She does not choose Jinwing.

### Order of the Unending Cycle

**Approximate Duration:** 30 years  
**Teacher:** [[Myxathra Hollowroot]]  
**Entry Phrase:** “I followed the butterfly and it landed here, please teach me.”

This is the heaviest of Jinwing’s training.

She learns:

- life, death, and rebirth
- decay as transformation
- rot as part of renewal
- scavengers, fire, and patience
- when to intervene and when to let the cycle continue

Jinwing respects the teaching, but finds the Order cold, solemn, and difficult.

### Order of the Beast

**Approximate Duration:** 25 years  
**Teacher:** [[Calla Beastwhisper]]  
**Entry Phrase:** “The birds tell of seasons, please teach me their ways.”

Jinwing studies:

- animal empathy
- wilderness movement
- predator and prey balance
- shapeshifting foundations
- instinct as wisdom
- survival without domination

This training broadens her beyond trees and stars into living motion.

### Order of the Trees

**Approximate Duration:** 40 years  
**Teacher:** [[Vaerith Thornveil]]  
**Entry Phrase:** “The trees whisper of wisdom, please teach me.”

The Order of the Trees comes most naturally to Jinwing.

She feels most alive among trees. Her connection to forest, root, canopy, and deep memory becomes her strongest alignment.

She studies:

- forest memory
- root-network rites
- sacred groves
- preservation magic
- balance between wild growth and guided growth
- the long patience of trees

This becomes her deepest druidic path.

---

## c. 899 OR — Chosen for the Deep Sleep

At roughly age 340, Duskseer chooses Jinwing for the Deep Sleep ritual.

She is not chosen because she is the strongest.  
She is not chosen because she is the highest-ranking.  
She is not chosen to become High Druid.

She is chosen because she can **hold steady**.

Others know of the decision, but it is Duskseer’s choice. Elunaeth influences the timing but does not make the selection.

The ritual is performed in the [[Rootbound Hollow]], a rare sacred site used for preservation rites.

---

## 899–1231 OR — The Deep Sleep

Jinwing remains in the Deep Sleep for roughly 330 years.

The Deep Sleep is a ritual state, not a distant place. It isolates, anchors, and preserves her in alignment with the old balance.

It protects her because:

- she is not exposed during the corruption’s spread
- the ritual isolates her
- the Hollow anchors her
- the old balance is preserved around her

It does not make her incorruptible.

She is not immune.

She is untested.

---

## c. 1021 OR — Fall of Nilanthalas

While Jinwing sleeps, [[Nilanthalas]] falls during the [[Clay Rising]].

The old woodland world she knew is shattered.

She does not witness the fall.

She does not fight in it.

She does not remember the collapse as lived experience.

She wakes into its aftermath.

This is one of the defining wounds of her life: the world died while she was preserved to survive it.

---

## 1231 OR — Awakening

Jinwing awakens in 1231 OR.

She enters a world changed beyond recognition:

- grugach survival and adaptation
- human persistence
- elven stagnation and fragmentation
- blight within the land
- broken old Orders
- lost groves and changed borders
- the long aftermath of Nilanthalas’s fall

She understands both the world as it was and the world as it is becoming.

That does not mean she knows everything.

It means she can feel what has been lost.

---

## Current Knowledge Boundary

### Jinwing Knows

- Duskseer found and raised her.
- Duskseer searched for her family and failed.
- She chose Duskseer as Father.
- She trained across all four Orders.
- Her strongest path is the Order of the Trees.
- The Deep Sleep preserved her.
- She was chosen by Duskseer.
- She is not a High Druid.
- She is not immune to corruption.
- The world has changed drastically since she slept.

### Jinwing Does Not Know

- Her birth father’s name.
- That her father was Thranduil’s maternal grandfather.
- That Thranduil’s mother was her half-sister.
- That she is Thranduil’s maternal half-aunt.
- Why she was truly lost as a toddler.
- Whether Duskseer suspected her lineage.
- The full meaning of Elunaeth’s guidance.
- The full cost of the Deep Sleep.
- What happens if she is exposed to corruption now.
- What role she is meant to grow into, if any.

---

## Thranduil Knowledge Boundary

[[Thranduil Stormarrow]] knows that Jinwing is his maternal half-aunt because [[Serelith Bludwulf]] told him.

He may not know all details of how Jinwing was lost, why the family line broke, or whether his mother knew of the lost half-sister.

Jinwing has not yet been told.

This should remain an in-character emotional reveal, not a casual genealogy footnote. Elves already make family trees complicated enough without everyone acting like ancestry is a tavern receipt.

---

## Canon Summary

Jinwing is a preserved druid of the old balance, born around 559 OR, found and raised by Duskseer, trained across the four Orders, and placed into the Deep Sleep around 899 OR.

She slept through the fall of Nilanthalas and awoke in 1231 OR into a broken world.

Her hidden bloodline ties her directly to Thranduil Stormarrow as his maternal half-aunt, but she does not yet know this.

She is not a High Druid.

She is not Duskseer’s replacement.

She is not incorruptible.

She is the old balance, still breathing, trying to decide what it means to act now.
