## **Overview**

Sir Morkken is one of those men people forget—right up until the moment everything would have gone wrong without him.

A **house knight of Baron Orin Kharne**, he serves as a steady, reliable presence in the Stormarrow court. Not brilliant, not flashy, not politically dangerous—but utterly dependable. The kind of man you leave holding the line, the keys, or the truth.

He is, quietly, one of the **load-bearing stones of the household**.

> “You almost forget he’s there—until you need someone who won’t let you down.” — Sorek Redmarch

---

## **Basic Information**

- **Age:** 39
- **Height:** 5’8”
- **Build:** Lean, wiry, deceptively strong
- **Hair:** Dark brown, short and practical
- **Eyes:** Brown
- **Notable Features:** Small scar on chin (jousting accident)

---

## **Heraldry**

**Blazon:**  
_Azure, a pale gules; in fess point a birch tree argent; in dexter flank an arming sword bendwise sinister argent; in sinister flank an arming sword bendwise argent._

### **Interpretation**

- **Azure (blue):** loyalty, service, endurance
- **Pale gules (red vertical):** duty carried through hardship
- **Birch tree argent:** resilience, renewal, rooted tradition
- **Crossed swords (offset):** readiness, but not aggression—defense, not glory

### **As a House Knight of Kharne**

Morkken now displays:

- **Personal arms** on shield and formal heraldry
- **Kharne colors (black and orange)** on surcoat and field gear
- Optional formal mark:
    - **Small canton** bearing Kharne device _or_
    - **Badge worn over the heart**

**Translation at the table:**  
He is **not independent anymore**—he is visibly _claimed_, but still himself.

---

## **Personality & Character**

- **Steadfast:** Does not waver once committed
- **Quietly ambitious:** Wants to matter—but won’t claw for it
- **Practical:** Solves problems without speeches
- **Chivalric:** Not performative—just built that way

He admires strength, especially the kind that doesn’t ask permission to exist.

Which is why Braga absolutely wrecks him.

---

## **Reputation (In Play)**

- “Reliable” (said with relief, not excitement)
- “Doesn’t make mistakes”
- “Not one for politics” (incorrect, he just refuses to play badly)
- “Safe to trust” (this is the big one)

Among soldiers:

> “If Morkken’s holding it, it’ll still be there when you get back.”

---

## **Appearance**

Nothing about him screams “hero.”

That’s the trick.

- Functional armor
- Clean but unadorned kit
- Moves like someone used to wearing weight
- Stands like someone who expects to be needed

He looks like a **retainer**—until he doesn’t break.

---

## **Holdings — Thistlefield**

A small ancestral estate on the edge of the King’s Preserve:

- Modest manor
- Few tenant farms
- Woodland access

Barely enough to sustain knightly status.

He makes it work anyway.

---

## **Skills & Achievements**

- Tournament veteran (endurance fighter, not flashy)
- Maintains estate under financial pressure
- Personally recommended into Kharne’s household
- Trusted with **responsibility, not glory**

---

## **Relationships**

**Orin Kharne**  
Respects him deeply. Sees him as the kind of lord worth following.

**Braga**  
Admiration bordering on painful. She is everything he thinks a knight should be—just louder and more terrifying.

**Beringer**  
Grateful. Knows exactly who opened the door for him.

**Venom**  
Terrified. Correctly.

**Blue Sylvanranth**  
Relaxes around her. She treats him like he matters.

**Kendal**  
Wary respect. Knows there’s steel under that control.

---

## **Challenges & Motivations**

- Wants to prove quiet service matters
- Knows his birth limits him
- Refuses to cut corners to get ahead

He is chasing **earned worth**, not status.

Which is a slower road—and a harder one.

---

## **Hobby — Woodcarving**

Keeps small carvings:

- Animals
- Figures
- Bits of memory

Precise. Patient. Quietly beautiful.

No one sees them unless he trusts them.

---

## **Equipment**

- **Armor:** Ancestral plate, maintained but not ornate
- **Surcoat:** Kharne black and orange over personal arms
- **Weapons:** Longsword, lance, round shield
- **Mount:** _Ironfoot_, dependable destrier

---

## **Role in the Household**

Morkken is not the tip of the spear.

He’s the part that keeps the spear from snapping.

- Holds positions
- Carries orders correctly
- Doesn’t panic when things go sideways

If Orin is the hammer and Braga is the fire—

Morkken is the **anvil that doesn’t crack**.

---

## **Legacy**

He will not be sung about.

He will be remembered.

Quietly. By the people who lived because he was there.
