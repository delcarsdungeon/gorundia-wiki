# Sir Aldos Verran

> [!quote] Sorek Redmarch
> Aldos is the only man I know who can gut a bandit with one hand and insult his cousin’s cooking with the other. If he ever stops laughing, it means the end is near.

## Overview

**Full Name:** Sir Aldos Verran  
**Title:** Knight-Champion of Southmere  
**Age:** 52  
**Height:** 6'4"  
**Weight:** 270 lbs  
**Affiliation:** Barony Southmere, County Blackvale, Duchy Grevandar  
**Lord Served:** Baron Cadaras Kendal  
**Residence:** Southmere Hall  

Sir Aldos Verran is the Knight-Champion of Southmere and the chief martial hand of [[Baron Cadaras Kendal]]. Towering, broad-shouldered, bald as a polished helm, and carrying a mustache with its own reputation, Aldos looks every inch the old campaigner who has seen too many fights and enjoyed a suspicious number of them.

He serves Baron Kendal as a friend more than a subordinate. Their loyalty was forged in muddy border skirmishes, hard choices, and long years of shared trust. Aldos respects Cadaras not because of rank, but because the baron earns the loyalty he receives.

## Appearance

![[Sir Aldos New.png|340]]

Aldos is immense, standing 6'4" and built with the sort of stubborn mass that makes him hard to move and harder to ignore. He is completely bald, with no living witness able to recall the last time he had hair.

His mustache is legendary. Thick, proud, and carefully kept, it has become almost as recognizable in Southmere as the man himself.

He rarely bothers with full armor unless the matter deserves it. Most trouble in Southmere becomes smaller when Aldos enters the room.

## Personality and Demeanor

Aldos is sardonic, sharp-eyed, and physically intimidating without needing to make a production of it. He speaks plainly, even to nobles from outside the barony, and has little patience for inflated titles, ornate speeches, or men who confuse rank with worth.

He favors scars, wit, competence, and people who understand when to stop talking.

Despite the rough edge, Aldos is deeply loyal to Baron Cadaras Kendal, the Kendal household, and the people of Southmere. He is not sentimental in public, but his loyalty is plain in the way he stands watch, remembers names, and quietly handles problems before they reach the baron’s door.

> [!quote] Sir Beringer Amadis
> If Aldos bows to you, you’ve either earned it—or he’s hiding the fact he’s about to lift you over his shoulder.

## Marriage to Mira

Aldos is married to [[Mira Verran]], a former field medic turned herbalist and quartermaster of Southmere’s militia.

Their marriage is famously blunt, affectionate, and sharp-tongued. They tease each other like soldiers before a brawl, trade corrections across crowded rooms, and appear to communicate entire arguments through looks, snorts, and unfinished insults.

Mira is one of the few people who can cut Aldos off mid-growl and live to do it again. Aldos, for his part, treats her judgment as battlefield-grade intelligence. He may grumble, but if Mira says a man is fevered, lying, starving, or about to do something stupid, Aldos believes her.

Their banter is part of Southmere Hall’s daily rhythm.

> [!quote] Mira Verran
> Aldos thinks glaring is a full sentence. Sadly, most men here have learned to understand him.

> [!quote] Aldos Verran
> Mira once told me I was too stubborn to die. I took it as a compliment. She says that was my first mistake.

Behind the teasing is real trust. Mira has patched Aldos up more times than either of them admits, and Aldos has more than once crossed a battlefield because he heard her voice in trouble. Neither would describe that as romance. Both would be lying.

## Family

Aldos and Mira have two sons.

### Taren Verran

**Age:** 27  
**Role:** Knight in service to [[Countess Zara Vallytrex]]

Taren serves in Countess Zara’s household. Blackvale gossip has made the usual insinuations about Zara’s knights, because apparently grain, taxes, and war were not enough to keep people busy. Taren’s reputation remains clean, helped by his own discipline and his father’s deeply educational glare.

### Jeren Verran

**Age:** 22  
**Role:** Logistics and town security in Southmere

Jeren is more tactician than warrior, though no less sharp. He works within Southmere’s logistics and town security, handling routes, watches, supply movement, and the quiet math that keeps a barony functional.

Aldos is proud of both sons, though he expresses this mostly through criticism, practical gifts, and pretending not to worry.

## Role in Southmere

As Knight-Champion, Aldos oversees Southmere’s militia and personal guard. He is responsible for Baron Kendal’s family escort and for keeping local trouble from becoming regional trouble.

He is a practical commander. He knows roads, watches, stores, horses, drunk soldiers, bad excuses, and the difference between panic and warning.

Across Blackvale, he is known for ending a barroom brawl with a broken stool and a bad pun, and for once hauling an entire wounded wagon back to camp after the oxen bolted.

## Reputation

Aldos is widely regarded as one of Southmere’s pillars: hard to move, harder to fool, and reliable when things turn ugly.

Soldiers respect him because he has done the work. Common folk trust him because he does not treat their lives as abstractions. Nobles tolerate him because he is useful, dangerous, and usually correct, which is an obnoxious combination.

His reputation rests less on stories than on the irritating fact that most of the stories are true.

- He keeps an old drinking horn engraved with: **S.R. owes me two chickens and a cloak.**
- The engraving is an old joke from his early years with [[Sorek Redmarch]].
