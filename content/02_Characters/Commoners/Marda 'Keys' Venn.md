# Marda “Keys” Venn

> “She bought my debt. Then she wrote the number down.
>
> “It stopped growing.
>
> “You may not understand how honest that felt.”

## Overview

**Marda “Keys” Venn** is the house steward and acting manager of the [[The Iron Rose|Iron Rose]].

When [[Volika Coinmane]] is absent, Marda runs the establishment.

Volika owns the house.

Marda makes certain it survives the evening.

She manages the rooms, staff, payroll, supplies, barred customers, healers, and the thousand daily problems created whenever money, drink, loneliness, and poor judgment are placed beneath the same roof.

Everyone in the Iron Rose answers to Marda while Volika is away.

That includes [[Lily]].

---

## Appearance



Marda is a half-orc woman in her late thirties.

She is tall, broad through the shoulders, and carries herself with the careful stillness of someone who learned young that visible anger gives other people something to use.

Her hair is dark, streaked early with grey, and usually tied tightly behind her head.

A heavy ring of keys hangs from her belt.

It includes keys for:

- guest rooms
- staff quarters
- storerooms
- the cellar
- the strongbox
- the rear gate
- the hidden shelter beneath the house

The keys announce her before she speaks.

This is usually enough.

---

## Background

Marda was born enslaved.

After escaping, she entered a relationship with a man who treated her freedom as an accounting error.

He controlled her wages, claimed ownership of her debts, and used violence whenever arithmetic failed to produce obedience.

Volika bought the debt.

She wrote the exact amount into the Iron Rose ledger and gave Marda terms.

The number did not increase.

There were no invented penalties, interest charges, damaged-property fees, or sudden discoveries that freedom cost slightly more than expected.

Marda paid it off.

When the final coin was entered, Volika closed the account and told her she could leave.

Marda stayed.

Not because Volika rescued her.

Because it was the first bargain in Marda’s life that ended when she fulfilled it.

---

## Personality

Marda is controlled, practical, and difficult to provoke.

She is less cynical than Volika, though this is not a particularly demanding moral achievement.

She speaks quietly and expects people to listen the first time.

Marda is:

- patient with frightened workers
- polite with customers
- suspicious of sudden generosity
- careful with money
- merciless toward repeat offenders
- deeply uncomfortable with Volika’s recruitment habits

She does not openly challenge Volika often.

When she does, Volika listens.

Not because Marda outranks her.

Because Marda rarely wastes an objection.

---

## Running the Iron Rose

Marda knows:

- every worker’s real name and working name
- who may receive which customers
- which patrons are barred
- which employees need medicine
- which rooms contain hidden bells
- how much money may safely remain downstairs
- who is frightened
- who is lying
- when Lily should intervene

She has authority to:

- close the house
- refuse any customer
- pay wages
- summon the healer
- hire temporary guards
- relocate threatened workers
- spend house funds during an emergency
- order Lily to remove someone

She cannot:

- sell or mortgage the Iron Rose
- alter long-term agreements
- authorize major loans
- hire senior staff permanently
- overturn Volika’s standing word

Volika trusts Marda because she protects the business.

Marda protects the workers because she remembers what it feels like to be listed among the assets.

---

## Marda and Volika

Marda does not call Volika kind.

She calls her reliable.

She understands that Volika made money from her desperation.

She also understands that Volika kept the bargain exactly as written.

Their relationship rests upon that uncomfortable foundation.

Marda serves as one of the few people capable of telling Volika:

> “That is a girl, not a prospect.”

Volika may argue.

She may roll her eyes.

She may produce several technically accurate points concerning wages, hunger, and available choices.

But she hears Marda.

Sometimes that is enough.

---

## Marda and Lily

Marda and Lily work with practiced efficiency.

Marda decides what needs to happen.

Lily makes it happen.

Typical instructions include:

> “The tall one leaves.”

> “The woman stays.”

> “No damage unless he reaches for steel.”

Lily obeys Marda without hesitation while Volika is absent.

She refers to her simply as **Keys**.

---

## Why She Is Called Keys

The nickname comes from the iron ring at Marda’s waist.

It also reflects her role within the house.

Marda controls:

- who enters
- who leaves
- which doors remain closed
- which secrets remain locked away
- who receives sanctuary

Volika owns the Iron Rose.

Marda holds the keys.
