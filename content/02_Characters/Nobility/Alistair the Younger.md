# Alistair, Lord Blackbriar

> [!quote] Sorek Redmarch
> Alistair the Younger is no fool. He knows his father sees farther, his sister thinks faster, and Beringer’s road has climbed higher. Lesser men turn that into poison. Alistair turned it into pride, which is rarer than genius and usually more useful.

![[Amadis the Younger.png|320]]![[Milena Wedding.png|320]]

> [!quote] Alistair the Younger
> A scar is a reminder of a battle fought and a lesson learned. I have many, and I’ve earned every one of them.

Alistair Amadis the Younger, usually styled **Lord Blackbriar** and often called **Amadis the Younger**, is the heir of [[County Blackbriar]] and son of [[Alistair Amadis|Count Alistair Amadis]].

He is a respected knight of [[House Amadis]], best known for his skill with the lance, his battlefield courage, and his uneasy position in a family full of people who cast very long shadows.

He has recently married [[Milena Orenic]], niece of [[Duke Vladek Orenic]]. The match is politically valuable, linking [[House Amadis]] and [[House Orenic]] at a dangerous moment.

It also appears to be personally sincere.

Perhaps too sincere.

[[Beringer]] suspects a love potion or some other form of alchemical or magical influence. No proof has yet surfaced, and no public accusation has been made.

## Appearance

Alistair is an imposing knight, standing roughly **6'2"** and weighing around **220 lbs**. He has a strong, knightly build hardened by years of training, tournament riding, and real battle.

His body bears numerous scars from near-death encounters and battlefield injuries. These lend him a rugged, experienced air, though he does not flaunt them for effect.

His brown hair is kept tidy. His deep brown eyes often carry a thoughtful, almost forlorn expression, as if he is weighing matters before speaking. Those who know him well do not consider him brooding or dour. He is contemplative, measured, and often gentler in manner than his scars suggest.

Despite his imposing build, Alistair has a humble and approachable bearing, shaped strongly by his father’s influence.

## Personality and Values

Alistair shares much of his father’s humility and strong sense of **noblesse oblige**. He believes deeply that nobility exists to serve, protect, and inspire rather than merely command.

> [!quote] Alistair the Younger
> To lead is not to demand loyalty but to inspire it.

He is chivalric, noble, brave, and deeply committed to the ideals of knighthood. He believes in courtesy, courage, mercy under discipline, and the duty of the strong to stand between danger and those who cannot meet it.

Unlike his father, Alistair takes more open pleasure in honors earned on the battlefield and in tournaments. He does not chase glory recklessly, but he values recognition when it is won honestly.

Everyone who knows the family understands the difficult truth: Alistair is no fool, but he is not the strategic genius his father is.

He knows this.

That knowledge has shaped him more than wounded him. [[Alistair Amadis|Count Alistair Amadis]] is a commander of rare strategic gifts. [[Sophia Blackbriar]] is simply brilliant, with a mind sharper and faster than most people are comfortable admitting. [[Beringer|Beringer]] has risen through war, revelation, and terrible responsibility into a kind of glory that few knights ever touch.

Alistair has lived beside all of that without becoming jealous.

He is proud of them.

Alistair occupies one of the hardest places in a noble house: the good knight standing beside rarer gifts. His father sees farther, his sister thinks faster, and Beringer has risen into a kind of hard-won legend. Alistair knows all of this. It stings sometimes. It has never made him smaller.

That steadiness matters.

Alistair’s self-knowledge is one of his better qualities. He knows he must choose his ground carefully. He knows when to defer to better minds. He knows when courage is needed and when pride would only make him expensive to rescue.

This does not make him passive.

It makes him disciplined.

He is:

- honorable
- chivalric
- brave
- humble without being meek
- quietly proud
- protective of family
- aware of his limitations
- capable of taking joy in the accomplishments of others
- occasionally too eager to prove he belongs among them

He has spent much of his life trying to carve out a place that is truly his own, not by surpassing his family, but by being worthy of them.

## Skills and Accomplishments

Alistair is best known as a **master of the lance**.

He rose to prominence through rigorous knightly training, tournament success, and proven battlefield courage. His reputation was sealed during the final battle against [[House Golitsyn]], where he faced and killed the Golitsyn heir in a mounted clash.

That feat secured his place in the martial history of [[House Amadis]] and reinforced his reputation as one of the notable cavalry knights of his generation.

Though best known for skill in the saddle, Alistair is also a capable tactician and trained in the broader arts of war. Some call him a prodigy. Others whisper that House Amadis has been effective at polishing his legend.

Both may contain truth.

> [!quote] [[Sir Beringer Amadis]], reminiscing
> He’s good. Maybe if he learned how to parry properly, he wouldn’t look like he tried to headbutt a millstone.

## Marriage to Milena Orenic

Alistair has recently married [[Milena Orenic]], the formidable niece of [[Duke Vladek Orenic]].

The marriage began within a political framework. [[House Orenic]] had reason to pursue the connection, and [[House Amadis]] had reason to consider it valuable. What followed, however, appears to have become personal very quickly.

Milena herself was clear with [[Sophia Blackbriar]]:

> [!quote] [[Milena Orenic]]
> They pushed. We met. We spoke. We laughed. We stopped pretending otherwise.

She also described arranged marriage in terms Sophia found both unsettling and persuasive:

> [!quote] [[Milena Orenic]]
> Arranged marriages are not cages. They are frameworks. Inside framework, one may still build.

Alistair is visibly and deeply in love with Milena. He is affectionate, attentive, and protective in a way that has become difficult for the family to ignore.

He frequently describes Milena as **delicate**, a claim that has caused several witnesses to reassess either Alistair’s judgment or their understanding of the word.

Milena appears quietly amused by this.

## The Suspicion

Alistair’s devotion to Milena appears sincere.

That sincerity is part of the concern.

[[Beringer]] suspects that Alistair may have been affected by a love potion or related influence. This suspicion has not become a public accusation, and Beringer does not currently appear to believe Milena knowingly enchanted him.

The concern is broader:

- [[House Orenic]] has enemies.
- [[House Amadis]] has enemies.
- A sudden marriage alliance carries major political weight.
- Alistair’s emotional intensity is unusually visible.
- Manipulating an heir through romance would be a practical and dangerous move.

For now, the suspicion remains quiet.

No proof has been found.

No accusation has been made.

The matter remains a private concern within the family orbit.

## Current Status: Kept from the War Host

Alistair is **not being allowed to join the main war host**.

His father, [[Alistair Amadis|Count Alistair Amadis]], has kept both Alistair and Milena away from the primary campaign forces. Officially, this is prudent protection of the heir and his new bride during a dangerous military crisis.

Unofficially, it also keeps Alistair from riding into the campaign while distracted, compromised, newly married, or otherwise vulnerable to political manipulation.

This frustrates him deeply.

Alistair wants to serve. He wants to prove that marriage has not softened him, that his duties remain clear, and that he is still worthy of his name.

His father is unmoved.

This restraint may be wise.

It is also painful.

## Relationship with His Father

Alistair deeply admires [[Alistair Amadis|Count Alistair Amadis]].

His father is one of the great figures in his life: model, commander, judge, and shadow. Alistair has come to terms with the fact that he may never equal his father’s strategic brilliance, but that acceptance does not remove the pressure.

He wants his father’s respect.

He already has much of it.

The difficulty is that Alistair still wants to feel that the respect was earned separately from blood, title, or expectation.

His father’s decision to keep him from the main war host has created strain. Alistair understands the logic, but understanding does not make confinement feel less like distrust.

## Relationship with Sophia Blackbriar

[[Sophia Blackbriar]] is Alistair’s younger sister.

Their bond is strong, frank, and vital. Alistair is protective of Sophia, but he respects her intellect and independence. He knows she is sharper than he is in many areas and does not resent it.

Sophia’s conversation with Milena helped stabilize some of the internal family uncertainty around the marriage. Sophia recognized that Milena was not simply being traded, and Milena recognized Sophia as someone worth speaking to honestly.

This has created a potentially important bond between the two women.

Alistair may not fully understand how significant that could become.

## Relationship with Beringer

Alistair and [[Sir Beringer Amadis]] grew up as brothers and rivals within [[House Amadis]].

Their relationship has always carried competition, affection, and mutual respect. They competed for attention, recognition, and martial distinction, but the rivalry was healthy and grounded in trust.

During the Amadis-Golitsyn conflict, the two served through terrible fighting together. At the close of that war, Alistair was the one who found Beringer on the battlefield, still impaled on [[Elizaveta Golitsyn]]’s spear and clutching her remains.

If Elizaveta haunts Beringer’s dreams, that image likely haunts Alistair as well.

The revelation of Beringer’s Vogel bloodline unsettled Alistair. It changed the shape of the family’s history and raised implications no one could easily ignore. Yet it did not break the bond between them.

> [!quote] Alistair the Younger
> Bloodlines mean little when weighed against bonds forged in trust and love. Sir Beringer is my brother in every way that matters.

Beringer’s suspicion regarding Milena and possible enchantment has created a new strain. Alistair may view the suspicion as insulting if raised poorly. He is likely to hear concern as distrust, especially while already frustrated by being kept from the war host.

Even so, their bond remains real.

## Relationship with Elizaveta Golitsyn

Alistair’s relationship with [[Elizaveta Golitsyn]] was uneasy but respectful.

He accepted his mother’s decision to bring Elizaveta into the household, but he was never fully comfortable with her closeness to the family. Elizaveta, for her part, worked hard to prove herself worthy of [[House Amadis]]’ trust.

Over time, they became cordial, though never truly close.

At the end of the Amadis-Golitsyn House War, Alistair was the one who found Beringer and Elizaveta together on the battlefield: Beringer impaled on her spear, Elizaveta dead, and the scene soaked in the terrible intimacy of what had happened between them.

This moment remains one of the defining wounds of Alistair’s war memory.

## Relationship with Milena

Alistair adores [[Milena Orenic]].

He sees her gentleness before her strength, her careful manners before her size, and her mind before the obvious fact that she is physically formidable.

He treats her with genuine tenderness, not merely political courtesy.

This makes the marriage unusually promising.

It also makes it politically dangerous.

If someone manipulated his feelings, then [[House Amadis]] may have been compromised through its heir. If no one manipulated him, then those who suspected it will have damaged trust at a fragile moment.

Milena, for her part, seems to understand more of the situation than she says aloud.

## Personality Quirks

- Often appears thoughtful or forlorn when lost in thought.
- Wears his scars without vanity but does take quiet pride in surviving what caused them.
- Enjoys earned praise more than his father does.
- Can become too eager to prove himself if he feels sheltered.
- Has a gentle and approachable manner despite his battlefield reputation.
- Tends to romanticize duty, marriage, and courage more than his family’s colder strategists would prefer.

## Rumors and Whispers

> “He finally found a woman who can stand beside him.”

> “Stand beside him? She could carry him.”

> “He looks at her like a bard wrote the lighting.”

> “Beringer thinks it’s alchemy.”

> “The elder Amadis won’t let him near the war host. Says it’s to protect the succession.”

> “Protect it from the enemy?”

> “Possibly from himself.”

Older rumors claimed that Alistair’s difficulty finding a bride came not from lack of options, but from high standards and a desire for a partner who could truly challenge and engage him.

His marriage to Milena may have answered that question.

Or complicated it.

## Current Story Function

Alistair currently serves as:

- the protected heir of [[County Blackbriar]]
- a major knightly figure of [[House Amadis]]
- a bridge between [[House Amadis]] and [[House Orenic]]
- a possible victim of magical or alchemical manipulation
- a source of tension with [[Beringer]]
- a reminder of the Amadis-Golitsyn war wounds
- a symbol of what House Amadis is trying not to lose in the current crisis

He is not absent from the main host because he lacks courage.

He is absent because his father is preserving the succession, limiting risk, and preventing a newly married heir from becoming a political casualty.

## Table Use

Use Alistair when the campaign needs:

- A good knight living under the pressure of a greater father and stranger brother.
- A noble heir who enjoys glory but still believes in duty.
- A personal face for [[House Amadis]] succession concerns.
- Tension around [[Milena Orenic]] and possible manipulation.
- A brotherly challenge to [[Beringer]] that is emotional rather than hostile.
- A reminder that the Golitsyn war scarred more people than Beringer alone.

## Roleplaying Alistair

Alistair speaks with calm courtesy and a thoughtful cadence. He is not slow, but he often pauses before committing to a statement.

He is most direct around family.

He is most guarded around anyone implying he is being protected, manipulated, or softened by marriage.

Common lines:

> “A scar is a reminder of a battle fought and a lesson learned. I have many, and I’ve earned every one of them.”

> “To lead is not to demand loyalty but to inspire it.”

> “Duty calls a man forward. Wisdom decides whether he should be allowed to answer.”

> “I know my father’s shadow. I have lived in it long enough to know where it ends.”

> “Bloodlines mean little when weighed against bonds forged in trust and love.”

> “If there is concern for me, speak it plainly. I have little patience for being managed by whispers.”

## GM Notes

Alistair should not be played as foolish simply because he is in love.

He is a competent, honorable knight with real accomplishments. His emotional visibility around Milena is unusual, but not automatically proof of enchantment.

The best tension is ambiguity:

- He may be enchanted.
- He may simply be deeply in love.
- He may be both politically useful and personally sincere.
- Beringer may be right to worry and wrong in how he handles it.
- Alistair may be insulted and still need protection.

His absence from the main war host should frustrate him. That frustration can drive future scenes with his father, Sophia, Milena, and Beringer.

The key is that Alistair is not weak.

He is precious.

That is worse.
