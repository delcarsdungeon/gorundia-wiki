Allynnora Vogel is an important genealogical bridge between the Vogel ducal bloodline and the royal line of [[House Ryomar]].

Born the daughter of [[Maletryx Vogel]] and [[Ellysara Mortrex]], she married [[Olsten Ryomar]] in 1168 OR. Through that marriage, she became the mother of [[Priam Ryomar]], [[Glandyr Ryomar]], and [[Lydena Ryomar]].

Her significance lies less in recorded deeds and more in bloodline consequence. Through Allynnora, Vogel blood entered the immediate Ryomar royal line, making her a major ancestral link in later questions of succession, inheritance, and shared blood.
