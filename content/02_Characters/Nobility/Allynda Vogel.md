Allynda Vogel is presently known primarily through noble genealogical records as the spouse of [[Maxen Vogel]] and mother of [[Galmyr Vogel]].

Her place in the line matters because Galmyr would become Duke of Grevandar after the deaths of [[Maletryx Vogel]] and Maxen at Red Gorge. Allynda herself remains thinly documented, because apparently the archives decided “wife and mother of a duke” was plenty, then went to lunch.

## Relationships

### Maxen Vogel

[[Maxen Vogel]] is listed as Allynda Vogel’s spouse.

Their marriage produced [[Galmyr Vogel]], the later Duke of Grevandar.

### Galmyr Vogel

[[Galmyr Vogel]] is listed as the child of Allynda Vogel and [[Maxen Vogel]].

Through Galmyr, Allynda stands in the direct ancestral line leading to later Vogel power in Grevandar.
