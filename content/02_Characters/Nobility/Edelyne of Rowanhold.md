# Lady Edelyne of Rowanhold

> “She’s carried more than her share of burdens, what with that first husband of hers—he never understood the worth of a woman like her. I hope she finds what she deserves this time around… or maybe, if we’re honest, her first real chance at it.”  
> — Sorek Redmarch

---

## Basic Information
**Full Name:** Lady Edelyne of Rowanhold  
**Age:** 30  
**Status:** Widow of a lesser knight; no children  
**Occupation:** Lady and overseer of Rowanhold  

---

## Overview
Lady Edelyne is the gentle yet capable steward of Rowanhold, a modest estate of meadows and woodland. Widowed young, she assumed control of her lands rather than retreating into dependency, earning the respect of her tenants through patience, fairness, and quiet resolve.

She is not a figure of courtly ambition, nor one given to intrigue. Yet circumstances—chief among them her association with [[Cornelia Bordella]]—have drawn her into a wider and more dangerous world than she would ever have chosen.

---

## Appearance
**Height:** 5’5”  
**Build:** Soft and solid, with the grounded presence of someone accustomed to real work  
**Hair:** Honey-blonde, worn simply—braided or loose  
**Eyes:** Hazel, calm and thoughtful  
**Notable Feature:** A small silver ring worn on her right hand, a keepsake from her late husband  

---

## Personality
Edelyne is warm, patient, and possessed of a quiet dignity that cannot be easily shaken. She listens more than she speaks, and when she does speak, it is measured and considered.

She does not seek authority, but neither does she yield it lightly. Beneath her gentleness lies a firm and immovable will when it comes to the safety and well-being of her household.

She is, above all else, steady.

---

## Skills & Hobbies
- Skilled in **herbal remedies** and practical healing  
- Maintains a well-regarded **herb garden** at Rowanhold  
- Enjoys **weaving simple tapestries**, favoring natural patterns and subdued colors  

---

## History
Edelyne was married young to a knight of modest standing. The match was one of obligation rather than affection. Her husband was often absent, and when present, brought with him a temper that left the household cold and strained.

His death in a border skirmish left her both unmoored and quietly relieved.

Rather than return to dependence under another household, Edelyne chose to remain at Rowanhold and assume control herself. Over time, she restored stability to the estate, earning loyalty not through force, but through fairness and consistency.

## Current Life
Edelyne travels at times with the extended entourage of [[Cornelia Bordella]], offering her practical knowledge of land management and herbal lore when needed.

In recent months, however, that association has become a source of growing uncertainty. With Cornelia’s position destabilized and events spiraling beyond quiet control, Edelyne finds herself increasingly exposed to dangers far removed from Rowanhold’s gentle boundaries.

Her visits home have grown less frequent. Her letters, more so.

---

## Relationship with Sir Morkken

**[[Sir Morkken]] — House Knight of Kharne**

What exists between Edelyne and Morkken is not a romance of bold gestures or whispered scandal.

It is something slower. More deliberate.

Built on shared silences, mutual respect, and the rare comfort of being seen plainly.

Neither is given to indulgence. Neither speaks more than is proper. And yet, in the space between their words, something enduring has taken root.

Circumstance has not been kind to it.

Morkken’s duties bind him tightly to Baron Kharne’s service. Edelyne’s responsibilities tie her to Rowanhold—and now, uncomfortably, to Cornelia’s uncertain orbit. The distance between them is not merely measured in miles, but in obligation.

They have not spoken of marriage.

Not directly.

But the question lingers—unvoiced, understood.

If it were to come to pass, it would not be simple. Their lands lie far apart, and for nobles of their modest standing, distance is not easily managed. One would have to yield ground—either in residence or in duty—or accept a life spent largely apart.

For now, reality has chosen for them.

They write.

Carefully.  
Properly.  
Often.

And with each passing letter, Morkken’s concern grows—not of rejection, but of absence. Of silence. Of a road that simply… stops returning her to him.
