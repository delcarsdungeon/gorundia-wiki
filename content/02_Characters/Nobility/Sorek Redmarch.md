# Sir Sorek Redmarch

> “Most disasters begin with a clever man explaining why the usual rules should not apply to him.”

**Sir Sorek Redmarch** is a veteran Gorundian knight, landed lord, investigator, and field commander presently serving as a **Lieutenant-Marshal of Gorundia**.

Once the trusted right hand of [[Theron Sylvanranth]], Sorek spent decades handling matters Theron could not safely entrust to flatterers, idealists, or men with delicate consciences. He hunted corruption, commanded soldiers, questioned prisoners, enforced royal law, and repaired dangerous situations before they became public catastrophes.

He is now one of the Crown’s most experienced operational commanders. He currently holds the outer approaches to [[Direhold]], preventing the fortress from being reinforced, resupplied, or quietly emptied while the royal armies complete the destruction of [[House Vogel]]’s remaining power.

Sorek has outlived enemies, commanders, friends, and one of his daughters.

He regards this less as an accomplishment than a clerical error.

## Appearance

![[Pasted image 20260713182555.png]]

Sorek stands approximately **six feet four inches tall**, though age, old wounds, and decades spent leaning over maps have given him a slight stoop.

He remains broad through the shoulders and heavily built. In his youth, he was regarded as a giant of a man. Even now, he becomes imposing when he bothers to straighten fully.

His features include:

- Salt-and-pepper hair, once dark brown
- Dark brown eyes
- A weathered and heavily lined face
- A jagged scar across his right cheek
- Large, scarred hands
- The stiffness of a man carrying several injuries that never received proper time to heal

He dresses plainly when not under arms. His clothing is durable, comfortable, and generally older than the fashions around him.

His armor and weapons are maintained with exacting care.

Sorek does not wear his family heraldry upon his shield or surcoat.

## Bearing

Sorek rarely raises his voice.

He does not need to.

He carries himself with the quiet authority of a man accustomed to entering rooms after everyone else has failed. He listens more than he speaks, watches hands as often as faces, and usually knows who is lying before they finish deciding upon the lie.

His resting expression suggests he has already heard the excuse being offered and found it disappointing the first three times.

Despite his size, Sorek has spent years learning how not to loom over frightened people. When he wishes to intimidate someone, the change is subtle.

He merely stops trying not to.

## Personality

Sorek is gruff, direct, observant, and possessed of an inconvenient moral compass.

He is loyal, but never blindly. He respects rank when rank is supported by duty and competence. He has little patience for nobles who expect a title to perform the work of character.

His humor is dry, cutting, and often delivered with the expression of a man commenting upon unfortunate weather.

He uses humor to:

- Break panic before it spreads
- Deflate noble posturing
- Keep soldiers listening
- Give frightened people room to recover
- Avoid speaking directly about things that still hurt
- Warn young knights that they are about to do something memorable for all the wrong reasons

Sorek’s kindness usually disguises itself as logistics.

He does not tell a wounded soldier that he cares. He finds the soldier a dry bed, pays the healer, sends word to the family, and threatens the quartermaster until proper food appears.

## House Redmarch

[[House Redmarch]] is an ancient but diminished Gorundian frontier house.

It possesses respectable lineage, a long history of Crown service, and considerably less wealth than the great houses whose messes Sorek is frequently expected to clean up.

The family seat is [[Redmarch Tower]], a fortified holding within [[Redmarch Vale]]. The estate guards an important river crossing and serves as a local center of Crown authority.

The duties of Redmarch Tower include:

- Guarding the river crossing
- Collecting lawful tolls
- Maintaining signal beacons
- Enforcing Crown law
- Conducting frontier patrols
- Protecting nearby settlements
- Watching for raiders and smugglers
- Monitoring goblin movement
- Providing a fortified refuge during unrest

The estate includes:

- Redmarch Tower
- A modest manor
- A small chapel
- Agricultural lands
- Timber rights
- A river landing
- A mining settlement
- Toll and crossing rights

## The Redmarch Silver

Sorek possesses rights to a **minor but reliable silver vein** within Redmarch Vale.

The holding and its extraction rights were secured during the ascendancy of [[Theron Sylvanranth]], who ensured that his trusted commander possessed an income independent of shifting political favor.

The mine is not among Gorundia’s great sources of wealth.

It does provide enough steady income to:

- Maintain Redmarch Tower
- Pay household retainers properly
- Equip Sorek’s sworn men
- Keep winter stores full
- Repair roads and fortifications
- Support the families of men lost in service
- Employ competent clerks and stewards
- Refuse improper gifts without worrying how his household will eat

Sorek is comfortably wealthy by knightly standards but lives without ostentation.

His silver becomes wages, stonework, grain, steel, lamp oil, horseflesh, and roof repairs.

Very little becomes jewelry.

## Early Life

Sorek was born in **1177 OR** to [[Tymek Redmarch]] and [[Mariska Redmarch]].

He grew up in Gorundia’s frontier country, where military service was less a profession than an expected stage of adulthood. The Redmarch family had old blood and limited means, leaving Sorek with a name worth respecting but little room for idleness.

He entered royal service young and distinguished himself through discipline, physical courage, and an unusual ability to recognize when a superior officer was about to make an avoidable mistake.

Unlike many promising young knights, Sorek survived long enough for that habit to become useful.

## Military Career

Sorek fought during the **Eastern Reclamation**, earning a reputation as a capable field officer and determined close combatant.

He was never the most elegant knight in Gorundia.

He was the one still organizing the retreat after the elegant knights had ridden into the trap.

His military strengths include:

- Infantry command
- Cavalry command
- Defensive operations
- Siege containment
- Counter-ambush tactics
- Military intelligence
- Prisoner handling
- Interrogation
- Logistics
- Morale management
- Identifying competent subordinates
- Turning confused situations into immediate orders

His preferred approach is patient, methodical, and deeply unpleasant for the enemy.

Sorek does not particularly enjoy glorious charges. He prefers preventing the enemy from sleeping, eating, communicating, retreating, or receiving reinforcements until surrender begins to resemble a sensible career decision.

## Service to Theron Sylvanranth

Sorek entered the service of [[Theron Sylvanranth]] during the later years of  [[The Caervane Reckoning]].

Theron had begun systematically dismantling the Dominion network responsible for the murder of [[Rowena Caervane]]. Sorek recognized both the justice of the campaign and the danger of leaving Theron surrounded entirely by men who agreed with him.

Their partnership lasted for decades.

Sorek became Theron’s:

- Field commander
- Investigator
- Enforcer
- Interrogator
- Military adviser
- Political troubleshooter
- Trusted critic
- Executor of lawful but unpleasant duties

Sorek’s loyalty to Theron was deep but never blind.

He understood Theron’s virtues, his grief, and his capacity for ruthlessness. He was one of the few men able to tell Theron that he was wrong without flattering him or challenging him merely for the satisfaction of doing so.

Theron trusted Sorek because Sorek did not confuse loyalty with obedience.

Sorek remained near Theron until his death during the attack on [[Stormarrow]].

## Reeve of Bard’s Gate

Sorek later served as a reeve and senior Crown authority in [[Bard's Gate]].

He was responsible for keeping order during a period when the city’s government, noble interests, guilds, criminal organizations, and foreign agents were all attempting to occupy the same streets without admitting they were at war.

Sorek’s administration was firm but pragmatic.

He understood that law in Bard’s Gate could not survive solely through punishment. It also required:

- Reliable witnesses
- Paid watchmen
- Useful informants
- Carefully chosen compromises
- Officials who could distinguish a criminal from a nuisance
- The occasional willingness to let one bastard lead him to a worse bastard

He disliked city politics and was exceptionally good at them.

This was not a contradiction he enjoyed examining.

## Lieutenant-Marshal of Gorundia

Following the fall of Theron and the widening national crisis, Sorek entered the service of [[Duke Beringer Amadis]] and the Crown as a **Lieutenant-Marshal of Gorundia**.

He does not regard Beringer as a replacement for Theron.

Beringer replaced Theron structurally, not personally.

Sorek respects Beringer’s courage, judgment, and willingness to bear responsibility. He also recognizes that Beringer has a habit of carrying enough duties to kill a lesser man and occasionally dropping the one task that appeared harmless.

Sorek will criticize him directly when necessary.

He will also defend him from criticism offered by people who have carried considerably less.

## Current Operations at Direhold

Sorek presently commands forces holding the approaches to [[Direhold]].

His responsibility is not to storm the fortress before the Crown is ready. His task is to make certain those inside cannot improve their position.

His operations include:

- Cutting supply traffic
- Searching wagons
- Seizing military provisions
- Turning away unauthorized travelers
- Intercepting messengers
- Monitoring hidden roads
- Testing the fortress command structure
- Preventing breakout attempts
- Denying reinforcement
- Maintaining a stable perimeter

Forces assigned or attached to his command include elements of:

- The [[Anvilstorm Brigade]]
- The [[Black Stag Lancers]]
- Royal infantry
- Scouts and outriders
- Engineers and support personnel

Sorek is not leading the glorious assault.

He is doing the less romantic work of ensuring nobody inside eats, escapes, receives orders, or becomes clever.

## Caelina Redmarch

[[Caelina Redmarch]] is Sorek’s wife and the practical foundation of House Redmarch.

While Sorek spent much of their marriage campaigning, investigating treason, or walking into political fires, Caelina managed the machinery of their life.

Her responsibilities include:

- Estate accounts
- Household correspondence
- Mine administration
- Toll records
- Stores and provisioning
- Retainer families
- Tenant disputes
- Servant management
- Necessary reminders that soldiers cannot eat promises

Caelina is Sorek’s closest counsel.

She understands him well enough to distinguish between silence caused by thought, silence caused by anger, and silence caused by the fact that he has not slept in two days and is becoming unreasonable.

Sorek trusts her judgment without reservation.

He may still argue with it.

## Valeara Vorthal

Sorek’s elder daughter, [[Valeara Vorthal]], is married to [[Baron Kelric Vorthal]] of [[Rystwell]].

Valeara is warm, kind, socially capable, and less interested than her father in treating every conversation as reconnaissance.

She inherited his stubbornness without his suspicion.

Her marriage to Kelric is considered a strong and successful match.

Sorek genuinely likes his son-in-law, a fact that initially caused some alarm among people familiar with Sorek’s standards.

Valeara and Kelric currently have no established child. Until they produce an heir, Kelric’s younger brother, [[Ser Arthon Vorthal]], remains heir to Rystwell.

## Baron Kelric Vorthal

[[Baron Kelric Vorthal]] is the man Sorek considers worthy of his surviving daughter.

Kelric is:

- A capable military commander
- A disciplined lord
- Personally honorable
- Fair with tenants and soldiers
- Loyal to [[Duchy Ravenster]]
- Loyal to the Crown
- Practical in the administration of Rystwell
- Uninterested in using his marriage for personal advancement

Sorek trusts Kelric with Valeara.

There is no greater endorsement he can give.

His only serious complaint is that Kelric is exactly the sort of decent, dutiful man likely to die performing some heroic necessity when a less responsible man would have found an excuse to remain safely behind.

Following the attack on Stormarrow, Kelric informed his liege, gathered his best household troops, and rode to protect the Redmarch family and support [[Orin Kharne]].

He did not send condolences and remain home.

Sorek remembers that.

## Sarielle Redmarch

[[Sarielle Redmarch]] was Sorek and Caelina’s younger daughter.

She died at **seventeen years of age** during the attack on [[Stormarrow]].

Sarielle was bright, affectionate, brave, and far less hardened than many of the young women around Theron’s household. She was friendly with [[Myra Durathen]] and spent time among the younger noblewomen, attendants, and protégés gathered around Stormarrow.

When the attack came, Sarielle found herself near one of the Raven Queen’s [[Raven Knights]].

She had only a dagger.

She deliberately drew the creature away from the Baroness and from others who could not defend themselves.

Sarielle had no realistic chance of defeating it.

She chose her ground and held it anyway.

Her death bought other people time to escape.

Later, [[Lady Calira Meroven]] told Sorek that Sarielle had done the Redmarch name credit.

Sorek did not answer immediately.

He rarely speaks about his daughter.

When he does, he does not call her foolish.

He does not call her fearless.

He says she knew what would happen and stood anyway.

## The Loss at Stormarrow

The attack on Stormarrow killed both [[Theron Sylvanranth]] and Sarielle.

For Sorek, the event cannot be divided neatly between professional and personal loss.

In a single attack, he lost:

- His commander
- His oldest professional companion
- The man around whom much of his adult life had been organized
- His youngest daughter
- Any remaining belief that competence and preparation guarantee survival

Sorek did not collapse.

He continued issuing orders.

Those who know him understand this was not proof that the deaths did not break him.

It was proof that they did.

## Blue Sylvanranth

Sorek helped train [[Blue Sylvanranth]].

He regarded Blue as brave, compassionate, impulsive, and badly in need of learning how cruel the world could be without becoming cruel herself.

Her spirit reminded him of Sarielle.

This became considerably harder to bear after Stormarrow.

Sorek’s approach to training Blue and other younger knights is built around two conflicting goals:

- Make them hard enough to survive
- Avoid destroying the qualities that make their survival worthwhile

He has seen commanders turn frightened young people into effective weapons.

He is less impressed by that accomplishment than he once was.

## Dana Sylvanranth

Sorek has a strong professional relationship with [[Dana Sylvanranth]].

They understand one another well enough to disagree without turning the disagreement into a contest of pride.

Dana respects his experience. Sorek respects her clarity, discipline, and willingness to make ugly decisions without pretending they are beautiful.

Their conversations are generally blunt, analytical, and dry.

By the standards of their social circle, this constitutes warmth.

## Orin Kharne

Sorek respects [[Orin Kharne]] as a younger commander with a brutal exterior and a knight’s soul.

He understands Orin’s instincts and trusts him in battle, though he occasionally worries that Orin regards personal survival as an administrative detail someone else was supposed to handle.

Sorek treats him with the mixture of respect, irritation, and guarded affection normally reserved for a younger brother who has become too large to physically restrain.

## The Crown

Sorek’s loyalty is to **Gorundia, lawful authority, and competent command**, roughly in that order.

He serves the Crown faithfully, but he does not believe kings become infallible when crowned.

A king who is wrong should be told.

A king who is dangerous should be stopped.

A king who bears the burden honestly should not be abandoned merely because the work has become ugly.

Sorek was publicly named among those who stood for the Crown during the [[Steel Purge]], placing him firmly within the trusted royal military circle.

## Combat

Sorek is a master swordsman and highly experienced battlefield combatant.

His style favors:

- Precision
- Timing
- Economy of movement
- Defensive positioning
- Counterattacks
- Using reach and size without relying upon them
- Allowing younger opponents to exhaust themselves dramatically

He was once known for overwhelming physical force.

Age has made him more dangerous.

He no longer wastes motion proving how strong he is.

## Professional Strengths

Sorek is particularly skilled as a:

- Field commander
- Investigator
- Interrogator
- Mediator
- Military administrator
- Trainer
- Frontier lord
- Judge of character
- Political fixer
- Reader of rooms
- Reluctant mentor

His greatest talent is turning chaos into the next three necessary actions.

He does not need to be the man who personally solves every problem. He identifies the person best suited to solve it, gives them the framing they need, and steps aside without surrendering control.

This has repeatedly allowed younger knights to become heroes while Sorek remains the man who put them in the correct corridor.

## Habits

Sorek’s established habits include:

- Maintaining his armor and weapons meticulously
- Wearing old, comfortable clothing when off duty
- Carrying a battered flask
- Smoking a pipe when circumstances permit
- Keeping detailed field notes
- Watching doors and windows automatically
- Sitting where he can see the room
- Remembering the names of dead soldiers
- Having a soft spot for dogs
- Pretending not to care about people whose welfare he has already arranged

## Heraldry

The arms of House Redmarch are:

> **Sable, a sword inverted Argent, hilt Gules.**

A silver sword points downward upon a black field, its hilt red.

The arms appear upon the Redmarch banner and official seals.

Sorek does not display them upon his shield or personal clothing.

He has seen too many men use heraldry as a substitute for character.

## Voice

Sorek speaks in short, plain sentences.

His voice is low, rough, and often tired. He avoids speeches when an instruction will do.

He uses:

- Dry understatement
- Soldier’s language
- Brief questions
- Uncomfortable silences
- Metaphors involving roads, weather, steel, dogs, and graves
- Humor placed precisely where other men might offer reassurance

He rarely says exactly how he feels.

He says what must be done next.

## Representative Quotes

> “I’ve seen kings come and go. A man who stands up when the world is trying to knock him down is rarer.”

> “Loyalty is glass. Strong enough, until somebody decides to test it with a hammer.”

> “I’m not a hero. I just know which way the sword swings. Most people never bother to learn.”

> “You give her dignity. Right now, that is what is actually at risk.”

> “This is the part people remember. Make it a good story.”

> “A siege is just patience with bookkeeping.”

> “The lad is brave. That is not the same as ready.”

> “You can call it a victory after we count who gets to go home.”

## Role in the Campaign

Sorek serves as:

- A reliable senior commander
- A source of military and political judgment
- A link between Theron’s old order and Beringer’s new one
- A mentor who understands the cost of making heroes
- A Crown loyalist who remains capable of criticism
- A narrator for the less glorious machinery of war
- A reminder that survival is not the same as escaping unhurt

His central conflict is not whether he will continue serving.

He will.

The question is how much of himself remains after a lifetime spent being the man who carries on when everyone else is allowed to grieve.
