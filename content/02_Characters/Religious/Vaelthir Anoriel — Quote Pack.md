# **Vaelthir Anoriel — Quote Pack**

## 🔪 Sharper (but controlled, not ranty)

> “You would ask the wolf to eat grass, so as not to offend the deer.”

> “You travel with hounds and wolves, yet wince when the hunt begins.”

> “You mistake discomfort for harm.”

> “You call it cruelty. I call it timing.”

> “You want the outcome… without the step that creates it.”

> “If the rot offends you, you should have spoken sooner—when it was still a seed.”

> “There is no gentle version of this. Only slower ones.”

> “We can take time. The land will not.”

> “What you propose will work… for a while.”

> “You are thinking of the people in front of you. That is why you are here.”
