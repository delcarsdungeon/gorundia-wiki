# Kommissar Dragan Veyr

**Alias:** Marshal-Captain Dragan Vale  
**Role:** Field Kommissar of the Iron Writ Company  
**Allegiance:** Dominion of Kesh; Iron Synod of Hextor  
**Superior:** Higher Kommissar Vesna Koryava  
**Status:** Active, deep-cover operative in former Vogel lands

## Overview

Kommissar Dragan Veyr is the field commander and discipline officer of the Iron Writ Company. Publicly, he operates under the name Marshal-Captain Dragan Vale, a bonded foreign mercenary hired for road security and rebel suppression. In truth, he is a Hextorite priest and Dominion field kommissar.

Dragan is not a subtle mastermind. He is Vesna Koryava’s doctrine made physical: disciplined, brutal, patient, and utterly committed to the belief that obedience is the only reliable form of truth.

## Appearance

Dragan is a broad, thick-necked man in his late forties with close-cropped iron-gray hair, a broken nose, and a short beard. He wears scarred blackened plate, a dark red field scarf, and a heavy gauntlet concealing a Hextorite symbol in the palm.

His preferred weapon is a flanged mace called **Correction**.

## Public Cover

As Marshal-Captain Dragan Vale, he claims to be a marcher veteran and neutral contract officer. He presents the Iron Writ Company as disciplined, useful, and legally bonded.

His paperwork is excellent.

Too excellent.

## Personality

Dragan is blunt, controlled, and almost impossible to frighten. He does not indulge in theatrical cruelty. He believes punishment is a tool, fear is a language, and mercy is useful only when it produces submission.

## Methods

### The Three Questions

Before punishment, Dragan asks:

> “Who commanded you?”
> “Who sheltered you?”
> “Who profits if you stay silent?”

The answers matter less than the fear created by asking.

### Public Mercy

Dragan often spares one person and gives them a task: carry a writ, name collaborators, bury the dead, or repeat his judgment to the next village.

### The Iron Tithe

Villages deemed unreliable surrender weapons, laborers, written confessions, and hostages for “future testimony.”

## Relationship with Vesna Koryava

Vesna defines doctrine. Dragan applies pressure. He obeys her completely and reveres her as a superior instrument of Hextorite order.

## Sample Lines

> “I do not need you guilty. I need you useful.”

> “A village is not loyal because it says so. A village is loyal when it fears lying.”

> “No, priest. I am not mocking your god. I am demonstrating mine.”
