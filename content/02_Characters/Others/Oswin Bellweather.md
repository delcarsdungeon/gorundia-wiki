## **Mayor Oswin Bellweather**

Full style:

> **Mayor Oswin Bellweather of South Desalle**

He has the perfect name for a man whose entire civic function is ringing the bell _after_ the disaster is already staged properly.

### Vibe

Oswin Bellweather is tidy, pleasant, and aggressively rehearsed. He has soft hands, clean boots, and the strained smile of a man who has said “we are grateful for the Count’s generosity” while standing in front of a suspiciously freshly painted barn with no animals in it.

He is not necessarily evil. He is a coward, a functionary, and a survivalist. Which, in politics, is apparently a whole career path.

### Public-facing description

> Mayor Oswin Bellweather is the appointed civic head of South Desalle, a model village frequently shown to visitors as proof of the late baronial administration’s benevolence. He is polite, well-spoken, and very careful never to answer a question directly when a pleasant phrase might die in its place.

### Table line

> “Naturally, my lord, the village prospers. The people are content, the ovens are warm, the accounts are balanced, and any suggestion otherwise is almost certainly the work of malcontents from North Desalle.”

### His tells

- Uses “model village” too often.
- Refers to villagers by household role, not name.
- Knows exactly which cottages are safe to show visitors.
- Panics if someone asks to see the grain stores, infirmary, graveyard, or old tax ledgers.
- Has one “random peasant family” ready for inspections, and they are clearly over-rehearsed.
