# Magister Ilyan Korzun

**Public Alias:** Master Ilyan Korvin  
**Role:** Company Notary, Signal-Magus, and Right Hand to Kommissar Dragan Veyr  
**True Role:** Dominion wizard-clerk, Secret Chancery liaison, communications officer of the Iron Writ Company  
**Affiliation:** Dominion of Kesh; Secret Chancery; Iron Writ Company  
**Faith:** Publicly secular; privately Hextorite-compliant  
**Superior in Field:** [[Kommissar Dragan Veyr]]  
**Strategic Superior:** [[Higher Kommissar Vesna Koryava]]  
**Status:** Active, deep-cover operative in former Vogel lands

## Overview

Magister Ilyan Korzun is the administrative spine of the [[Iron Writ Company 1]]. Publicly known as Master Ilyan Korvin, he serves as the company's notary, signal-magus, and record officer. In truth, he is a Dominion wizard-clerk and Secret Chancery liaison embedded beside [[Kommissar Dragan Veyr]].

Dragan breaks villages. Korzun files the breakage, certifies it, and sends the copy upstairs before the bodies are cold.

Korzun is not theatrical, not openly cruel, and not especially brave in the martial sense. His horror is procedural. He believes that if the writ is valid, the act is valid. If the category is assigned, the treatment is justified. If the report is accepted, the truth has been established.

## Appearance

Korzun is a narrow, tidy man in his early fifties with thinning brown-gray hair, pale hands, and small round spectacles. He wears a dark civilian coat over a concealed mail shirt, polished boots, black gloves, and a high collar.

He carries a black leather dispatch case containing coded message strips, copper sending mirrors, wax seals, warrant blanks, spell-marked ink, narrow ledgers, and a collapsible writing board. An ivory pointer serves as both map tool and spellcasting focus.

His fingers are usually stained with ink, never blood. He would consider blood on documents unprofessional.

## Personality

Korzun is polite, exact, and nearly empty of visible emotion. He does not rant or threaten unless the threat has been phrased as procedure.

He does not say "hostages." He says **retained witnesses**.

He does not say "execution." He says **final correction**.

He does not say "torture." He says **coercive clarification**.

He does not say "we burned the village." He says **combustible denial measures were enacted following persistent noncompliance**.

He is not a sadist in the common sense. He is worse: a man who can destroy a household without raising his pulse because the ledger balanced.

## Role in the Iron Writ Company

Korzun keeps the [[Iron Writ Company 1]] connected and legally disguised. He maintains magical communications, authenticates field orders, tracks prisoners, prepares corrected confessions, verifies seals, and classifies settlements by loyalty risk.

His duties include:

- Maintaining magical communication with [[Higher Kommissar Vesna Koryava]] and Dominion handlers.
- Coordinating scattered Iron Writ detachments.
- Issuing authenticated field writs.
- Tracking prisoners, witnesses, seized property, and "retained persons."
- Preparing or correcting confessions.
- Forging continuity between real local warrants and Dominion operations.
- Classifying villages by loyalty risk and future usefulness.
- Ensuring every atrocity can be described as lawful correction.

## Magic

Korzun is not primarily a battlefield wizard. He specializes in communication, detection, documentation, and control.

Common magic includes:

- Long-distance message relays
- Mirror-speech
- Encoded sending
- Seal verification
- Document duplication
- Anti-tamper ink
- Prisoner tagging
- Silence fields
- Sleep or paralysis
- Truth-compulsion support
- Fear-dampening for friendly troops
- Map-marking and movement tracking

He avoids dramatic spellwork unless forced. Fireballs are for amateurs and people with poor filing habits.

## Relationship with Dragan Veyr

Dragan does not like Korzun, but he respects him. Dragan finds him bloodless and fussy. Korzun finds Dragan blunt, useful, and sometimes inefficient.

Their partnership works because each understands the other’s purpose.

Dragan applies force.

Korzun makes the force legible.

> **Dragan:** "The village sheltered rebels."  
> **Korzun:** "Unproven."  
> **Dragan:** "Then prove it."  
> **Korzun:** "Retroactively or prospectively?"  
> **Dragan:** "Does it matter?"  
> **Korzun:** "Only to the file."

## Relationship with Vesna Koryava

Korzun fears [[Higher Kommissar Vesna Koryava]] more than Dragan. Dragan can kill him. Vesna understands him.

Vesna uses Korzun to monitor Dragan, evaluate field results, compare village compliance outcomes, and determine when the Iron Writ should escalate, withdraw, or reveal its true nature.

Korzun's reports to Vesna are concise, cold, and precise.

## Sample Lines

> "Your innocence has been noted. It is not presently relevant."

> "You are not being punished. You are being reclassified."

> "The correction was authorized before your objection was received."

> "Mercy remains available pending compliance."

> "No, Marshal-Captain, I did not forge the confession. I regularized it."

> "The dead are statistically quiet. Survivors are more useful."

> "Please do not bleed on the witness roll."

## Adventure Use

Korzun should make the Iron Writ Company feel larger, smarter, and harder to expose. Killing Dragan may end a terror action. Capturing Korzun may reveal the network.

His dispatch case is a major intelligence prize. It may contain coded links to Vesna, Dominion handlers, compromised Gorundian officials, false warrants, and village compliance ledgers.

Korzun should not fight to the death unless cornered. He will retreat, erase records, abandon subordinates, and preserve the archive.
