# **Professor Vianola Erinleaf**

_Master Enchantress of Gorundia’s University of Magery_

---
![[Vionola Erinleaf.png|320]]
## **Overview**

Vianola Erinleaf is a high elf of nearly four centuries—an academic fixture of the [[University of Magery]] and one of the foremost living authorities on enchantment in Gorundia.

For sixty years, she has taught not the art of persuasion, but the **mechanics of will itself**—how it bends, how it breaks, and why it must sometimes be left untouched.

She is, by reputation, uncompromising.

By reality, she is worse.

---
## **Appearance**

Tall and composed, Vianola bears the refined, angular features of her kind, framed by thick, curly red hair. Her gray eyes are sharp, still, and deeply observant—less like a scholar examining a problem, and more like a surgeon deciding where to cut.

Her robes are of fine make but muted color—deep greens, charcoals, and wine-dark tones. No unnecessary enchantments, no ornament meant to impress.

If she is displaying power, something has already gone wrong.

---
## **Demeanor**

- **Controlled Presence:** She does not dominate a room—she _stabilizes_ it.
- **Intellectual Precision:** Sloppy thinking irritates her more than arrogance.
- **Dry, Surgical Wit:** Rare, cutting, and usually deserved.
- **Earned Loyalty:** She gives it sparingly—and expects it returned in full.

Students fear disappointing her more than they fear failing.

---
## **Philosophy of Enchantment**

> “You are not changing a thought.  
> You are changing the person who would have had it.”

Vianola teaches enchantment as a **moral discipline disguised as a technical one**.

Her core tenets:

- Influence is easy. **Understanding consequence is not.**
- Memory is identity. Alter one, and you trespass upon the other.
- Compulsion is not persuasion—it is **the removal of choice**

She draws a hard, unambiguous line:

> “If a spell leaves a man unable to refuse you—  
> you have not convinced him.  
> You have replaced him.”

---
## **Academic Focus**

### **Enchantment (Primary Discipline)**

- Will-binding
- Suggestion structures
- Emotional and cognitive influence mechanics

### **Arcane Psychology (Secondary Discipline)**

- Memory alteration and degradation
- Identity persistence under magical interference
- Behavioral drift following enchantment exposure

Her lectures frequently blur into ethical interrogation.

Students are expected to answer.

---
## **Position Within Gorundia**

Vianola is a **loyal servant of the Crown**, but not a political creature.

- She will consult when called
- She will advise truthfully—even when inconvenient
- She will not maneuver, scheme, or court influence

This has made her **trusted**—and occasionally avoided.

She is known to have declined positions that would place her closer to court.

---
## **Position on Coercive Magic**

Vianola is a **hardline opponent** of coercive mind-control.

Not because she lacks the skill—

—but because she understands it too well.

> “There are spells we _can_ cast, and spells we _should not._  
> Only fools confuse the two.”

---
## **Maurakappi Research**

Recent events have drawn her attention to the [[Maurakappi]] and their use of **larval cognitive influence** .

Her stance is… complicated.

- **Fascinated** by the sophistication of pheromonal communication and identity drift
- **Horrified** by the erosion of autonomy in implanted hosts
- Actively researching the long-term psychological effects of such influence

Privately, she has stated:

> “This is not enchantment.  
> This is architecture.”

She is one of the few minds in Gorundia capable of even beginning to understand it.

Which is not, necessarily, a comfort.

---
## **Reputation at the University**

- One of the most demanding instructors in the institution
- Produces elite enchanters—or breaks mediocre ones early
- Frequently consulted in matters where magic and ethics intersect
- Known to end debates—and careers—with written critiques alone

Her classes are always full.

No one attends casually.

---
## **Private Habits**

- Late-night study of ancient and obscure texts
- Prefers solitude to academic politics
- Keeps a quiet ritual of cherry brandy and reading

Those rare enough to be invited into her private study quickly realize:

She is far less guarded—

—and far more dangerous—when relaxed.

---
## **Relationship with [[Norwin Stoneyeye]]**

Vianola and Norwin shared a rare intellectual rapport.

Where she is controlled, he is exploratory.  
Where she is precise, he is inventive.

Their debates became a fixture of university life—often continuing long past reason, fueled by mutual respect and shared curiosity.

She considers him one of the few minds worth arguing with.

Which, from her, is high praise.

---
## **Aide: Arannis Valeir**

A constant presence at her side, Arannis Valeir is a long-lived elf whose exact age is difficult to pin down—likely over three centuries.

### **Role**

- Senior teaching aide
- Research assistant
- Quiet gatekeeper to her time and attention

### **Demeanor**

- Soft-spoken, observant, unfailingly polite
- Possesses a near-perfect memory for lectures, citations, and prior conversations
- Intervenes only when necessary—and always effectively

### **Relationship to Vianola**

Arannis is not merely an assistant.

He has chosen to remain at her side **indefinitely**, so long as she permits it.

Why, exactly, is unclear.

Speculation includes:

- Devotion to her work
- Personal loyalty bordering on reverence
- A long-standing intellectual partnership not publicly acknowledged

Vianola allows it.

Which is answer enough.

---
## **Use in Play**

Vianola functions as:

- A **mentor who enforces consequences**
- A **moral authority on mind-affecting magic**
- A **trusted but reluctant advisor to power**
- A **bridge to understanding alien cognitive systems (Maurakappi, etc.)**

She will not tell players what to do.

She will make sure they understand what it _means_ if they do it.
