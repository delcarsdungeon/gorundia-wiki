# Delcar Blackheart

> “Foreknowledge is not prophecy. Sometimes the future is merely behaving badly enough to become obvious.”

**Full Name:** Delcar Blackheart  
**Count Delcar Blackheart**  
**Archmage of Blackheart**  
Count of Blackheart, senior arcane adviser to the Crown of Furyondy
**Realm:** Furyondy  
**Seat:** County Blackheart  
**Occupation:** Noble, wizard, senior arcane adviser  
**Public Reputation:** Brilliant, loyal, deeply useful, and faintly unsettling  

---

# Overview

Count Delcar Blackheart is one of Furyondy’s senior noble wizards and has held the County of Blackheart for roughly twenty years.

He is also recognized as a **High Magus**, an old and prestigious Furyondian arcane title denoting exceptional standing and experience rather than command of a formal magical hierarchy.

The office carries influence rather than direct authority.

A High Magus may be consulted on matters involving:

- magical threats,
- national defense,
- arcane intelligence,
- unusual military problems,
- ancient lore,
- and matters in which ordinary advisers have reached the limits of ordinary expertise.

Delcar has never shown much interest in turning that influence into a political faction of his own.

This has not prevented him from acquiring considerable influence anyway.

---

# Archmage of Blackheart

In Delcar’s case, **Archmage of Blackheart** is primarily an advisory distinction.

He does not command every wizard in Furyondy.

He does not administer a royal magical bureaucracy.

He is one of the people rulers consult when the question has become sufficiently strange, old, dangerous, or magically complicated that conventional expertise is no longer enough.

His opinions carry weight because he has an irritating habit of having considered problems years before everyone else notices them.

---

# Before the Greyhawk Wars

Delcar’s reputation was secured before the Greyhawk Wars fully erupted.

He was among those who believed the gathering threats around Furyondy were not temporary border troubles and that the kingdom should prepare for something substantially worse.

He advocated preparation while preparation was still politically inconvenient.

Among the measures associated with his influence were:

- strengthening magical defenses,
- preserving strategic reserves,
- identifying vulnerable routes and fortifications,
- preparing for disrupted communications,
- studying the likely use of hostile magic,
- and taking seriously enemies whom more comfortable voices preferred to dismiss.

When war eventually came, some of those precautions proved extremely valuable.

This is the foundation of Delcar’s reputation at court.

He was not right about everything.

He was right early enough to matter.

---

# Castle Eyeberen

Delcar is strongly associated with **Castle Eyeberen**, where his magical expertise and defensive preparations became particularly important during the years surrounding the Greyhawk Wars.

His work there contributed heavily to his reputation as a wizard who thinks in terms of:

- fortifications,
- contingencies,
- long preparation,
- layered defenses,
- and surviving the first disaster well enough to respond to the second.

He is considerably less impressed by spectacular magic than by magic that is still functioning when everything else has gone wrong.

---

# County Blackheart

County Blackheart has developed a somewhat unusual reputation under Delcar.

It is orderly without being particularly fashionable.

Practical competence is valued heavily.

People with unconventional backgrounds have historically found more opportunity there than in many noble households, provided they demonstrate loyalty and responsibility.

Delcar has a particular tendency to judge people by:

- courage,
- competence,
- loyalty,
- and what they do when someone weaker has been placed in their care.

This has produced a household and extended following containing a number of people who might have struggled to find comfortable places elsewhere.

Critics occasionally describe Blackheart as a collection of respectable misfits.

Delcar has never appeared offended by this.

---

# Neltara Blackheart

Delcar is married to [[Neltara Blackheart]].

Neltara is a Tallfellow halfling noblewoman and one of the more socially capable members of the Blackheart household.

Where Delcar is reserved, cerebral, and occasionally alarming without apparently trying, Neltara is charismatic, politically agile, and considerably easier to underestimate.

Their marriage has endured long enough that attempts to treat Neltara as merely the High Magus’s pleasant little wife have become a reliable method of making very expensive mistakes.

Delcar appears to find this amusing.

Quietly.

---

# Public Reputation

Delcar is widely regarded as:

- exceptionally intelligent,
- magically formidable,
- politically cautious,
- strategically farsighted,
- personally loyal to Furyondy,
- difficult to surprise,
- and somewhat uncomfortable company.

The last quality is difficult to define.

He is not habitually rude.

He does not threaten people for amusement.

He is capable of dry humor and even genuine warmth.

Nonetheless, people meeting him for the first time frequently come away with the impression that **Delcar is watching slightly more of the conversation than everyone else is**.

Among lesser wizards, Delcar is famous. Among greater wizards, he is known.

---

# Loyalty

Delcar’s loyalty to Furyondy has been tested repeatedly enough that serious accusations of treason have never stuck.

Foreign observers have nevertheless regarded him with varying degrees of suspicion.

He has been described in diplomatic assessments as difficult to categorize.

This may be partly due to his personality.

It may also be because Delcar rarely discusses his life before entering Furyondian service in any useful detail.

When pressed, he tends to answer truthfully.

This is not always the same thing as answering completely.

---

# Celene

Delcar has an old and complicated relationship with Celene.

Approximately twenty-five years ago, he underwent an unusual act of contrition there.

Accounts differ regarding exactly what prompted it.

The better-known version says Delcar crossed a memorial path barefoot, passing markers representing victims of old elven violence and tragedy.

Whatever occurred, the act appears to have changed his standing with certain Celene authorities.

It did not produce friendship.

It produced something closer to:

**acknowledged penance.**

Delcar does not discuss the matter casually.

Those who know more tend to follow his example.

---
### The Circle of Eight

Delcar is **not a member of the Circle of Eight** and has never claimed otherwise.

He does, however, maintain professional contact with members of that unusually influential group and has served as one of Furyondy’s more reliable points of contact when the interests of the Circle intersect with the kingdom.

The relationship is deliberately informal.

Delcar may:

- exchange intelligence concerning major magical threats;
- provide or request specialist research;
- convey Furyondian concerns;
- assist with problems touching Furyondian territory;
- and occasionally receive warnings whose sources are not discussed in polite company.

He possesses no authority over the Circle, nor they over him.

Exactly how well he knows individual members is not something Delcar generally elaborates upon.

---
# Age

Delcar’s precise age is rarely stated.

He is clearly older than his appearance would initially suggest.

Exactly how much older is a subject on which polite company generally manages not to speculate aloud.

Neltara finds questions about this significantly funnier than Delcar does.

---

# Languages and Scholarship

Delcar possesses an unusually broad linguistic education.

He has demonstrated familiarity with several old forms of speech that are unusual even among experienced scholars.

His pronunciation of certain archaic languages has occasionally been described as:

> “Uncomfortably good.”

He has particular knowledge of:

- old magical traditions,
- subterranean ruins,
- planar hazards,
- elven history,
- ancient fortifications,
- forgotten military magic,
- and the ways powerful civilizations arrange their own destruction.

When asked where he learned some particularly obscure piece of information, Delcar often replies:

> “Experience.”

Further questions are not always rewarded.

---

# Names and Identity

Delcar is unusually precise about names.

He dislikes casual magical alteration of identity, memory, or appearance and tends to treat questions involving disguised persons more seriously than most wizards.

He has also been known to distinguish carefully between:

- what someone is called,
- what they were called,
- and what they choose to be called now.

People who ask why generally receive a different subject.

---

# Magic

Delcar is an extremely experienced wizard.

His public reputation emphasizes preparation rather than theatricality.

He favors magic that:

- gathers information,
- denies an opponent options,
- controls terrain,
- protects allies,
- preserves escape routes,
- counters hostile spellcasters,
- and allows dangerous situations to be approached on favorable terms.

He is fully capable of spectacular destructive magic.

He simply considers needing to use it evidence that earlier stages of the plan may have failed.

---

# Military Philosophy

Delcar’s experience surrounding the Greyhawk Wars left him with little patience for assumptions that magical superiority automatically produces victory.

He frequently emphasizes:

- logistics,
- communications,
- redundancy,
- retreat plans,
- intelligence,
- prepared positions,
- and ordinary soldiers who know what they are supposed to do when the wizard is unavailable.

One of his better-known observations is:

> “Any defense that requires its greatest wizard to remain conscious is not yet a defense.”

---

# Temperament

Delcar is calm almost to the point of discomfort.

He rarely raises his voice.

Anger usually makes him quieter.

He listens carefully and dislikes repeating himself.

He is capable of great patience with genuine ignorance.

He has substantially less patience for deliberate stupidity.

He has a strong protective streak toward people placed under his responsibility and reacts particularly badly to the abuse of dependents, prisoners, servants, or subordinates.

---

# Social Manner

Delcar is polite without being especially sociable.

He does not waste conversation.

He tends to let other people speak until they reveal what they actually want.

Long silences do not trouble him.

This occasionally results in other people filling those silences with information they had not intended to provide.

Neltara has accused him of doing this deliberately.

Delcar has never provided a useful denial.

---

# Appearance

Delcar presents himself with restrained elegance rather than ostentation.

His clothing is expensive but conservative and generally selected for function.

He favors darker colors, particularly when traveling.

His appearance is carefully maintained without seeming fashionable.

There is something slightly timeless about him.

Portraits painted years apart sometimes make this more noticeable than meeting him in person does.

---

# Oddities Commonly Noted

People who have spent significant time around Delcar occasionally remark upon small peculiarities.

None amount to anything particularly significant by themselves.

Among them:

- an unusually good memory for underground spaces;
- remarkable comfort in very low light;
- obscure knowledge of old elven disputes;
- careful language concerning his youth;
- surprising familiarity with customs far outside Furyondy;
- reluctance to discuss his ancestry;
- and a tendency to notice magical disguises with uncomfortable speed.

Neltara treats most speculation about her husband as excellent entertainment.

Delcar does not.

---

# Relationship With Authority

Delcar advises rulers.

He does not appear eager to become one beyond the responsibilities already attached to County Blackheart.

This distinction matters to him.

He has spent enough time around powerful people to regard the desire for additional authority as something deserving examination rather than admiration.

He prefers:

> competence with limits

to

> ambition with good intentions.

---

# Reputation Among Wizards

Among professional spellcasters, Delcar is respected more than loved.

Younger wizards frequently discover that presenting an ingenious magical solution to Delcar results in questions such as:

> “What happens when it is dispelled?”

or:

> “What does the enemy do while you are casting it?”

or, particularly cruelly:

> “And if you are dead?”

He considers these useful questions.

Students have other words.

# Sample Quotes

### On preparation

> “Planning for disaster does not summon it.”

> “Failure to plan merely ensures that disaster finds comfortable accommodations.”

### On prophecy

> “I am not a prophet.”

> “People insist upon calling obvious consequences prophecy because it excuses them for ignoring the causes.”

### On magic

> “Power is useful.”

> “Preparation is usually cheaper.”

### On war

> “The first duty of a fortress is not to be impressive.”

> “It is to remain a fortress tomorrow.”

### On identity

> “People are entitled to become more than the circumstances of their birth.”

If pressed:

> “I consider the matter rather important.”

### On Blackheart

> “I have little interest in where a person began.”

> “I am considerably more interested in whom they protect when things become difficult.”

# Player-Facing Summary

Count Delcar Blackheart is one of Furyondy’s most experienced noble wizards and holds the respected title of **High Magus**.

His warnings and preparations before the Greyhawk Wars helped Furyondy survive threats many others had underestimated.

He is brilliant, reserved, loyal, strategically cautious, and mildly unsettling.

Delcar has served Furyondy faithfully for decades.

There are older chapters of his life about which he says very little.

Those who seem to know those chapters generally appear content to leave them closed.
