### Mirelle Frostwind

 

> “She has the good sense not to marry a noble, and the worse sense to marry a knight instead. At least the garden flourishes.” – Sorek Redmarch

 

- **Full Name:** Mirelle Frostwind
- **Age:** 24
- **Allegiance:** Castle Kharne
- **Marital Status:** Married to Sir Alden Frostwind
- **Known For:** Quiet wisdom, kindness, and the herb gardens of Castle Kharne

 **Overview:** Born the daughter of a village healer, Mirelle grew up among herbs and remedies rather than courts or castles. Warm-hearted and witty, she has adjusted to life alongside Alden with surprising ease. She carries no title and has no pretensions—she is simply the wife of a knight—but her presence has already made her a fixture in the life of Castle Kharne. **Current Situation:** Mirelle tends a thriving herb garden in the castle grounds and helps the household healers with salves and tonics. She offers counsel freely to squires, servants, and knights alike, never as someone above them, but as someone who listens and understands. Her good humor and practical hands make her quietly indispensable. **Personality:** Gentle, wry, and steadfast. Mirelle is protective of Alden but never smothers him; she delights in his earnestness even as she teases him for it. She is grounded, pragmatic, and brave in her own quiet way. **Appearance:** Dark-haired, with warm brown eyes and a ready smile. Her dresses are simple and often stained with soil or herbs, and her hands are roughened by work. She has a plain but comforting beauty, more hearthfire than court jewel. **Court Gossip:**

- Braga tolerates Mirelle because she is useful, though some whisper that the baroness keeps a wary eye on her influence over Alden.
- Servants adore her, as she tends injuries and shares food without judgment.
- Aylassa sometimes sends her anonymous baskets of squash, keeping the old joke alive. Mirelle takes it as friendly mischief, never guessing the larger truth.

 **DM Notes:** Mirelle is an anchor NPC for Castle Kharne: she provides healing, perspective, and emotional grounding. She can soften scenes of intrigue or violence by offering players a reminder of “ordinary” goodness. If the truth of Alden’s liberation is ever revealed, she will be the voice urging him to endure—either as his strength or as the one left to steady him if his faith cracks.
