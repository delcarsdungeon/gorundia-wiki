# **Ser Garran Bronnel**

_Master of Physical Security (Acting), Bordella Holdings_


> [quote!] “Bronnel? He’s not here to win arguments. He’s here to make sure they don’t need finishing.  If Skell is the law on paper… Bronnel’s what it looks like when it stands up.” - Sorek Redmarch

---

**Title:** Ser (knighted retainer, not landed) 
**Allegiance:** Sworn to **House Moffet**
**Current Posting:** Bordella Lands (under Moffet mandate)
**Role:** Head of Physical Security / Enforcement 
**Age:** Late 50s 
**Status:** Active   
**Reputation:** “The man you send when talking has already failed”

## **Appearance**

- Thick, compact build — age has not softened him
- Weathered face, deep lines, heavy brow
- Short, practical greying hair
- Permanent stubble or rough-cut beard
- Eyes that track movement, not conversation

**Armor & Dress:**

- Dark brigandine over chain
- Functional, worn, maintained—not decorative
- Heavy belt, sword always present
- **Gold medallion of office** — black field, stylized gold tower (rook)

The medallion matters.

It marks him as:

> the hand of authority, not the voice of it

He does not loom.

He doesn’t need to.

The way he stands—square, balanced, always aware of where every person in the room is—does the work for him. Standing next to taller men, he doesn’t look smaller. He looks like the one you’d regret choosing first.

---
## **Overview**

Ser Garran Bronnel is a sworn sword of House Moffet, placed in Bordella territory to impose order where authority has become… negotiable.

He is not a Bordella man.  
He does not pretend to be one.

His authority exists in a narrow, dangerous space between:

- Moffet expectation
- Bordella tolerance
- and the simple reality that someone has to keep the place from tearing itself apart

Bronnel understands this balance intimately—and survives in it by being:

> useful, controlled, and absolutely reliable when things turn ugly

---
## **Personality**

Bronnel is a professional.

Not cruel.  
Not kind.  
Not interested in being liked.

- Speaks plainly, briefly
    
- Observes constantly
    
- Does not bluff
    
- Does not escalate unless necessary
    
- Does not hesitate when escalation is required
    

He has no patience for:

- politics as theater
    
- officers who posture
    
- men who mistake rank for competence
    

He respects:

- discipline
    
- clarity
    
- people who understand consequences
    

---

## **Command Structure & Authority**

Bronnel’s authority is **delegated, not inherent**, and he knows it.

- Holds **operational control** over security matters when invoked
    
- Can **detain, remove, or isolate threats** within defined limits
    
- Avoids stepping into noble jurisdiction unless forced
    

He survives by:

> never overreaching… and never failing when given the line

---

## **The Moffet Bootmen**

Bronnel’s preferred instruments.

### Composition

- Veteran soldiers drawn from House Moffet retainers
    
- Rotated in small numbers to avoid political friction
    
- Quietly embedded where possible
    

### Characteristics

- Disciplined
    
- Professional
    
- Loyal to Bronnel first, Moffet second, politics last
    

They are not:

- decorative guards
    
- noble-born officers
    
- sycophants
    

They are:

> men who understand that sometimes the job is ugly, and still do it cleanly

### Operational Use

- Bronnel deploys them for:
    
    - arrests that must _stick_
        
    - removals that must be _quiet_
        
    - situations where hesitation gets people killed
        

### Reputation

Among soldiers:

> “If Bronnel brings his Bootmen, it’s already decided.”

---

## **Bordella Forces (Local Reality)**

Bronnel is not a fool.

He knows:

- He is outnumbered **ten to one or worse**
    
- Bordella men control the ground, the gates, and the gossip
    
- Open conflict is suicide, no matter how right he is
    

### His Approach

- Uses Bordella men when he must
    
- Keeps orders **clear, simple, and limited**
    
- Avoids giving them room to:
    
    - interpret
        
    - delay
        
    - undermine
        

He does not trust them.

But he does not openly oppose them either.

Because:

> surviving the job matters more than winning arguments

---

## **Relationship: House Moffet**

Bronnel’s loyalty to Moffet is not ceremonial.

It is earned.

- They gave him position without demanding pretense
    
- They value results over pedigree
    
- They trust him to handle problems others avoid
    

In return:

> He does the jobs they cannot be seen doing.

---

## **Relationship: Bordella Authority**

Complicated. Tense. Functional.

- Seen as an outsider
    
- Tolerated because he is effective
    
- Quietly resented by lesser officers
    
- Occasionally relied upon by those who know better
    

Among Bordella men:

> “He’s not ours… but when things go bad, he’s the one you want.”

---

## **Relationship: Arch-Provost Halvaren Skell**

Professional alignment. Minimal friction.

They are very different men—  
but operate on the same principle:

> The system must function.

- Skell defines structure
    
- Bronnel enforces reality
    

They do not socialize.  
They do not debate.

They coordinate.

Efficiently.

---

## **Tactics & Methods**

Bronnel does not improvise theatrics.

He solves problems.

### Preferred Methods

- Early positioning over reaction
    
- Quiet intervention over public spectacle
    
- Controlled force over chaotic escalation
    

### When Violence Happens

It is:

- fast
    
- decisive
    
- limited to objective
    

No speeches.  
No warnings beyond what is necessary.

---

## **Known Behaviors**

- Stands where he can see exits
    
- Watches hands, not faces
    
- Keeps trusted men close, unknown men distant
    
- Rarely sits unless the room is secure

## **Assessment**

Ser Garran Bronnel is not a political figure.

He is not a noble.

He is not a hero.

He is:

> a necessary man in an unstable place

As long as he remains:

- useful
    
- controlled
    
- and alive
    

…the system holds.

If he fails—

it won’t fail loudly.

It will fail all at once.
